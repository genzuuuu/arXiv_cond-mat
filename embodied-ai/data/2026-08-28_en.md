# Robotics & Embodied AI Papers Summary

## Dexterous Manipulation & Robotic Hands

**[Pre-training Visual Dexterity in Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15917)** — Introduces SPD, a pre-training framework for dexterous manipulation using VR-collected data in simulation (75 hours, 5 operators). Fine-tuning on 1-2 hours of real-world demonstrations on a 56-DoF bimanual setup outperforms training from scratch. Shows simulation teleoperation is viable for real-world dexterous manipulation.

**[Rapid On-Robot Learning for Dynamic Manipulation Skills: Robot Juggling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26800)** — Presents an online learning framework for bimanual robot juggling. Uses regularized memory-based learning that builds on prior knowledge rather than replacing it. Achieves 5 canonical three-ball juggling patterns within 5 minutes of real-world interaction, with safety guarantees via mutually reachable sets.

**[Active Surface-Driven Reconfigurable Gripper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26883)** — Novel gripper combining an active surface with underactuated compliance for grasping thin objects (books, paper, fabric). The active-surface thumb performs in-hand repositioning without arm movement. Demonstrates robust grasping and long sequential "grasp-place" tasks.

## Tactile Sensing & Soft Robotics

**[Pneumatic-Tomographic Tactile Skin for Multicontact Localization and Force Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13036)** — Dual-channel tactile skin integrating EIT layer with pneumatic pressure layer. EIT handles localization; pneumatic layer provides stable force estimation. Location-aware correction reduces force estimation RMSE by 35-60% vs EIT-only approaches.

**[Design of a Biomimetic Joint-Covering Skin with Tissue-Like Structure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23304)** — Biomimetic skin for musculoskeletal humanoids with pressure and stretch-sensitive elements. Achieves joint angle estimation with ~3° error, improving when integrated with muscle sensing.

**[Relaxation-Aware Multimodal Sensing of Soft Gripper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26622)** — Variable-stiffness soft gripper with onboard vision and infrared thermography for viscoelastic force control. Maintains desired force with 0.066N MAE over 280s grasp-and-hold tasks, outperforming baselines by 80-95%.

**[Task-space model-based control of pneumatic soft actuators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27186)** — Real-time dynamic-model-based task-space control using discrete elastic rod models. Achieves 1.5-2.3mm RMSE for precision motions and handles cross-platform generalization with different actuator geometries.

**[Tensegrity Continuum Robots Enable Task-Adaptive Morphologies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27221)** — Modular reconfigurable robots combining tensegrity-based compliant bodies with claw connections. Robots self-reconfigure into chains, loops, and branches for cooperative manipulation and locomotion.

## Visuomotor Policies & VLA Models

**[Tactile-WAM: Touch-Aware World Action Model with Tactile Asymmetric Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26663)** — World Action Model with asymmetric attention blocking video queries from tactile keys while preserving tactile access for action queries. Achieves 49.2% success on real-robot tasks vs 15.6% baseline, addressing the "tactile pollution" problem.

**[PredVLA: A Sub-Million-Parameter Predictive-Coding Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26673)** — Language-conditioned predictive-coding policy with only 0.68M trainable parameters and no robot-data pretraining. Achieves 86.9% mean success on LIBERO short-horizon suites. Demonstrates prediction-error-driven online state correction mechanism.

**[TemporalFlow-VLA: Learning Physically Grounded Execution History](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26821)** — VLA model learning compact execution history via robot-surface temporal flow supervision. Achieves 97.63% average success on LIBERO including 96.60% on LIBERO Long. Shows clear advantages on longer-horizon, multi-stage manipulation.

**[Unified Condition-Action Modeling for Accurate One-Step Action Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16153)** — UCA-Flow unifies observation conditions and actions in a shared token space for one-step generation. Improves average success by 9.3pp over strongest baseline with 45.6× and 33.4× speedups over DP3 and Simple DP3.

**[GRAFT: Grounded and Efficient Online Reinforcement Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27079)** — Online VLA adaptation framework using region-level supervision for fine-grained biomedical manipulation. Improves success rates by 25pp under matched adaptation budgets while reducing computational overhead.

**[Riemann-1.0: An Embodied World Action Model for Physical AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27033)** — Unified causal autoregressive World Action Model jointly modeling multi-view observations, robot states, and actions. Trained on 200K+ hours of interaction data. Achieves 94.3% on RoboTwin2.0, 99.0% on LIBERO, and 85.0% SR on long-horizon real-world tasks.

## World Models & Simulation

**[WALL-SS: Scaling Long-horizon World Models via Next-Scale Autoregression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26239)** — World model generating visual futures through scale-wise autoregressive generation. Enables action-controllable long-horizon robotic simulation with coherent minute-long streaming rollout under bounded memory.

**[CLAP: Cross-Embodiment Video World Models are Zero-Shot Physical Simulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27406)** — Framework for cross-embodiment action-conditioned video generation trained on internet-scale videos. Uses curriculum-based learning from latent actions to end-effector action spaces for zero-shot deployment. Approaches or surpasses state-of-the-art single-embodiment models on DROID.

**[Zero-WAM: In-Context World-Action Modeling from Human Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26103)** — Causal video-action model following in-context human video guidance for unseen tasks. Uses automatic pipeline converting robot trajectories into matched human videos. Achieves 47.0% success on 7 unseen tasks in RoboTwin 2.0.

**[Beyond Instance Slots: Semantically Rich World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22294)** — Task-conditioned world model structured around five functional roles (gripper, target, goal, relation, phase). Transforms object-centric prediction into semantic interface for planning-oriented decision making across LIBERO suites.

**[4DSynth: Controllable Procedural World Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26947)** — Procedural system generating editable 4D environments from natural language, blueprint masks, or photographs. Includes interactive navigation benchmark with reproducible failures across difficulty tiers.

## Robot Learning & Continual Learning

**[Memory Anchors for Continual Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26545)** — Identifies "Memory Anchors" - critical experiences in regions where new-task representations collapse onto old-task ones. Excluding only 10% of anchors increases catastrophic forgetting by 4.5× on LIBERO. Enriching replay buffers with anchors decreases high-conflict forgetting by 63%.

**[FLARE: Failure-Aware Framework for Autonomous Correction and Recovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26645)** — Endows VLAs with "Retry" and "Reset" mechanisms for handling execution errors. Trains recovery skills on targeted data from MLLM-based failure analysis. Significantly improves task success and robustness on contact-rich manipulation.

**[Residual Reward Models for Efficient Preference-based RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00611)** — Combines prior reward knowledge (heuristics, language-generated, IRL) with preference-learned residuals. Improves sample efficiency of PbRL methods substantially, validated on physical Franka robot.

**[STITCH-OPE: Trajectory Stitching with Guided Diffusion for OPE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20781)** — Model-based generative framework for off-policy evaluation using denoising diffusion. Prevents over-regularization by subtracting behavior policy score and generates long-horizon trajectories via stitching. Exponential variance reduction vs long-horizon trajectory diffusion.

## Teleoperation & Control

**[Constraint-Aware Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Continuum Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26273)** — PINN for static shape estimation of tendon-driven continuum robots. With 140 samples and 50% noise, reduces error by 67.88% vs data-driven ANN. Achieves 0.1597% relative configuration error with 0.1773ms inference.

**[Residual Deep RL-Based Computed Torque Control for Rehabilitation Robot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26739)** — Bounded DDPG policy supplements computed torque control for cable-driven rehabilitation robots. Improves tracking under disturbances while preserving interpretable model-based structure.

**[WALL-SS: Scaling Long-horizon World Models via Next-Scale Autoregression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26239)** — See World Models section.

## Object Navigation & Planning

**[RTNav: Towards Real-Time Zero-Shot Object Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26496)** — Navigation architecture treating inference latency and asynchronous execution as design considerations. Improves success rate by up to 11% on real-time variants of HM3D benchmarks.

**[Embodied Scene Rearrangement Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27371)** — New task requiring agents to rearrange furniture in 3D scenes using egocentric observations only. Includes ESRP-Bench with 5,400+ scene pairs showing current methods struggle significantly.

**[Decoupling Planning and Control for Instructable Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26788)** — Combines VLM planners with world-model controllers. Controllers trained to be language-instructable via trajectory relabeling. Consistently outperforms controller-only and direct VLM action-generation variants.

## Humanoid Locomotion

**[SOLO: Stable Omni-terrain Long-Horizon Perceptive Humanoid Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26583)** — Framework addressing terrain reconstruction detail loss and pointwise imitation issues. Achieves 97.5% traversal success on stress-test terrains and completes 1.5km outdoor route with zero-shot deployment.

**[Closing the Loop on the Poppy Humanoid](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26505)** — LQR-based closed-loop walking controller with learned quadratic cost function from open-loop data. Demonstrates statistically significant walking improvements on the open-source Poppy platform.

## Additional Notable Papers

**[TADP: Task-Aware Deformable Prediction for 3D Object Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27282)** — Task-aware deformable prediction head for single-stage 3D detectors. Achieves 80.91% car mAP on KITTI, with plug-and-play deformation modules transferable to other methods.

**[NVIDIA Cosmos-H-Dreams: Real-Time Generative Physics Simulation for Surgical Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24199)** — Interactive surgical world model for robotics. Streams at ~160 FPS on single workstation GPU. Supports live control via keyboard, VR headset, and surgical robot consoles.

**[Online Joint Calibration of Steering Offset and Planar LiDAR Extrinsics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26789)** — EKF-based online calibration for warehouse mobile robots. Reduces cross-track error substantially by correcting steering offset and LiDAR extrinsics during operation.
