## 一、灵巧操作与多指抓取

- **OpenDexGrasp** 提出开词汇任务导向的灵巧抓取框架，用双源监督数据让抓取保留任务功能语义，无需单独的手势到姿态推断阶段。[OpenDexGrasp: Open-vocabulary Task-Oriented Dexterous Grasping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18117)
- **Grasping by interconnection** 用粗物体模板+人类抓取类型+顺从滑动接触设计闭合运动规划，无反馈下容忍约 1cm 尺寸误差和数厘米位姿误差，在 Shadow 手上抓取 82.5% 日常物体。[Grasping by interconnection: robust closing motions from coarse object templates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19228)
- **TacSushi** 触觉接地的世界-动作模型，指端触觉经门控融合进入动作表示，训练时用未来后果监督，在寿司操作任务上显著提升。[TacSushi: Tactile-Grounded World-Action Modeling for Dexterous Sushi Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19613)
- **DexTouch-WM** 从人类触觉学习动作条件触觉世界模型，人机共享传感布局与动作空间，人类交互数据可显著提升机器人域预测与策略学习。[DexTouch-WM: Learning Action-Conditioned Tactile World Models from Human Touch for Dexterous Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20649)
- **Towards High-DoF Dexterous Manipulation through VLA Post-Training** 提出四步后训练流水线（手部动作编解码、SFT、DAgger、真实残差 RL），在五项真实灵巧任务上达 100% 成功率。[Towards High-DoF Dexterous Manipulation through VLA Post-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19666)
- **VT-MUSE** 多模态统一序列视觉-触觉表示学习，两阶段跨模态对齐+条件变分潜模型，仿真超强基线 11 个百分点。[VT-MUSE: Multimodal Unified Sequential Visuotactile Representation Learning for Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21290)
- **AnyViewDex** 通过多视角对比对齐+特权 3D 几何监督实现视角不变灵巧操作，部署仅用单目 RGB 与本体感知，xArm7+LEAP Hand 上 76.7% 抓取成功率。[AnyViewDex: View-Invariant Dexterous Manipulation from RGB Observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20107)

## 二、触觉感知与接触丰富操作

- **Compliance for Free** 用四通道双边遥操作使每轴刚度可辨识，零标注代价生成顺从标签，微调 VLA 输出刚度，Franka 擦拭任务力随指令变化。[Compliance for Free: Learning Identifiable Impedance via Bilateral Teleoperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19976)
- **Agile-WAM** 触觉世界动作模型，视觉与触觉多时域预测（视觉大间隔、触觉逐帧），9 个仿真+5 个真实接触任务，推理延迟 11.9ms，真实成功率相对增益 29.4%。[Agile-WAM: An Agile Tactile World Action Model for Contact-Rich Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20761)
- **Dreaming the Sound of Contact** 联合生成视频与音频，从接触声音响度导出期望力曲线并用闭环力调节器执行，零样本完成接触任务并可生成训练数据。[Dreaming the Sound of Contact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19137)
- **Hybrid Residual RL for Contact-Rich Robotic Book Insertion** 保留名义任务空间控制器，残差 PPO 提供有界局部修正并决定释放时机，真实 xArm7 成功率 26.7%→63.3%。[Hybrid Residual Reinforcement Learning for Contact-Rich Robotic Book Insertion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19962)
- **Execution-Aware Pre-Execution Ranking for Grasp-Conditioned Robotic Placement** 在执行前对抓取-放置候选打分，联合选择 top-1 85.63%，冻结迁移到 xArm7 无需重训。[Execution-Aware Pre-Execution Ranking for Grasp-Conditioned Robotic Placement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19946)
- **M3-Tele** 面向顺从全身移动操作的统一多模态遥操作框架，力跟踪误差 4.132N→0.346N，触觉形变误差降 65%。[M3-Tele: A Unified Multimodal Teleoperational Framework for Compliant Whole-Body Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07859)

## 三、视觉-运动策略与 VLA 模型

- **FASA** 免训练运行时框架，利用视觉与夹爪力反馈自适应调节扩散采样步数，推理提速至 1.45×。[FASA: Feedback-Aware Sampling Adaptation for Efficient Diffusion-Based VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19475)
- **GeoAAC** 基于去噪轨迹几何的自适应动作分块，无需额外训练，真实成功率 53.3%→74.4%。[GeoAAC: Geometry-Based Adaptive Action Chunking from Denoising Trajectories in VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20776)
- **MoWAM** 用显式未来运动预测替代未来视频生成，推理时移除视频生成但保留未来表示，OOD 鲁棒性更强并支持推理时扩展。[MoWAM: Explicit Future Motion Prediction for Efficient World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20709)
- **CSWAM** 用 V-JEPA 2.1 构建因果语义专家，RoboTwin 2.0 Clean→Randomized 成功率 10.16%→45.18%，真实 OOD 任务提升 42.5 个百分点。[CSWAM: Better Causal Semantic Representations for Out-of-Distribution Generalization in World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18462)
- **UniExo** 多技能肌肉骨骼运动策略与共适应外骨骼控制，四技能跟踪成功率 94.7%，单一髋部控制器泛化到四种速度与技能切换。[UniExo: Unified Multi-Skill Policies for Musculoskeletal Locomotion and Co-Adaptive Exoskeleton Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19690)
- **Recovering Aggressively Pruned VLA Models** 宽度剪枝保持残差流同形，离线隐状态蒸馏无需投影器，约 8 GPU 小时恢复 63% 剪枝学生至教师 3.5 点内。[Recovering Aggressively Pruned Vision-Language-Action Models with Offline Hidden-State Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19579)
- **Not All Layers Need Tuning** 测量 VLA 各区域适配成本，从十个无标注观测诊断并分配变秩 LoRA，物理 xArm-7 上以 0.04% 可训练参数匹配全量微调。[Not All Layers Need Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18084)
- **StarVLA-α** 极简 VLA 基线，统一多基准训练，在 RoboChallenge 上超 π0.5 20%。[StarVLA-α: Reducing Complexity in Vision-Language-Action Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11757)
- **Decoupling Vision, Language, and Action** 用微调 DINOv3+冻结 NeoBERT+MeanFlow 头，吞吐量为 VLM 骨干预策略的 8–17 倍、能耗低 6–15 倍。[Decoupling Vision, Language, and Action for Efficient Multi-Task Robot Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18374)
- **LayerRoute** 动作条件的分层混合路由接口，自适应访问 VLM 各层与动作中间表示，LIBERO Long 最高 +7.2，仅增 0.31%/3.87% 参数。[LayerRoute: Action-Conditioned Mixture-of-Layers Routing for Vision-Language-Action Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06079)
- **Q-VGM** 用 Q 引导的价值梯度匹配微调流匹配 VLA，避免 BPTT，真实双臂任务 66.7%→98.3%。[Q-VGM: Q-Guided Value-Gradient Matching for Offline-to-Online RL of Flow-Matching VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08015)
- **M2Tok** 多头多码本离散动作分词器，降低重建误差并提升 VLA 成功率。[M2Tok: Multi-head Multi-codebook Discrete Action Tokenization for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18259)
- **EmbodiedMind** 自适应数据挑选+前缀树 RL，18 基准平均 70.02%，长程规划显著领先。[EmbodiedMind: Adaptive Data Curation and Prefix-Tree Reinforcement Learning for Efficient Embodied Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19659)

## 四、跨具身迁移与人机数据协同

- **Improving Cross-embodiment Transfer in Latent Action Models** 用动作相似度监督替代动作预测辅助损失，双臂机器人跨具身成功率翻倍以上。[Improving Cross-embodiment Transfer in Latent Action Models with Action-Similarity Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19846)
- **Sim-and-Human Co-training (SimHum)** 仿真提供机器人可行动作、人类提供真实视觉先验，每任务 80 条机器人轨迹即达 OOD 62.5%。[Sim-and-Human Co-training for Data-Efficient and Scene-Generalizable Bimanual Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19406)
- **Co-VLA** 用 ADMM 共识优化做联邦 VLA 训练，支持全模型与参数高效微调，性能接近集中式。[Co-VLA: Consensus-based Federated Training for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19923)
- **ReShoot** 用 VLM+边缘条件视频生成器对已录演示重渲染外观，动作与本体标签原样保留，真实平台重着色物体成功率 0%→42.9%/47.5%。[ReShoot: Generative Visual Domain Randomization of Recorded Robot Demonstrations for Visuomotor Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19661)

## 五、遥操作与人机交互

- **DITTO** 7-DOF 灵巧手与运动学等价电动外骨骼 1:1 驱动映射，统一手持数据采集与带关节力反馈的双边遥操作。[DITTO: Dexterous Interface for Transparent TeleOperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19196)
- **HIL-UMI** 在 UMI 手持采集中查询当前策略而不执行，用 Energy Score 触发 OOD 采集，配合优势条件行为克隆，无需机器人部署即可后训练。[HIL-UMI: Bringing Human-in-the-Loop Post-Training of VLA Models to Universal Manipulation Interface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20659)
- **Affective Shared Autonomy** 从面部视频、心电与双臂运动估计操作者情感状态，仅在持续不利状态触发辅助，生产性状态提升至 39.7%。[Affective Shared Autonomy: Temporal Affect Dynamics and Subjective Evaluation in Bimanual Teleoperation Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19802)
- **Tele-Traversability** 将可通行性从机器人中心扩展到含操作者认知状态（注意力、负荷、风险容忍）的人类中心“远程可通行性”。[Tele-Traversability: Rethinking Traversability for Teleoperated Ground Robots in Terrain Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19577)
- **系列 SEA 相关工作**：3D 打印扭簧串联弹性驱动器、柔性元件机械臂握手实验，均提升物理人机交互的安全与舒适感。[3D Printed Torsional SEA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19367)、[Flexible Elements in Robotic Arm for Handshake](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19375)
