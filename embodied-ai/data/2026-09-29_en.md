### **Dexterous Manipulation, Robotic Hands, and In-Hand Manipulation**

This group features a strong set of papers on dexterous control, from learning complex tasks like solving a Rubik's Cube to developing novel hardware and bimanual systems.

- **DexTaG: Tactile-as-Guidance in Reinforcement Learning for Dexterous Manipulation** ([2609.33882](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33882)): Proposes a framework that uses tactile signals from a motion-capture glove to guide policy search in simulation, enabling the learning of complex, contact-rich tool-use tasks like manipulating a marker pen.

- **Evolving Dexterous Robots from Scratch** ([2609.33101](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33101)): Uses an evolutionary approach with contrastive learning to discover novel robotic manipulator designs from scratch, without presupposing any specific morphology. The winning designs were 3D printed and tested in the real world.
- **Optimizing H-Graph Hybridization for Diffusion-Guided RRT** ([2609.32897](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32897)): Explores inference-time diversification strategies for a pretrained diffusion model guiding an RRT planner, using H-Graph hybridization to improve path quality.

It reduces hand motion underestimation and improves perceptual accuracy by incorporating touch.
