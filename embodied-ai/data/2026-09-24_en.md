## Dexterous & Multi-Finger Manipulation

- **[The Cartesian Hand: In-Hand Manipulation with All-Linear Fingers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25696)** — A 7-DoF end-effector with two independent parallel grippers plus four translating fingertips. Enables cap opening, pipetting, screwdriving, trigger actuation across 35 objects; same procedures transfer from a fixed-base arm to a humanoid.

- **[Relative Contact Velocity-Controlled Hand-Object Mechanism for Dexterous Tool Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25619)** — Models hand+tool as a unified "hand-object mechanism" expressed via Cartesian relative contact velocities, enabling pick-up → load → wield on five different robotic hands from simple reference trajectories.

- **[VisForce: Visual Grounding of Current and Desired Forces for Goal-Conditioned Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25785)** — Renders current and desired force cues at fingertip locations on wrist/goal images; goal-conditioned cross-attention drives force-aware actions. On a UR10 + RH56F1 hand, achieves 70–80% grasp-and-lift and 40–70% on multi-stage tasks.

- **[Touch2Robot: Robot Touch in the Human Demonstration Loop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24660)** — Uses tactile gloves + object motion during human demonstration to guide object-specific RL policies, then distills into a real-time retargeter. Improves replay completion from 37.9% → 72.1% and downstream Diffusion Policy by 29.1 pts.

- **[DexJoCo: A Benchmark and Toolkit for Task-Oriented Dexterous Manipulation on MuJoCo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16257)** — 11 functionally grounded tasks (tool use, bimanual coordination, long-horizon), 1.1K demos, domain randomization, and multi-setting model benchmarking.

- **[High-Bandwidth Biomimetic Finger for Tactile-Transparent Remote Texture Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26256)** — Biomimetic fingertip with multilayer mechanical gradient + fingerprint morphology; establishes a design→signal→perceptual-transparency transmission chain for teleoperation.

## Bimanual & Whole-Body Manipulation

- **[JAMB: Joint Action-Motion Diffusion for Bimanual Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25322)** — Jointly denoises bimanual actions and future 3D point tracks in a shared Transformer. 83.4% avg on 16 RoboTwin 2.0 tasks (+23.9 pts over strongest baseline); +50 pts real-world over action-only.

- **[HOTICE: Whole-Body Humanoid Object Transportation in Cluttered Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25363)** — Decoupled potential fields for robot + payload collision avoidance, dual-agent RL (upper/lower body), specialist-to-generalist distillation. Validated on Unitree G1 in MuJoCo and real world.

- **[Brace Yourself: Task-Conditioned Environmental Bracing for Forceful Humanoid Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25486)** — "Supporting Hand Strategy": one hand braces against environment while the other applies force. Lifts usable contact force from 13.5 N → 60 N on Unitree G1; generalizes across task regions without retraining.

- **[Whole-Body UMI: Transferring UMI Manipulation Skills to Humanoid Whole-Body Manipulation via Real-Time Motion Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22829)** — Task-agnostic motion generator decoupling whole-body coordination from task semantics; 90% drawer-closing, 80% shelf pick-and-place, 40% Loco-PnP on G1.

- **[MATE: Multi-Agent Virtual Teleoperation Platform for Humanoid Collaboration Data Collection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26520)** — Distributed operators teleoperate multiple humanoids in shared physics sim; 24.1 hrs / 2,500 episodes. Zero-shot transfer to physical humanoid without fine-tuning.

- **[ULTRAMAN / Hierarchical Modularity for General Continual Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25146)** — Brain-inspired modular adaptation for continual learning; +50 pts over replay-free alternatives in embodied VLA manipulation.

- **[A Deployable Four-Finger Payload for Teleoperated Free-Flying Manipulation with Astrobee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25614)** — 17-DoF four-finger gripper on linear rails, VR teleoperation in ISS simulator; cargo bag transport trials.

## Visuomotor Policies & VLA Models

- **[StrataVLA: Hierarchical and Efficient 3D Geometric Grounding for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26071)** — Frozen geometry foundation model + layer-specific cross-attention adapters inside the VLM backbone. 98.53% on LIBERO, up to 88% fewer geometry-model calls via LRU caching.

- **[HABILIS Brain 0: Geometry-Change Supervision for Vision-Language-Action and Residual Flow Recovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25558)** — Predicts multiview future–current geometry-change tokens; Stage 4 residual flow adds closed-loop recovery. 95.20% → 99.55% on LIBERO.

- **[AffordanceWAM: Affordance-Aware Joint World-Action Modeling for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22332)** — Scalar affordance + heatmap as a shared interface between human and robot video; monotonic gains as annotated human video scales on RoboCasa.

- **[MachEmbodied-U0: Unified Understanding and Generation Model for Embodied Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25627)** — Mixture-of-Transformers coupling subtask prediction, affordance grounding, future RGB/depth/normals/optical flow, and actions; ~4,200 hrs pretraining; 99.0% LIBERO / 82.5% LIBERO-Plus.

- **[VLAQuantBench: Closed-Loop Evaluation of Post-Training Quantization for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25376)** — 409 runs / 94,574 episodes. Key finding: expanding a π₀.₅ action-head protected subset from 126→167 layers raises success 7.0%→70.5%.

- **[IndustrialVLA-Bench: A Traceable Multi-Axis Evaluation of Open Robot Policy Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25562)** — Clean LIBERO scores differ by only 1.58 pts across six systems, but robustness/paraphrase spread 14.6 / 31.1 pts — capability ≠ robustness.

- **[RoboFollow: Unveiling the Instruction Following Mirage in Embodied Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25636)** — Diagnoses "low scene entropy" as the reason language is barely used. Nine VLA/WAM policies fail to transfer from L0 to L1–L3; mitigations don't close the gap.

- **[SafeLoop: Risk-Aware Rollback for Vision-Language-Action Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26313)** — Non-invasive wrapper predicting collision/object-failure risk and selecting noop/record/rollback. ~70% fewer hazard cases across 24 LIBERO tasks and 3 real tasks.

- **[RouteRLT: Learning When and Which RL Specialist Should Control a Vision-Language-Action Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26467)** — Learned router between generalist VLA and phase-specific RL specialists; validated on LIBERO and real cable pickup / port insertion.

- **[RoboMP-DINOv2: Prompts, Not Filters for Robust Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25506)** — Treats masks as spatial prompts rather than hard filters; strong gains under unseen object colors (72.5% vs 35.1%).

- **[Think Like a World Model, Act Like a VLA: Distilling World-Model Representations into Compact Robot Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24682)** — Single feature-alignment term to a cached frozen world model. 97.9% LIBERO, 32 ms / 1.86 GB deployed; works on single-arm and bimanual hardware.

- **[Zeva: In-Context Causal Learning for Generalizable Embodied Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30880)** — Frozen policy + causal interaction memory enables self-evolution without gradient updates.

- **[Unleashing Agility of Wheeled-Legged Robots for High-Dynamic Reflexive Obstacle Evasion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23761)** — Learns rolling/stepping/hybrid evasion behaviors; verified on M20.

- **[TRIWORLDBENCH: A Tri-View Consistency Perspective on Embodied World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26314)** — 500 episodes / 50 bimanual tasks, 19 metrics checking whether head + both wrist videos describe the same action/object state.

- **[4DGS-JEPA / PatchWAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25961)** — [PatchWAM: Unified World-Action Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25961) treats continuous actions as patches in a shared generative backbone: 91.8% LIBERO-Plus, 96.12% RoboTwin 2.0.

- **[MedVLA: A Hierarchical Vision-Language-Action Framework for Closed-Loop Precision Medical Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25756)** — Function-constrained execution + CoT data pipeline; 95% vs 8% (OpenVLA) / 15% (π₀) on 100 electrode implantation trials.

- **[StenoVLA-3D: 3D-Aware Reasoning VLA for Navigation Through Gastrointestinal Stenoses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24187)** — Point-map fusion + temporal state branch; 88.9% / 77.8% task success on esophageal/colonic phantoms.

## Teleoperation, Shared Control & HRI

- **[Capability-Aware Arbitration for Semantic Intent-Based Shared Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25369)** — Combines VLM intent confidence with VLA capability confidence (from action-trajectory dispersion). 92% success vs 83% teleop, 44% intent-only, 10% fixed blending; 12 participants.

- **[Digital Twin-Driven VR Teleoperation with Multi-View Spatial Perception for Surgical Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25527)** — Markerless digital twin streamed to HMD with natural parallax. On dVRK: 86% shorter path length, 95% lower jerk vs HoloLens 2 baseline.

- **[Learning from Humans for Proactive Assistance in Human-Robot Collaborative Transport (PROACT)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25351)** — Transformer trained on dyadic human transport demos predicts future object motion; 108 trials on a 9-DoF mobile manipulator, -59.2% interaction work vs compliance-only, -20.4% vs MPC.

- **[Learning to Plan in Human-Robot Collaboration: Multimodal Reinforcement Learning for Adaptive Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25352)** — RL-generated multimodal interaction manager for object-finding; validated in a human study.

- **[Towards Adaptive Interaction Strategies for Human Companion Robot via Deep Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25031)** — MPPI + Control Barrier Functions for dynamic companion positioning; +24% success / +47% accuracy; tracks at up to 1.7 m/s.

- **[From Instrument-Mounted Demonstrations to In-Vivo Execution: Learning Bimanual Laparoscopic Appendectomy Without Robot-Collected Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25625)** — Clip-on instrument logger captures surgeon motion; diffusion policy with fine-tuned DINOv3 trained on 849 in-vivo demos, deployed on 4 live rabbits (3 successes).

- **[Recording Hand-Held Laparoscopic Instrument Motion in the Operating Room: Magnetometer-Free Fusion of Inertial, Range and Visual Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25577)** — Error-state Kalman filter under trocar RCM constraint; 1.21 mm RMS translation, 0.34° RMS rotation vs Franka ground truth.

- **[BLIND ROBOT / Towards Reliable Underwater Diver-Robot Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23392)** — Seven-gesture vocabulary + landmark recognition + command-level logic; tank and pool evaluations.

- **[Toward Self-Repairing Ubiquitous Robots Using Goal-Oriented Agentic AI in HRI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26155)** — Agentic repair assistant, 95% completion (19/20); physical interaction reduced perceived social presence vs online baseline.

- **[Mirror Skin: In Situ Visualization of Robot Touch Intent on Robotic Skin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11472)** — Cephalopod-inspired mirroring of human body parts onto corresponding robot regions; improved accuracy and response times in VR and physical studies.

- **[SPINE: Bridging the Cyber-Physical Gap with Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13049)** — Agentic debugging/deployment for bimanual teleop; 76%→100% success on DOBOT X-Trainer, 100% on AgileX PiPER.

- **[MAP / MAVP: Map-Aware Visuomotor Policies for Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26378)** — Map-frame base pose prediction with feedforward + feedback tracking; outperforms unanchored velocity control in all six real tasks.

- **[AgenticDiffusion / Multi-View Reasoning with View-Conditioned Diffusion Planning for UAV Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04111)** — Mostly UAV navigation, but the view-selection + diffusion-planning + NMPC pipeline is relevant to manipulation-style planning.

## Sim-to-Real, Simulation & Data Generation

- **[Norm2Tex: Augmenting Visuo-Tactile Simulations with Texture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25398)** — Modifies object depth maps with normal-map textures before a tactile simulator's rendering pipeline; preserves material-dependent tactile information across sim-to-real.

- **[PhyVisGen: Physically and Visually High-Fidelity Robotic Manipulation Data Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25653)** — IPC-based arm–gripper coupling for soft contact + real-scene reconstruction + path tracing. Policies trained solely on synthetic data achieve 65–95% across five real tasks without fine-tuning.

- **[Uranus: Building the Next-Generation Simulation Infrastructure for Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24815)** — Joint-trajectory-conditioned autoregressive diffusion simulator; open-ended streaming rollout at 24 FPS.

- **[MotionForge: Data Generation Pipeline and Large-Scale Benchmark for Long-Horizon Manipulation of Dynamic Objects with Domain Shifts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25689)** — 40 dynamic tasks / 11 motion patterns / 17 long-horizon tasks with single- and joint-domain-shift protocols and a latency-aware execution protocol.

- **[RoboTwin-Phys: Do WAMs and VLAs Understand the Physical World?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26292)** — 13 physical attributes varied within plausible ranges, >5,000 expert demos with ground-truth physical parameters; reveals substantial robustness gap when physics varies.

- **[DynaForge: Planning-Guided Residual Learning for Dynamic Manipulation Demonstration Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25631)** — 41.3% → 78.4% demonstration-generation success across nine sim tasks; DP3 trained on DynaForge data: 49.1% vs 7.1% (DOMINO); 30–60% real-world.

- **[PAKT: Physically-Aligned Kinesthetic Teaching for Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25630)** — Admittance-based kinesthetic guidance with matched kinematic limits; -23–48% cycle time, -62–86% interventions vs HIL-SERL on four insertion/assembly benchmarks.

- **[MicroPush: A Lightweight Simulator for Sim-to-Real Microrobotic Pushing and Assembly](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23607)** — 37× real-time with 100 particles; zero-shot sim-to-real policy transfer: 1.91 μm median path error on a figure-eight trajectory.

- **[φ-RIE: From Photorealistic Reconstruction to Interactive Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26795)** — Gaussian-native pipeline converting scanned scenes into movable simulator assets with coupled removal/completion.

- **[CODA: Depth-Aligned Scene Completion and Object Decomposition from a Single RGB-D Image](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25654)** — Reconstructs complete scene geometry then separates environment from movable objects, reducing drift via two explicit 3D grounding mechanisms.

- **[AgriGen: Large-Scale Scene Generation Framework for Photorealistic Agricultural Robotics Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25725)** — ROS + Isaac Sim procedural agricultural environments with domain randomization.

- **[CAR: Cross-Vehicle Kinodynamics Adaptation via Mobility Representation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06866)** — Transformer + AdaNorm embeds trajectory transitions into a shared mobility latent space; 67.2% error reduction with 1 minute of new data.

- **[KEX-Bench / Point Cloud & Map Priors paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03772)** — peripheral.

## Deformable & Contact-Rich Manipulation

- **[Manipulation with Stability Guarantees: Linear Deformable Objects with Non-negligible Physical Response Grasped at Multiple Locations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26004)** — Fully dynamic model of linear deformable objects via discrete strain parameterizations, SE(3) actuation coordinates, and nonlinear feedback with explicit steady-state convergence conditions. Real-time closed-loop with online shape estimation.

- **[Manipulation of Deformable Linear Objects Using MPPI with BiLSTM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26238)** — biLSTM dynamics trained on MuJoCo synthetic data + MPPI control for cable manipulation.

- **[Closed-Loop Object-Informed Control for Non-Prehensile Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30778)** — ADMM consensus between object-level and robot-level MPPI plans. +35% sim / +43% hardware success on planar pushing with obstacles.

- **[CableVLA: Simulation-Privileged Global-Local Representation Learning for Cable Routing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25606)** — TopoHead distills cable topology into visual context; TacSense learns contact dynamics from resistive arrays. 62.6% → 84.9% over π₀.₅-V on 345 MuJoCo evaluations; cross-simulator and real-robot zero-shot transfer tested.

- **[What is the Better Curriculum: Controller-Shaped Grasping Behavior for Contact Force-Sensitive Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25887)** — Uses a 25 Hz tactile reflex controller as a collection-time teacher. ACT recovers the teacher's grasp profile → 95% stable grasps on plastic cups; failure analysis shows disturbance rejection remains controller-dependent.

- **[Towards Intent-Aware Human-Robot Teaming / Search-and-Rescue Platform](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26051)** — Platform paper.

- **[CricRAG / Cricket Coaching VLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26056)** — Peripheral but close to visuomotor skill assessment.

## Soft Robotics & Continuum / Tendon-Driven Systems

- **[Cosserat Modeling of Trimmed Helicoid Soft Arms with a Separated-Section Constitutive Law](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25264)** — Separated-section constitutive law for trimmed helicoid arms; strongly anisotropic stiffness; 6.7–7.8% pooled normalized position error across 103 configurations, ~0.3 s per full-arm solve on one CPU core.

- **[History-Conditioned Flow Matching for Probabilistic Dynamics of Tendon-Driven Continuum Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25658)** — Flow-matching distribution over next joint configurations conditioned on motion/actuation history; 5-second Energy Scores of 11.91/11.42 mm on hardware under unseen tendon commands.

- **[Shaft-Configuration-Adaptive Catheter Tip Position Estimation via Motor-History Conditioned Residual Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26304)** — GRU residual estimator on top of a calibrated geometric backbone; 3.3 mm open-loop tip RMSE (59% reduction) across four ICE catheters and 16 bent configurations.

- **[Prescribed-Time Contracting-Boundary Control of a Tendon-Driven Flexible Arm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22963)** — Curvature tracking with prescribed-time boundary; -32.5% terminal curvature RMSE across six trials.

- **[Contact-Stable Deformable Tissue Simulation Using Implicit Integration and Live-Pose Grasp Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25642)** — Fixed-view RGB-D reconstruction → tetrahedral mesh; 13/15 constraint-grasp successes vs 0/15 friction grasps; designed for surgical robot closed-loop policy screening.
