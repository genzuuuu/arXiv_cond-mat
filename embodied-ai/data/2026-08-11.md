# arXiv Robotics & AI Papers — Manipulation-Focused Summary

## Dexterous Manipulation & Tactile Sensing

**[AdaDexGrasp: Adaptive Dexterous Grasping via 3D Visuo-Tactile Representation Fusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07600)**
Introduces a unified visuo-tactile grasping framework that integrates grasp generation, feasibility prediction, and adaptive refinement. The core contribution is an efficient visuo-tactile representation that fuses object geometry with tactile feedback via finger identities, enabling contact-aware grasp pose generation and tactile-guided post-contact refinement. Validated in simulation and real-world, showing improved success rates and generalization across objects.

**[A Mixed-Stiffness Anthropomimetic Fingertip Broadens the Operating Range for Coin Grasping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07887)**
Explores fingertip design with mixed Shore E10/A60 silicone stiffness for precision grasping of thin, flat objects (coins) on hard surfaces. The mixed-stiffness pair maintains high success rates across more tested settings than uniform soft fingertips, showing that stiffness placement within a nail-supported pad is a critical design variable for thin-object grasping.

**[Ultra-Low-Impedance Robotic Gripper for High-Bandwidth and Transparent Physical Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09198)**
Presents a 9-DOF, three-fingered Differential Direct-Drive (DDD) gripper combining DD motors with a low-ratio (1:2) differential transmission. Achieves ~18 N nominal grasping force while maintaining low mechanical impedance (max 50.1 N/m unpowered), enabling high-bandwidth physical interaction and sensorless proprioceptive force estimation.

## Visuomotor Policies & Imitation Learning

**[Self Supervised Learning from Automatically Generated Demonstrations for Visual Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07553)**
Proposes a self-supervised pipeline where a robot automatically generates demonstrations around a target pose and learns relative pose corrections from wrist-mounted RGB images, requiring no camera-to-robot extrinsic calibration. A coarse-to-fine controller with CNN-based regression achieves 66.6% and 63.6% real-world grasp success on two objects without rotation.

**[SpeedTuning: Speeding Up Policy Execution with Lightweight Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09138)**
Introduces a reinforcement learning framework that learns to predict optimal execution speed for manipulation policies, complementing a base policy without additional data collection. Achieves >2.4x speed-up while preserving success rates across dynamic tasks like pouring, throwing, and picking.

**[Learning When to See and When to Feel: Adaptive Vision-Torque Fusion for Contact-Aware Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01414)**
Provides a controlled comparison of vision + force/torque integration strategies within diffusion-based policies. Proposes an adaptive strategy that ignores F/T signals during non-contact phases and leverages both modalities during contact, outperforming the strongest baseline by 14% in success rate.

## Visuomotor Policies & Imitation Learning

**[Generalizing deep reinforcement learning across cable-driven parallel robot configurations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07546)**
Trains an actuator-level policy that controls each motor to achieve target cable length, rather than learning end-to-end robot control. This enables a single shared policy to generalize across CDPR configurations with different actuator counts. Successfully transfers from a simulated 4-motor planar robot to a real 8-motor 3D CDPR.

**[Projection-Retraction MPPI: Exact Constraint-Manifold Control for Manipulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07573)**
Enforces equality and inequality constraints inside sampled dynamics of MPPI for dual-arm 14-DoF systems. Constraint satisfaction is maintained to numerical tolerance, with real-hardware validation on a Unitree H1-2 humanoid reactive obstacle avoidance.

**[Event-Time Hybrid Optimal Control for Robotic Table Tennis Serves](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08157)**
Formulates spin-controlled serve generation as event-time OCP optimizing racket impact and bounce/net-crossing times. Achieves mean landing error of 13.1±7.3 cm with spin rates up to 30 rps on a KUKA Agilus robot, and 4.1x faster solve times than fixed-step formulations.

**[DAP-Pose: Deep Temporal Alignment and Physics-aware Cross-modal Sensor Fusion for Robust Pose Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23755)**
Presents a unified end-to-end model for multimodal pose estimation with bi-level cross-modal fusion and deep temporal alignment of asynchronous sensor streams, achieving state-of-the-art on KITTI (t_rel 1.31%).

## Teleoperation & Human-Robot Interaction

**[Spatiotemporal Context-dependent Personalized Movement Compensation in Delayed Telemanipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08200)**
Fits personalized delay-, direction-, and distance-specific scaling parameters for teleoperation with communication delay. Achieves 20-25% performance gains, most pronounced at longer delays, demonstrating that personalization provides additional accuracy benefits for inward reaching under moderate delay.

**[TAMS: Task-Aware Multi-View Adaptive Streaming for Wireless Telerobotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09731)**
Allocates video bitrate according to current manipulation phase, prioritizing the camera view most relevant to the operator. Reduces mean completion time from 68.9s to 43.9s and increases success from 48% to 71% under constrained bandwidth.

## World Models & Action Models for Manipulation

**[GWM-VLA: Geometry-Aware Latent World Modeling for Vision-Language-Action Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07619)**
Combines geometry-aware multi-view state encoding with target-view prediction, emphasizing wrist view for end-effector motion and local gripper-object interactions. Demonstrates robustness to visual and environmental shifts.

**[Vid2WAM: Distilling Video Diffusion Priors into World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08558)**
Offline distillation framework transferring visual diffusion priors from a video foundation model into a compact WAM, with source-aware residual action adaptation to handle noisy pseudo-actions. Improves novel-task generalization under limited expert demonstrations.

**[4D-WAM: Infusing Spatiotemporal Awareness into World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08023)**
Injects 3D trajectory field knowledge into WAMs via motion alignment and destination alignment objectives, improving spatial understanding, execution precision, and generalization for embodied manipulation.

**[SG-WAM: Text-Grounded and Spatial-aware Semantic Guidance for World-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08839)**
Uses a VLM-based planner to inject text-grounded and spatial-aware semantic foresight into WAMs, improving instruction following for future-video generation and action prediction.

**[JEPA-WAM: Learning Vision-Language-Action Policies with Joint-Embedding World Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09381)**
Builds a latent WAM in pretrained V-JEPA space with shared predictor coupling transition prediction and action generation. Achieves 79.2% on LIBERO-Plus without large-scale pretraining, 86.3% with π0.5 instantiation.

## Robot Learning Frameworks & Generalization

**[MoRSE: Task-Oriented Multi-Agent System with Mixture of Role-Subtask Experts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09251)**
Introduces (role, subtask)-conditional specialization with dynamic Mixture-of-LoRA-Experts module and hierarchical GRPO, improving multi-agent performance on code generation and long-horizon tasks.

**[V-Simba: Unleashing the Architectural Potential of RL in Visual Continuous Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07870)**
Shows that architectural design alone (normalization layers + pointwise convolutions) can significantly improve sample efficiency in visual RL, matching or outperforming state-of-the-art methods on DMC, Adroit, and Meta-World.

## Embodied Agents & Navigation

**[LUCID: Latent-Skill Unified Control via Imagined Dynamics for Long-Horizon Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07746)**
Uses structured latent-conditioned low-level policies with high-level planning over reusable skills via imagined rollouts in a learned dynamics model. Improves full-task success in multi-object rearrangement scenarios.

**[SAIN: Structure-Aware Interactive Navigation with Active Dialogue Grounding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09196)**
Turns active dialogue into persistent navigation state via compiled target evidence, route-level corridor memory, and object-candidate labels. Improves SR from 20.2 to 25.4 on VL-LN IIGN benchmark.

**[Unordered Landmark Visual Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06833)**
RGB-only framework for navigation free from temporal and odometric priors, constructing 2D topological maps with graph-based belief propagation filtering. Outperforms state-of-the-art in simulation and real-world deployments.

## Sim-to-Real & Simulation Infrastructure

**[EsaacSim: A Multimodal Event Camera Add-on for NVIDIA Isaac Sim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08522)**
Provides online simulation of configurable event cameras with grayscale and Bayer RGGB event generation, supporting multiple resolutions with synchronized RGB, APS, event, depth, and IMU outputs through ROS2.

**[Evolutionary Curriculum Learning Improves Biological Sequence Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00697)** (adjacent)
Though not manipulation-focused, this contains useful curriculum learning insights applicable to robot learning.

## Dataset & Benchmark Contributions

**[Data Pyramid for Embodied Manipulation: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24744)**
Organizes embodied data ecosystem as a pyramid spanning five complementary sources: real-robot, UMI-style, egocentric/exocentric, simulation, and general vision-language data. Analyzes embodied foundation models through data recipes and discusses six open challenges including tactile datasets and dexterous manipulation.

**[Omega-0: A Latent Predictive World Action Model for Concurrent Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06375)**
Introduces ω-0, a latent predictive whole-body world-action model that directly predicts controller-compatible action latents for real-robot humanoid execution, with ω-HOME dataset (40+ hours of real-world household humanoid data).

**[Hermite Curves as Trajectory Priors for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01265)**
Parameterizes action chunks as piecewise cubic Hermite curves, with Hermite Regularization improving π0 baseline success from 95.9% to 98.7% on LIBERO without inference overhead.

## Robotics & Policy Evaluation

**[XPolicyLab: A Unified Standard and Open Ecosystem for Robot Policy Evaluation and Deployment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09892)**
Reduces policy-to-environment integration cost from O(NM) to O(N+M) via common schemas and a client/server architecture. Integrates 42 robot policies, reducing integration effort from five hours to thirty minutes.

**[TrustRoboReward: Preference-Ordered Isotonic Score Editing for Multi-Paradigm Robot Reward Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08491)**
Proposes POISE to calibrate pointwise scores against pairwise preferences, eliminating score-pair reversal conflicts. Achieves 77.96% overall reward score, nearly matching GPT-5-mini teacher while using a 4B model.

**[WorldSimProbe: Diagnosing Simulator Faithfulness in Action-Conditioned World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09298)**
Formalizes an Observable Simulator Contract evaluating whether ACWMs induce correct agent motion, with five controlled suites detecting action-realization degradation and structured failures across RoboTwin, ManiSkill, and LIBERO.

## Physical Interaction & Contact-Rich Tasks

**[High Fidelity Capture, Reconstruction, and Transfer of Human Demonstrations for Robot-Assisted Bathing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09127)**
Presents a framework using contact regions as processing primitives for capturing and transferring dynamic, contact-rich human bathing demonstrations. Includes the first synchronized motion, shape, contact, and force dataset for pHRI, with transfer to robotic bathing tasks.

**[Calibrated Particle-sets for Trans-dimensional Uncertainty Representation (CaPTURe)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09166)**
Presents particle-based conformal prediction for uncertainty calibration in contact-rich configuration spaces, achieving up to 30% absolute improvement in task success for peg-in-hole insertion.

**[Nonlinear Model Predictive Control of a Robotic Soft Esophagus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09602)**
Implements MPC on a soft robotic esophagus with embedded TOF and pressure sensors for autonomous peristalsis, using SINDy-C for dynamics estimation.
