## 🦾 Dexterous Hands & In-Hand Manipulation

**[DITTO: Dexterous Interface for Transparent TeleOperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19196)**
Co-designed 7-DOF hand + kinematically matched motorized exoskeleton with 1-to-1 actuator mapping. Unifies in-the-wild data collection and bilateral teleoperation with joint-level force feedback in one platform — directly tackles the force-feedback vs. embodiment-gap tradeoff in high-DOF hand data collection.

**[OpenDexGrasp: Open-vocabulary Task-Oriented Dexterous Grasping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18117)**
Generates task-consistent grasps from free-form language by learning a shared perception-action latent coupling VL context with dexterous action generation. No separate affordance-to-pose stage. Simulation + real-robot validation on unseen categories.

**[Towards High-DoF Dexterous Manipulation through VLA Post-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19666)**
Four-step pipeline (hand-action codec → SFT → DAgger → latent residual RL) to adapt VLAs to high-DoF hands. Reports 100% success across five real tasks spanning bimanual transfer, in-hand reorientation, and tool use.

**[DexTouch-WM: Action-Conditioned Tactile World Models from Human Touch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20649)**
Learns contact dynamics from scalable human touch using shared piezoresistive arrays on human and robot hands. Scaling human data 0→100 hrs substantially improves held-out robot-domain contact prediction despite disjoint task sets.

**[AnyViewDex: View-Invariant Dexterous Manipulation from RGB Observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20107)**
Achieves view invariance without test-time 3D sensing via multi-view contrastive alignment + privileged 3D geometric supervision during training. 76.7% grasp success across eight objects and six uncalibrated viewpoints on xArm7 + 16-DOF LEAP Hand.

## 🤏 Grasping & Tactile Sensing

**[Grasping by interconnection: robust closing motions from coarse object templates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19228)**
Motion planner using coarse templates, human grasp types, and compliant sliding contacts. Tolerated ~1 cm size and multi-cm pose errors without feedback — outperforming a data-driven planner in 25/27 conditions on a Shadow Hand. Nice result on designing robustness *into* the closing motion.

**[Execution-Aware Pre-Execution Ranking for Grasp-Conditioned Robotic Placement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19946)**
Scores grasp-placement candidates before planning, accounting for how the grasp changes required end-effector pose and transport motion. Frozen transfer to xArm7/MoveIt without retraining.

**[TacSushi: Tactile-Grounded World-Action Modeling for Dexterous Sushi Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19613)**
Gated fingertip tactile fusion + training-only future-consequence supervision (decoder removed at deployment). The ablations cleanly isolate the benefit of gated fusion vs. naive concatenation and of consequence supervision.

**[Agile-WAM: An Agile Tactile World Action Model for Contact-Rich Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20761)**
Multi-horizon multimodal prediction exploiting the fact that vision and touch evolve at different timescales. 29.4% relative success gain over the strongest baseline at 11.9 ms inference latency.

**[VT-MUSE: Multimodal Unified Sequential Visuotactile Representation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21290)**
Two-stage framework explicitly modeling temporal contact evolution via masked-view consistency and tactile depth change prediction. +11 points over strongest baseline on simulation benchmarks.

## 🎮 Teleoperation & Data Collection

**[HIL-UMI: Human-in-the-Loop Post-Training for VLAs via Universal Manipulation Interface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20659)**
Robot-free human-in-the-loop post-training using handheld UMI. An Energy Score triggers collection when human and policy trajectories diverge, while low online advantage predictions identify segments for refining a progress estimator. Decouples data collection from robot deployment.

**[M3-Tele: A Unified Multimodal Teleoperational Framework for Compliant Whole-Body Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07859)**
Aligned visual, tactile, force, and proprioceptive capture during whole-body mobile manipulation. Reduces force tracking error from 4.132 N to 0.346 N and tactile deformation error by 65%.

**[Compliance for Free: Learning Identifiable Impedance via Bilateral Teleoperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19976)**
Four-channel bilateral teleop makes per-axis stiffness identifiable using only existing joint-torque sensing — "compliance labels at zero annotation cost." The only policy of five whose contact force shifts when the instruction asks for a firmer wipe.

**[Affective Shared Autonomy: Temporal Affect Dynamics in Bimanual Teleoperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19802)**
Modulates assistance based on real-time operator affect (facial video, cardiac, arm kinematics). Increases productive states by up to 39.7%. New multimodal dataset included.

**[Tele-Traversability: Rethinking Traversability for Teleoperated Ground Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19577)**
Position paper extending traversability from robot-centric to human-centric, accounting for operator attention, workload, and risk tolerance.

## 🧠 Visuomotor Policies & VLA Models

**[StarVLA-α: Reducing Complexity in Vision-Language-Action Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11757)**
Minimal-complexity VLA baseline showing a strong VLM backbone + minimal design suffices. Outperforms π₀.₅ by 20% on RoboChallenge. Valuable for disentangling which design choices actually matter.

**[Decoupling Vision, Language, and Action for Efficient Multi-Task Robot Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18374)**
DEM reaches comparable success to GR00T N1.7 and π₀.₅ at 6.1 ms per forward pass — 8–17× throughput and 6–15× less energy. Strong data point for the efficiency question in VLA deployment.

**[Robust Workflow Generation via Adversarial Learning for Audio Deepfake Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20761)**
*Note: I've flagged this as likely mis-linked. The abstract describes adversarial learning for deepfake audio detection — outside the embodied-AI scope. Flagging in case the title/URL pair is wrong.*

**[GeoAAC: Geometry-Based Adaptive Action Chunking from Denoising Trajectories in VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20776)**
Adapts action horizon from Flow Matching denoising trajectory geometry — no training needed. Up to +8.7 points in simulation and 53.3% → 74.4% on real-world tasks.

**[LayerRoute: Action-Conditioned Mixture-of-Layers Routing for VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06079)**
Enables adaptive access to VLM layers and reuses prior action representations. +7.2 on LIBERO Long with 0.31–3.87% extra parameters.

**[M²Tok: Multi-head Multi-codebook Discrete Action Tokenization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18259)**
Addresses the discretization bottleneck in autoregressive VLAs via multi-head decomposition with per-head codebooks. Lower reconstruction loss, higher task success.

**[A Morphing Aerial Robot With Thruster-Integrated Flexible Continuum Links](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19328)**
First aerial manipulator with flexible continuum links that also integrate distributed thrusters — combining soft manipulator adaptability with an expanded wrench space.

## 🔄 Sim-to-Real & Domain Transfer

**[RebarSim: Visual Sim-to-Real Learning for Robotic Insertion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20477)**
Trained entirely in simulation, seats rebars in 91.3% of real-robot rollouts at 1.4 mm clearance. Key finding: both appearance randomization and DAgger mixture are essential; removing either sharply lowers success.

**[ReShoot: Generative Visual Domain Randomization of Recorded Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19661)**
Re-renders recorded demonstrations under altered appearances via VLM captioning + edge-conditioned video generation. Action labels copied verbatim. Lifted recolored-object success from 0% to 42.9–47.5%.

**[MILER: Semantic Mid-Level Representation for Sim-to-Real RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20747)**
Zero-shot sim-to-real using BEVFusion to produce a semantic BEV representation matching the training simulator. 17.3 km driven without intervention on a 3.0 km test track.

**[RoboGPU: Accelerating GPU Collision Detection for Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01517)**
RoboCore accelerator embedded in a robotics-focused GPU — 13.3× faster than CUDA baseline, 3.4× end-to-end speedup on a neural motion planner. Infrastructure paper worth tracking.

**[Sim-and-Human Co-training for Data-Efficient Bimanual Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19406)**
Exploits complementarity: simulation provides robot-valid actions, human data provides real-world observations. 62.5% success on OOD scenes with only 80 real episodes per task.

## 🤖 World Models

**[WorldContact: A Contact-Centric World Model for Scalable Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19600)**
10× speedup over source simulator on deformable-object manipulation. Bag-lifting success improves from 65% (source sim alone) to 95% with the world-model-expanded dataset.

**[MoWAM: Explicit Future Motion Prediction for Efficient World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20709)**
Replaces future video generation with compact future motion prediction, keeping future dynamics explicit while removing video generation at inference. Supports inference-time scaling via motion-aware verification.

**[Feeling Terrain Before Crossing: World Models for Off-Road Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19863)**
First off-road navigation world model conditioned on proprioception, predicting both what the camera sees and what the robot feels. Outperforms visual-only world models on wheeled and legged platforms.

**[JEPA-WAM: Connecting Generated Visual Instructions to World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20277)**
Augments text instructions with stochastically generated visual goal references encoded by frozen V-JEPA 2.1. Outperforms π₀ and Fast-WAM by 10–27 points across instruction-following settings.

**[CSWAM: Better Causal Semantic Representations for OOD Generalization in WAMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18462)**
V-JEPA 2.1-based causal semantic expert raises RoboTwin Clean-to-Randomized transfer from 10.16% to 45.18%. +42.5 points on real-robot OOD tasks.

**[LIFD: Anchored Diffusion for 3D-Aware Scene Memory in Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19796)**
Rectified-flow scene-token completion with anchor-guided cross-attention. 56.0% mean success on four UR5e task families vs. 40.5% for OpenVLA-7B.

## 🦿 Locomotion & Legged Robots

**[AthenaZero: A Low-Inertia, Bimanual Robot for Dynamic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19194)**
Endpoint mass comparable to a human — ~1 order of magnitude less than conventional manipulators. Throws at 30+ m/s, catches and bats at 14+ m/s. Torque transparency makes it well-suited for millisecond-timescale dynamic tasks.

**[Learning Slope-Adaptive Whole-Body Locomotion for Humanoid Roofing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20558)**
Scene-grounded human motion learning for construction tasks on pitched surfaces. Work-clearance errors of 0.256–0.531 cm across nailgun, hammering, and pushing.

**[GigaBrain-WBC-0.5: A Behavior World Model for Robust Humanoid Tracking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18234)**
81.3% terrain-interaction success (4.3× best baseline) and 99.3% fall-recovery rate. First real-time cross-terrain whole-body teleoperation demonstration on a humanoid.

**[RoM-Nav: Learning Safe Humanoid Navigation from Reduced Order Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19272)**
Decomposes navigation into reduced-order LiDAR policy → full-order student. Multi-floor navigation with 10+ m vertical displacement and 100+ m path length on Unitree G1.

**[HOPHY: Hierarchical Hypergraph Representation for Off-Road Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20694)**
100% planning success with <0.01% median cost deviation from oracle. Reduces MRTA computation 79× vs. pixel A* on a 1.5-km eight-task physical mission.

**[OmniMimic: Dynamics-Completed Motion Augmentation for Omnidirectional Quadruped Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20566)**
Turns directionally limited animal demonstrations into a single multi-gait policy. 63.1% reduction in velocity-tracking RMSE over the matched APEX baseline.

## 🧪 Benchmarks & Evaluation

**[VA-Bench: Measuring Embodied Spatial Intelligence through Visual Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19554)**
Evaluates the full observe-reason-act-revise loop without privileged poses or learned action heads. Best model: 100% target localization but only 53.93% task success. Held-out geometric transfer drops success by 30+ points.

**[HALTER: A Graph-Based Harness for Autonomous Long-Horizon Manipulation Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19413)**
Restores scenes via LLM-planned sequences of learned reset skills. 76% scene restoration vs. 52% for AutoEval. Cuts operator time by 72%.

**[When Faster VLA Deployment Changes Closed-Loop Behavior](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14146)**
Critical finding: requested-INT8 ONNX artifacts were byte-identical FP32 graphs, and "requested-FP16/INT8" dropped Spatial success from 70% to ~40%. Deployment evaluation must report latency, artifact inspection, *and* closed-loop success together.

**[Not All Nodes Are Created Equal: Homophily-Aware Stratification for GNN Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19210)**
Argues class stratification alone is insufficient for graphs. Homophily-aware stratification achieves the lowest mean stability rank on 13/15 datasets.

**[AVTrace: Diagnosing Audio-Visual Temporal Reasoning in Omni Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19991)**
All five off-the-shelf omni models score below the majority-label baseline on synchronization verification.

## 🛡️ Safety, Security & Governance

**[Runtime Safety Filtering for Two-Terminal Hazards in Robotic Battery Recycling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19665)**
Key insight: *fallback strategy* matters more than predicate structure for the safety-utility tradeoff. Holding reduces success by up to 0.302 without reducing hazard.

**[Beyond Patch Removal: Persistent Adversarial Effects in VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19669)**
Only 36.2% of episodes remain recoverable after patch removal, vs. ~89% for deviation-matched controls. Timely intervention is critical.

**["Your Robot Was Trained on a Lie": Collision Mesh Poisoning Attacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18122)**
Attack modifies only the collision mesh, leaving the visual mesh untouched. Policies behave normally in simulation but degrade in the real world. Existing defenses insufficient.

**[Coding Agents with an Obstacle-Aware Harness for Safe Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20822)**
Coding agents collide with obstacles in most cases despite the prompt forbidding it — the failure is in planning, not perception. SafeHarness achieves 87.5% collision avoidance (27.0% over SOTA).

**[Legislating World-Model-Based Planning with Legal Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15113)**
Uses Defeasible Deontic Logic to constrain motion planning for ex ante governance. Documents two "isomorphism gaps" that must be closed.

**[Predict Before You Deploy: Offline Prediction of Quantization-Induced Task Degradation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19441)**
Shows task losses can't be explained by bit width alone. W4A4 achieved 1.37× speedup and ~44% lower peak memory on a Franka Research 3.

### Today's Threads

Three themes stand out:

1. **Tactile world models are maturing fast** — DexTouch-WM, TacSushi, Agile-WAM, and VT-MUSE all push contact-rich prediction in different directions. The shared insight is that touch and vision evolve at different timescales, and modeling that asymmetry matters.

2. **Data efficiency via heterogeneous co-training** — SimHum, ReShoot, RebarSim, and HIL-UMI all seek to substitute collection cost with generation, simulation, or human-in-the-loop triggers.

3. **Benchmark realism pressure** — VA-Bench, HALTER, SLAMSqueezeBench, and the SmolVLA deployment analysis all argue that standard metrics miss consequential failure modes (held-out geometry transfer, artifact inspection, resource constraints, closed-loop behavior).
