# arXiv Robotics & AI Papers — Embodied Intelligence, Manipulation, and Policy Learning

## Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models & Policy Learning

**[G0.5: One Autoregressive Stream for Robot Reasoning and Action](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11739)** — A unified autoregressive VLA that emits reasoning and action tokens with a single transformer decoder. Uses cross-embodiment action tokenizer, native chain-of-thought, and visual memory. Surpasses SOTA across 7 regimes: 76.7% on R1lite/R1pro (vs 53.3% for π0.5), 98.9% on LIBERO, 93.3% on RoboTwin 2.0.

**[StellaVLA: In-Context Structured Demonstration for Generalizable VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11671)** — Adapts VLAs at test time via single retrieved structured demo (task plan, sub-goals, verbalized 3D motion). Ranks first on VLA-Arena (0.63 vs 0.44 π0.5), 98.8% LIBERO, 85.1% LIBERO-Plus, and transfers across embodiments including XR demos.

**[World Tokens: Enhancing Embodied Policies with Training-Time World Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09730)** — Trains VLA with world-model branch (future-video denoiser) during training only; at deployment the branch is removed, keeping VLA-level latency. Competitive on LIBERO, best-reported on SIMPLER, improves real-world R1 Pro success.

**[MiDAS: Adaptation of Generalist Robot Policies with Minimal Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11363)** — Offline-to-online RL recipe that anchors pre-trained VLA with 1–few demos via BC, then improves through value-based online RL on residual policy. Works on LIBERO, RoboCasa, and bimanual YAM platform (~6 hrs of interaction).

**[RLinf-VLA: Unified Framework for RL of VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06710)** — Scalable RL training framework with hybrid fine-grained pipeline allocation (1.61–1.88× speedup on ManiSkill). Achieves 98.11% on 130 LIBERO tasks, 97.66% on ManiSkill, 84.63% on RoboTwin.

**[Adaptation of Generalist Robot Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11363)** — See MiDAS summary above.

**[Early Warning Signals for OpenVLA Failure under Visual Distribution Shift](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29699)** — Logistic probe on internal activations predicts imminent failure (AUROC 0.972 under occlusion). Layer 16 strongest; monitors transfer to camera jitter but color shift is benign.

**[D3D-GEN: Robot-Aware Domain-Grounded Interactive 3D World Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11876)** — Domain agent + RAG pipeline to generate interactive 3D worlds for social robotics. Builds domain databases and produces floorplans/object placements loadable in Isaac Sim and Gazebo.

## World Action Models & Future Conditioning

**[RIFT: Rollout-free Imagination via Future Tokens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11521)** — Constructs complete future K/V cache in one backbone pass, eliminating iterative video rollout. 98.8% on LIBERO; 92.9% on RoboTwin 2.0; reduces action-chunk latency 68–89%.

**[4D-WAM: Infusing Spatiotemporal Awareness into World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08023)** — Injects 3D trajectory-field knowledge into WAMs via motion alignment and destination alignment objectives. Model-agnostic; improves spatial understanding, execution precision, and generalization.

**[ForeWAM: Latent Futures for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11605)** — Dynamics-conditioned direct-policy WAM. Future-KV performs single prefill over current latent + stochastic future slots, reuses K/V during action denoising. 96.7–96.9% on LIBERO; 61.6% on LIBERO-Plus.

**[From World Models to World Action Models: A Concise Tutorial](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00836)** — Tutorial defining world models vs world action models; compares World Labs' spatial intelligence, LeCun's JEPA, NVIDIA's Cosmos.

## Policy Robustness & Domain Adaptation

**[RoboHarness: Memory-Augmented Policy Harness for VLA Robustness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24060)** — Upgrades frozen VLAs via dual-memory RAG, attribution-driven MLLM orchestration, and MCP interventions. +56.6% absolute success gain on LIBERO-PRO; 89.1% on long-horizon chaining.

**[RoboSaGA: Saliency-Guided Augmentation for Behavior Cloning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11870)** — Pixel-level adaptive augmentation intensity using policy-driven saliency. Preserves in-domain performance while improving robustness to shadows, distractors, backgrounds.

**[VANE: Reliable Test-Time Training for VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09448)** — Conditions prompt adaptation on vision-language context; commits candidate updates only when future evidence confirms. +3.2pp on SimplerEnv WidowX over TTT baseline.

**[Policy-Induced Hand Priors in Humanoid Dual-Arm Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11769)** — Diagnoses initial-pose-dependent hand preference in VLA policies using HandPriorScore. Expanding pose coverage reduces bias; targeted augmentation improves robustness.

## Dexterous Manipulation & Tactile Sensing

**[Deformable In-Hand Slip-Aware Tactile Sensor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11952)** — First sensor integrating velocity, force/torque, and pressure map sensing in one compliant structure for slip-aware in-hand manipulation. Low-cost PCB-based fabrication.

**[FACTR 2: Learning External Force Sensing for Commodity Robot Arms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12406)** — Neural External Torque Estimation (NEXT) trains from 10 min of free-motion data without force sensors. Force-Informed Re-Sampling improves BC policy learning by >17% over prior force-aware policies.

**[FEWT: Frequency-Enhanced Wavelet-based Transformer for Bimanual Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.11109)** — Exoskeleton teleoperation + wavelet-based imitation framework. Smart Tactile Fabric sensors provide contact stress feedback; improvements over ACT baseline on bimanual insertion tasks.

**[Generalized Theory of Load Distribution in Redundantly-actuated Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11431)** — Theoretical characterization of wrench distributions for multifingered grippers and overconstrained mechanisms. Explicit, efficient solutions to wrench synthesis/analysis.

## Sim-to-Real & RL

**[Learning Loco-Manipulation From SMPC Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12063)** — SMPC in simulation generates offline datasets; sparse-reward off-policy RL trains high-level agent. Deploys to arm-equipped Spot and G1 humanoid without dense reward shaping.

**[TMRL: Diffusion Timestep-Modulated Pretraining for RL Finetuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12236)** — Bridges BC pretraining and RL finetuning by injecting forward-diffusion noise in inputs; dynamic timestep modulation grants explicit exploration control. Enables real-world fine-tuning < 1 hour.

**[From Synthesis to Removal: Physics-Grounded Reflection Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11562)** — Physics-grounded simulation to paired video data for diffusion-based video dereflection. Closed loop: S2R-Synthesis → S2R-Removal → S2R-Bench.

## Benchmarking & Evaluation

**[HandEdit: Benchmark for Egocentric Human-to-Robot Hand Image Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12122)** — 200M+ editing instances converting human hands to dexterous robotic embodiments. 26 URDFs, 5 datasets, 11 baselines evaluated.

**[DaViNCi: Outdoor VLN with Continuous Actions and Dynamic Elements](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11901)** — First outdoor VLN dataset with continuous actions and dynamic obstacles. Shows >10% success drop vs discrete environments.

**[Ego-OSCAR: Low-Cost Egocentric Stereo Capture System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08285)** — Open-hardware head-mounted stereo-inertial capture device (<$200 BOM), ~550 hours of egocentric stereo with IMU and 3D hand reconstructions.
