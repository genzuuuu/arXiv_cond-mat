### 🔬 Dexterous Manipulation & Robotic Hands

- **[Function beyond Form: Functional Correspondence for Cross-Embodiment Dexterous Grasp Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39006)**: Introduces FunCo-Grasp, which establishes "functional correspondences" across different robotic hands to allow grasp knowledge to transfer to unseen hand embodiments without retargeting.
- **[OccluDex: Hierarchical 3D Visuo-Tactile Representation Learning for Egocentric Dexterous Manipulation under Self-Occlusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39017)**: A framework using multi-scale masked autoencoders to fuse 3D geometry and tactile data, enabling robust manipulation even when the robot's hand occludes its vision.
- **[Combinatorial Optimization of Robotic Hand Kinematic Structures Using a Potential-Dexterity-Based QUBO Formulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15510)**: Formulates the design of a robotic hand's kinematic structure as a QUBO problem, solvable by classical and quantum annealing, to optimize for dexterity.
- **[DexHoldem: An Agentic Robotics Benchmark for Dexterous Manipulation in Texas Hold'em](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18727)**: A new benchmark for dexterous manipulation using a ShadowHand, evaluating agents on a series of Texas Hold'em-related tasks that require perception, decision-making, and careful execution.

### 🤖 Visuomotor Policies & VLA Models

- **[BIND: Binding 3D Robot Actions to 2D Image Features](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38443)**: Proposes an action representation that links 3D end-effector actions to their 2D image projections, leading to significant gains in data efficiency and robustness for visuomotor policies.
- **[Systematically Exploring the Capabilities of GPT-6 Astra as Embodied Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38537)**: A comprehensive evaluation of GPT-6 Astra's ability to act as an embodied policy, finding it excels at high-level task decisions but struggles with reliable low-level physical control.
- **[Correcting WHERE, Preserving HOW: Compositional Generalization for Vision-Language-Action Models via Referential Guidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38616)**: Introduces ReGuide, a training-free wrapper that uses a grounding module to correct a VLA's spatial intention, improving generalization to new object-destination combinations.
- **[Online Evolution Strategy for Flow-Matching VLA Policies via Self-Supervised Trajectory Distribution Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38855)**: Presents Online-ES, a method that refines the action distribution of Flow Matching VLA policies through online interaction and self-supervised reward, without needing a value model.
- **[Discrete Forcing: Infusing Discrete Guidance into Continuous Denoising for Few-Step Action Experts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39526)**: A flow-matching framework that first predicts a coarse discrete action structure and then uses it to guide continuous refinement, improving efficiency and performance.
- **[Text-to-3D Policy: Fine-Grained Language-Behavior Alignment for Unseen Specification Generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39599)**: Introduces T3DP, a framework that establishes token-level correspondence between linguistic elements and behavioral segments, enabling 3D policies to generalize to unseen, fine-grained instructions.

### ✋ Grasping & In-Hand Manipulation

- **[Optimize, Learn, Refine: Whole-Body Grasping and Pick-and-Throw with a Spiral Soft Robot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38202)**: A framework using derivative-free optimization to enable a soft spiral robot to perform whole-body grasping and throwing tasks by learning warm-start policies.
- **[Beyond the Current Scene: Event-Referential Grasping with Active View Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39375)**: A system for grasping objects based on their role in past events (e.g., "the cup I used earlier"), using an event prior and active view selection to find occluded targets.
- **[Adaptive Vision-Language Grasping via Composable Foundation Priors and Generalizable Grasp Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04096)**: Introduces AdaRoboVLG, a framework that decouples grasp synthesis from task understanding, allowing it to leverage foundation models for context-aware grasping without retraining the core policy.
- **[Tactile Curiosity Drives Robot Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40134)**: Introduces TacEx, a reinforcement learning framework that uses tactile feedback as an intrinsic motivation signal, directing exploration towards contact-rich interactions and improving sample efficiency.

### 🎮 Teleoperation & Human-Robot Interaction

- **[NEXUS: Perceptive Whole-Body Control for Terrain-Adaptive Teleoperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39000)**: A framework for whole-body teleoperation that enables a humanoid to adapt its motion to its own terrain, even when the human operator is on flat ground.
- **[CEER2: Directional and Tunable End-Effector and Root Compliance for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38709)**: Presents a compliance framework for humanoids that allows for directional stiffness control at the end-effector and tunable compliance for the robot's root, enabling safer physical interaction.
- **[Embodiment-aware control by inference over the operator: a simulation study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38437)**: A simulation study of a teleoperation controller that infers the operator's "Sense of Embodiment" from implicit signals like gaze and pupil data to adapt device settings, aiming to improve the operator's experience.

### 🌐 Sim-to-Real & World Models

- **[An Real-Sim-Real (RSR) Loop Framework for Generalizable Robotic Policy Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10118)**: Proposes an information-theoretic cost function to guide real-world data collection for improving sim-to-real transfer, applicable to both manipulation and locomotion tasks.
- **[SimEX: Simulation-Integrated Robotics AutoResearch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38982)**: An "autoresearch" framework where a coding agent uses a simulator to build a robot toolbox, then adapts it to the real world with only a few physical trials.
- **[PneuTac: Tactile Manipulation with Soft Pneumatic Robots via Unified MPM-Gaussian Splatting Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38418)**: A unified simulation framework for soft pneumatic robots with tactile sensors, using MPM for dynamics and 3D Gaussian splatting for rendering to enable sim-to-real transfer for contact-rich tasks.
- **[Learning to Build: Autonomous Robotic Assembly of Stable Structures Without Predefined Plans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23934)**: An autonomous robotic assembly framework that uses reinforcement learning to build stable structures from discrete blocks without a predefined blueprint.
- **[ExpertGen: Scalable Sim-to-Real Expert Policy Learning from Imperfect Behavior Priors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15956)**: A framework that automates expert policy learning in simulation by using RL to steer a diffusion policy trained on imperfect demonstrations, achieving high success rates on long-horizon tasks.
- **[Scaling Sim-to-Real VLA Reinforcement Learning with Generative 3D Worlds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18532)**: Leverages 3D world generative models to create a diverse dataset of interactive scenes for RL fine-tuning of VLA models, significantly improving sim and real-world success rates.

### 🧠 Tactile Sensing & Representation Learning

- **[What to Attend, What to Keep: Skill-Conditioned Visuotactile Representation with Progress-Guided Event Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38494)**: Proposes a skill-conditioned representation that dynamically fuses vision and touch based on the task, using a sparse "event memory" to retain key observations and reduce error on contact-rich tasks.
- **[Uni-VLaT: Whole-Body Tactile Adaptation of VLA Policies for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35450)**: Adapts VLA policies for humanoids by integrating whole-body tactile sensing via a predictive objective, leading to significant improvements on contact-rich loco-manipulation tasks.

### 🏗️ Benchmarks & Datasets

- **[Fiatlux: A Long-Horizon Benchmark for Humanoid Ladder Climbing and Light-Bulb Replacement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38216)**: A new benchmark for humanoids that combines vertical mobility (climbing a ladder) with dexterous manipulation (replacing a light bulb) in a single long-horizon task.
- **[TALK-Dem: Benchmarking Embodied Task Planning under Dementia-Associated Communication Patterns](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38371)**: A benchmark that evaluates LLM-driven task planners on their robustness to ambiguous and incomplete instructions typical of people with dementia.
- **[Benchmarking and Enhancing Skill-Level Memory for Partially Observable Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38886)**: Introduces the HIDE benchmark and SEEK framework to evaluate and improve memory mechanisms in policies for tasks requiring recall of hidden task states.
- **[Colosseum V2: Benchmarking Generalization for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27759)**: A large-scale simulation benchmark for evaluating the generalization of VLA models across 28 tasks and two robot morphologies, revealing significant gaps under distribution shifts.
- **[SafeVLA-Bench: A Benchmark for the Success-Safety Gap in Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00773)**: A benchmark that goes beyond task success to measure safety violations in VLA policies, using Signal Temporal Logic to quantify unsafe behaviors even in successful episodes.
- **[LIBERO-Agent: Evaluating General-Purpose Agents for Direct Embodied Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39507)**: An agent-native benchmark that evaluates general-purpose LLMs on their ability to perform robot manipulation by directly issuing native action commands.
