# arXiv Robotics & AI Summary — Embodied Intelligence Focus

## Robotic Manipulation & Grippers

**[Koala Gripper: Co-designing Robotic Grippers and Data-Capture Devices for Scaling Dexterous Manipulation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20546)** — A co-design framework for handheld gripper data collection and robotic execution. Features a novel force-optimized finger/trigger linkage, monolithic dual-thumb design, and backdrivable fingers (~tens of grams effective mass). Validated with a full end-to-end data collection and policy execution pipeline.

**[Hybrid Roller-Jamming Gripper for Object Acquisition and Retention Under Pose Uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20962)** — Integrates active object intake (rollers) with post-capture retention (granular jamming) in a single gripper. 812/840 success across planar and orientation offset trials; ablation shows both mechanisms contribute differently.

**[CoToGrasp: Contact-Topology-Conditioned Dexterous Grasp Synthesis via Canonical Workspace Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19776)** — Object-agnostic generative framework for synthesizing grasps conditioned on specific contact topologies. Trained in a canonical workspace, achieves zero-shot generalization on DexGraspNet and demonstrates physical viability on a real robot.

## Visuotactile Learning & Physical Property Estimation

**[VT-MUSE: Multimodal Unified Sequential Visuotactile Representation Learning for Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21290)** — Two-stage representation learning that captures cross-modal dependencies and temporal evolution of contact. Uses masked visual sequences with full tactile histories; outperforms strongest baseline by 11pp in simulation with real-world gains.

**[ViTacPhys: Physical Property-Aware Grasping from Human Visual-Tactile Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21355)** — Estimates mass, friction coefficient, and stiffness from human demonstrations. Achieves 95% grasping success in-distribution, 83.4% OOD. Transfer from human to robot domain via limited teleoperation data.

## Imitation Learning & Policy Self-Improvement

**[Beyond Imitation: Self-Improving Robot Policies via Off-Policy Q-Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21204)** — Equips a BC policy with a small Q-function that absorbs failed rollouts for value-guided action selection. LIBERO-10: 93%→99%; real bimanual tasks improve from 25-40% to 80-90% without modifying BC weights.

**[Rethinking Demonstration Unlearning in Imitation Learning for Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20784)** — Introduces a retrain-calibrated audit measuring behavior and evidence axes of unlearning. Shows edits can repair behavior while leaving evidence unchanged, or vice versa.

**[Update-Free On-Policy Steering via Verifiers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10282)** — UF-OPS uses verifier functions trained on policy rollouts to steer black-box diffusion policies at execution time. Average 49% success rate improvement across 5 real tasks without changing base parameters.

## Sim-to-Real & Locomotion

**[Natural Sit-to-Stand Motion Synthesis for Humanoids via Guided Assistance Curricula and Staged Rewards](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20823)** — RL from scratch for humanoid STS without demonstrations. Uses coupled force/chair-height curriculum; achieves >97% balanced standing across 8 chair heights.

**[Demonstration-Guided Humanoid Stand-Up on an Emulated Deformable Surface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20852)** — Reference-guided RL transfer from hard to soft ground using MuJoCo's solref/solimp parameters. Recovery rewards are essential; tracking alone is insufficient.

## Manipulation Planning

**[Fast Coordinated Bimanual Motion Planning With Hard Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20946)** — Leader-follower parameterization enforces rigid relative transformation between end effectors continuously. 19.4× faster planning than prior work; validated on Kinova Gen3 tray transport.

## World Models & Action Models

**[ForeTime-VLA: Causal Future-Token Distillation from a World Action Model for Conveyor-Belt Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20735)** — Distills future-aware representation from a frozen world action model for dynamic manipulation. Achieves 81.1% stationary and 58.9% slow-moving grasp success, exceeding pi0.5 by 12-22pp.

**[DECOWAM: Decoupled Whole-Body World-Action Model for Legged Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20114)** — Separates camera ego-motion from base and arm actions via dedicated conditional interfaces. Reduces action MSE by 21.7% over FastWAM; introduces ARMDOG real-robot dataset.

## Safety & Robustness for Manipulation Policies

**[CertVLA: Certified Defense against Physical Visual Attacks for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20791)** — Certified defense for closed-loop VLA control under bounded patch/texture attacks. Proves task success guarantee under dual-mask rollout correctness; validated in simulation and real-world.

## Tactile Sensing Security

**[GhostTac: Manipulating Tactile Sensors without Physical Contact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20817)** — First contactless attack on tactile sensing via electromagnetic interference. Can induce harmful robot behaviors including excessive force; evaluated on 10 sensors and two dexterous hands.

## Task Learning from Demonstrations

**[TaPeR: Probabilistic Recovery of Sparse Task Precedence Graphs from a Handful of Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21035)** — Recovers task dependency structure from kinematic graphs and object pose distributions. Enables flexible subtask reordering while preserving task validity.
