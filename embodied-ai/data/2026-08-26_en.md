# Today's arXiv Papers: Robotics & AI — Embodied Intelligence and Manipulation Focus

## World Models & Action-Conditioned Prediction

**[Learning to Act While Waiting: RL Finetuning of Generalist Robot Policies Under Inference Latency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23831)** — Introduces ARLI, a latency-aware RL framework that enables finetuning of large VLA policies under inference delays. State augmentations and mid-inference observations restore near-Markovian structure, allowing RL to succeed where standard methods fail entirely.

**[TrAct: Bridging Robot Control and Visual Prediction with Visual Tracks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24101)** — Proposes using visual tracks as an embodiment-agnostic interface between control and prediction. Combines a VLA-and-Track model with a track-conditioned world model, improving success from 27%→55% in simulation and 49%→76% on real Franka tasks over a strong π0.5 baseline.

**[Latent Action as Intention Enables Efficient Future Imagination for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24882)** — LAWA uses compact latent actions as operational future intentions, enabling test-time imagination without video generation. Achieves SOTA on RoboCasa (65.6% few-shot) with 42.9% lower inference latency than Joint-WAM variants.

**[Do Robotic World Models Really Follow Actions? Diagnosing and Aligning Action-Conditioned Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24885)** — Introduces WorldEcho benchmark showing current world models fail on off-expert actions, plus WorldSync which improves action-following through distributional coverage, representational grounding, and intervention-effect alignment.

**[GaussianWAM: Distilling Geometry and Semantics from 3D Gaussian Fields into World-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24714)** — Training-time framework that organizes geometric and semantic supervision through 3D Gaussian fields. Improves FastWAM from 52.05%→71.29% and Cosmos Policy from 71.52%→77.30% on LIBERO-Plus.

## Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models & Policy Learning

**[Hierarchical Skill Retrieval for Data-Efficient Adaptation of VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24042)** — HSR decomposes target tasks into skill sequences and performs hybrid retrieval combining subtask-level language matching with behavior-feature reranking. Improves success by 10.3% (LIBERO) and 21.3% (real-world) over strongest baselines.

**[Gripper-aware Vision Language Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24603)** — Introduces MiGA dataset (103K demos across 5 gripper types) and GVLA model with multi-gripper tokenizer and adapter-based policy routing. Demonstrates that different gripper types require distinct interaction strategies.

**[Trajectory-Level Continuous Action Representation for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24111)** — CAT encodes action trajectories into continuous latent tokens with frequency-aware positional encoding, avoiding representation growth with timestep density. Consistently outperforms VQ-based baselines across control frequencies.

**[PonderPounce: A Pretrained MLLM as an Episode Context Engine for Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24115)** — Reuses an MLLM's native causal context as robot memory without purpose-built memory modules. Achieves 60.83% on RoboMME (9B) with 20Hz action playback via asynchronous cognition refresh.

**[VGGT-DP: Generalizable Robot Control via Vision Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.18778)** — Integrates geometric priors from pretrained 3D perception (VGGT) with proprioceptive feedback, plus frame-wise token reuse for latency reduction. Outperforms DP and DP3 on MetaWorld precision-critical tasks.

**[Inferring Action from Future Latent State for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22067)** — DELE-w0.5 infers actions from predicted future latent states rather than video generation, achieving 62.5% full-task success and 81.3% ordered-stage progress across 480 real-robot trials.

## Tactile Sensing & Manipulation

**[Robust Slip Detection and Material Classification via Spatiotemporal Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24162)** — Visuo-tactile sensor with uniform RGB illumination and dual-head TimeSformer for fine-grained 8-class slip direction classification (91.5%) and 3-class contact states (95.5%) on unseen objects.

**[A Durable Vision-Based Tactile Fingertip for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24242)** — New design with TPU protective film achieves >2 orders of magnitude durability improvement over GelSight Mini/DIGIT under accelerated sanding and repetitive loading tests.

**[Sensorless damage-safe grasping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23983)** — Bounds deformation rather than force using only encoder position and motor effort, achieving ≥98% grasp with 0% damage across fruit-stiffness ranges where fixed-force baselines fail.

**Briefly noted:** [Fiber Optic Sensing Glove for Dexterous Manipulation Capture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24572) achieves 7.2mm fingertip error for hand pose capture; [Fiber Bragg Grating Whiskers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24724) enable underwater hydrodynamic perception.

## Learning from Demonstration & Contact-Rich Manipulation

**[One-Shot Learning from Demonstration of Contact-Rich Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24741)** — Explicitly models physical interactions (contact making/breaking) using hybrid position-force control. Reproduces door opening, bolt picking, and screwing from single demonstrations with robustness to geometric variation.

**[Macro-Operator Generation and Predicate Selection for TAMP Operator Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23629)** — Automatically discovers macro-operators from demonstration data, achieving up to 4.6x planning speedup and solving long sequential tasks that baseline methods cannot.

**[ForceFlow: Learning to Feel and Act via Contact-Driven Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11048)** — Force-aware reactive framework with asymmetric multimodal fusion, achieving 37% success improvement over ForceVLA across six contact-rich real-world tasks.

**[Concept-Guided Exploration: Building Persistent, Actionable Scene Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23650)** — Concept-first architecture where asynchronous agents instantiate rooms and doors through hierarchical constraint propagation.

## Teleoperation & Data Collection

**[Fiber Optic Sensing Glove for High Performance Dexterous Manipulation Capture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24572)** — Multi-core shape-sensing fibers with inverse-kinematics solver reconstruct hand pose at 60Hz. Achieves 7.2mm mean fingertip error (4.9mm after calibration), enabling bimanual virtual teleoperation.

## Robot Motion Planning & Control

**[NeurRAFT: Robot Motion Planning via Anchor-Level Flow Matching with Clearance-Aware Preference Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24026)** — Generative planner operating on compact anchor waypoints with DPO for clearance. Demonstrates zero-shot transfer to Franka under noisy, partially occluded depth.

**[Safety-aware Model Predictive Path Integral Control with Signal Temporal Logic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23972)** — Encodes STL constraints as time-varying CBFs within MPPI, validated on Mars Rover scenarios and quadcopter planning in Isaac Lab.

**[Trusted Polytopic Action Sets for Fast Planning in Underactuated Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24019)** — Generates trusted convex sets of short-horizon actions, solving cluttered scenes 14-78x faster than kinodynamic RRT baselines.

## Sim-to-Real & Robustness

**[Resilience Matters for Embodied Agents System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23839)** — New resilience evaluation framework exposing Rebound, Stability, and Graceful Extensibility across 400 household tasks, revealing process-level distinctions hidden by outcome metrics.

**[CARO: Contact-Agnostic Residual Observation for Zero-Shot Robust Quadruped Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24217)** — Embeds Euler-Lagrange model in RL loop for torque-level residual observations without torque sensors, improving OOD payload and terrain robustness.
