## 1. Failure Detection & Safety for VLA Policies

**[FailureSpot: Label-Efficient Timestamp-Level Failure Detection for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04277)**
- *Jie Ma, Zongxi Liu, Yi Zhu*
- Addresses a key gap: existing proactive failure detectors for VLA policies use **trajectory-level labels**, which mislabel normal pre-failure behavior as failure. Proposes a **data-efficient framework** that leverages unlabeled action chunks to build weak supervision signals (e.g., inconsistent chunks, frozen actions, random motions), then uses **active learning** to select uncertain trajectories for timestamp-level annotation. Improves both timestamp- and trajectory-level detection across VLAs.

**[LIBERO-RECOVER: Beyond Task Success Towards Failure Recovery in Robotic Manipulation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05178)**
- *Lin Liu, Zhicheng Bao, Lu Zhang, et al.*
- Argues near-perfect success on LIBERO masks a missing capability: **failure recovery**. Introduces a benchmark with 1,000+ scenarios across four recovery levels: Action Retry, Action Adaptation, Object State Recovery, and Environmental Recovery. Evaluates spatial understanding, object structure reasoning, interaction understanding, and topological reasoning. Shifts evaluation from "Can it succeed?" to "Can it recover?"

**[RedVLA: Physical Red Teaming for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22591)**
- *Yuhao Zhang, Borong Zhang, Jiaming Fan, et al.*
- First **red-teaming framework for physical safety** in VLA models. Two-stage process: (1) Risk Scenario Synthesis identifies critical interaction regions from benign trajectories and positions risk factors to entangle with execution; (2) Risk Amplification iteratively refines via gradient-free optimization. Achieves up to 95.5% attack success rate (ASR) within 10 iterations across six models. Also releases SimpleVLA-Guard, a lightweight safety guard trained on RedVLA data.

**[Evaluating Uncertainty and Quality of Vision-Language-Action-enabled Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17049)**
- *Pablo Valle, Chengjie Lu, Shaukat Ali, Aitor Arrieta*
- Empirically evaluates 8 **uncertainty metrics** and 5 **quality metrics** for VLA robots across 908 successful executions (3 models, 4 tasks). Finds several metrics show moderate-to-strong correlation with human expert judgments (used as a human oracle). Challenges binary success-rate evaluation, supporting quality-aware monitoring and adaptive enhancement.

## 2. VLA Training, Efficiency & Architecture

**[VLA-Precision: Asymmetric Co-Bootstrapping for Efficient Real-World Online RL of Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04355)**
- *Chenyu Su, Zhaolong Shen, Yuan Qian, et al.*
- Presents an **online RL framework** for VLA post-training on precision tasks. Introduces **ACoB (Asymmetric Co-Bootstrapping)** across timescales (intervention-guided behavioral learning → global return + local preference ranking), and **ACoB-Stream architecture** with invariant-state decoupling and on-demand streaming (up to 10.9× throughput gains). Achieves 98.3% mean success on nine precision chemistry tasks in 45.8 min/task across four robot embodiments.

**[RL-VLA³: A Flexible and Asynchronous Reinforcement Learning Framework for VLA Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05765)**
- *Haoran Sun, Yongjian Guo, Zhong Guan, et al.*
- Identifies that synchronous RL frameworks mismatched VLA training latencies. Introduces a **fully asynchronous distributed RL framework** with dynamic batching schedulers and flexible environment sharding. Achieves up to 85.2% throughput improvement over synchronous baselines with identical sample efficiency, scaling from 8–256 GPUs.

**[CF-VLA: Efficient Coarse-to-Fine Action Generation for Vision-Language-Action Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24622)**
- *Fan Du, Feng Yan, Jianxiong Wu, et al.*
- Flow-based VLAs are expressive but need multi-step inference from noise. Proposes a **two-stage formulation**: coarse initialization learns a conditional posterior over endpoint velocity to construct an *action-aware starting point*, followed by single-step local refinement. Outperforms NFE=2 methods, matches NFE=10 π₀.₅ baselines, cuts latency by 75.4%, and achieves 83.0% real-robot success.

**[Reasoning Without Inference Cost: Latent Semantic Scaffolding for Robot VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04893)**
- *Andrew Ting Yan Li, Zhuo Li, Zhelin Yang, et al.*
- Asks whether causal reasoning benefits for VLA can be captured **during training** and discarded before deployment. **LSS (Latent Semantic Scaffolding)** aligns action-token representations to rationale text embeddings via a small projection head (dropped at inference, zero added cost). Key finding: **Dense LSS** — aligning per action token to its own manipulation-phase rationale — transfers markedly better than pooled episode-level alignment to held-out tasks.

**[Knowing When to Stop: Adaptive Action Chunking via Internal Cross-Attention Dynamics in VLAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00908)**
- *Runze Xu, Xiaolong Shan, Shuang Dai, Yu Wang, Jincheng Yu*
- Fixed execution horizons in VLA action chunking impose a trade-off. Observes that as prediction horizon extends, **action-to-observation cross-attention entropy rises** toward a plateau, correlating with higher prediction errors. Proposes a **training-free truncation mechanism** detecting sustained high-entropy plateaus to select execution horizon online. Improves success over fixed-horizon and adaptive baselines on π₀.₅ and X-VLA across RoboTwin 2.0, LIBERO, and three real tasks.

## 3. Tactile Sensing & Contact-Rich Manipulation

**[TacPAC: Tactile Prediction and Real-Time Action Correction in World-Action Models for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05266)**
- *Zipei Ma, Xiaofei Wei, Junzhe Jiang, Shunlin Lu, Li Zhang*
- Naively predicting future tactile observations as added views recovers only ⅓ of achievable gain — timing mismatch (predictions precede execution; feedback arrives during). **TacPAC** caches predicted contacts with plan representation; a tactile expert reads new tactile images against the cache to correct *not-yet-executed* actions. One correction pass is **20.7× cheaper** than regenerating the chunk. Raises average success from 22% (vision-only) to 64% across 5 real-robot tasks (insertion, fragile-object handling, reorientation, long-horizon).

**[Temporal Tactile Encoding and Compliance for Intent-Aware Robot-to-Human Bimanual Handover](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05282)**
- *Pasquale Marra, Stefano Berti, Gabriele Mario Caddeo, Lorenzo Natale*
- Treats handover as a **multimodal problem**: couples a VLA model with a compliance controller. Fine-tunes VLA with RGB + **temporally encoded tactile feedback** + proprioception. Human-subject study vs. baselines (no tactile / no compliance). Shows complementary benefits: compliance facilitates physical interaction; tactile history captures sustained taking intent. Substantially outperforms baselines on objective metrics and questionnaire.

**[Measuring Proximity to Standard Planes During Fetal Brain Ultrasound Scanning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07124)** *(noted for tactile-adjacent sensing)*
- Semi-supervised segmentation model with 6D plane pose regression, deployed on NVIDIA Clara AGX at 39 Hz (exceeds clinical acquisition rates). Validated on real scan videos from 17 sonographers; shows operators freeze near local minima of proximity signal.

## 4. Robotic Hands & Dexterity

**[Morphology and Actuation as Inductive Biases in Robotic Hand Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05206)**
- *Zalán Tari, Eszter Birtalan, Péter Polcz, Miklós Koller*
- Presents a **unified framework** analyzing kinematic and actuation stages separately and in composition via conditioning of the task Jacobian, actuation matrix, and product. Applied to Shadow Dexterous Hand vs. Anatomically Correct Biomechatronic Hand across four aspects (joint axis geometry, actuator-to-DOF ratio, coupling, authority). Anatomical fidelity carries no uniform advantage: oblique axes help the thumb but hurt long fingers; branching tendons improve fingers but worsen thumb control. Validated with RL experiments (PPO, DDPG+HER, TQC+HER) across three tasks.

**[A Kinematic Framework for Screening Candidate Pinch Configurations in Robotic Hand Design without Object or Contact Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20692)**
- *HyoJae Kang, Joonho Lee, Hyunmok Jung, Dong Il Park*
- Evaluates pinch capability from **fingertip workspace interactions alone** — no object geometry or contact force models needed. Computes reachable workspaces, detects feasible pinch configurations from fingertip-pair relationships. Compares four kinematic hand structures, supporting design-stage pinch analysis.

**[Rapid On-Robot Learning for Dynamic Manipulation Skills: Robot Juggling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26800)**
- *Taeyoon Lee, Chunpeng Wang, Christopher G. Atkeson, Alfred A. Rizzi, Nicolas Rojas*
- Key lesson: **imperfect models are still useful**. Presents regularized memory-based online learning — builds a local model from accumulated experience while retaining global prior for extrapolation. Constructs a *mutually reachable set* for safe transitions. A bimanual robot with multi-fingered hands learns five three-ball juggling patterns (cascade, tennis, half-shower, shower, box) in <5 minutes of real interaction.

## 5. Visuomotor Policies & World Models

**[Dressing in Motion: A Human Motion-Aware Diffusion Policy for Robot-Assisted Dressing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04759)**
- *Haoxiang Sun, Fangyuan Wang, Songhao Huang, et al.*
- Visuomotor policy learning dressing skills from static demos, generalizing to **dynamic user motion**. Diffusion policy on partial point clouds with varied arm postures; an **object-centric PDE-diffusion representation** captures arm axial distribution for reactive trajectory adaptation. Real study with 9 participants, 3 garment types, 6 arm-motion patterns. Outperforms baselines in dressing progress, freedom of movement, and comfort.

**[One Word, Different Action: A Real-Robot Benchmark for Language-Conditioned Embodied Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05260)**
- *Yiwei Liu, Luwei Yang, Shunbo Lei*
- Introduces benchmark with **task-preserving and task-changing instruction pairs** to jointly evaluate Decision Invariance and Decision Sensitivity (plus multi-constraint reasoning, real-RGB grounding). Modern models are near saturation on single constraints but degrade when multiple constraints must be integrated into one executable action.

**[What Matters, When? Diagnosing and Improving Conditional Visual Grounding in Visuomotor Imitation Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05376)**
- *Vivek Chavan, Pengtao Xie, Yahuan Shi, et al.*
- Studies *conditional visual grounding*: when the visual target changes with task state (e.g., pick A then B). Systematically introduces distractors with controlled color/shape similarity, localizes failures to picking vs. placement. Evaluates **distractor augmentation, phase-dependent attention regularization, appearance-based visual prompting**. Substantially improves robustness in simulation and on a UR3e — including transfer to a pretrained VLA.

## 6. Loco-Manipulation & Whole-Body Control

**[FWBC-VLA: Force-Aware Whole-Body Compensation for Contact-Rich Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03889)**
- *Yutian Zhang, Siyuan Ma, Liwen Yang, et al.*
- Bridges task-level VLA action generation with low-level whole-body control (WBC). Introduces **HSR-Force**, a sensorless residual-torque estimator encoding contact strength as tokens injected into the VLA action expert. Contact state + proprioception + Jacobian-derived force feed a compensation generator for corrective actions. Real-world validation: whiteboard wiping and door opening (with door closer) on wheeled-legged robots.

**[TONAV: Task-Oriented Navigation and Action-Velocity Chunk Learning for Articulated Object Quadrupedal Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22296)**
- *Haoran Lin, Mingyu Yang, Pengfei Qi, et al.*
- Unified framework closing the **navigation-manipulation gap**: (1) position-velocity-coupled teleoperation for consistent demos; (2) task-oriented navigation with VLM reasoning for manipulation-ready configurations; (3) **action-velocity chunk learning** jointly modeling joint positions and temporal transitions. Real-world success across articulated-object tasks.

**[CoFreeVLA: Short-Horizon Collision-Free Dual-Arm Manipulation via Vision-Language-Action Model and Risk Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21712)**
- *Yaohua Liu, Binkai Ou, Hengjun Zhang*
- Augments end-to-end VLA policies with a lightweight **self-collision risk estimator** predicting likelihoods from proprioception, visual embeddings, and candidate actions. Proactively gates risky commands, synthesizes recovery trajectories, biases policy refinement. Two-stage training (model-based synthetic + real-robot rollouts). Across 5 bimanual tasks, 6 VLA backbones, 30 trials each: collision rate drops 0.54→0.23; success rises 0.45→0.61.

## 7. World Models & Embodied Reasoning

**[Persistent Robot World Models: Stabilizing Multi-Step Rollouts via Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25685)**
- *Jai Bardhan, Patrik Drozdik, Josef Sivic, Vladimir Petrik*
- Action-conditioned world models degrade autoregressively. Proposes **RL post-training** on the model's own rollouts (not ground-truth history), adapting a contrastive RL objective for diffusion models. Compares multiple candidate futures and reinforces better-fidelity predictions via multi-view perceptual rewards. State-of-the-art on DROID: LPIPS ↓14% (external), SSIM ↑9.1% (wrist), 98% paired wins, 80% human preference.

**[From Interaction Traces to Persistent Skills: Online Evolution for Computer-Use Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04869)** *(relevant to teleoperation/interaction learning)*
- *Longtao Hu, Xiao Liang, Linchao Zhu*
- Converts trajectories + evaluator feedback into a **persistent, versioned skill library**. Compares full evolving-library vs. empty-library control across OSWorld domains (5.7–18.6 pp improvements). Finds benefits are conditional; repeated revision does not guarantee recovery.

## 8. Sim-to-Real & Efficient Learning

**[Squint: Fast Visual Reinforcement Learning for Sim-to-Real Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21203)**
- *Abdulaziz Almuzairee, Henrik I. Christensen*
- Visual SAC variant achieving **faster wall-clock training** than prior visual off-policy and on-policy methods, via parallel simulation, distributional critic, resolution squinting, layer normalization, tuned update-to-data ratio, and optimized implementation. Evaluated on SO-101 Task Set (8 manipulation tasks in ManiSkill3 with heavy domain randomization). 15-min training on one RTX 3090 — most tasks converge <6 minutes; successfully transfers to a real SO-101 robot.

**[MINT: A Unified Model for World-Space Camera and Hand Motion Estimation from Scalable Egocentric Pipeline Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04958)**
- *Zijie Zhu, Weiren Cai, Yizhou Wang, et al.*
- First foundation model directly producing **complete world-space two-hand trajectories** from egocentric RGB. Jointly predicts camera trajectory, hand states, hand presence from a shared spatiotemporal representation. Open-source EGOPIPELINE converts large egocentric video collections into structured camera-and-hand supervision. Releases model, code, pipeline, and 1,021-hour trajectory dataset.

**[X2-N: A Transformable Wheel-legged Humanoid Robot with Dual-mode Locomotion and Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21541)**
- *Yan Ning, Xingzhou Chen, Delong Li, et al.*
- High-DoF robot transforming between humanoid and wheel-legged forms. RL-based whole-body control for hybrid locomotion, transformation, and manipulation. Validated on dynamic skating, stair climbing, package delivery.

## 9. Grasping, Object Recognition & Skill Transfer

**[Where Appearance Fails, Geometry Recognizes: A CAD-Free 3D Shape Prior That Complements Vision Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04381)**
- *Chenxi Tao, Seung-Kyum Choi*
- For industrial parts without CAD models: a short object-centric scan → 3DGS reconstruction → per-class shape prototype, fused with frozen DINOv2 features. Geometry alone reaches 0.920 (vs. 0.832 image-only) on shape-distinctive HOPE; modest but consistent gains on confusable textureless parts (T-LESS). The worth lies in **geometry, not rendered pixels**; benefit grows under occlusion.

**[Pack It My Way: Triadic Human-Robot Collaboration for Personalized Autonomous Packing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04620)**
- *Sandeep Chowdary Kotapati, Yanxin Gao, Tsung-Chi Lin*
- Compares **human-expert vs. voice-agent mediation** for correcting robot packing preferences (Protection, Compactness, Grouping). Uses a Show-Correct-Generalize process. Voice agents match human expert in 2/3 preference categories despite shorter instructions, though humans are perceived as more reliable regarding generalization.

**[SocioGesture: Real-Time and Adaptive Social Gesture Perception for Human-Robot Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04545)**
- *Wenjin Fu, Li-Fan Wu, Jerin Peter, et al.*
- Real-time social gesture recognition for HRI using a **confidence-aware body-hand skeleton representation** and lightweight dual-stream model fusing body motion with hand articulation. Trained with occlusion-aware skeleton corruption for robustness. Runs in real time on edge devices; supports vocabulary expansion post-deployment.

**[CFAMs: Continual Field-Adaptive Models for Post-Deployment Physical AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04552)**
- *Amarjot Singh, Tanmay R. Pancholi, et al.*
- **Complimentary learning architecture**: frozen slow-learning (Sensor/Reasoning/Action cortices) + fast-learning Capsule Field with one-shot, gradient-free learning. Evaluated on five embodiments (manipulator, quadruped, humanoid, quadrotor, off-road). Reaches full-prior-training operating point using **40% of data**; autonomous near-OOD capture improves action success by 13.9 pp; backward transfer −0.5 pp vs. −11.4 for LoRA.

## 10. Embodied Reasoning & Long-Horizon Tasks

**[Towards Neuro-Symbolic Procedural Reasoning for Long-Horizon Vision-Language-Action Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05369)**
- *Vivek Chavan, Yahuan Shi, Oliver Heimann, et al.*
- Combines learned VLA control with **explicit task graphs and multimodal procedural memory**. These structures guide object selection, destination grounding, subgoal dispatch, and state-transition verification. Also uses pseudo-gaze annotations in teleoperated videos to guide VLA fine-tuning. Evaluates ordered workspace-clearing and surgical-instrument handling (conditional branching, ordered execution).

**[RoboSPA: Can VLA Models Go Beyond Simple Scenes and Short-Horizon Tasks?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05324)**
- *Zhenxuan Fan, Bo Zhang, Yutong Lin, et al.*
- Large-scale dataset and benchmark diagnosing **fine-grained spatial reasoning** and **long-horizon procedural planning**. 10 task categories, 56 base tasks, 5 difficulty levels (280 variants), 527K trajectories. Current VLA systems still struggle with complex spatial relations, precise low-level execution, and memory-intensive planning.

**[One Diffusion Model, Two Roles: Guided Trajectory Planning and Safety-Critical Scenario Generation in Closed-Loop Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04921)**
- Uses a pretrained diffusion traffic model both as an ego motion planner and as a controllable safety-critical scenario generator for stress-testing planners, showing benchmark superiority does not imply robustness.

## 11. Benchmarks, Evaluation & Human-Robot Interaction (Noted Briefly)

- **[NavArena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04602)**: Automated construction of goal-oriented navigation benchmarks from 3DGS reconstructions (22.2M expert trajectories).
- **[HaptiNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04799)**: Networked haptic robots for distributed rehabilitation (validated with 284 healthy participants, 111 patients).
- **[Same Trajectory, Contradictory Rewards (ROBORMBENCH)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05401)**: Paraphrase fragility in VLM reward models — rewriting instructions can flip identical behavior between failure and success across 2,390 trajectories.
- **[Align Under Pressure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17295)**: AR-HMD support tools for action-team alignment (healthcare coordination).
- **[Humanoid Robot Prototype for Multimodal HRI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05361)**: 12-DoF dual-arm + 2-DoF head testbed with gesture/object/speech AI modules (~1.83 cm manipulation error).
- **[Strict Modes Everywhere](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04817)**: Potential design densely filling configuration space with strict nonlinear normal modes for efficient two-DoF manipulator periodic trajectories.
