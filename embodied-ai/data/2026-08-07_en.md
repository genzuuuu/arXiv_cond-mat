# Robotics & AI Papers — Focused Summary

## Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models & Manipulation

**[VLAff: Vision-Language-Affordance Model for Unified Actionable Affordances](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05215)** — Proposes a VLM-based framework learning visual, grasp, and trajectory affordances from egocentric human videos (204K episodes). Shows SOTA visual affordance prediction and zero-shot real-robot manipulation.

**[World-to-Wrist (W2-VLA)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05369)** — A VLA that models future wrist states conditioned on global task context via latent modeling tokens. Improves fine-grained contact-sensitive manipulation across LIBERO, RoboTwin 2.0, and real bimanual tasks at >80 Hz action rate.

**[Beyond Flat Policies: Hierarchical Post-Training (HiRoC)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05999)** — Hierarchical VLA post-training decoupling LLM-based task planning from RL-optimized low-level execution, outperforming flat baselines across manipulation benchmarks.

**[DyPES-VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06374)** — Cross-embodiment VLA with shared dynamics priors (via future prediction) and embodiment-specific MoE action head. SOTA on LIBERO (98.0%), RoboCasa-GR1 (59.25%), and RoboTwin 2.0 (89.02%).

**[MMaDA-VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25406)** — Fully native discrete diffusion VLA jointly denoising goal observations and action chunks. Average 98.0% success on LIBERO, 4.78 seq length on CALVIN.

**[DFM-VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26320)** — Discrete flow matching VLA with iterative action token refinement and metric-aligned tokenizer. Evaluated on CALVIN, LIBERO, and real-world tasks.

**[In-Context VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05738)** — Argues CoT degrades low-level control in VLAs; proposes in-context post-training with grounded language consumption and agentic tool use. SOTA on RoboCasa-GR1, SimplerEnv, LIBERO, and 8 real tasks.

**[JoyAI-RA 0.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05674)** — Generalist VLWA framework with dual action alignment (implicit latent actions + explicit canonical representation), scaling manipulation learning from human egocentric video.

**[SkillMemo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05970)** — Skill-based episodic memory with MoE-guided trajectory segmentation for compositional manipulation. Outperforms π0.5 on simulation and real tasks.

## Cross-Embodiment & World Models

**[XEWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05799)** — Controlled cross-embodiment world model testbed. Finds current models are 2D visual pattern matchers gated by visual rather than kinematic similarity, limiting zero-shot generalization.

**[GeniWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06332)** — Interactive world model using URDF-based rendering to convert numerical actions to visual action representations, improving OOD generalization.

**[Robust-WAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05903)** — Post-training method preserving VAE-based generation while adding semantic foresight alignment on actions, improving OOD robustness under illumination shifts.

**[Tactile-WAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26663)** — Touch-aware world action model with asymmetric attention preventing "tactile pollution." Improves contact-rich manipulation success from 15.6% to 32.7% on ManiFeel.

**[DynamicWAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00793)** — Adds optical-flow history and kinematic descriptor conditioning for dynamic object manipulation. Achieves 46.7% avg success across 12 real-world dynamic tasks.

**[ω-0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06375)** — Latent predictive whole-body world-action model for humanoid concurrent loco-manipulation. Demonstrates 11 real-world household tasks with a single model.

**[Enfold](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26657)** — Distills future-generative computation into a representation predicted from the present, reducing action latency 3.7–10.1× vs Fast-WAM baselines.

## Tactile Sensing & Contact-Rich Manipulation

**[Near-sensor Computing for Rapid Visuotactile Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05725)** — FPGA-based near-sensor pipeline for visuotactile depth reconstruction (128×128 at 0.211 ms fixed latency), closing a robot protective reflex loop in 28.3 ms vs 169.9 ms host-based.

**[VIDP: Variable Impedance Diffusion Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06210)** — Infers compliance from task-parameterized trajectory distributions without force sensors. Outperforms fixed-impedance baselines in task success and interaction force reduction.

**[CADRE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14768)** — RL framework for catching falling objects using NDF-inspired contact descriptors and implicit recovery affordances. Zero-shot generalizes to unseen object geometries.

**[Tactile-WAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26663)** — (see World Models section) — asymmetric tactile attention improves slip and contact-direction awareness.

## Teleoperation & Human Demonstrations

**[Touchscreen Teleoperation for Robotic Manipulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06219)** — Comparative study (n=20): touchscreen interface achieves 53.5% faster task completion and lower cognitive load vs conventional joystick for surface contact tasks.

**[Handwritten Alphabet Trajectories LfD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06221)** — GMM/GMR-based LfD framework on 3,142 handwriting demonstrations, incorporating force and time; evaluated for perceived human-likeness (71.5/100 score).

**[MDIR: Task-Manifold Impedance Retargeting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29271)** — Single-demonstration impedance retargeting via manifold decomposition, reducing force peaks, impulse, and variability in contact-rich teleoperation executions.

**[TRACT: Temporal Routed Action Chunks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29285)** — Phase-structured action chunking with chronological authority for contact-rich tasks. Achieves 10/10 full-sequence success on six real-robot variants (vs 6/10 flat baseline).

## Sim-to-Real & Embodiment Adaptation

**[Simulator-Side Dynamics Normalization (S3N)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01697)** — Normalizes serial-tree simulator dynamics for parallel-link leg mechanisms, reducing joint torque RMSE by 82.1% and sim-to-real gap from 17.3% to 9.9%.

**[Rapid Embodiment Adaptation for Quadrupedal Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01506)** — Online embodiment identification from 0.5 s interaction; maintains stable locomotion on Unitree Go2 with a fully locked leg and 5 kg payload.

**[TRACE: Tokenized Robust Attention Odometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05975)** — Learned proprioceptive odometry with foot-aware cross-attention, robust to unreliable contact conditions across varied terrains.

## Ergodic Control & Surface Reconstruction

**[ErgoSurf](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06208)** — Online ergodic control for surface coverage with GPIS-based geometry learning from tactile sensing. Validated in simulation and real robot for sanding/polishing-like tasks.

**[TRACE: Ergodic Trajectory Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02304)** — For active Gaussian-splatting scene reconstruction; treats coverage as ergodic problem, improving PSNR by 1.5 dB over NBV baselines on Replica.

## Observation Alignment & Multi-View Learning

**[ARGUS: Aligning Robot Scene Geometry Under Shifting Views](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05579)** — Uses large 3D vision models to canonicalize observations from arbitrary viewpoints before visuomotor policies. Converges 4–6× faster on viewpoint-diverse training data (DROID, BridgeV2).

## RL & Post-Training for Manipulation

**[Prophet + FlowScale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20633)** — World-model-based RL post-training for flow-based VLA action heads; 5–17% success gains on public benchmarks and 24–30% on real robots.

**[Observation-Grounded Self-Predictive RL (OG-SPR)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05989)** — Combines latent self-prediction with observation prediction via lightweight adapters. Improves aggregate performance across 28 DeepMind Control Suite tasks.

## Path Planning & Multi-Robot Systems

**[PathCover](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05586)** — Randomized convex decomposition from raw point clouds in expected linear time. Order-of-magnitude speedup over SOTA for safe trajectory planning on quadrotors and quadrupeds.

**[Search-Aided Joint RL (SJRL)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05588)** — For lifelong MAPF with rotations; jointly optimizes agent and environment policies. Validated with 8 physical + 248 virtual robots in mixed-reality warehouse.

## Failure Handling & Safety

**[Physical Prompt Injection in VLM Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05715)** — Systematic study: adversarial text in robot's visual field acts as indirect prompt injection (27% success on GPT-4o). Evaluates mitigations including text masking (100% effective).
