# Summaries of arXiv Papers on Robotics and AI

## Dexterous Manipulation & Tactile Sensing

**[SiMDex: Mining Similar Egocentric Videos for Cross-Embodiment Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04196)** — Presents a similarity-based data mining framework to select task-relevant human egocentric videos for VLA post-training in dexterous manipulation. Using only ~1.49M mined samples (5% of a 32M pool), it improves success rates from 47.7% to 61.1%, showing selective data curation outperforms indiscriminate mixing.

**[Tactus: Open-Vocabulary Object Recognition from Low-Cost Pressure Arrays](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04043)** — Demonstrates open-vocabulary object recognition from resistive pressure arrays alone, matching supervised closed-set performance without a trained classifier head. Uses masked-autoencoder pretraining and sensor calibration, showing tactile representation learning is viable for low-cost sensors.

**[Zero-shot Sim2Real Transfer for Magnet-Based Tactile Sensor on Insertion Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02915)** — Proposes GCS, a sim-to-real technique for magnet-based tactile sensors (u-skin) that enables zero-shot transfer of RL policies with raw 3-axis tactile readings on blind insertion tasks, addressing the large sim-to-real gap without lossy binarization.

**[RORA: Realistic Object Reconstruction with Articulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04842)** — End-to-end pipeline for reconstructing simulation-ready articulated objects from single static videos, combining 3DGS for photorealistic rendering with mesh-based geometry. Includes automatic joint suggestion and demonstrates real-time dexterous hand manipulation in Isaac Sim.

## Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models & Policies

**[Retrieve in Time, Correct in Frequency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04527)** — Introduces RTCF, a training-free test-time correction framework for frozen VLAs that separates retrieval from action transfer, applying low-frequency residual corrections. Improves LIBERO-Long success from 61.6% to 68.6% with only 10.99ms added latency, no parameter updates.

**[Mind-VLA: Instruction-Aware Spatial Representation Alignment for VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04633)** — Addresses instruction-agnostic representation alignment by aligning VLA latents with target-object tri-view features. Achieves 93.9% on LIBERO with a 345M backbone and outperforms instruction-agnostic methods by 32pp on real-robot target occlusion tasks.

**[DeepThinkVLA: Enhancing Reasoning Capability of Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15669)** — Identifies decoding alignment and causal alignment as necessary for effective CoT in VLAs. Uses hybrid-attention decoder and two-stage SFT-then-RL pipeline to reach 97.0% success on LIBERO and 79.0% robustness on LIBERO-Plus.

**[DreamWAM: Beyond RGB Future Prediction for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04996)** — Reforms world model future prediction to include motion, geometry, and semantics beyond RGB, improving robustness on LIBERO-Plus (51.36%→63.44%) and real-world manipulation under unseen perturbations.

**[BridgeVLA++: A Data-Efficient, Generalizable, and Memory-Augmented VLA Framework for 3D Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05042)** — Extends BridgeVLA with unified spatio-temporal memory architecture for memory-dependent manipulation, achieving SOTA on two challenging benchmarks while preserving data efficiency and generalization.

**[Explicit Language Memory for Long-Horizon Planning in VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04765)** — Proposes hierarchical architecture with explicit language-memory module for long-horizon tasks, converting temporal observations into textual memory sequences with temporal logic, improving success rates and providing interpretable decisions.

**[GUARD: Grounding Uncertainty and Ablation-Based Risk Detection for Diffusion-Based VLAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04510)** — Introduces test-time failure detection by probing action-head dependence on multimodal evidence. Improves unseen-task ROC-AUC by 5.73pp over competing runtime monitors across Pi0, SmolVLA, and Alpamayo-1.5.

**[SAFECAST: Robust Failure Detection for VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04246)** — Uses contrast-set perturbations to improve hidden-state probe training for deployment-time failure detection, significantly improving ROC-AUC on DROID and LIBERO with sim-to-real calibration benefits.

**[Suppression Sticks, Locality Is Fragile: A Closed-Loop Target-and-Control Audit of Task-Vector Negation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04692)** — Audits task-vector subtraction in VLA policies, finding three behavioral regimes across skills with only 52% mean control retention and fragility to held-out initial states.

**[RESample: A Robust Data Augmentation Framework via Exploratory Sampling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17640)** — Coverage-guided data augmentation that generates failure recovery trajectories using conservative coverage functions and exploratory sampling, improving policy success by up to 12% with no more than 20% additional samples.

## Manipulation & Kitchen Tasks

**[Kitchen Robotic Manipulation utilizing Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04042)** — Presents a modular perception pipeline for household dishware tasks integrating open-vocabulary detection, multi-view segmentation, and 6D pose estimation. Best configuration (LLMDet+SAMv2+DINOv2+GeoTransformer) achieves 89.12% ADI in cluttered scenes and deploys without retraining.

**[Interpretable Fuzzy Inference for UAV Target Tracking Using Bounding-Box Geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04121)** — Briefly noted: interpretable fuzzy-inference framework for UAV yaw control with MAE of 0.140° using YOLO features — relevant for vision-guided control.

## Sim-to-Real & Simulation

**[SimFoundry: Modular and Automated Scene Generation for Policy Learning and Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28276)** — Zero-shot real-to-sim scene construction from video with digital cousins for generalization. Simulation evaluations strongly predict real-world (Pearson r=0.911), with improvements of 17-40% from object, scene, and task variations.

**[GenTrack: Physical Alignment for Robot-Native Motion Generation and Zero-Shot Humanoid Tracking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01410)** — Online generator-tracker co-training for humanoid tracking with execution-grounded alignment, expanding zero-shot coverage on Unitree G1 with ProtoMotions/SONIC backbones.

**[RoboReact: Agentic Skill Distillation from Generated Egocentric Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03387)** — Full-body humanoid manipulation skills synthesized from single egocentric RGB-D observations via video generation, depth-aware 3D reconstruction, and VLM-guided refinement — no teleoperation required.

**[Arnold: A multi-task, multi-embodiment muscle transformer policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18066)** — Multi-task/multi-embodiment musculoskeletal control policy using sensorimotor vocabulary and transformer architecture, matching specialist policies across 14 tasks including dexterous manipulation and locomotion.

## Tactile & Transparency Perception

**[From Transparent Labware Segmentation to Collision Avoidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04769)** — Edge-aware segmentation for transparent glassware, achieving 97.80 Boundary F-score with 7.1ms inference and 93.3% collision avoidance success on real robots for fragile transparent objects.

## Motion Planning & Control

**[GASP: GPU-Accelerated Safe Planner for Real-Time Collision-Aware Motion Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04612)** — GPU-accelerated joint-space planning using B-spline parameterization with residual neural network prediction, achieving analytical-level success with near-millisecond inference and demonstrating value as an RL reset planner.

**[Geometry-Aware Sampling-Based Motion Planning on Riemannian Manifolds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00992)** — Introduces midpoint-based Riemannian distance approximation with third-order accuracy for planning under configuration-dependent metrics, consistently outperforming Euclidean planners on low-cost trajectories.

## Embodied Navigation & Agents

**[SpikingNav: Robust Embodied Navigation with Spiking Neural Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05078)** — Spiking neural framework for navigation improving ObjectNav success from 31.05% to 34.12% and robustness under corruptions (8.45%→13.71%), validated on Thruster-V2 neuromorphic chip.

**[Mimir: A Neuro-Symbolic Memory System with Dynamic Grounding for Embodied Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04933)** — Separates world/task memory with dynamic grounding before each action, improving EB-ALFRED and EB-Habitat by up to 42.5% and achieving 86.0% on long-horizon subsets.

**[SCOPE: Field-of-View-Aware Path Planning in Unknown 3D Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04420)** — Safety-volume certification with observation planning, proving conditional complete planning and demonstrating zero unsafe entries across 60 randomized tasks with 27% mean mission time reduction.

## Datasets & Evaluation

**[ResPlan: A Large-Scale Vector-Graph Dataset of 17,000 Residential Floor Plans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14006)** — Briefly noted: dataset for floor-plan understanding with vector geometry and room-connectivity graphs — relevant for embodied navigation benchmarks.

## World Models & Learning

**[Overcoming Statistical Bias in Action-Controllable World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04653)** — Counterfactual consistency framework (CoCo) reduces shortcut reliance in action-conditioned world models, achieving 73.1% success on VP2 visual planning while preserving prediction quality.

**[Curiosity-Diffuser: Curiosity Guide Diffusion Models for Reliability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14833)** — Uses RND curiosity to guide diffusion models toward training distribution, improving policy reliability and behavior similarity through classifier guidance.

## Policy Learning & Reinforcement Learning

**[Imitation Learning from Human Motion Alone Does Not Guarantee Biomechanically Plausible Gait Kinetics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12408)** — Shows motion-only IL produces significantly erroneous GRF and joint moments despite kinematic accuracy, with kinetics-aware IL incorporating GRF/CoP in rewards substantially more biomechanically consistent.
