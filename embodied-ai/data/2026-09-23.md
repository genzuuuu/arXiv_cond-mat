### **Dexterous Manipulation & Robotic Hands**

This batch features several papers on dexterous manipulation, from new hardware designs to novel control and learning frameworks.

- **The Cartesian Hand** introduces a new 7-DoF end-effector that uses linear motion for in-hand manipulation, showing versatile capabilities like cap opening and pipetting. [The Cartesian Hand: In-Hand Manipulation with All-Linear Fingers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25696)
- **VisForce** proposes a method to visually ground current and desired forces at fingertip locations, enabling force-aware control for dexterous hands. [VisForce: Visual Grounding of Current and Desired Forces for Goal-Conditioned Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25785)
- **Touch2Robot** presents a framework where human demonstrations are collected while visualizing how the target robot hand would contact the object, improving the quality and efficiency of dexterous data collection. [Touch2Robot: Robot Touch in the Human Demonstration Loop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24660)
- **Relative Contact Velocity-Controlled Hand-Object Mechanism** models the hand and tool as a unified system, enabling five different robotic hands to perform complex tool manipulation tasks like picking up and wielding. [Relative Contact Velocity-Controlled Hand-Object Mechanism for Dexterous Tool Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25619)
- **DexJoCo** is a new benchmark and toolkit for task-oriented dexterous manipulation, featuring 11 tasks and 1.1K trajectories to evaluate tool use and bimanual coordination. [DexJoCo: A Benchmark and Toolkit for Task-Oriented Dexterous Manipulation on MuJoCo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16257)

### **Visuomotor Policies & Bimanual Manipulation**

Several papers focus on improving visuomotor policies for complex, often bimanual, manipulation tasks, emphasizing geometric awareness and coordination.

- **JAMB** is a diffusion policy that jointly models bimanual actions and future 3D point tracks, allowing action and motion hypotheses to refine each other for better coordination. [JAMB: Joint Action-Motion Diffusion for Bimanual Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25322)
- **RoboMP-DINOv2** uses segmentation masks as spatial prompts rather than hard filters for a DINOv2 vision encoder, improving generalization for robot manipulation. [RoboMP-DINOv2: Prompts, Not Filters for Robust Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25506)
- **StrataVLA** proposes a framework for hierarchical 3D geometric grounding in Vision-Language-Action models, injecting features from a frozen geometry model at multiple depths. [StrataVLA: Hierarchical and Efficient 3D Geometric Grounding for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26071)
- **RouteRLT** is a routing framework that learns when to use specialized RL policies for precision-critical phases of a task, while relying on a generalist VLA model otherwise. [RouteRLT: Learning When and Which RL Specialist Should Control a Vision-Language-Action Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26467)
- **MAVP (Map-Aware Visuomotor Policies)** improves mobile manipulation by predicting explicit base-pose targets in a shared map frame, which are then tracked with a low-level controller. [MAVP: Map-Aware Visuomotor Policies for Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26378)
- **SafeLoop** is a non-invasive safety wrapper for VLA models that adds hazard prediction and rollback-based recovery to prevent failures like collisions. [SafeLoop: Risk-Aware Rollback for Vision-Language-Action Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26313)

### **Tactile Sensing & Sim-to-Real**

Papers in this section explore improving tactile simulation and transferring contact-rich skills from human demonstrations or simulation to robots.

- **Norm2Tex** is a method to augment vision-based tactile simulations with high-frequency surface details from normal map textures, reducing the sim-to-real gap for texture recognition. [Norm2Tex: Augmenting Visuo-Tactile Simulations with Texture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25398)
- **What is the Better Curriculum** uses a tactile reflex controller as a "teacher" during data collection to shape grasping behavior, resulting in better policies for contact force-sensitive manipulation. [What is the Better Curriculum: Controller-Shaped Grasping Behavior for Contact Force-Sensitive Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25887)
- **World In Your Hands (WIYH)** is a large-scale ecosystem for learning manipulation from human-centric data, including a wearable collection kit and a dataset of over 1,000 hours of data. [World In Your Hands: A Large-Scale and Open-Source Ecosystem for Learning Human-Centric Manipulation in the Wild](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24310)

### **Humanoid & Whole-Body Control**

This group of papers addresses challenges in humanoid robotics, including forceful manipulation, transport in clutter, and learning from demonstrations.

- **HOTICE** is a whole-body learning framework for humanoid robots to transport objects through cluttered environments, using potential fields and a dual-agent RL architecture. [HOTICE: Whole-Body Humanoid Object Transportation in Cluttered Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25363)
- **Brace Yourself** introduces a "Supporting Hand Strategy" where a humanoid braces against the environment with one hand to perform forceful manipulation tasks with the other. [Brace Yourself: Task-Conditioned Environmental Bracing for Forceful Humanoid Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25486)
- **Whole-Body UMI (WB-UMI)** transfers manipulation skills from a Universal Manipulation Interface (UMI) to humanoid whole-body control, enabling tasks like shelf pick-and-place from end-effector trajectories. [Whole-Body UMI: Transferring UMI Manipulation Skills to Humanoid Whole-Body Manipulation via Real-Time Motion Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22829)

### **Teleoperation & Shared Control**

These papers present new methods for teleoperation, shared control, and data collection for complex robotic systems.

- **Capability-Aware Arbitration** is a shared-control framework that combines human intent confidence from a VLM with a VLA policy's online capability confidence to improve task success and reduce over-helping. [Capability-Aware Arbitration for Semantic Intent-Based Shared Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25369)
- **MATE** is a multi-agent virtual teleoperation platform for collecting collaboration data for humanoids, removing the need for physical robots during the collection process. [MATE: Multi-Agent Virtual Teleoperation Platform for Humanoid Collaboration Data Collection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26520)
- **Digital Twin-Driven VR Teleoperation** presents a VR platform for surgical robots that uses a digital twin to provide multi-view rendering and improved depth perception over traditional consoles. [Digital Twin-Driven VR Teleoperation with Multi-View Spatial Perception for Surgical Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25527)
- **A Deployable Four-Finger Payload** details a bimanual teleoperation pipeline for free-flying manipulators like Astrobee, controlled via a VR interface for tasks like cargo handling. [A Deployable Four-Finger Payload for Teleoperated Free-Flying Manipulation with Astrobee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25614)

### **Other Notable Papers**

- **MachEmbodied-U0** is a unified foundation model for embodied AI that combines understanding and generation experts to guide action generation with predicted visual dynamics. [MachEmbodied-U0: Unified Understanding and Generation Model for Embodied Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25627)
- **Learning to Plan in Human-Robot Collaboration** proposes a multimodal RL approach to automatically generate interaction policies for collaborative tasks, like helping a user find objects. [Learning to Plan in Human-Robot Collaboration: Multimodal Reinforcement Learning for Adaptive Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25274)
- **PAKT** is a framework for kinesthetic teaching in reinforcement learning that uses admittance control to keep human-guided demonstrations within the robot's kinematic limits. [PAKT: Physically-Aligned Kinesthetic Teaching for Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25630)
- **Zeva** is an in-context learning framework that allows a frozen policy to adapt from its own physical interactions by storing and retrieving causal interaction signals. [Zeva: In-Context Causal Learning for Generalizable Embodied Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30880)
- **VLAQuantBench** provides a closed-loop evaluation of post-training quantization for VLA models, identifying recipe-dependent interactions and providing concrete precision assignments. [VLAQuantBench: Closed-Loop Evaluation of Post-Training Quantization for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25376)
