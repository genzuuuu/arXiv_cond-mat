### Dexterous Manipulation & Robot Hands

- **GOTT: Object-centric Dexterous Manipulation with a Reusable Cross-Embodiment Primitive**: Introduces GOTT, a reach-acquire-move framework centered on a single, reusable contact-acquisition primitive. It enables multi-fingered robots to establish stable contact from an approximate initialization, allowing them to execute object-centric trajectories from various sources like human demos and planners. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03861)
- **AgenticTactileVLA: Contact-Guided Execution-Time Supervision for Generalizable Dexterous Manipulation without VLA Retraining**: Proposes an execution-time supervisor for a fixed Vision-Language-Action (VLA) model. It uses proprioceptive feedback (finger position, motor effort) to refine grasps and adapt to novel objects, improving completion rates from 61.3% to 84.0% on a Unitree G1 without needing tactile sensors or VLA retraining. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04391)
- **TacOT: Learning Contact-Rich Dexterous Manipulation from Human Demonstrations via Tactile-Guided Optimal Transport**: Presents TacOT, a framework for transferring contact-rich manipulation skills from human videos to robots. It uses action-tactile dynamic time warping to find human-robot correspondences with consistent contact dynamics, improving success rates in tasks like in-hand reorientation and plug insertion by up to 20 points. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04363)
- **PatternDex: Learning Interaction Patterns to Guide Reinforcement Learning of Bimanual Dexterous Manipulation of Articulated Objects**: Introduces PatternDex, a method for bimanual dexterous manipulation of articulated objects. It learns "interaction patterns" from human demonstrations to guide a reinforcement learning policy, achieving a 92.8% success rate with Allegro hands, significantly outperforming the 52.2% baseline. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04765)
- **DexForge: High-Fidelity Physics-Informed Dexterous Retargeting**: Presents DexForge, a differentiable physics-grounded framework for converting human video demonstrations into high-fidelity robot trajectories. It combines contact-aware kinematic retargeting with force-aware dynamics retargeting, showing a 35-53% success rate improvement across seven dexterous hands. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06331)

### Grasping & In-Hand Manipulation

- **Robot Learning with Visual Predicted Force**: Demonstrates that force-aware manipulation can be achieved through visual force prediction from the deformation of a compliant Fin Ray gripper. A policy is trained to generate actions and their expected forces, and at test time, it selects the action with the predicted force closest to a target from demonstrations. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04741)
- **GraRe: Grasp Candidate Re-Ranking for Frozen 6-DoF Grasp Detectors**: Proposes GraRe, a method to re-rank grasp candidates from frozen 6-DoF grasp detectors. By estimating grasp quality from candidate attributes, local geometry, and object context, it improves the Average AP on the GraspNet-1Billion benchmark by up to 13.56 points. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00946)
- **RAGrasp: Geometry-Semantic Template Retrieval and Grasp Transfer**: Introduces RAGrasp, a retrieval-augmented pipeline for planar parallel-jaw grasping. It uses a small set of locally collected, grasp-annotated RGB-D templates to achieve high success rates (20/20 on seen, 19/20 on unseen objects) without requiring end-to-end retraining for new deployments. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04438)

### Visuomotor Policies & VLA Models

- **Sparse Calibration-Based Personalization of Kernel-Based Gait Phase and Speed Estimation Using Wearable IMUs**: A study on personalizing gait estimation models from wearable IMUs using sparse calibration data. It found that personalized kernels reduced error but the effect was not always statistically significant, highlighting challenges in real-world deployment. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03931)
- **SUAVE: Unified Video-Action Models via Masked Diffusion**: Presents SUAVE, a unified model using a masked diffusion transformer to jointly generate video and actions as discrete tokens. A single model can act as a world model, a robot policy, or a video-action model, and can be co-trained on both labeled robot data and unlabeled human video to improve policy performance. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04009)
- **REDIRECT: A 1% Fix for Bad Robot Habits**: Introduces REDIRECT, a method to repair "bad habits" in robot policies learned from mixed-quality teleoperation data. By identifying and fixing local behavioral differences, it raises the mean success rate from 68.2% to 90.3% while using only 1% of the full-training sample-backward budget. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03997)
- **Grounded in Time: A Multi-Source Dataset and Benchmark for Temporal Grounding in Robotic Manipulation**: Introduces GiT, a dataset and benchmark for history-dependent manipulation tasks. It includes 18 bimanual tasks with real-robot and UMI-style demos, plus a simulation suite, and finds that current VLA models have substantial room for improvement in tasks requiring memory of past events. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04255)
- **ExStereo: Lifting 2D Vision-Language-Action Models to 3D with Explicit Stereo Representations**: Proposes ExStereo, a module that augments pre-trained 2D VLAs (like $\pi_{0.5}$ and SmolVLA) with 3D perception from stereo images. It uses action-stereo cross-attention to condition actions on 3D scene information, consistently outperforming RGB-only baselines in simulation and on a real bimanual robot. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04805)
- **Vela: Scaling Vision-Language-Action Models with Adaptive Action Curve Parametrization**: Introduces Vela, a VLA model that represents future motion as continuous trajectories using splines rather than fixed-rate action chunks. This approach allows a fixed output budget to adapt its temporal resolution across motions, demonstrating strong results on long-horizon real-world tasks. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05230)
- **RACE: When to Switch: Reliable Action-Chunk Extension for Vision-Language-Action Models**: Proposes RACE, a framework that improves the reliability of executing longer action chunks in VLAs. It predicts the optimal transition timing between sub-skills within a chunk, enabling longer chunks and reducing idle time from "stop-and-go" execution by about 5x on a real robot. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05719)
- **Rethinking Off-Policy Ratios: An Entropy-Normalized Trust Region for Asynchronous Reinforcement Learning**: Introduces the Entropy-Normalized Trust Region (ENTR) to stabilize asynchronous RL for LLM post-training. It addresses the instability from stale data by recognizing that the policy ratio's scale is set by token entropy, improving performance on long-horizon agentic tasks. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22186)

### Sim-to-Real & Data Generation

- **Tackling Sim-to-Real Mismatch Through Sampling-Based Disturbance Observers: From Analytical Models to Learned World Models**: Introduces the sampling-based disturbance observer (SDOB), which extends the classic disturbance observer (DOB) principle to work with simulators and learned world models via state-rollout or cost-query interfaces. It compensates for sim-to-real mismatch in both traditional and learned models. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04896)
- **TUCO: Curating Simulation Demonstrations for Sim-to-Real Robot Policy Co-Training**: Presents TUCO, a systematic study on data curation for sim-to-real policy co-training. It uses influence functions to measure the value of simulation demonstrations, selecting a complementary subset that reduces redundancy and improves policy performance across single-simulator, sim-to-sim, and sim-to-real settings. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05407)
- **Dual Variational Autoencoders for Efficient Sim-to-Real Transfer in Low-Cost Robotic Navigation**: Proposes a hybrid transfer learning framework that combines domain randomization with feature-level domain adaptation. The dual VAE architecture learns a common latent space from simulated and real images, achieving a ~91% success rate on real-world indoor navigation tasks. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06327)
- **TDC: Sim-to-Real Transferable Directional Compliance for Contact-Rich Manipulation**: Introduces TDC, which learns a "directional component" of force regulation from simulation. A visuomotor policy predicts a task frame and control mode, which an admittance controller uses with human-specified stiffness to achieve adaptive compliance on real-world tasks like microwave opening and peg insertion. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14174)

### Teleoperation & Human-in-the-Loop

- **DITTO-X: Forward and Reverse Teleoperation for Dexterous Manipulation and Human Intervention**: Presents DITTO-X, a dexterous teleoperation interface that provides joint-level force and fingertip contact feedback. Its key feature is reverse teleoperation, where the robot back-drives the operator's fingers to match its current configuration before control transfer, improving demonstration quality and intervention in contact-rich tasks. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00781)
- **TacGELLO: Tactile Contact Feedback for a 3D-Printed Teleoperation Leader**: Introduces TacGELLO, a system that uses the existing servos of a 3D-printed leader arm for haptic feedback. It holds the gripper trigger on contact detection and uses follower joint current to adjust servo output, providing resistance during lifting. This improves command tracking and reduces closure error. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05583)
- **Reward-DAgger: Robot-Gated Interactive Imitation Learning with General-Purpose Progress-Based Reward Models**: Proposes Reward-DAgger, an interactive imitation learning framework using a general-purpose reward model to gate human interventions. It is agnostic to policy architecture and requires no retuning of the gating mechanism across tasks, improving autonomous success rates in simulated and real-world tasks. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04054)
- **Mulligan: Performance-Guided Data Collection for Efficient On-Robot Learning**: Introduces Mulligan, a method for efficient on-robot learning that makes the initial-state distribution a decision. It starts data collection rounds from observed failures and untried states, and incorporates a value function trained on all data (including failures) to improve data efficiency, showing 10-34 percentage point gains in final real-task success. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05882)

### Tactile Sensing & Force Control

- **Tactile Perception through Fluid-Solid Interaction**: Presents a class of soft tactile sensors that operate without electronics at the sensing site. The sensor uses a fluid-filled channel connected to external pressure sensors; touch displaces the fluid, creating pressure patterns that encode position and force. It works in environments where electronic sensors fail, like underwater. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03773)
- **Embracing Modern Text-to-Image Models with Post-Training Fine-Tuning**: Introduces a method to verify whether an item is real, addressing the challenge of LLMs generating plausible but non-existent references. The framework, CiteCheck, combines scholarly retrieval with a structured LLM verifier to detect citation hallucinations, achieving 88.7 macro-F1 on a physics benchmark. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27700)
- **TORL-VLA: A Tactile-Aware Vision-Language-Action Model for Contact-Rich Robotic Manipulation**: Presents TORL-VLA, a VLA model that integrates tactile feedback for contact-rich tasks. It uses a pre-trained vision-language model and a tactile expert with a novel routing mechanism, improving success rates on contact-rich tasks like wiping and assembly. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10301)

### World Models & Planning

- **FLEX-WAM: Flexible Block-Causal World-Action Models for Long-Horizon Imagination and Planning**: Introduces FLEX-WAM, a flexible and efficient block-causal World-Action Model. It supports variable-length contexts and non-causal prediction horizons, enabling stable, long-horizon open-loop rollouts. A single checkpoint can serve as a play policy and an expected-outcome predictor. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05483)
- **RoboIRS: Inference-Time Internal Representation Steering for Generalist Robot Policies**: Proposes RoboIRS, an inference-time method to improve VLA and WAM performance under distribution shift. It trains linear classifiers on success/failure rollouts to find steering directions in the policy's internal representations, improving success rates on 15 simulation tasks from 44.4% to 66.2% without updating policy parameters. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04681)
- **Test-Time Training as Residual Memory for Robot Policies**: Introduces TTT-RM, which uses Test-Time Training (TTT) as a "residual memory" for long-horizon tasks. It learns a history decoder to reconstruct past representations from the current observation and retrieved memory, and the reconstruction *residual* becomes the learning target for TTT, preserving task-relevant historical information that cannot be recovered from current context. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04701)
- **GAMBIT: Learning to Plan Continuous Multi-Robot Trajectories**: Presents GAMBIT, a framework for learning coordinated multi-robot trajectory planning. It uses imitation learning followed by reinforcement learning to learn "self-sacrificial" behaviors (like giving way) for better team performance, and includes a safeguarded rollout mechanism to guarantee collision-free execution. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06290)
- **Continual Humanoid Motion Learning**: Addresses catastrophic forgetting in humanoid whole-body controllers. It introduces Similarity-guided LoRA-PNN, a progressive neural network that reuses knowledge across skills via low-rank adaptation, achieving high forward transfer and parameter savings while being deployed on a physical Unitree G1. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04231)
