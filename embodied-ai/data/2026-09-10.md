## World Models & Latent Dynamics

- **Identifying Habit, Physics, and Nuisance in Robot World Models** — Splits teleop multimodality into operator habit, shared physics, and observation nuisance via a structural causal model; a "freeze physics, adapt a thin interface" rule improves low-shot transfer. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09210)
- **JEPA Policy: Diffusion-Free Imitation Learning via Paired Action and Future Representation Prediction** — Pairs action chunks with observed future representations in a shared Transformer; beats Diffusion Policy with minimal added latency. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09630)
- **DUET-DINO: Simultaneous Cross-View World Modeling for Latent Planning in Robot Manipulation** — Jointly predicts side- and wrist-camera latents for full 7-DoF latent planning; 92% on reach, 72.5% angled-reach, 60% lift. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10506)
- **HaWMPO: Hallucination-Aware World Model-based Policy Optimization** — Estimates reliability of imagined rollouts and suppresses unreliable action chunks in GRPO; +15% over base on LIBERO, 67.5%→80% on a G1. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09941)
- **Compact Visuotactile World Models for Lifting** — Touch cuts force-prediction error substantially, but force-constrained success stays low; careful separation of sensing gains from control gains. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09597)
- **Semigroup-JEPA: Latent Dynamics Consistency for Zero-Shot Physics Generalization** — Action-conditioned physics parameters in latent rollouts; most gain comes from the encoder learning carry-forward features. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10464)
- **Zero-shot World Models Are Developmentally Efficient Learners** — Sparse temporally-factored predictors trained on one child's first-person experience show staged emergence of physical understanding. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10333)

## Dexterous Manipulation & Robotic Hands

- **Assembling Two Parts in One Hand** — RL for in-hand mating of two rigid objects in a single hand (no fixture, no second arm); three tasks, zero-shot sim-to-real with one camera. Notable for exposing hand-morphology demands. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10137)
- **Grounding Generated Video Plans in Simulation Towards Versatile Dexterous Controllers** — Uses generated HOI videos as references for a simulated HOI tracker; 1,500+ videos grounded, >25pt success gains, functional grasps and non-prehensile manipulation on hardware. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10050)
- **Dex-X: Learning Visual-Tactile Dexterous Manipulation From Human Videos with Simulated Interaction** — Treats simulation as a "tactile completion engine" for human videos; 93% real-world cube picking, 53% on table cleaning. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07747)
- **MuJoCable: Reduced-Order Surface-Routed Cable Transmission for Tendon-Driven Robots** — Adds config-dependent cable routing, unilateral tension, and Capstan friction to MuJoCo; validated on SpiRobs hardware. Directly useful for tendon-driven hand/tool design. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09612)

## Tactile Sensing & Grasp Force Control

- **Tracing Energy Flow: Learning Tactile-based Grasping Force Control to Reduce Slippage** — Physics-informed "virtual energy container" abstraction infers slip from applied-vs-retained power; learns in minutes, no external sensing or prior object knowledge. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21043)
- **Tactile Memory with Soft Robot (TaMeSo-bot)** — Masked tactile trajectory Transformer with a soft wrist; retrieval-based control transfers to unseen pegs and conditions in peg-in-hole. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19275)

## Vision-Language-Action Policies

- **Frequency-Conditioned Flow Matching for Vision-Language-Action Models (FreqFM)** — Makes action frequency an explicit conditioning dimension in DCT space; +9.3 on LIBERO-Plus, plus six real-robot tasks. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10405)
- **Time-Frequency Geometric Cross-Attention for Chunked Vision-Language-Action Models (TFGCA)** — Wavelet tokens plus a dot/wedge-product hybrid attention; +28.5 under RoboTwin randomization, +11.67 on real AgiBot A2 tasks. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09925)
- **VLA-Precision: Asymmetric Co-Bootstrapping for Efficient Real-World Online RL of VLA Models** — Real-robot online RL with streaming architecture; 98.3% on nine high-precision chemistry tasks, 10.9× throughput. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04355)
- **Proxy Policy Steering (PPS)** — Inference-time adaptation via two lightweight proxy policies steering a frozen base sampler; +53% absolute over π0.5 on 8 real + 4 sim tasks, without modifying base parameters. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09148)
- **RoboDrop: Curating VLA Post-Training Data via Local Gradient Compatibility** — Gradient-compatibility scoring filters corrupted demonstrations; real-robot success 35.0%→67.5%. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10021)
- **FiberTune: Preserving Action-Fiber Visual Residuals in VLA Fine-Tuning** — Preserves action-independent visual structure during fine-tuning; +10.7 SR(5) on CALVIN, 72.7%→78.1% on SO-101. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08653)
- **LM-X: Explainable VLA Modeling via Progress, Event, and Uncertainty Prediction** — Jointly pretrains return-to-go, event-to-go, and action-flow variance with control; 74.1% on RoboTwin2.0 vs 55.4% for GR00T N1.7. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25757)
- **Bit-Flip Attacks on Vision-Language-Action Models** — Gradient-selected INT8 bit flips drive closed-loop success to 0%; flow-matching heads are markedly more robust (~100–300 flips) than regression/token heads (1–5). [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15475)
- **GTA-2: A Multi-VLM Framework for Synthesizing Robot Manipulation Skills via Grounded Task Axes** — Four VLM agents build keypoint/axis-based skills zero-shot; 73.9% avg, 90.7% with targeted human refinement, over 14 real tasks. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09808)
- **Show-Harness: Just a VLM Agent Can Play Robots** — Compact semantic action interface lets frontier VLMs control robots zero-shot and small VLMs cheaply; includes a GUI demonstration collection path. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10522)

## Imitation Learning & Benchmarks

- **Monkey See, Can Monkey Do? A Benchmark for Evaluating Robot Skill Learning by Observation (RoboReel)** — Ten manipulation tasks, four test suites, seven+ LfO algorithms; long-horizon and low-tolerance tasks remain hard. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08209)
- **FolDeX: A Physical-World Benchmark for Long-Horizon Robotic Manipulation of Deformable Objects** — 2,000+ hours of real-robot garment folding data across 20+ tasks and 10+ embodiments; four data-reuse research axes and a public evaluation platform. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10243)
- **Deformable Object Manipulation under Partial Observability via Real-Time Full-Shape Estimation** — Lightweight cRVAE estimates full rope/fabric state from corner nodes; 350–1500× faster than XPBD, demonstrated on a Unitree Go2. (From an earlier listing, but squarely on-topic.) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10308)

## Teleoperation & Shared Control

- **Adaptive Shared Control with Online Bounded-Rational Human Behavior Estimation** — Level-k bounded rationality with a forgetting-factor posterior over candidate human policies; distribution-aware one-step best response has a closed form. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10215)
- **A Confidence-Aware Multimodal Fusion Framework for Industrial Human-Robot Collaboration** — Fuses 6D pose, gaze, skeleton, and IMU with confidence-trend-driven dynamic fusion; 91.86% intention accuracy on a UR3 platform. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10339)

## Sim-to-Real, System ID & Real2Sim

- **RealSimLoop: Online Real-to-Sim Adaptation via Differentiable Reduced-Order Simulation with Vision Feedback** — Differentiable simulation in a reduced-order neural subspace plus differentiable rendering; tracks time-varying material properties in quasi-real-time. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09828)
- **Posterior-driven Heuristic Support Adaptation in a Probabilistic Treatment of Real2Sim2Real for Vision-Driven DLO Manipulation** — Addresses misspecified support in likelihood-free inference; better length/stiffness classification and more robust policies for deformable linear objects. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26656)
- **What Symmetry Buys a Learned Motion Planner** — Expressing trajectories in a start-goal frame lifts held-out collision-free rate from 14.6% to 51.1%; architecture-level equivariance mechanisms add under a point. Relevant to anyone weighing analytic structure against learned equivariance. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10033)

## Legged & Whole-Body Locomotion

- **Sumo: Dynamic and Generalizable Whole-Body Loco-Manipulation** — Test-time steering of a pretrained whole-body policy with a sample-based planner; uprights a tire heavier than Spot's nominal lift capacity, generalizes to humanoids in sim. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08508)
- **SwingBot: Learning Whole-Body Brachiation for Humanoid Robots** — Biomimetic keyframes plus recurrent privileged-state estimation enable continuous bar traversal with passive wrist hooks. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10283)
- **Learning Terrain-Adaptive Humanoid Locomotion on Granular Terrain** — 3D resistive force theory contact model; first agile humanoid locomotion on real basalt, dry sand, and beach sand. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10286)
- **PGMT: Perceptive General Motion Tracking for Humanoid Robots** — Motion-conditioned terrain glimpses and terrain-aware tracking relaxation; zero-shot on G1 over obstacles up to 37 cm. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08511)
- **ViBe: Visual Behavior Adaptation for Perceptive Humanoid Whole-Body Control** — Low-rank adapters graft a pretrained visual encoder onto a motion tracker; zero-shot sim-to-real on curbs, parkour, loco-manipulation, dodgeball. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09918)
- **Actuator Dynamics Curricula for Narrow-Viability Tasks in Legged Robot Learning** — Annealing joint stiffness enlarges the viability kernel; enables a Spot quadruped-to-handstand transition under fixed-stiffness training plateaus. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09492)
- **Unleashing Infinite Motion: Scaling Expressive Quadrupedal Motion via Generative Video Priors** — LLM generates motion prompts, video diffusion synthesizes behaviors, lifted to 3D references; 96.7% real deployment success on Go2. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28237)

## Motion Planning & Control

- **Kino-PAX⁺: Near-Optimal Massively Parallel Kinodynamic Sampling-based Motion Planner** — Focused-node propagation with proved asymptotic δ-robust near-optimality; up to three orders of magnitude faster than serial methods. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02846)
- **PccDiffuser: Multi-solution Motion Planning for Continuum Robots** — Conditional diffusion over configuration-space paths with GNN obstacle encoding and analytical differential kinematics in denoising; 91% success. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09745)
- **Bilevel Planning with Learned Symbolic Abstractions from Interaction Data** — Probabilistic symbolic rules propose plans; learned continuous effect models verify them and forward search on failures. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08599)
- **Risk-Constrained Belief-Space Optimization for Safe Control under Latent Uncertainty** — CVaR-regularized belief-space MPPI with probabilistic safety guarantees; 82% success with zero contact violations in vision-guided dexterous stowing. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03868)

## Robot Learning, Evaluation & Surveys

- **No Free Checker: A Survey of Verifiers for Robot Policies** — ~150 verifiers organized along availability vs. credibility; the central claim is that credibility falls as availability rises. Useful framing for eval design. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09250)
- **A Decade of Bayesian Optimization for Controller Tuning and Robot Learning** — Tutorial/review plus an initial lightweight benchmark suite for control-oriented BO. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09403)
- **Seven Sources of Physical AI Capability Formation** — Taxonomy of capability-formation sources (recorded experience, predictive modeling, surrogate environments, mechanism grounding, embodied coupling, etc.) with saturation claims. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09627)
- **Meta-RL with Bayesian Linear Task Models (GLiBRL)** — Conjugate Bayesian inference with learnable nonlinear bases; strongest aggregate zero-shot on MuJoCo and MetaWorld. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20974)
