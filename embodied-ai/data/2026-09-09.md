# arXiv Robotics & AI Papers — Focused Digest

## 1. Dexterous Manipulation & Robot Hands

**[Benchmarking Dexterity of Multifingered Robot Hands: A Review and Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05585)** — Reviews benchmarking approaches for multifingered hand dexterity, introduces a three-level framework for evaluation (component, system, task levels), proposes new benchmarks/metrics focused on fine in-hand manipulation, from the NSF HAND ERC.

**[KaRMA: A Kinematic Metric for Fine Manipulation Ability in Robotic Hands](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15548)** — Introduces a kinematic-only metric quantifying reachable in-hand translation/reorientation within a two-finger pinch through rolling motions. Evaluated on 16 hands; separates hands ranked identically by static proxies.

**[BiDexGrasp: Coordinated Bimanual Dexterous Grasps across Object Geometries and Sizes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06589)** — Proposes a large-scale bimanual dexterous grasp dataset (9.53M grasps on 6351 objects) plus a learning framework with bimanual coordination and geometry-size-adaptive grasp generation.

**[Play2Perfect: What Matters in Dexterous Play Pretraining for Precise Assembly?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26428)** — RL framework for task-agnostic play pretraining before fine-tuning on precise assembly. 33× more sample-efficient than from-scratch RL; 60% success on 0.5mm-clearance insertions with sim-to-real transfer.

## 2. Tactile Sensing & Visuo-Tactile Policies

**[DeCAL: Towards Physically-Grounded Dexterous Vision-Language-Action Models via Contact-Aware Latent Co-Imagination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09119)** — Dexterous VLA with Mixture-of-Transformers, adaptive visuo-tactile gating, and co-imagination of visual+tactile dynamics. 71% average success, strong generalization.

**[TacVLA: Contact-Aware Tactile Fusion for Robust Vision-Language-Action Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12665)** — Fine-tuned VLA with contact-aware gating that activates tactile tokens only during contact. +20pp in disassembly, +60pp in in-box picking over baselines; 2.1× improvement under occlusion.

**[BIFTA: Brain-Inspired Few-Shot Tactile Adaptation for Unknown Sensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08673)** — Adapts frozen tactile encoders to unseen sensors from small labeled sets via dual-view statistical memory + spectral graphs + uncertainty-gated propagation. Raises Sparsh accuracy from 6.86% to 87.09% with only 10% target data.

**[WM-Craftnet: World Synesthesia Model for Generalizable Dexterous In-Hand Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07002)** — World-model-conditioned RL using multimodal reconstruction (proprioception, depth, tactile). Clean-depth denoising target enables robust in-hand rotation across 49 objects and sim-to-real transfer.

**[TacClip: a clip-on sensor measures dynamic contact forces without covering the fingerpads](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08214)** — FBG-based fingernail clip measuring contact force/vibration (<0.5N error over 0–8N range) while leaving fingerpads exposed—compatible with vision-based hand tracking.

**[ContactWorld: What Representations Matter in Vision-Tactile World Models for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13877)** — Systematic study across 12 tasks finding point-cloud observations + tactile force fields achieve best planning (36.1% vs 20.7% with RGB), stressing spatial structure/temporal continuity/cross-modal compatibility.

## 3. Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models & Manipulation Policies

**[LayerRoute: Action-Conditioned Mixture-of-Layers Routing for Vision-Language-Action Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06079)** — Adaptive VLM layer-mixture routing per action layer; up to +7.2 on LIBERO Long with <4% added parameters.

**[SimpleMemVLA: A Simple but Effective Native-Video Memory for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05533)** — No dedicated memory module—passes full timestamped video history to pretrained backbone. State-of-the-art on 4 memory benchmarks; outperforms retrieval/compression/recurrent mechanisms.

**[DLS: Failure-Boundary Learning for Robust Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06114)** — Views VLA adaptation as discovering/localizing/shaping the failure boundary via real-grounded priors, digital-twin rollouts, semantic progress localization, and directional boundary shaping. Outperforms SFT/online RL on robustness.

**[Proxy Policy Steering (PPS)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09148)** — Inference-time adaptation: trains two lightweight proxy policies whose calibrated velocity-space difference steers a frozen base policy (π0.5). +53pp absolute success on 8 real tasks with zero base modification.

**[GE-Act 2.0: Pretraining and Scaling a World-Action Model for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05588)** — World-action model with separate visual planning + inverse dynamics pretraining. Scaling co-training 300h→30k hours: 17.1%→44.1% success on G1-OP; cross-embodiment transfer gains.

**[VLA-Corrector: Stage-Aware Observable State Understanding for Prompt-Based Recovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06508)** — Closed-loop correction of frozen VLAs using learned verifier (progress + risk) over visual/proprioceptive/action history. Stage-conditional recovery prompting without parameter updates.

**[MemCorr-DP: Counterfactual Correspondence Conditioning for a Diffusion Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06615)** — Uses RoMa v2 matches as explicit 3D correspondences between scene and reference trajectory; 96.67% success under combined position+camera shifts vs 88% for transformer baseline.

**[CASD: Chunk-Aligned Semantic Distillation for Multi-Stage Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08638)** — Derives semantic targets for entire action chunks (including transitions) from VLM-segmented demonstrations. 98.9% on LIBERO vs 98.0% reference; gains on RoboTwin and MolmoSpaces.

**[ICI-VLA: In-Context Imitation with Spatiotemporally Aligned Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07581)** — Few-shot test-time adaptation via retrieved aligned micro-demonstrations without gradient updates. 97.7% LIBERO, 60.4% RoboTwin (best baseline 41.1%), 83.2% on real tasks.

**[GloVLA: Let Geometry Move and Local VLA Interact for Robust Object-Centric Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06256)** — Hyrid: geometric transport for end-effector traversal + local VLA for contact-rich interaction. Retains 88.5% on LIBERO-Challenge where full VLA drops to 20.9%.

**[A4A: Cross-Embodiment Transfer of Action-Oriented 4D Affordances](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05892)** — Language-conditioned future trajectories of interaction-relevant 3D points for cross-embodiment pretraining of VLAs.

**[RefGuard: Identity-Aware Language-Guided Manipulation via Joint Target-Anchor-Frame Grounding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06221)** — Joint posterior over target/anchor/frame with scene graph; executes/clarifies/reobserves/aborts. 0 identity switches on ambiguity-stress trials vs 33–46% for baselines.

**[TANGO: Humanoid Navigation with Whole-Body Vision-Language-Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09158)** — 29-DoF joint prediction for cluttered traversal; zero-shot deployment on real Unitree G1 without real training data.

**[CC-VLA (CR-VLA-Force): Control-aware Compliance VLA for Contact-rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05832)** — Force-aware VLA with MoE encoding + VLA-guided adaptive compliance controller for reactive force-position tracking; succeeds under partial OOD pose shifts.

## 4. World Models & Planning for Manipulation

**[OpenWAM: Open Modular World-Action Model Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07398)** — Factorized WAM design space; 6400h egocentric+robot data; consistent SOTA from simulation to real (single-arm, bimanual, dexterous hands).

**[SG-WAM: Self-Guided World Modeling in Geometry-Aware Policy Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01397)** — World-action model learning geometry-aware dynamics within policy-derived representation space. 98.5% LIBERO, 73% LIBERO-Plus at only 0.9B.

**[Ostrich: Large-Stride Differentiable Contact Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08800)** — Non-smooth Newton contact resolution at h~0.1s timesteps, differentiated via implicit function theorem. Holds sim-to-real accuracy at 50× larger timesteps; 211× faster gradients than MJX; 8192 parallel worlds on one GPU.

**[WorldAgen: Unified State-Action Prediction with Test-Time World Model Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08162)** — Shared backbone for world/agent heads with test-time training; surpasses SOTA on CALVIN/LIBERO with TTT on few samples.

**[FM global WAM benchmark — ARC-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05461)** — Reveals frozen JEPA world models rank candidate actions incorrectly; closed-loop replanning masks broken action ranking (manipulation + maze domains).

## 5. Sim-to-Real & Teleoperation for Manipulation

**[Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Contact-Rich Assembly via Proprioception-Anchored Cross-Modal Pretraining (PACE)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07534)** — Visual/F-T encoders supervised to predict proprioceptive transitions. 93.3% real success, only 2.7pp sim-to-real drop, no real-world fine-tuning.

**[Dex-X: Learning Visual-Tactile Dexterous Manipulation From Human Videos with Simulated Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07747)** — Simulation as tactile completion engine for human-video demonstrations. Zero-shot sim-to-real on dexterous hand-arm: 93% cube picking, 53% table cleaning, generalization to unseen geometry.

**[C2Dex: Contact-Consistent Reconstruction and Retargeting for Dexterous Manipulation from Monocular Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07045)** — Stable object contacts recovered across frames guide reconstruction + retargeting. 57.78% success on DexYCB vs 17.78% strongest baseline.

**[WHIRL: Learning from What a Human Would Avoid (Intervention-Aware RL for Dexterous Manipulation)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06009)** — Human-in-the-loop RL using intervention predictions as forward risk signal. 96.7% LEAP Hand grasping success with 84% fewer operator interventions.

**[M3-Tele: Unified Multimodal Teleoperation for Compliant Whole-Body Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07859)** — Teleop framework capturing vision/tactile/force/proprioception jointly. Reduces force error from 4.132N to 0.346N; validates tactile+force value in Diffusion Policy learning.

**[SPOT: Spatial Perception-Oriented Long-Horizon Humanoid Teleoperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07933)** — VR teleop with wide-field fisheye stereo, viewpoint-decoupled free-looking, visual stabilization for long-horizon loco-manipulation data collection.

**[AURORA: Active Uncertainty-Driven Re-Orientation for In-Hand Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08493)** — Closes loop between object-centric reconstruction uncertainty and in-hand rotation for active NBV; robust under hand occlusion.
