# Robotics & AI Papers Summary (Focused on Embodied Intelligence & Manipulation)

## Tactile Sensing & Haptic Perception

**[The Potential of Haptic Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28664)** — Discusses the path to Haptic Foundation Models (HFMs), transitioning from passive LLMs/VLMs to active models across four dimensions: action coupling, physical dynamics representation, time-series granularity, and action-conditioned prediction. Benchmarks UniTouch, AnyTouch, T3, and Sparsh on TacBench.

**[N₀-Foundation: Towards the Age of Tactile Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29601)** — Large-scale tactile-enabled manipulation paradigm: 30,000+ hours of synchronized visuo-tactile demonstrations, a tactile UMI, and NeoForce (a visuo-tactile representation model). Policies benefit from physical contact state, not sensor appearance.

**[SpectraTac: A Compact Camera-Free Optical Tactile Sensor with Distributed Color Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30368)** — Compact (19.2mm x 4mm), sub-$5 tactile sensor using RGB illumination with 3 distributed color sensors. Achieves 3D force MAEs of 0.161/0.164/0.429 N (x/y/z) and 99.9% contact-region classification.

**[Cross-Modal Visuo-Tactile Representation Learning with Action Chunking Transformers for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00514)** — CLIP-style visuo-tactile contrastive pretraining integrated into ACT policies. Improves task completion from 30% (vision-only) to 54% with contrastive pretraining on contact-rich tasks.

**[Anomaly Detection on Small Industrial Components via Vision-Based Tactile Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30506)** — Evaluates unsupervised AD methods (SPADE, PaDiM, FAPM, InReaCh) on a GelSight Mini dataset covering 5 industrial components. Analyzes minimum nominal contacts (Good Fraction), cross-position generalization, and resolution trade-offs.

**[Toward Trustworthy Robot-Assisted Sliding Palpation for Shallow Vessel Localisation with a Calibrated Digital Twin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29396)** — Sim-to-real tactile palpation with calibrated digital twin; spatio-temporal GNN for vessel classification. 0.50mm marker alignment error and 1.05-5.49mm vessel localization error across domain shifts.

**[GhostTac: Manipulating Tactile Sensors without Physical Contact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20817)** — Security: First contactless attack on tactile sensors via EMI, causing DC offsets that induce harmful robot behaviors. Evaluated on 10 sensor modules and 2 dexterous hands.

## Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models & Manipulation Policies

**[SMILE: Smooth Motion for Improved Long-Horizon VLA Execution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29432)** — Architecture-preserving B-spline action representation for VLAs. SMILE-Evo1 reaches 98.0% on LIBERO; reduces non-boundary acceleration by 78.6% at long horizons. Improvements across SmolVLA, Evo1, VPP, DAWN.

**[AdaVLA: Adaptive Step Flow Matching for Training-free Acceleration of Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29208)** — Training-free acceleration using trajectory-curvature confidence metric. Achieves 1.87x/2.24x speedups for π₀.₅ and X-VLA on LIBERO with negligible success-rate degradation.

**[DriftingVLA: Native One-Step Vision-Language-Action Generation via Per-Dimension Temporal Drifting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29749)** — One-step VLA via distribution-drifting objective with Per-Dimension Temporal Drifting (PDTD). 98.32% on LIBERO, 3.36x action-generation speedup.

**[Temporal Forcing: 4D Representation Alignment for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30643)** — Aligns VLA latent representations with a pretrained 4D foundation model that captures evolving 3D world geometry. 98.8% on LIBERO (+2.2), full-task success from 20% to 43.3% on physical hidden-placement task.

**[PHR-VLA: Planning Horizon Reasoning for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27609)** — Auxiliary future head aligns VLA internal representations with latent dynamics from future observations. Wrist-camera patch-level supervision improves LIBERO from 84.1% to 88.4% and real-world disassembly from 63.3% to 82.5%.

**[SCALE: Self-uncertainty Conditioned Adaptive Looking and Execution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04208)** — Single-pass inference strategy modulating both perception (visual perturbation) and action decoding based on self-uncertainty, inspired by active inference. No extra training or verifier needed.

**[CorrectVLA: Training-Free Action Correction for VLA Model Failures via Language Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29967)** — Translates task-level natural-language corrections into additive action adjustments without weight changes. Recovers execution misalignment failures; identifies correctable failure subset vs. fundamental comprehension breakdowns.

**[IntentVLA: Short-Horizon Intent Modeling for Aliased Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14712)** — History-conditioned VLA encoding recent observations into compact short-horizon intent representations to reduce inter-chunk conflict. Introduces AliasBench (12-task ambiguity benchmark).

**[Perturbation-Based Epistemic Uncertainty for Failure Detection in VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20754)** — Training-free failure detection via low-rank weight perturbations; estimates epistemic uncertainty from disagreement across perturbed predictions. Best AUROC on LIBERO-PRO.

**[DREAM: Deployment-Time Demonstration Generation via Real-to-Sim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29078)** — Generates fine-tuning data from captured workspace + language instruction using LLM translation and TAMP, without task-specific human demonstrations.

**[CometVLA: Co-Training on an Embodied Data Pyramid Towards Physical Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30289)** — Embodied physical VQA with Global Action Prior tokens; co-trains across teleoperation, simulation, egocentric video, and VQA layers, improving real manipulation success.

## World Models & Action Models

**[Motus2: A Self-Evolving General World Model for Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30237)** — Unified world-action model with three interfaces (policy, simulator, evaluator) in a closed decision-and-learning loop. Uses large-scale egocentric stereo data plus robot-domain adaptation on a biomimetic platform.

**[Flex-π: A Multi-Stream World-Action Model with Compute Flexibility](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10860)** — 6B WAM supervising 3D geometry and DINO semantics in shared latent space. 2-7x stronger than baselines on dexterous bimanual tasks while running faster than π₀.₅.

**[DELE-w0.5: Inferring Action from Future Latent State for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22067)** — Skips video generation, predicting from latent future state. Best among baselines at 62.5% full-task success across 640 real-robot trials, outperforming strongest baseline by 32.5 points.

**[TrAct: Bridging Robot Control and Visual Prediction with Visual Tracks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24101)** — Uses visual tracks as embodiment-agnostic interface between control and prediction. Improves LIBERO-INTEGRAL from 27% to 55% and real-world from 49% to 76%.

**[PredVLA: Predictive Sensorimotor Modeling for Sub-Million-Parameter Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26673)** — 0.68M-parameter predictive-coding policy; hierarchical recurrent dynamics predict features/proprioception via prediction-error-driven inference. 86.9% on LIBERO short-horizon suites.

**[Hydra: Navigation World Action Model with Discrete Latent Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28995)** — Discrete latent planning with unified manifold over visual states, poses, and actions; flow-matching execution for smooth control. Tested on two physical robot platforms.

**[RoboPhys-3D: Embodied World Model Evaluation via 3D Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28718)** — 3D-grounded EWM benchmark on RoboTwin 2.0 with 50 tasks; 50 metrics across 4 levels (pixel → task completeness). RoboPhyscore correlates with human eval (Pearson r=0.976).

**[PAWBench: Probabilistically Aligned World Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27345)** — Evaluates video generators as stochastic samplers; introduces PAWEval for outcome-level distribution comparison. No current system matches reference probabilities across 50 scenarios.

## Dexterous Hands & Manipulation

**[Aero Hand Open: Simulation-Ready Tendon-Driven Hand](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28578)** — Open-source tendon-driven anthropomorphic hand with cable transmission simulation, identified actuation map (incl. thumb coupling), and RL training package. Zero-shot sim-to-real transfer.

**[A Kinematic Framework for Screening Candidate Pinch Configurations in Robotic Hand Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20692)** — Evaluates pinch capability from fingertip workspace interactions without object or contact models. Analyzes four kinematic hand structures during early design.

**[Blind Dexterity: Whole-Body Humanoid Manipulation via Pure Proprioception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29487)** — Unitree G1 policies using only joint encoders (no cameras/FT/tactile): push-resilient walking, ball trapping, suitcase lifting, skateboard mounting. Joint encoders under compliant contact act as a whole-body tactile channel.

## Teleoperation & Human-Robot Interaction

**[GHOST: Real-Time Exocentric Dual-Robot VR Teleoperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29080)** — Single-operator control of two mobile manipulators via onboard cameras; 1.6-4x success rate vs tablet for novices, 1.47x faster for experts. Mode-switching architecture.

**[TriPilot-FF: Coordinated Whole-Body Teleoperation with Force Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09888)** — Foot-operated pedal with lidar-driven haptic feedback for base control, coupled with bimanual leader-follower. Pedal feedback incorporated into ACT policies improves performance.

**[Teaching Robot Policies to Humans Using Erroneous Examples](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29023)** — Uses erroneous demonstrations to teach robot policies; improves retention. IRL-like learners perform best on policy prediction.

## Sim-to-Real & Domain Adaptation

**[Task-Relevant Feature-Dynamics Fidelity Enables Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Transfer for Robotic Ultrasound Scanning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29516)** — Introduces TR-FDF metric; simulator with shared intermediate domain achieves 390/400 zero-shot deployments. Contraction analysis shows sensitivity of TR-FDF mismatch affects closed-loop margins.

**[Learning Agile Perceptive Traversal of Sparse 3D Structures for Humanoids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29769)** — Monkey-bar brachiation via RL with attention-based lidar encoding. Phase-scheduled teacher-student pipeline; 14/15 hardware trials, speeds to 0.5 m/s. Sim-to-real with modeled noise, voltage sag, thermal limits.

**[FetchMan: Learning Visual Humanoid Loco-Manipulation from Simulated Experiences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17027)** — RL refinement of cloned policies with Flow-GRPO on sparse rewards. Single-object reach-and-pick at 73.3% on real Unitree G1 across unseen scenes.

## Dexterous Manipulation Imitation & Learning

**[Blind Dexterity (recap above)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29487)**
**[AffordTrajDP: Dynamic Affordance-Guided Visuomotor Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01603)** — Propagates anchor affordances through object SE(3) pose for temporally consistent guidance. 70.0% avg on ManiSkill3 (+17.8% over baselines); validated on Galaxea A1 and UR7e.

**[One Demonstration Is Enough for Real-World Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01651)** — AutoSERL uses a single demo with sliding-window intervention, safety recovery, and automatic intervention termination. Matches HIL-SERL on contact-intensive tasks, outperforms SERL-20 demos.

**[Learning to Infer and Manipulate Through Distributed Whole-Arm Interaction in a Soft Robot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30773)** — RL with memory-based policy on hybrid rigid-soft arm with embedded IMUs (only proprioception). Learns blind whole-arm grasping: exploration, object localization, property inference, and wrapping.

## Benchmarks, Frameworks & Infrastructure

**[Behavior-Skill: Fine-Grained Benchmark for VLA Long-Horizon Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30536)** — 235K skill instances from 50 household tasks; exposes contact-rich manipulation as persistent bottlenecks. Trajectory-level and skill-level metrics.

**[Agri-Sim: Agricultural Simulation Platform for Greenhouse Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29100)** — Unity + ROS2 platform with mobile dual-arm harvesting robot, virtual RGB-D/LiDAR/IMU sensors, and closed-loop navigation/manipulation evaluation.

**[SOLO: Stable Omni-Terrain Long-Horizon Perceptive Humanoid Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26583)** — Fourier-encoded query reconstruction + trajectory-aware MSE distillation. 97.5% stress-test traversal; 1.5km outdoor route zero-shot.

## Additional Relevant Systems

**[RoboGesture: Real-Time Semantic-aligned Co-Speech Gestures for Humanoid Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28693)** — Semantic-acoustic alignment from raw audio + anti-inertia CFG masking + MPC safety filter for Unitree G1.

**[Zeva: In-Context Causal Learning for Generalizable Embodied Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30880)** — Causal Interaction Extractor encodes action-state changes into retrievable memory; policy improves during deployment without gradient updates.

**[AnyWorld: Factorized Egocentric World Models for Cross-Embodiment Generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29242)** — Factorizes human interactions into action/camera/embodiment for recomposition into robot-native rollouts; improves RoboCasa and real IRON humanoid manipulation.

**[SUN: Persistent Programs for Language-Grounded Control-to-Learning Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31167)** — Typed executables compiling into MPC costs, RL rewards, and transition guards. 82.03% macro-success on 9 tasks; trains DP3 policies to 46% sim success.
