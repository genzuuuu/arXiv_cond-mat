# Today's arXiv: Embodied Intelligence & Robotic Manipulation Highlights

## 🖐️ Dexterous Manipulation & Robotic Hands

**DexPolicy** introduces scheduled exploration for trajectory-guided dexterous manipulation, annealing action noise from broad to narrow during RL. Boosts real-robot Target success from 25% to 85% on a RealMan arm with an Inspire/RH56 hand. [DexPolicy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00360)

**DITTO-X** presents a hand-agnostic teleoperation interface that renders joint-level force and fingertip contact from existing hand sensing, enabling reverse teleoperation so operators enter the loop already matched to the dexterous state they inherit. [DITTO-X](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00781)

**FlashDexRetarget** accelerates physics-based dexterous motion retargeting with an RL framework, reaching 90% success across 50 motions at up to 100× less training compute than RL baselines. [FlashDexRetarget](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01849)

**DEX-X** learns visual-tactile dexterous manipulation from human videos by using simulation as a "tactile completion engine", achieving 93% real-world success on cube picking. [Dex-X](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07747)

**DexHoldem** benchmarks dexterous manipulation through Texas Hold'em primitives with a ShadowHand — 1,470 demos, with π0.5 reaching 61.2% task completion. [DexHoldem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18727)

## 🤏 Grasping & Tactile Sensing

**Continual Learning for 6-DoF Grasp Synthesis** adapts grasp scoring in a learned embedding space via memory rather than finetuning, reaching >90% success on challenging categories after just 50 online attempts. [Continual 6-DoF Grasping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01301)

**TacDyn-WAM** learns implicit tactile dynamics in a heterogeneous visuo-tactile world action model, avoiding unreliable pixel-level tactile reconstruction under contact drift — 81.5% on UniVTAC. [TacDyn-WAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00638)

**TacGooseBumps** encodes shear mechanically via a passive domed film, improving contact-rich insertion success by up to 36 points without extra electronics. [TacGB](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34006)

**Deformable In-Hand Slip-Aware Tactile Sensor** integrates velocity, force/torque, and pressure-map estimation into a single compliant pad for in-hand manipulation. [Slip-Aware Tactile Sensor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11952)

**TouchTherm** builds multimodal digital twins of objects for coupled tactile and thermal rendering from real objects. [TouchTherm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01943)

## 🧠 Vision-Language-Action & Visuomotor Policies

**CRAFT** (Same Scene, Different Task) targets vision shortcuts in VLAs via counterfactual instruction pairs, improving compositional generalization on undemonstrated skill combinations. [CRAFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00524)

**DriftOPD** offers teacher-free, rollout-free sequence-level distillation for one-step VLA policies via reverse-KL decomposition into chunk-level and future-potential terms. [DriftOPD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00317)

**K-MF (Kinematic MeanFlow)** enables one-step action generation for robotic foundation models, cutting action-head latency of GR00T-N1.6 by 67–74%. [Kinematic MeanFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00864)

**eRLT** improves VLA RL sample efficiency through action-relevant token routing across tokens and layers, up to +23.7% AUC; 108.9% and 46.7% gains on real connector-insertion tasks. [eRLT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00913)

**TOAST** applies stochastic action tokenization for autoregressive VLAs, gaining 6.8 points on LIBERO at 1/16 data and 15.8 on real robots. [TOAST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00899)

**Direct Action-Head Injection** of a grounded 3D point into the action head lifts GR00T-N1.6 from 31.2 to 77.5 on LIBERO-PRO under task perturbation. [Action-Head Injection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27663)

**DynamicVLA** tackles latency-induced perception-execution mismatch for dynamic object manipulation with latent-aware action streaming. [DynamicVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22153)

Also see **Screw Attention** (rigid-body equivariant transformer achieving 97.3% on LIBERO-Spatial with 16K params) [Screw Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00904), and **λ₀ / HumanVerse-500** (egocentric whole-body humanoid VLA pretraining) [HumanVerse-500](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00438).
