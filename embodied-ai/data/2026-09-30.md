### Dexterous Manipulation & Robotic Hands

Research on dexterous hands and in-hand manipulation is prominent, with a strong focus on learning complex tasks and transferring human skills.

- **FINGR** presents a policy for solving a Rubik's Cube with a single dexterous hand, using future-supervised interaction and geometric representations to achieve a 99% turn success rate. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33973)
- **HACo** introduces a haptic active compliance policy that learns force-regulating actions from combined fingertip tactile and joint-torque feedback, achieving an 83% success rate on force-sensitive tasks. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36596)
- **Unified Visual-Tactile-Action Modeling (UVTA)** leverages scalable human tactile demonstrations to improve robot policies, achieving a 70% average success rate on contact-rich tasks by mapping both embodiments to aligned tactile and action representations. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34182)
- **TaRL (Tactile Reward Learning)** learns general and physical rewards from tactile demonstrations, improving sample efficiency and success rates for contact-rich manipulation tasks like Nut threading (34% to 56%) and cube pickup (37% to 97%). [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36785)
- A novel 17-DoF anthropomorphic hand with **bidirectional antagonistic tendon routing** is presented, allowing for grasping on both the palmar and dorsal sides, validated by performing all 33 GRASP taxonomy grasp types. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36241)
- **ContactExplorer** is a general exploration method for dexterous manipulation that encourages the discovery of novel contact patterns, improving sample efficiency across five different hand embodiments. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10971)
- **ACPPO** extends PPO with a chunked actor and a stepwise feedback corrector, improving performance on 25 simulated robotics tasks, including dexterous hand-object interaction. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36250)

### Visuomotor Policies & VLA Models

A large number of papers focus on Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, addressing challenges in efficiency, long-horizon tasks, and adaptation.

- **RoboActualizer** proposes a "tiny actualizer" model that operates on a frozen world model to predict actions, achieving strong performance on simulation benchmarks with up to 100x fewer trainable parameters than existing models. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36413)
- **Staircase Policy (S-WAM)** introduces a streaming inference framework for world-action models that partitions large action chunks into sub-chunks, achieving a 3.62x throughput increase and reducing time-to-first-action. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36471)
- **V-JEPA Policy** demonstrates that a world-action model can be built on the latent space of a frozen V-JEPA 2.1 encoder, achieving competitive performance with far fewer trainable parameters. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37250)
- **SimpleARM** introduces a training-free memory layer for frozen generalist robot policies, achieving 67.17% mean success on the memory-dependent RoboMME benchmark. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36595)
- **T²Mem** learns a test-time memory for robotics by encoding observation history into compact fast weights, improving average success on 16 RoboMME tasks from 17.93% to 56.83%. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36720)
- **LIBERO-MAX** is a new benchmark that reveals current VLA policies suffer an 11.0-25.7 percentage point drop in success when the world changes mid-task (e.g., a target moves). [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36518)
- **Spotter** proposes a framework where an embodied model leads execution and a VLM monitors in parallel, intervening only to reflect on and correct failures, improving success rates on RoboCasa and RoboTwin 2.0. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36808)
- **CoRe-VLA** is a plug-and-play framework that improves VLA robustness under camera shifts by rendering observations from the VLA's training viewpoint, boosting PI0.5's success rate from 13.3% to 83.3% at a 1.6m shift. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37150)
- **Rho** presents a family of open-weights VLA models designed for data-light adaptation across multiple dual-arm robot platforms. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38164)
- **FP2** is a lightweight interface that equips task-adapted robotic foundation models with explicit force control, improving performance on contact-rich manipulation tasks. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37433)

### Simulation, World Models & Sim-to-Real

These papers explore using world models for planning and control, as well as improving the sim-to-real transfer of policies.

- **WorldLine** presents an action-driven visual simulator that learns manipulation dynamics from over 10,000 hours of action-free video, enabling policy evaluation and planning. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38059)
- **PRISM** is a real-to-sim-to-real framework that uses video-to-video generation to create "counterfactual" human-object interaction videos, scaling the training data for a humanoid loco-manipulation policy. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38172)
- **F4R** is a failure-driven real-to-sim-to-real closed-loop learning framework that converts real-world failures into targeted policy improvements, achieving 90% OOD success on four manipulation tasks. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35575)
- **World4Scorer** builds a trajectory-conditioned JEPA-style predictor for autonomous driving, using simulator outcome labels to supervise the scoring of both executed and unexecuted plans. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36438)
- **RynnWorld-Teleop** introduces "digital teleoperation," where a generative world model replaces a real robot for data collection, enabling zero-shot Sim2Real transfer for bimanual tasks. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06558)
- **AnisoWM** introduces an anisotropic representation for JEPA world models that improves planning performance by better aligning the latent space geometry with the task cost. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37441)
- **Dual-WM** proposes a dual-latent world model that separates local execution and long-range planning, improving success rates on long-horizon visual control tasks. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37644)

### Human-to-Robot Skill Transfer & Teleoperation

Several papers focus on learning from human data, including videos and demonstrations, to train robot policies.

- **EgoHumanoid-V2** presents a framework for direct human-to-humanoid transfer of coordinated whole-body loco-manipulation skills from egocentric human demonstrations. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37181)
- **EgoAlign** is a data-construction framework that converts egocentric human demonstrations into action and state supervision for a general-purpose humanoid controller, enabling zero-shot deployment on a physical robot. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38046)
- **VidAct** learns object-centric, 3D-aware manipulation policies from a single monocular in-the-wild video per task, enabling zero-shot real-world deployment. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36870)
- **SAKI** (Skill Assembly and Kinematic Imitation) learns reusable skills from human videos and composes them for long-horizon mobile manipulation tasks. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36031)
- **OTRetarget** jointly retargets robot and multi-object motion from human demonstrations using optimal transport, achieving a high interaction Jaccard score on the OMOMO dataset. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36602)
- **DROM** is a language-guided diffusion framework that uses Dynamic Movement Primitives (DMPs) to augment a small set of expert demonstrations for learning multi-skill manipulation. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37348)
- **Track-and-Complete (TRACC)** learns humanoid skills by imitating the useful portion of a failed human video and then completing the task based on the inferred outcome. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36924)

### Tactile Sensing & Force Control

New developments in tactile sensors and force-aware policies aim to improve the reliability of contact-rich tasks.

- A new **single-sensing-element tactile sensor** is presented that can concurrently detect pressure and tackiness by using a Hall effect sensor and a soft magnetic composite. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36558)
- **Wrench-ACT** proposes an imitation learning policy that predicts wrenches as its sole action output for a pure force controller, matching or outperforming position-based baselines on five contact-rich tasks. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37552)
- **TacForcing** is a streaming action-generation framework that incorporates execution-time tactile feedback, achieving average success rates of 65% in simulation and 66% in the real world on contact-rich tasks. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25798)
- A compact **disk-shaped magnetoelastic torque sensor** for robotic joints is developed, achieving 1.34% accuracy relative to its 50 Nm full scale. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33542)

### Benchmarks & Datasets

New benchmarks are introduced to systematically evaluate agent performance on long-horizon, interactive, and safety-critical tasks.

- **FineART** is a densely annotated bimanual manipulation dataset with 40,543 episodes and 533,913 subtasks. A VLA model mid-trained on it shows significant gains, including zero-shot generalization to new hardware. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36416)
- **RoboChrono** is a new real-robot benchmark for streaming task understanding, revealing that models with strong visual matching abilities do not consistently exhibit strong temporal ordering. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36605)
- **RoboMME** is used as a benchmark for memory-dependent manipulation tasks, with new methods like **SimpleARM** and **T²Mem** showing significant improvements over baselines. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36595)
- **RoboDojo** is a benchmark with instructions that leave the goal unstated, used to evaluate methods that learn from demonstrations, such as **ENCORE**. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37359)
- **ArticuRiddle** is a new dataset of objects whose appearance implies the wrong articulation, used to test a method for learning hidden kinematics. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36553)

These papers cover a range of topics from novel control methods to foundational models for specific robotic applications.

- **KPI** presents a "promptable kernel" that allows a humanoid's whole-body tracker to adapt its interaction behavior (e.g., track, comply, or hold force) based on instructions from a vision-language agent. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36151)
- **EquivDP3** proposes a SIM(3)-invariant point-cloud encoder for humanoid loco-manipulation, demonstrating dramatic improvements in data efficiency in the low-data regime. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36575)
- **Learning to Explore Hidden Kinematics** presents a method for actively discovering the kinematic parameters of articulated objects by training a policy to maximize information gain from interactions. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36553)
- **AquaMend** explores strategies for embodied agents to recover from failures caused by latent-belief mismatches, comparing re-probing, rollback, and supported continuation. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28973)
- **Manifold-Constrained MPPI (MC-MPPI)** is a real-time sampling-based control framework for nonlinear equality-constrained systems, achieving a 95% success rate on a 14-DoF closed-chain dual-arm system. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24813)
- **CrossBFM** distills a shared latent behavior space across different humanoid embodiments, enabling zero-shot transfer of behaviors like motion tracking and goal reaching. [Paper Title](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38087)
