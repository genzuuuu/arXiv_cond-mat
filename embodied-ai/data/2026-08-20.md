# Daily Robotics & AI Papers Summary

## Dexterous Manipulation & Robotic Hands

**[LabDex: A Hierarchical Benchmark for Dexterous Manipulation in Laboratories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18618)**  
Introduces a large-scale benchmark for dexterous manipulation in chemistry labs, unifying real-world and simulation platforms under a common framework. Organized hierarchically into atomic skills, compositional tasks, and long-horizon workflows—the first benchmark to jointly capture dexterous hand use, real lab interactions, and multi-stage procedures.

**[ADEPT: Accelerating Dexterity via Pre-Training and Post-Training using Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19182)**  
A large-scale RL framework for learning sim-to-real transferable dexterity across high-DoF hands (23-DoF Kuka-Allegro and 29-DoF Flexiv-Sharpa) directly from visuo-tactile perception. Introduces a stable post-training recipe combining behavior-cloning distillation, critic warm-up, and conservative updates; zero-shots sim-to-real transfer at human-level speed.

**[PartialBiGrasp: Inferring Hidden Local Geometry for Bimanual Grasping from Partial Views](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19188)**  
Dual-arm grasp generation from partial point clouds using convolutional occupancy networks to reason about graspability, collision-free contact regions, and object thickness implicitly. Evaluated with force-closure metrics, large-scale simulation, and real-world tests on novel objects.

## Tactile Sensing & Sim-to-Real

**[Tactile Sim2Real without Tactile Simulation via Bottlenecked Latent Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15897)**  
Framework that avoids sensor-specific simulation entirely by training on an oracle sensor (point-cloud + fingertip forces) and aligning real sensor latents to oracle embeddings at inference. Achieves 85–97.5% zero-shot success on peg insertion and gear meshing with GelSight Mini and DIGIT, outperforming physics-based simulation baselines.

**[Zero-Shot Transfer of Force Map Estimation Across GelSight Mini Sensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18240)**  
Domain adaptation method generalizing 3D force map estimation across different GelSight Mini units using UniT-based image reconstruction followed by U-Net force estimation. Achieves SSIM of 0.9338 in reconstruction and MAE of 1.13N in force estimation.

**[SoftVTBench: A Deformation-Aware Visuo-Tactile Dataset and Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18701)**  
Visuo-tactile dataset with 4,000 demonstrations of deformable-object manipulation, synchronizing multi-view RGB, dual-finger tactile markers, flexion, language, and FEM ground-truth states. Introduces Deformation-aware Success Rate (DSR) showing successful policies can still violate deformation tolerances.

## Visuomotor Policies & Imitation Learning

**[Revisiting Open-Loop Execution in Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15938)**  
Key finding: long open-loop execution primarily helps short-context policies imitate non-Markovian demonstrations. With sufficiently long context, closed-loop reactive policies perform best. Challenges prevailing explanations about compounding errors motivating long action chunks.

**[Unified Condition-Action Modeling for Accurate One-Step Action Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16153)**  
UCA-Flow unifies conditions and actions in a shared token space with a Unified Condition-Action Transformer, achieving 9.3% higher success rate than baselines while running 45.6x faster than DP3.

**[Counterfactual Behavior Cloning: Offline Imitation Learning from Imperfect Human Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10760)**  
Generalizes behavior cloning by expanding the dataset with counterfactual actions near demonstrated behaviors, recovering cleaner policies from noisy human demonstrations. Includes theoretical guarantees on extracting simple, similar-to-demonstration policies.

## Teleoperation & Learning from Human Data

**[WARP: Whole-Body Retargeting for Learning from Offline Human Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29940)**  
First framework achieving zero-shot whole-body mobile manipulation from offline human demonstrations using a closed-form Shoulder-Elbow-Wrist solver for consistent whole-body actions. Eliminates need for human-in-the-loop teleoperation data.

**[RoboEdit: Turning Human Manipulation Videos into Scalable Robot Experience](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18948)**  
Video editing suite converting human manipulation videos into action-consistent robot videos with aligned 3D hand states. Introduces RoboEdit-14M dataset (174K pairs, 14M frames) spanning seven robot embodiments with downstream control policy support.

## Whole-Body Control & Humanoids

**[GigaBrain-WBC-0.5: A Behavior World Model for Robust Whole-Body Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18234)**  
First Behavior World Model for humanoid whole-body control—a causal Transformer predicting joint action, next state, and latent command distribution. Achieves 81.3% terrain-interaction success (4.3x baseline), 83.1% under implausible commands, and 99.3% fall recovery. Hardware-tested on Unitree G1 and Maker L01.

**[Real-Time Control-Constrained DDP for Underactuated Balancing of Legged Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18552)**  
Accelerated Projected Gradient-based control-constrained DDP for real-time MPC on legged robots. First demonstration of static two-leg standing of a quadruped using finite-horizon MPC.

## Manipulation & Grasping Tasks

**[HarvestPoint-ACT: Explicit Target Selection for Robotic Fruit Harvesting under Occlusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18446)**  
Imitation learning with explicit harvest-point prediction and target selection ranking by occlusion/travel distance. Achieves 88% success on canopy mock-ups, 75% under heavy occlusion using an 8-D state encoding of target information.

**[GAPL: Grounded Action-effect Policy Learning for LLM-Based Trajectory Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18254)**  
Integrates LLM effect estimation, simulation grounding, and PPO policy learning for autonomous driving trajectory planning, reducing collision rate, ADE, and FDE by 0.76/0.86/2.00 respectively.

## Robot Foundation Models & Embodiment

**[The Embodiment Gap in Robot Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18433)**  
Survey introducing the "embodiment gap"—work required between reusable models and execution on specific robot bodies. Proposes a two-axis map of shared structure and adaptation stages, plus a reporting framework for cross-embodiment learning comparisons.

**[EATR-Stereo: Embodiment-Aware Token Routing of Paired Stereo Evidence for Humanoid VLA Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17453)**  
Selectively routes auxiliary stereo-view tokens to complement primary-view VLA processing on a 33-DoF humanoid. Achieves 60% full-task success, 100% grasp success; improves recovery under severe asymmetric occlusion from 30% to 80%.

## Skills & Policy Learning

**[SkillGate: Training In-Policy Skill Selection in Long-Horizon Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18852)**  
Identifies "selector credit starvation"—a structural failure where correct skill-selection decisions receive vanishing or wrong-signed credit. Partitions token support into disjoint credit channels, lifting a 9B policy from 40.8% to 53.2% trial success.

**[SkillNet: Create, Evaluate, and Connect AI Skills](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04448)**  
Open infrastructure with 600,000+ skills in a unified ontology, reporting 40% higher average rewards and 30% fewer execution steps across ALFWorld/WebShop/ScienceWorld benchmarks.

## Visuo-Tactile & Multi-Modal Learning

**[Mask2Real-WM: Segmentation Masks as a Sim-to-Real Bridge for Dexterous World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04546)**  
Two-stage world model decoupling dynamics (segmentation mask prediction) from rendering (photorealistic RGB via ControlNet-augmented Stable Video Diffusion). Pre-trained on 50h synthetic + 2.5h real data for 23-DoF manipulation.
