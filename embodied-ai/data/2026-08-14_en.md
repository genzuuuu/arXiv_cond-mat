# Today's arXiv Papers: Robotics and AI Feeds

## Dexterous Manipulation & Policy Learning

**[RoboSynChallenge: Mastering Real-World Dexterity via Generalizing Synthesized Manipulation Skills](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12416)**
Introduces a unified benchmark combining large-scale synthetic data generation with real-world robot evaluation for generalizable manipulation. Provides baseline implementations across Transformer-, Diffusion-, VLA-, and World-Action-Model-based policies.

**[NestDex: Nested Policy Learning with Copilot Assisted Teleoperation for Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13362)**
Presents a nested policy framework where operators control the arm and regulate learned hand skills via a single-DoF clutch, reducing demonstration burden. A vision-language selector activates task-appropriate skills, and the outer visuomotor policy handles deployment.

**[FACT-R 2: Learning External Force Sensing for Commodity Robot Arms Improves Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12406)**
Neural External Torque Estimation (NEXT) estimates external joint torques without dedicated force sensors, training in 1 minute from 10 minutes of free-motion data. Force-Informed Re-Sampling Training (FIRST) upsamples pre-contact/contact segments during BC, improving task progress by over 17% across five long-horizon tasks.

**[Deliberate Practice: Learning Robot Skills under a Budget](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13415)**
Active skill learning algorithm that computes budget-optimal allocations via a bilinear program, deciding which skills to practice to maximize cumulative reward within time constraints. Validated on long-horizon manipulation tasks in simulation and real world.

**[Attention from Action, for Action: Emergent Visual Bottlenecks for Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13422)**
Seeker learns progression-aware regions of interest (ROIs) from action supervision alone using frozen DINOv3 features, providing spatial interfaces for RGB cropping and point-cloud filtering. Raises real-world success from 48.3% to 76.7% in-domain and 20.0% to 60.0% under domain shifts.

## Contact-Rich Manipulation & Tactile/Force Sensing

**[ContactGuard: Pre-Contact Execution Monitoring with Action-Conditioned Latent World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13438)**
Pre-contact execution monitor for chunked visuomotor policies. Predicts short-horizon consequences in latent visual space from planned action chunks and aborts before errors compound, trained from unlabelled trajectories with a small labelled failure probe.

**[Contact-Rich Bimanual Dexterous Grasping of Large Objects from Single-View Observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10383)**
Real-world bimanual grasping framework combining a multimodal dataset, DDPM-based grasp generation from segmented point clouds, and motion planning with online grasp refinement. Achieves high success rates on unseen objects without requiring full 3D models.

## World Action Models & Video Prediction

**[DreamX-Phi 1.0: Action-Conditioned Video World Model for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13489)**
Action-conditioned video world model using PRoPE-style geometric encoding for per-arm SE(3) transformations, depth branch for geometry, and SAM3/V-JEPA for object consistency. First place on WorldArena 2.0 Track 1.

**[JEPA-WAM: Stage-Level Joint-Embedding Prediction for World-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10780)**
Augments a Motus-based WAM with Stage-JEPA, predicting stage-level latent targets alongside short-term physical futures. Achieves 90.25% success across 50 RoboTwin 2.0 tasks with fewer execution steps.

**[Flex-π: A Multi-Stream World-Action Model with Compute Flexibility](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10860)**
6B-parameter WAM supervised on 3D pointmaps and DINO semantics alongside RGB using shared VAE latents. Per-stream dropout enables a single checkpoint to run in fast action-only or full joint generation modes. Beats baselines by 2-7× on bimanual manipulation tasks.

**[Keep the Future, Drop the Rollout: RIFT for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11521)**
Shows that fixed future-cache values can be reused across models, then proposes RIFT using learned anticipation tokens to construct future K/V caches in one pass. Reduces action-chunk latency by 68-89% while maintaining ~98.8% success on LIBERO.

**[ForeSight Without Seeing: Latent Futures for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11605)**
ForeWAM uses Future-KV from a single Video DiT prefill over current latents and stochastic future slots, supervised by a frozen latent action teacher. Achieves 96.7-96.9% on LIBERO without explicit video generation at deployment.

## Teleoperation & Humanoid Control

**[BrainWAM: Action-Space Coordination of Semantic Priors and Predictive Dynamics for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12854)**
Structured action-space coordination converting semantic reasoning and predictive dynamics into two specialized pathways aligned at action-representation level. State-of-the-art on NAVSIM v1 (89.5 PDMS) and v2 (89.6 EPDMS).

**[Predictive Relative-Velocity Steering for Safe Robotic Manipulator Teleoperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13284)**
Lightweight modular collision avoidance operating at end-effector velocity-command level. Predicts collisions via time-to-collision with overshoot protection, then rotates relative velocity vectors using Rodrigues' formula to avoid deadlocks.

**[HumanTracker: Towards Comprehensive and Human-Aligned Motion Tracking Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13555)**
Benchmark with 153 hours of optical motion trajectories and HumanScore, a preference-aligned metric trained on 12K motion pairs that reveals contact/stability failures kinematic metrics miss.

## Spatial Reasoning & Navigation

**[FUSE: Active Functional Affordance Grounding through Adaptive Semantic-Geometric Evidence Acquisition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12683)**
Introduces Active Functional Affordance Grounding where agents explore scenes to identify objects by function rather than identity. Combines uncertainty-driven exploration with a learned amortized planner, achieving highest non-oracle grounding with 1.33× less computation.

**[SAP-Nav: Spatial Semantic Representation Meets Active Perception for Hierarchical Open-Vocabulary Object Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12707)**
Zero-shot hierarchical OVON framework with simultaneous spatial-semantic representation and active viewpoint verification. 12.2% SR improvement over training-based methods on region-level navigation.

**[AirForesight: Current-to-Future Spatial Map Imagination for UAV-VLN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12835)**
Current-to-future spatial map imagination framework learning structured representations supervised by reconstruction and trajectory prediction, with cross-space planning consistency. Strong results on OpenUAV and AerialVLN-S.

**[DreamFly: Causal Memory and Receding-Horizon Diffusion Planning for Aerial VLN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12308)**
Diffusion-based aerial VLN with causally-aligned historical memory, plan-K execute-one strategy, and explicit stop estimation. Outperforms all compared methods on OpenFly seen/unseen splits.

## Surgical & Medical Robotics

**[Capstan-driven Continuum Surgical Robot: Design, Modeling, and Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13396)**
Actuation-perception co-design enabling real-time cable tension measurement via compliant motor mount elements. Parallel multibody short-thick-beam model captures shear effects and multi-cable interactions for shape/force sensing.

**[S2-HWM: Sparse Event-Structured Hierarchical World Model for Long-Horizon Surgical Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13103)**
Learns sparse event evidence from latent trajectories to coordinate event-level manager and primitive-step worker. Achieves 98.7% success on SurRoL PegTransfer, beating flat GAS DreamerV3 by 22.7 points.

## Embodied AI & Safety Monitoring

**[Decoding Task Progress from VLA Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13474)**
Probes π₀.5's residual stream and finds task progress is linearly readable, present in the pretrained backbone before robot training. Used as a label-free OOD detector competitive with SOTA methods.

**[JailWAM: Jailbreaking World Action Models in Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05498)**
First jailbreak evaluation for WAMs, using Visual-Trajectory Mapping to unify action outputs, Risk Discriminator with severity levels, and Dual-Path Verification. Reports 84.2% attack success, showing WAMs are susceptible to unsafe physical behaviors.

## RL & Optimization for Manipulation

**[Temporal GRPO: Beyond Trajectory-Level Credit in Vision-Language-Action Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13026)**
Addresses trajectory-level credit aliasing in GRPO-based VLA post-training by constructing detectable task stages and applying stage-specific advantages. Improves success and sample efficiency on RoboTwin 2.0 and LIBERO-Long.

**[TMRL: Diffusion Timestep-Modulated Pretraining Enables Exploration for Efficient Policy Finetuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12236)**
Context-Smoothed Pretraining injects forward-diffusion noise creating a continuum between imitation and exploration. Timestep-Modulated RL trains agents to adjust this conditioning during fine-tuning, enabling real-world manipulation in under an hour.

**[Learning Loco-Manipulation From SMPC Demonstrations With Sparse Offline-to-Online RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12063)**
Uses SMPC as a rapidly tunable expert to generate offline datasets, then trains off-policy RL with sparse rewards. Policies surpass the original teacher and deploy zero-shot on arm-equipped Spot and G1 humanoid.

## Grasping & Affordance

**[Real-World Cooperative Bimanual Dexterous Grasp of Large Objects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10383)**
Framework for bimanual grasping of large objects from single-view observations using DDPM-based grasp generation and online refinement. Demonstrated on a dual-arm robot with high success rates on unseen objects.

## Skill Learning & Budget-Aware Methods

**[Deliberate Practice: Learning Robot Skills under a Budget](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13415)**
Bilinear program for budget-optimal skill practice allocation. Provably optimal under budget constraints and validated in simulation plus real-world long-horizon tasks.

## Human-Robot Interaction

**[EgoPHI: Estimating Contact and Force from Egocentric Vision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13014)**
First method jointly estimating dense contact maps and 3D force distributions on hand/object meshes from single RGB images. Physics-based simulation pipeline augments datasets with dense per-vertex force supervision.

**[RGB-D Video Generation for Improving Human-to-Robot Object Handover Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13028)**
Hand2Bot RGB-D dataset with PassGen generative pipeline using stable video diffusion, achieving robust zero-shot transfer and earlier intention anticipation via morphology-based depth editing.

## Omnidirectional & Aerial Manipulation

**[FAM-DQ: A Dual-Quadrotor-Based Fully Actuated Aerial Manipulator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13220)**
Dual-quadrotor fully actuated platform achieving decoupled 6-DoF control with gear-driven servo regulation. Maximum torque of 1.019 N·m at 0.447 kg (torque-to-mass ratio 2.28).

## VLA Models & Autonomous Driving

**[FIRE-VLA: Failure-Informed Self-Evolution for Vision-Language-Action Models in Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13395)**
Self-distillation from frozen round-start copies for unresolved low-reward groups. Reduces G=4 mean L2 from 1.848 to 1.500 m and persistent failure prevalence from 13.03% to 11.20%.

## Simulation & Benchmarks

**[H2R-Bench: Benchmarking Human-to-Robot Manipulation Video Generation in World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13049)**
Evaluates cross-embodiment human-to-robot video generation across five dimensions. Reveals current video world models fail at embodiment consistency, functional interaction, and task execution.

**[HumanoidVLN: Physics-Grounded Simulator and Benchmark**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12860)
Physics-grounded VLN benchmark across four humanoid embodiments with 933 reference episodes. JanusVLN achieves highest success rate at 43.55%, with strong sim-to-real correlation (r=0.935).

## Robot Skill Learning & Diversity

**[Trajectory First: A Curriculum for Discovering Diverse Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01568)**
Two-stage curriculum using spline-based trajectory priors for diverse high-reward behaviors, then distilling into reactive policies. Increases behavioral diversity while maintaining task performance.

## Visuomotor Policy Learning

**[SpatialVAM: Spatial-Aware Multi-View Video Diffusion as a Data-Efficient Robot Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03181)**
First 3D Video Action Model predicting spatial-aware heatmap videos and RGB simultaneously. With only 10 demonstrations, handles long-horizon contact-rich tasks with 22%↑ on Meta-World, 15%↑ on RoboCasa, 16%↑ real-world.

## G0.5: Unified Autoregressive VLA

**[G0.5: One Autoregressive Stream for Robot Reasoning and Action](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11739)**
Pretrained autoregressive VLA with single transformer decoder for reasoning + action tokens. Cross-embodiment tokenizer and native chain-of-thought stream. Surpasses π₀.5 (76.7% vs 53.3%) and GR00T-N1.7 on real-world fine-tuning, 98.9% on LIBERO.

## Semantic Radiance Fields

**[Semantic Radiance Fields as Simulators for Spatial Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13095)**
Uses Semantic Radiance Fields to jointly encode geometry, appearance, and semantic identity for training embodied agents. Demonstrates an orchard apple-reaching task where the field supplies rendering, ground-truth semantics, and occupancy queries.

## Continual Learning & Social Robotics

**[Mind the Context: Continual Learning of Socially Appropriate Robot Actions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13448)**
Explicit Disentanglement Dual-Branch (EDD) framework separating environmental and social-agent knowledge with replay-based rehearsal for domain-incremental learning of socially appropriate actions.

## Soft Robotics & Actuators

**[Manufacturing Complex Airtight Soft Pneumatic Actuators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13233)**
Evaluates fabrication routes for complex pneumatic actuators. FDM provides the most adaptable path with airtightness depending on extrusion-path architecture and support-free geometry.
