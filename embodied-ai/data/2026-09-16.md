### 🤖 Dexterous Manipulation & Robotic Hands

Several papers focus on improving the dexterity and adaptability of robotic hands, from learning from human video to developing new benchmarks for multi-hand comparison.

- **UniDex-ViTac** ([2609.16504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16504)): Presents a framework that learns a unified visuo-tactile dexterous manipulation policy from human video data, using simulation to generate robot-specific demonstrations.
- **ProxiDex** ([2609.16586](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16586)): Introduces a dynamics-guided proximity policy for dexterous manipulation. It uses proximity cues as a hardware-agnostic contact representation, improving robustness where vision is occluded and tactile sensors are unavailable.
- **ConGraspXL** ([2609.16319](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16319)): Extends the GraspXL work with controllable, constraint-conditioned grasp motion synthesis, allowing for flexible control over task-driven constraints like approach direction and contact regions.
- **Residual Fault Adaptation for Dexterous In-Hand Manipulation** ([2609.17404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17404)): Proposes a teacher-anchored framework to compensate for hidden joint faults during in-hand manipulation, enabling zero-shot deployment of an adaptation policy on a real robot.
- **Bench2Dex** ([2609.15726](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15726)): A new simulation benchmark for visuo-tactile bimanual manipulation across 12 different dexterous hands, providing a consistent setting to study learning algorithms despite hardware differences.
- **Fingers as Legs** ([2609.17172](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17172)): Demonstrates an anthropomorphic hand learning to use its fingers for self-supported locomotion (crawling, steering) in addition to manipulation, creating a compact mobile manipulator.

### 🧠 Visuomotor Policies & VLA Models

Research into vision-language-action (VLA) models continues to mature, with a focus on improving robustness, efficiency, and temporal awareness.

- **TEMPO** ([2609.16864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16864)): Addresses failures in dynamic manipulation by augmenting a pretrained VLA with temporal inputs: a motion summary to resolve ambiguity and a proprioceptive history to resolve state aliasing.
- **SAVLA** ([2609.16641](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16641)): Proposes a symmetry-aware VLA model that uses an equivariant flow-matching action head to improve data efficiency and robustness to novel object poses.
- **Rethinking Visual Embodiment Dependence in Visuomotor Policies** ([2609.16815](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16815)): Argues that visual cues of the robot's own body can become a shortcut for policies. The paper proposes "embodiment canonicalization" to structure this information and improve human-to-robot transfer.
- **Dense to MoE Adaptation for Compact VLA Policies** ([2609.16503](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16503)): Introduces AdaDE, a method to adapt dense feed-forward blocks into Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) layers, reducing the active parameter count of VLA models for deployment on resource-constrained platforms.
- **FiberTune** ([2606.08653](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08653)): A training-time objective to preserve visual residuals in VLA fine-tuning, preventing "residual visual collapse" and improving performance on simulation and real-world tasks.
- **vla-eval** ([2603.13966](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13966)): An open-source evaluation harness for VLA models designed to eliminate the per-benchmark integration cost, supporting 14 simulation benchmarks and six models.

### 🦾 Sim-to-Real, Teleoperation & Tactile Sensing

Bridging the gap between simulation and reality remains a key theme, often leveraging tactile sensing and new methods for data collection.

- **XRoboToolKit-T** ([2609.16437](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16437)): A teleoperation system for contact-rich manipulation that incorporates tactile-informed force control, using a haptic module to analyze force distribution and a VLA model to refine actions.
- **Proprioception-Anchored Cross-Modal Pretraining** ([2609.07534](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07534)): Introduces PACE, a method for zero-shot sim-to-real transfer in contact-rich assembly. It uses proprioception as a consistent anchor to supervise visual and force/torque representations, bridging the domain gap.
- **SlipSense** ([2609.15910](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15910)): A multimodal framework for low-latency slip detection using a custom sensor (TacV5) with a piezoresistive array and accelerometer, demonstrating generalization to new objects and robots.
- **Bi-MoDe** ([2609.16040](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16040)): A bilateral control-based imitation learning framework that allows an operator to specify execution modifiers like speed and contact intensity at inference time.
- **MR-GLi** ([2609.16041](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16041)): Investigates a Mixed Reality (MR) interface for underwater robot arm teleoperation, showing that spatially-registered visual feedback reduces the operator's perceived burden.

### 📚 Surveys & Benchmarks

New surveys and benchmarks aim to structure the rapidly growing field of embodied AI.

- **World-Action Models for Robot Learning and Control: A Survey** ([2609.16074](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16074)): A comprehensive survey on World-Action Models (WAMs), which couple future world prediction with executable action generation. It provides a unified taxonomy and discusses applications in manipulation, navigation, and autonomous driving.
- **The Robot Data Factory** ([2609.16705](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16705)): Proposes a mission-driven infrastructure for continuously generating and validating "robot experience," arguing that this is the key scientific resource for Physical AI, beyond just raw data.
- **No Free Checker: A Survey of Verifiers for Robot Policies** ([2609.09250](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09250)): Surveys roughly 150 verifiers for robot policies, comparing them on "availability" and "credibility." It highlights a fundamental trade-off: credibility tends to fall as availability rises.
- **AssemblyGrid v1** ([2609.16075](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16075)): A new benchmark for multi-robot production that includes temporary coalitions, local information, and geometric constraints, designed to evaluate both learning-based and traditional control methods.

### 🏗️ Manipulation Systems & Control

A variety of papers present novel systems and control methods for specific manipulation challenges.

- **Occupancy Network-Guided Autonomous Robotic Partial Nephrectomy** ([2609.16186](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16186)): Presents a vision-guided autonomous system for complete tumor resections on soft tissue, integrating conditional occupancy networks to track deforming anatomy.
- **Tendon-Driven Continuum Robot with In-Situ Self Pose Estimation** ([2609.16256](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16256)): Introduces a modular continuum robot with interchangeable joints and onboard magnetic sensors, enabling self-sensing without external tracking.
- **ManiSkillFormer** ([2609.16331](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16331)): A neuro-symbolic framework for demonstration-free compositional manipulation. It uses task-conditioned geometric contracts to structure the interface between perception and action, enabling zero-shot generalization.
- **IL-ACT** ([2609.16696](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16696)): A motion control framework for a 30-ton excavator that combines imitation learning with adaptive Cartesian tracking to improve precision and reliability.
- **ReWeight** ([2609.13851](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13851)): A framework for VLA post-training that incorporates egocentric human data by using optimal transport to retrieve relevant demonstrations and assign sample weights based on cross-embodiment similarity.

### 📝 Other Notable Papers

- **WholeBodyWAM** ([2609.16644](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16644)): Extends pre-trained world-action priors to humanoid loco-manipulation by grounding whole-body controller semantics.
- **Weave** ([2609.16683](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16683)): A unified framework for learning whole-body dexterous humanoid-object interaction from captured human demonstrations.
- **Collision-Aware Humanoid Whole-Body Control** ([2609.16405](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16405)): Proposes RECAL, a layer that wraps a blind whole-body controller to trade off target tracking against collision avoidance using external scene geometry.
- **The Latent That Never Was** ([2609.16745](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16745)): A forensic re-run of the CVAE ablation in Action Chunking Transformer (ACT), finding that the original paper's reported performance drop from removing the CVAE encoder does not reappear in their tests.
- **UniRank** ([2606.21847](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21847)): Formulates rank allocation for low-rank LLM compression as a global sorting-and-truncation pipeline, combining local singular energy with global functional importance.
- **Regen** ([2607.19450](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19450)): Proposes a method for expert-to-generalist distillation by recycling the replay memory from specialized RL training, significantly reducing the cost of post-training.
