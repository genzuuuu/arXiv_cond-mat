### Embodied Intelligence & Dexterous Manipulation

**1. InDex: Empowering VLA Models with Intent-Conditioned Arm-Hand Coordination for Dexterous Manipulation**
*   **Paper:** [InDex: Empowering VLA Models with Intent-Conditioned Arm-Hand Coordination for Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12109)
*   **Summary:** Introduces a framework for adapting parallel-gripper VLA models to dexterous hands. It separates the decision of *when* to contact (grasp intent) from *how* to grasp (hand trajectory). A first stage predicts end-effector and intent chunks, and a diffusion decoder generates multi-finger actions, improving coordination and task completion for arm-hand systems.

**2. Tri-Manual Visuomotor Imitation Learning of Robot Policies**
*   **Paper:** [Tri-Manual Visuomotor Imitation Learning of Robot Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25731)
*   **Summary:** Presents TriManPolicy for training tri-manual (three-arm) robot manipulation from a single human operator. It uses a Dependency-Aware Tri-Arm Scheduling (DATS) method to retime single-arm demonstrations from sequential to parallel execution, enabling a single synchronous policy for efficient multi-arm coordination.

**3. DR-LfD: Decompose and Reorganize Planning with Primitives and Visuomotor Policies Learned from Demonstrations**
*   **Paper:** [DR-LfD: Decompose and Reorganize Planning with Primitives and Visuomotor Policies Learned from Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25397)
*   **Summary:** Combines task and motion planning (TAMP) with imitation learning (IL) for long-horizon, dexterous tasks. It decomposes human demos into atomic skills (visuomotor policies or object-centric primitives) and reorganizes them via TAMP, reducing the need for exponential data while enabling generalization to unseen setups.

**4. TriManPolicy (mentioned above)**
*   **Note:** This paper is already covered in point 2. It is the tri-manual manipulation paper.

**5. FIRMGrasp: A Friction-Informed Risk Margin for Robust Grasp Synthesis**
*   **Paper:** [FIRMGrasp: A Friction-Informed Risk Margin for Robust Grasp Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25049)
*   **Summary:** Addresses the fragility of grasps under variable friction. Proposes a risk-aware friction metric based on Conditional Value-at-Risk (CVaR) to evaluate force-closure margins. This metric better predicts real-world grasp success, especially for grasps that a standard metric rates highly but that fail due to adverse friction conditions.

**6. S2A2: Audio-Visual Imitation Learning for Manipulation Tasks Using Acoustic Spatial Information**
*   **Paper:** [S2A2: Audio-Visual Imitation Learning for Manipulation Tasks Using Acoustic Spatial Information](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26047)
*   **Summary:** Introduces a framework for manipulation tasks requiring auditory cues (e.g., sound source localization and identification). It integrates visual features with spatial and spectral audio information for imitation learning, showing effectiveness in tasks where both position and timbre are important.

### Visuomotor Policies & Sim-to-Real

**7. HiFi-UMI: Learning Deployable Manipulation Policies from High-Fidelity UMI Data Alone**
*   **Paper:** [HiFi-UMI: Learning Deployable Manipulation Policies from High-Fidelity UMI Data Alone](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25895)
*   **Summary:** Aims to remove the need for real-robot post-training in robot-free data collection (UMI style). By increasing the fidelity of the data (via SLAM, ultra-wide FOV, microsecond sync), they show that policies can be deployed zero-shot on a real robot, matching or exceeding teleoperation baselines. Releases 2000 hours of high-fidelity demos.

**8. CoTinyVLA: Chain-of-Thought Distillation for a Sub-Billion-Parameter Vision-Language-Action Model**
*   **Paper:** [CoTinyVLA: Chain-of-Thought Distillation for a Sub-Billion-Parameter Vision-Language-Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25487)
*   **Summary:** Proposes a very small (0.9B) but robust VLA model. Achieves top performance on the LIBERO-Plus benchmark by using structured supervision: dual-view temporal input, hierarchical CoT distillation from a larger teacher, and paraphrase augmentation. It outperforms 7B baselines on challenging perturbation axes while requiring minimal memory.

**9. CycleVLA: Proactive Self-Correcting Vision-Language-Action Models via Subtask Backtracking and Minimum Bayes Risk Decoding**
*   **Paper:** [CycleVLA: Proactive Self-Correcting Vision-Language-Action Models via Subtask Backtracking and Minimum Bayes Risk Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02295)
*   **Summary:** Equips VLAs with proactive self-correction. It integrates a progress-aware VLA, a VLM-based failure predictor that triggers subtask backtracking, and a Minimum Bayes Risk (MBR) decoding strategy to improve retry success. Shows significant improvement on LIBERO and real-robot tasks, especially under injected perturbations.

**10. Context-Adaptive Policy Framework...**
*   **Paper:** [A context-adaptive policy framework for robust and reactive robotic manipulation via uncertainty-aware imitation learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24035)
*   **Summary:** Proposes a context-adaptive framework using Dynamical-System-based LfD. It combines a task-parameterized policy with uncertainty-aware policies via a Mixture of Experts to improve robustness to out-of-distribution scenarios, demonstrated on tasks like force-conditioned grasping and manipulating deformable objects.

**11. DC-WAM: Dynamic-Centric Visual Supervision and Reasoning for World-Action Models**
*   **Paper:** [DC-WAM: Dynamic-Centric Visual Supervision and Reasoning for World-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25918)
*   **Summary:** Rethinks the video prediction task in World-Action Models (WAMs) by shifting focus from pixel-perfect reconstruction to dynamic-centric features. It uses temporal-difference flow matching and a token-level attention bias (DynaRoute) to guide the model toward control-relevant visual changes, improving policy robustness under visual perturbations.

**12. SAM3D-Guided Object-Centric Representation Alignment for Vision-Language-Action Models**
*   **Paper:** [SAM3D-Guided Object-Centric Representation Alignment for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25912)
*   **Summary:** Improves VLA models (like π0) by endowing them with 3D understanding without requiring 3D data at test time. It uses a frozen SAM3D teacher to align the model's visual features with object-centric 3D representations during training, yielding improved long-horizon manipulation where different target objects are important.

**13. $\pi\mathbf{R}^2$: Reactive Real-time Flow Policies**
*   **Paper:** [$\pi\mathbf{R}^2$: Reactive Real-time Flow Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26055)
*   **Summary:** Addresses the non-reactivity and high latency of action-chunking flow policies. It splits conditioning into fast (proprioception) and slow (vision) channels and uses a latency-adaptive flow schedule that treats in-flight actions as inpainting. This enables closed-loop replanning at ~25Hz, leading to significantly higher success rates on real-world tasks.

**14. IDR Framework...**
*   **Paper:** [A Causality-aware Infer-diagnose-refine Framework for Test-time Modality Adaptation in VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25516)
*   **Summary:** Proposes a model-agnostic framework to refine VLA action predictions at test time. It infers actions under factual and counterfactual visual conditions to diagnose the causal importance of visual observations, then uses this importance to refine predictions. This improves performance across multiple VLA backbones without retraining.

### Tactile Sensing & Contact-Rich Manipulation

**15. BayesContact: Uncertain Pose Estimation via Visuo-Tactile Proposals and Simulation-based Inference**
*   **Paper:** [BayesContact: Uncertain Pose Estimation via Visuo-Tactile Proposals and Simulation-based Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16123)
*   **Summary:** Proposes a framework for peg-in-hole pose estimation using a particle filter that fuses depth and force/tactile evidence via simulation-based inference. It actively disambiguates poses by probing for information gain. Shows a 30% improvement in insertion success over vision-only baselines.

### Navigation & Motion Planning

**17. Reactive 3D Motion Planning for a Franka Arm via Star-World Workspace Reshaping**
*   **Paper:** [Reactive 3D Motion Planning for a Franka Arm via Star-World Workspace Reshaping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25138)
*   **Summary:** Investigates Star-World reshaping for reactive control of a Franka arm. It clusters intersecting inflated obstacles into star-shaped proxies to overcome the issue of closely spaced obstacles. While it resolves overlapping obstacles, it can over-aggressively merge others and close navigable corridors.

**18. Leveraging Semantic Maps for City-Scale Cross-View Localization**
*   **Paper:** [Leveraging Semantic Maps for City-Scale Cross-View Localization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25215)
*   **Summary:** Uses OpenStreetMap semantic data for city-scale localization from a robot's egocentric view. A VLM extracts landmarks and proposes correspondences to overhead map entities. A lightweight matcher is distilled to compute all correspondences efficiently, and a Bayes filter fuses this with a time series of pose estimates, generalizing across weather and lighting.

**19. Motion Generation With Environmental Constraints**
*   **Paper:** [Motion Generation With Environmental Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25053)
*   **Summary:** Advocates for "Environmental Constraint Exploitation" (ECE), where deliberate contact simplifies motion planning by reducing dimensionality and uncertainty. Integrating ECE into RRT-based planners improves robustness in complex environments.

**20. Stochastic Agent Navigation (SONG)**
*   **Paper:** [SONG: A Photorealistic 3D Gaussian Simulation Platform for Benchmarking Social Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25219)
*   **Summary:** A high-fidelity social navigation benchmark built on 3D Gaussian splatting. It features realistic environments, moving human avatars with semantically grounded trajectories, and a multi-dimensional metric suite. Evaluation reveals a safety deficit even before social performance becomes a concern.

### World Models & Planning

**21. VisualPatchWorld: Code World Models as Latent Structured Representations for Planning**
*   **Paper:** [VisualPatchWorld: Code World Models as Latent Structured Representations for Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25236)
*   **Summary:** Represents world dynamics as executable Python code. The model first explores an environment to select a qualitative dynamical form and then fits its parameters. The resulting programs are inspectable, can be rolled forward for planning, and approach ground-truth simulator performance on navigation and grasp-rich tasks.

**22. INTACT: Isomorphic Intent-to-Action Learning for Search-Free World Models**
*   **Paper:** [INTACT: Isomorphic Intent-to-Action Learning for Search-Free World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26056)
*   **Summary:** Proposes a Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) for learning an intent-to-action mapping. It learns a latent space where distances represent physical intent, allowing for search-free planning by directly computing the action from the latent coordinates. It achieves high success rates on LeWM tasks with zero search.

**23. Temporal-Distance JEPA: Plan-Aware Representation Learning...**
*   **Paper:** [Temporal-Distance JEPA: Plan-Aware Representation Learning for Latent World Model Predictive Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25337)
*   **Summary:** Improves JEPA planners by learning a directed temporal cost from reward-free offline logs. It uses same-trajectory step order as supervision to mine an explicit progress cost, which is then deployed as the planning objective, leading to improved success rates on tasks requiring temporal reasoning.

**24. P3: Probabilistic Policy Propagation for Stable VAE-Based Robot Learning**
*   **Paper:** [P3: Probabilistic Policy Propagation for Stable VAE-Based Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25541)
*   **Summary:** Identifies a mismatch between PPO optimization and stochastic VAE policies. Proposes P3, a distribution-aware framework using moment-based methods for stable and efficient learning, boosting data efficiency and convergence in tasks like humanoid parkour.

**25. Amortising Trajectory Optimisation for Residual MPC...**
*   **Paper:** [Amortising Trajectory Optimisation for Residual MPC via Implicit Contact Differentiation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24959)
*   **Summary:** Introduces an AD-assisted implicit derivative for contact-rich simulations (Mujoco MJX) using the Implicit Function Theorem. This new method avoids unrolling the solver, thereby saving significant memory. The paper also proposes optimizing a policy via "optimiser distillation" to guide a short-horizon MPC, improving long-horizon success.

### Robotics Systems & Simulators

**26. Transformer Transformer: A Unified Model for Motion-Conditioned Robot Co-design**
*   **Paper:** [Transformer Transformer: A Unified Model for Motion-Conditioned Robot Co-design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25798)
*   **Summary:** Generates robot designs (embodiments) that can track target trajectories. It trains a diffusion transformer on a unified tokenization of embodiments, states, and actions. It can zero-shot optimize designs for unseen rewards and trajectories, leading to fabrication of a better ALOHA arm.

**27. Nautilus: From One Prompt to Plug-and-Play Robot Learning**
*   **Paper:** [Nautilus: From One Prompt to Plug-and-Play Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11665)
*   **Summary:** An open-source harness that transforms a user prompt into a complete robot learning workflow (reproduction, evaluation, fine-tuning). It provides plug-and-play interfaces for policies, simulators, and real robots, drastically reducing the engineering burden of cross-family robot learning experimentation.

**28. RoboProcessBench: Benchmarking Process-Aware Understanding in Vision-Language Robotic Manipulation**
*   **Paper:** [RoboProcessBench: Benchmarking Process-Aware Understanding in Vision-Language Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13040)
*   **Summary:** A benchmark for evaluating VLM's fine-grained understanding of manipulation execution processes, not just final success. It decomposes "process understanding" into static monitoring and dynamic reasoning, revealing that current VLMs still lack robust process-aware understanding. It also provides a dataset for post-training to improve this skill.
