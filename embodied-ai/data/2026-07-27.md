### Dexterous Manipulation & Robotic Hands

**[A Monolithic Hand with Asymmetric Origami Bending and Dual-chamber Actuators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22320) A Monolithic Hand with Asymmetric Origami Bending and Dual-chamber Actuators**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22320)
Presents the OSOR hand, a soft robotic hand using asymmetric origami bending (AOB) patterns and dual-chamber actuators. It achieves bio-inspired finger-palm motions and adequate output forces (6.3 N finger, 16 N palm) within a monolithic, easily manufactured structure. The hand can perform pinching, various grasps, and power grasps.

**[Robot Learning to Communicate through Projected Visual Abstractions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22434) Robot Learning to Communicate through Projected Visual Abstractions**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22434)
A robotic system using a 21-DoF dexterous hand with soft skin to create dynamic shadow expressions. A learned differentiable model maps hand configurations to shadow appearance, enabling the robot to perform sign language, shadow puppetry, and animal motion imitation through gradient-based optimization.

### Multi-Finger Grasping & In-Hand Manipulation

**[Embodying Multi-Hand Manipulation Policies by Searching the Assignment and Null Spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22020) Embodying Multi-Hand Manipulation Policies by Searching the Assignment and Null Spaces**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22020)
Addresses the "execution gap" for multi-hand manipulation policies on physical multi-arm robots. Proposes a search-based framework that simultaneously solves the assignment of abstract hand trajectories to physical arms and uses Jacobian null spaces of redundant manipulators to avoid inter-arm collisions while tracking prescribed motions.

### Visuomotor Policies & Imitation Learning

**[GRACE: Gradient-Free Robot Action Generation via Combined Diffusion-MPPI Posterior Mean Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21661) GRACE: Gradient-Free Robot Action Generation via Combined Diffusion-MPPI Posterior Mean Estimation**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21661)
Guides a pretrained diffusion policy with Model Predictive Path Integral (MPPI) control using only forward cost evaluations, avoiding the need for differentiable guidance costs. This enables steering toward constraints like collision checks. Demonstrated on a real 7-DoF manipulator, successfully avoiding deployment-time obstacles.

**[Ordered Action Tokens for Visuomotor Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21670) Ordered Action Tokens for Visuomotor Policy Learning**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21670)
Introduces Ordered Action Tokenization (OAT), a learned action tokenizer for visuomotor policies. OAT maps action chunks into an ordered sequence of tokens, placing coarse control in early tokens and refining detail later. This enables anytime inference trade-offs and improves performance across multiple policy backbones and tasks.

**[One Hand Watches The Other: Dynamic Multi-Agent Cooperation for Sample-Efficient Bimanual Manipulation in Dynamic Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22119) One Hand Watches The Other: Dynamic Multi-Agent Cooperation for Sample-Efficient Bimanual Manipulation**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22119)
Proposes DynaMAC, a policy-agnostic framework for bimanual manipulation that treats the opposite arm as a dynamic task parameter, resolving the causal issue of dynamic environments. It outperforms leading baselines by over 35 percentage points while requiring 20x fewer samples and generalizes zero-shot from static demonstrations to dynamic environments.

**[Industrial Dexterity Benchmark: A Hardware-Software Benchmarking Platform for Industrial Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14021) Industrial Dexterity Benchmark: A Hardware-Software Benchmarking Platform for Industrial Dexterous Manipulation**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14021)
Introduces the Industrial Dexterity Benchmark (IDB) boards and a scalable imitation learning framework (AG-iDP3) for tasks like cable management. A multimodal diffusion policy (combining RGB, point clouds, and wrench data) achieves 78% success on a cable insertion task, significantly outperforming single-camera baselines.

**[GuidedAttention: Interpretable and Correctable Visual Attention for OOD-Robust Robot Manipulation via Imitation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21049) GuidedAttention: Interpretable and Correctable Visual Attention for OOD-Robust Robot Manipulation**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21049)
Proposes a visuomotor imitation learning framework where task-relevant attention keypoints are predicted from camera images and condition a diffusion-based action policy. Users can inspect and correct these attention keypoints once at initialization, improving performance under out-of-distribution conditions.

**[Addressing the Orchestration Gap in Generalist Robots via Physical Agency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21725) Addressing the Orchestration Gap in Generalist Robots via Physical Agency**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21725)
Introduces Pigey, a "physical agency orchestrator" that performs high-level planning, subgoal decomposition, and recovery for existing visuo-language-action (VLA) policies. It significantly advances state-of-the-art on LIBERO-PRO (12.8% -> 53.3%) without task-specific fine-tuning and lifts frozen policies from near-zero to over 90%.

### Tactile Sensing & Visuotactile Policies

**[Design and Human Evaluation of Tactile Withdrawal Reflexes for a Skin-Covered Robot Arm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22249) Design and Human Evaluation of Tactile Withdrawal Reflexes for a Skin-Covered Robot Arm**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22249)
Investigates artificial nociception for a robot arm with whole-body tactile sensing. Compares three bio-inspired withdrawal reflexes. A user study found that a simple, predictable "uniform reflex" was rated highest for anthropomorphism, safety, and naturalness, highlighting the importance of predictability over biological fidelity.

**[ViTacWorld: Scaling Visuo-Tactile World Models for Contact-Rich Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22530) ViTacWorld: Scaling Visuo-Tactile World Models for Contact-Rich Robot Manipulation**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22530)
Proposes the first action-conditioned visuo-tactile world model. It leverages public tactile datasets and simulation to scale training, predicting both visual observations and tactile feedback. It improves downstream tactile policies through data augmentation and enables action-conditioned policy evaluation.

### Sim-to-Real for Manipulation & World Models

**[Impedance Control of Ship-Borne Manipulators via Optimization-based Task-Space Inverse Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22030) Impedance Control of Ship-Borne Manipulators via Optimization-based Task-Space Inverse Dynamics**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22030)
A torque-level optimization-based control framework for ship-borne manipulators that integrates trajectory tracking with task-space impedance. It compensates for base motion using an error-state Kalman filter, reducing tracking error by 25.7% and enabling dynamic peg-in-hole insertion with 1mm clearance.

**[Plug, Play, and Comply: A Modular Framework for Online Variable Impedance with Arbitrarily Oriented Compliance Axes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22483) Plug, Play, and Comply: A Modular Framework for Online Variable Impedance with Arbitrarily Oriented Compliance Axes**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22483)
A robot-agnostic, plugin-based compliant-control framework for ROS. It allows online updating of principal compliance directions according to local task geometry, which is crucial for contact-rich manipulation where desired constraint directions vary.

**[Compliant Non-Prehensile Pushing Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25672) Compliant Non-Prehensile Pushing Manipulation**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25672)
Extends a pushing model to integrate with impedance-controlled robots. A model predictive control framework enables compliant pushing by modulating robot position/velocity to achieve desired object motion while an energy tank filter ensures passivity during human-robot interactions.

**[Agentic Real2Sim: Physics-based World Modeling with Vision-Language Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19190) Agentic Real2Sim: Physics-based World Modeling with Vision-Language Agents**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19190)
A framework using vision-language agents to automatically convert real-world recordings of object-robot interactions into simulatable digital twins. It recovers geometries, states, and physical parameters, spanning rigid and deformable object scenes, enabling downstream policy learning in simulation.

**[Robot-Factored World Models via Robot Rendering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22535) Robot-Factored World Models via Robot Rendering**
[Paper Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22535)
Proposes "robot-factored" world models that decouple action realization by rendering the robot's geometry directly into the model's input. This allows the model to learn how objects respond to visible robot motion, generalizing to unseen robot embodiments and even human demonstrations.
