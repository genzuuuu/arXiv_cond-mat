# arXiv Robotics & AI Digest — Embodied Intelligence & Manipulation

## Dexterous & Bimanual Manipulation

**[Real-World Cooperative Bimanual Dexterous Grasp of Large Objects from Single-View Observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10383)**
Li et al. propose a real-world bimanual grasping framework for large objects that includes a multimodal dataset (joint angles, vision, forces), a DDPM-based module generating joint-level grasp configurations from segmented point clouds, and an execution strategy with motion planning and online grasp refinement. Demonstrated on a dual-arm robot with high success on unseen objects.

**[Precise Top-Layer Fabric Segmentation for Fabric Destacking with Edge- and Shape-Aware Deep Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10648)**
Dong et al. present a segmentation architecture for top-layer fabric detection in stacked textiles, adding edge-aware and shape-aware branches to a classic encoder-decoder, with shape supervision from CAD reference masks. Outperforms established baselines on real-world fabric datasets.

## Visuomotor & World-Action Models

**[FACT: Failure-Aware Causal Training for World-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10232)**
Peng et al. introduce FACT, a causal World-Action Model that conditions future video prediction and task progress on executed actions. By training on failure rollouts as valid targets, the model learns both successful and failed action outcomes. Outperforms baselines on simulation and real bimanual manipulation, improving with incorporated failure data.

**[JEPA-WAM: Stage-Level Joint-Embedding Prediction for World-Action Models in Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10780)**
Liu et al. present JEPA-WAM, augmenting a Motus-based WAM with Stage-JEPA, a goal-conditioned JEPA predictor for stage-level semantic futures. On 50 RoboTwin 2.0 tasks, achieves 90.25% success and reduces execution steps by ~6% vs. strongest baseline.

**[Flex-π: A Multi-Stream World-Action Model with Compute Flexibility](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10860)**
Yan et al. show that a frozen video-generation VAE encodes 3D pointmaps nearly losslessly without pointmap-specific training. Flex-π — a 6B-parameter WAM — is supervised on RGB, 3D geometry, and DINO semantics with per-stream dropout for compute flexibility. Beats baselines by 2–7× on dexterous, precise, real-world bimanual tasks.

**[Surgical WAM: A World-Action Model for Data-Efficient Surgical Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11204)**
Bao et al. propose Surgical WAM, a unified generative model on Cosmos Policy for future endoscopic observation and executable surgical action chunks. Action-free video pretraining improves average success from 63.5% to 77.8% with the largest gains on contact-rich, bimanual tasks.

**[World Action Models in Real Time: An Empirical Study of Smooth Execution via Asynchronous Deployment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01880)**
Motubrain Team compare six synchronous/asynchronous deployment strategies for high-latency WAMs on a 10 Hz bimanual robot. Prefix-conditioned generation achieves the best balance of task performance, speed, and smoothness; alignment errors produce discontinuities not fixable by blending alone.

**[Deployment Is Not Destiny: Robot Recomposition in the Field with Unseen Software, Hardware, and Compute Payloads](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11063)**
Swanbeck et al. present a framework for runtime robot recomposition that integrates previously unseen modular software/hardware/compute payloads. Demonstrated in two disaster-response scenarios (radioactive source localization, thermal-guided search), reducing reconfiguration to minutes without developer intervention.

## Skill Learning & Action Representations

**[Lost in Reconstruction: Aligning Action Representations with Language in Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10484)**
Li et al. introduce SALT, a semantically aligned action tokenizer that augments VQ-VAE with an auxiliary objective for recovering episode instructions from quantized action latents. On BridgeV2/SimplerEnv, SALT reaches 71.9% average success vs. 42.7% for reconstruction-only tokenizers, showing action trajectories carry language-grounding information.

**[BooST: Bridging Semantics and Motions for Efficient Skill Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10600)**
Lee et al. propose a two-stage framework using a cross-modal VQ-VAE to capture both semantic intent and motion dynamics into a unified skill representation, then distilling into a lightweight policy. Demonstrates superior few-shot adaptation and cross-domain transfer on simulation and real robots.

**[TCAM for Autonomous Deformable Manipulation: The RMC2 Champion System for WBCD 2026 Track 4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10718)**
Shen et al. describe the champion T-shirt loading system built on the TCAM framework with a custom 3D-printed gripper, four-camera wrist-centric setup, UMI-style demonstrations, and multi-view VLA policy. Achieved ~23 seconds per attempt with 22/25 shirts meeting smoothness requirements.

## Tactile Sensing & Contact-Rich Manipulation

**[OmniVTA: Visuo-Tactile World Modeling for Contact-Rich Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19201)**
Zheng et al. present OmniViTac, a large-scale visuo-tactile-action dataset (21,000+ trajectories, 86 tasks) and OmniVTA, a world-model-based framework with self-supervised tactile encoder, two-stream visuo-tactile world model, contact-aware fusion policy, and 60Hz reflexive controller. Outperforms existing methods across six interaction categories.

**[Diffusion-Based Impedance Learning for Contact-Rich Manipulation Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19696)**
Geiger et al. present Diffusion-Based Impedance Learning, combining a Transformer diffusion model conditioned on external wrenches to reconstruct simulated Zero-Force Trajectories, with an energy-based estimator for online impedance adaptation. Achieves 100% success in multi-geometry peg-in-hole insertion on a KUKA LBR iiwa.

## Teleoperation & Human-Robot Interaction

**[Enabling Scalable Kinesthetic Teaching via Observer-based Hand-guiding with Active Support](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10847)**
Tuma et al. propose RHOAS, a hand-guiding scheme with model-based external torque estimation and active support without additional hardware. In a 16-participant user study on a KUKA LWR iiwa, they report significant reductions in physical effort and improved maneuverability for precise and agile tasks.

**[A Neural Network Based Teleoperation for Remote Controlled Vehicles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10367)**
Ding and Eskandarian present a unilateral teleoperation framework combining Wave Variable passivity for stochastic delays with adaptive RBFN compensation for vehicle uncertainties. Hardware-in-the-loop experiments on a 1/10th scale vehicle over 4G validate robustness under physical road uncertainties.

**[Tri-Manual Visuomotor Imitation Learning of Robot Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25731)**
Zhao et al. present TriManPolicy, a tri-manual imitation learning system with Dependency-Aware Tri-Arm Scheduling (DATS) that retimes demonstrations offline to remove interface-imposed delays. Policies trained on retimed data show more efficient coordination across six real-world tasks.

## Locomotion & Embodied Control

**[Whole-Body Planning for Humanoids Navigating Confined Spaces via Self-Collision Avoidance References](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10220)**
Gonzalez and Sentis propose a three-stage whole-body planning framework for humanoids in confined spaces, using differentiable collision avoidance with reachability constraints and residual RL for robust execution. Validated on Unitree G1 across confined testbeds with restricted confinement ratios.

**[Hip Energized Monopedal Hopping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10387)**
Rozen-Levy et al. present a stepping strategy for pitch-unlocked planar monopeds where pitch stabilization torque recruits energy to counteract damping losses. Hybrid averaging yields closed-form gait predictions; physical experiments on Penn Jerboa show stable locomotion from 1.02–1.77 m/s.

**[Toward the Cognitive–Physical Limits of Embodied Intelligence through a World-Model-Centric Autonomous Racing Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10618)**
Shan et al. demonstrate a world-model-centric racing agent that learns from near-limit successes/failures, achieving robust localization/perception at 256.3 km/h and 88.3% interaction success across simulated racing scenarios.

## Navigation & Mobile Manipulation

**[AECNav: Active Evidence Consolidation for Efficient Zero-Shot Open-Vocabulary Object Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10817)**
Liu et al. present a training-free pipeline with evidence-gated perception, cluster-level log-odds belief consolidation, and active evidence acquisition. Achieves SOTA success rates of 84.7%/57.3%/51.3% on HM3D-v2/HM3D-OVON/MP3D and 95% success across 40 physical quadruped trials.

**[Hierarchical Fast-Slow ReAct Agent for Zero-Shot Object-Goal Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09816)**
Lan et al. propose a hierarchical agent with coordinate-anchored memory and a deliberative layer reasoning over text-before-image. Reaches 68.75% SR on HM3D v1 val and 47.29% on MP3D val, the highest among compared zero-shot methods.

**[Embodied Multimodal Grounding for Open-Vocabulary Mobile Manipulation via Semantic 3D Gaussian Splatting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10756)**
Ou et al. present a framework integrating active multi-view Semantic-3DGS with diffusion VLA policies. Achieves 60% long-horizon success vs. 40% (PointVLA) and 28% (DexVLA), with 74% success in cluttered manipulation scenarios.

**[Learning in ImaginationLand: Omnidirectional Policies through 3D Generative Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06191)**
Ren and Johns show that 3D generative models can augment single-demonstration datasets to learn omnidirectional policies that generalize from starting states far from demonstrations (including opposite-side starts). Demonstrated on grasping, drawer opening, and trash bin placement.

**[X-NavDP: Generalizing Navigation Diffusion Policy to Novel Behavior and Embodiments with Group Q-score Reweighted Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28560)**
Yang et al. introduce GQRM, a diffusion RL post-training framework with self-bootstrapped exploration and group Q-score normalization. X-NavDP improves cross-embodiment visual navigation success from 61.20% to 84.28% in simulation and 10% to 65% on real-world hard cases.

## Sim-to-Real & Reinforcement Learning

**[SpeedTuning: Speeding Up Policy Execution with Lightweight Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09138)**
Yuan et al. introduce SpeedTuning, an RL framework that learns to predict optimal execution speed, achieving over 2.4× speed-up while preserving success rate across pouring, throwing, and picking tasks.

**[MPPI Planning with Gaussian-Based Human Cost Function for Social Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08323)**
Mundane proposes Predictive Gaussian Interaction Fields (PGIF), a spatiotemporal cost formulation with anisotropic Gaussian repulsive fields aligned with pedestrian motion. PGIF-MPPI achieves 0% collision rate at all density levels vs. up to 82% for vanilla MPPI.

**[A 26-Gram Tailless Butterfly-Inspired Flapping-Wing Robot with Onboard Attitude Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06811)**
Gu et al. present AirPulse, a 26g tailless flapping-wing robot with onboard sensing/computation/power, using Stroke Timing Asymmetry Rhythm (STAR) modulation for stable untethered pitch and directional control during climbing and turning.

**[Reconfiguration of pivoting cube ensembles under local sensing constraints using geometric deep learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.03140)**
Dobreva et al. demonstrate that local sensing suffices for global reconfiguration of pivoting cube modular robots in 2D, with near-optimal reconfiguration achieved using nearest-neighbor interactions and information passing. Grid symmetries reduce model size at minor training cost.

## Datasets & Benchmarks

**[XPolicyLab: A Unified Standard and Open Ecosystem for Robot Policy Evaluation and Deployment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09892)**
XPolicyLab Community present an ecosystem integrating 42 robot policies with common schemas and a client/server architecture. Reduces integration from over five hours to two hours (30 minutes with packaged skills), serving RoboTwin, RoboDojo, and real-robot evaluation from one interface.

**[TomaMMU: A Comprehensive Multimodal Understanding Benchmark for Tomato Leaf Diseases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08727)**
Truong et al. introduce TomaMMU, a tomato leaf disease dataset with 28,808 images and 213,119 VQA pairs, plus TomaBench covering recognition to expert diagnosis. Simple fine-tuning on TomaMMU improves MCQ accuracy to 96.09%.

**[EsaacSim: A Multimodal Event Camera Add-on for NVIDIA Isaac Sim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08522)**
Bugueno-Cordova et al. present EsaacSim, an event camera simulation add-on for Isaac Sim with synchronized RGB/APS/event/depth/IMU outputs via ROS2, supporting effective event rates from 240–960Hz with under 400MB additional GPU memory.
