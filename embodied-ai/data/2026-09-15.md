### 🧠 Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models & Generalist Policies

This area sees continued work on improving VLA robustness, safety, and efficiency, alongside a focus on better evaluation.

- **Safety & Robustness**: [ShieldVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13231) proposes a safety-aligned fine-tuning framework using Hamilton-Jacobi reachability to create a "safety critic" that gates policy optimization. A different angle is taken by [ActSafeGuard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11697), which integrates a differentiable, training-aligned safety layer into flow-matching policies to ensure hard action constraints are met. [Cross-View Action Consistency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06965) improves robustness to camera movements by regularizing the action-flow velocity field to be consistent across action-equivalent viewpoints.
- **Architecture & Efficiency**: [Attention-DP3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13318) enhances 3D diffusion policies by injecting object-level geometric cues via a "Tri-field Attentional Conditioning" mechanism, making it more robust in cluttered scenes. [EffVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13984) performs a controlled study on VLA action-head design, finding that initializing the action head with weights from the language backbone is the most significant lever for performance, often more so than decoder architecture. [Freeze, Share, Shrink](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.12101) shows that a shared, frozen action backbone can be effective for multiple tasks, suggesting that action backbones in VLAs are over-parameterized. [TIDAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14945) is a hierarchical framework that decouples slow semantic reasoning from high-frequency control, enabling better performance on dynamic tasks by interleaving single-step flow integration with execution.
- **Learning from Human Data**: [ReWeight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13851) addresses the challenge of post-training VLAs with egocentric human data by using optimal transport to retrieve relevant demonstrations and assign weights to samples, mitigating cross-embodiment discrepancies. [Perceptual Reality Transformer (PRT)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.09852) introduces an evidence-linked workflow to help models understand and communicate unusual perceptual experiences without changing their meaning.
- **Explainability & Evaluation**: [IMPACT-VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15005) proposes a method for attributing the success of a VLA policy to specific phases and modalities by using counterfactual re-execution. [STAGE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13458) introduces a benchmark to diagnose the "semantic-action gap," where a model can identify the correct object but fails to act on it, and proposes a lightweight interface to improve action exposure. [Colosseum V2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27759) is a new large-scale simulation benchmark for systematically evaluating VLA generalization across 28 tasks and distribution shifts. [vla-eval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13966) is an open-source evaluation harness designed to make cross-benchmark VLA evaluation more practical by decoupling model inference from benchmark execution.

### 🖐️ Dexterous Manipulation & In-Hand Reorientation

Research continues to push the boundaries of what's possible with multi-fingered hands, focusing on generalization, real-world learning, and combining RL with model-based methods.

- **In-Hand Reorientation**: [POISE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13761) is a sim-to-real RL framework for in-hand 6D object pose reaching, using diverse grasp initialization and an adaptive curriculum to achieve robust performance on hardware. [Primitive-Informed Sampling-Based MPC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14868) biases sampling in MPC with low-dimensional manipulation primitives, enabling reliable continuous in-hand rotation that is difficult to achieve with direct joint-space exploration.
- **Real-World RL**: [Real-World RL with MPC Scaffolding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14878) uses sampling-based MPC to provide structured guidance and a replay buffer for an off-policy RL agent, enabling a 16-DoF Allegro hand to learn continuous in-hand rotation in just 7 minutes of online training with minimal human intervention. [GIFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14173) presents a pipeline for transferring human grasp-and-hold skills to a robot hand by using a wearable sensing glove and estimating robot fingertip force from actuator-current residuals, avoiding the need for tactile sensors.
- **Sim-to-Real & Benchmarks**: [PACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07534) uses a proprioception-anchored cross-modal encoder to learn robust visual and force/torque representations for zero-shot sim-to-real transfer in contact-rich assembly tasks. [Bench2Dex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15726) introduces a simulation benchmark for visuo-tactile bimanual manipulation across 12 different dexterous hands, providing a common platform for algorithm development.
- **Learning from Video**: [Generating Video Plans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10050) proposes a method to ground generated hand-object interaction videos in a simulator to train a multi-object HOI tracker, achieving diverse grasps in the real world.

### 🛠️ Robotic Manipulation & Assembly

Papers in this section cover a range of manipulation tasks, from assembly to deformable object handling.

- **Assembly & Insertion**: [Constraint-Grounded RL (CG-RL)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13516) uses variable impedance control for contact-rich insertion, adapting gain online based on force limits to eliminate manual retuning. [Vision-Force Admittance Learning (VFAL)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14133) fuses asynchronous vision with high-frequency force feedback to enable peg insertion into a moving hole. [RodForesight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12103) is a world-model-enhanced diffusion policy for inserting high-aspect-ratio rods that can bend during manipulation. [Learning from Edits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25130) introduces a dataset of 53.6K in-IDE developer edits of AI-generated code, providing insights into how and when developers modify AI output.
- **Learning Frameworks**: [Installer-in-the-Loop RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13234) presents a framework for industrialized construction that learns from sparse installer interventions and teleoperated demonstrations to achieve high-precision robotic assembly. [REVOLVE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14633) is an automated closed-loop framework for evolving robot manipulation, integrating data collection, failure recovery, and continual learning to reduce human effort. [MoPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12081) aligns subsystem-specific perception with action for coordinated mobile manipulation on a mobile base.
- **Representation & Physics**: [Learning Manipulation-Sufficient Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13235) proposes an outcome bottleneck to learn representations optimized for action outcomes, rather than fidelity, improving grasp success. [Physical Kernel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13244) explores "dark manipulation," where a policy completes contact-rich skills from a single visual encoding, without further pixel-level input. [PG-VLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10164) enhances reasoning VLMs for physical puzzle tasks by explicitly modeling object properties like friction and mass to improve planning.

### 🦾 Robot Learning, Control & Sim-to-Real

This category includes foundational work on learning algorithms, control frameworks, and simulation tools.

- **Reinforcement Learning**: [GzDRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13243) is a new, single-process RL framework for Gazebo that enables deterministic, high-throughput, and reproducible experiments, validated by deploying a learned policy directly onto a physical quadrotor without fine-tuning. [SGWIB](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13966) introduces a structure-aware information bottleneck for video highlight detection, preserving temporal relationships between segments. [Sampling headroom is not selection gain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13257) provides a critical audit of test-time scaling for video world models, showing that additional sampling often fails to produce better final outputs. [Policy and World Modeling Co-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02388) co-trains a policy and a world model on the same RL rollouts, using the policy's own experience to supervise its understanding of environmental dynamics.
- **Sim-to-Real & Simulation**: [RealSimLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09828) is a differentiable framework for online real-to-sim adaptation of deformable objects, using vision feedback to refine physical parameters like material stiffness in quasi-real-time. [Habitat-GS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12626) is a navigation simulator that integrates 3D Gaussian Splatting for photorealistic rendering and introduces drivable gaussian avatars for human-aware navigation.
- **Control & Planning**: [IMM-based Multiple Object Tracking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13307) improves radar-based object tracking by integrating a learned neural network as a motion model within a classical Interacting Multiple Model (IMM) filter. [Koopman-Based Robust MPC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02079) develops a Koopman-based stochastic MPC framework for nonlinear systems with intermittent measurements. [Ergodic Control and Controlled Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13295) is a comprehensive survey and tutorial on using controlled diffusion for robot learning, connecting it to ergodic control.
- **Hardware & Actuation**: [Breaking speed scaling in quadrupedal robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13290) shows that inter-limb inertial coupling, inspired by Huygens' coupled pendulums, can reduce peak joint torque and allow a quadruped robot to achieve speeds over 10 m/s without brute-force actuator scaling. [Extending the Speed Limit of Quadrupedal Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13289) achieves record-breaking quadrupedal speeds by refining actuator modeling in simulation to close the sim-to-real gap.

### 👁️ Tactile Sensing & Visuotactile Learning

A strong focus on making tactile sensing more practical, robust, and integrated with vision.

- **Tactile Representation & Sensors**: [Visible Touch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14156) proposes rendering contact signals in the same spatial frame as the visual scene, allowing any image-conditioned policy to use tactile information without architectural changes. [Language-Guided Representation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14783) uses language to distill sensor-robust tactile representations, learning a shared semantic space that is invariant across different tactile sensor hardware.
- **Tactile for Manipulation**: [SlipSense](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15910) presents a multimodal tactile framework for low-latency slip detection, using a piezoresistive array and an accelerometer to achieve robust cross-platform generalization. [PredTac](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15198) explores using *predicted* touch from visual observations as an alternative to measured tactile input, showing it can significantly improve the success rate of contact-rich manipulation policies. [Touch2Trace](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15921) demonstrates a tactile-driven imitation learning system for dexterous cable tracing, achieving zero-shot transfer to unseen cables and routing conditions.

[DRTP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13191) is a hybrid TCN-Transformer model for early prediction of satellite collision probability.

### 🗣️ Language, Reasoning & Agents

A large number of papers focus on language models, reasoning, and agentic frameworks, with many relevant to embodied AI.

[GeomVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13812) unifies scene, motion, and action in a 3D coordinate frame for VLA models. [CAB](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14811) is a benchmark for evaluating models on crypto-asset accounting tasks.
