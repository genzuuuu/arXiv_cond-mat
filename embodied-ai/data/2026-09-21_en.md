## 1. Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Policies

Strong day for VLA work — touching on smoothing, adaptation, safety, and continual learning.

- **[Fewer Steps, Better Actions: Rethinking Flow-Matching Inference for VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21216)** — Replaces extra integration steps with a single learned endpoint correction; 30% latency cut with higher success.
- **[Catch Me If You Can: Real-Time Feedback Denoising for Responsive VLAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21022)** — Two-timescale architecture keeping the final denoising step as a feedback interface; big gains on dynamic tasks.
- **[FOCAL-VLA: Subtask-Guided Geometry Distillation and Implicit World Modeling for VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21228)** — Focuses geometric learning on the current subtask to avoid redundant scene supervision.
- **[FAN: Foresight Action Normalization for Continual Adaptation of VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21358)** — Useful systematic study of action normalization; frozen statistics beat drift-prone protocols.
- **[VLA-Scope: Shift-Aware Failure Prediction for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21246)** — Predicts failure during OOD rollouts using execution history, not just OOD detection.
- **[SafeStage: Evaluating Safety Before, During, and After Vision-Language-Conditioned Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21223)** — Lifecycle-structured safety benchmark; shows task success often co-occurs with violations.
- **[CommitFlow: Semantic Commitment Verification and Local Correction for Long-Horizon VLA Execution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21908)** — Monitors whether a stage's physical preconditions are met before advancing; +22.7 over π0.5.
- **[GALA: Geometry-Aware Latent Action Modeling for VLA Pretraining across Embodiments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21948)** — Adds 3D end-effector geometry to latent actions; strong RoboCasa-GR1 results.
- **[Skel-WAM: A Hand-Skeleton-Conditioned World Action Model for Human-to-Robot Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21514)** and **[SkelWAM: Skeleton-Guided World-Action Model for Zero-Shot Cross-Embodiment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21983)** — Two related skeleton-interface approaches for human videos and cross-embodiment transfer.
- **[SynthDemo-RL: Breaking the Zero-Reward Barrier in VLA Adaptation with LLM-Guided Synthetic Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21650)** — Teacher-student pipeline rescues all 27 zero-reward LIBERO-PRO tasks.
- **[AtomEgo: Ego-Robot Integration for Embodied Foundation Model Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21461)** — 2,659-hour corpus study; "scale × alignment quality → capability gain."
- **[HuRo: Robotizing Human Videos for Scalable VLA Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10706)** — ~630K robotized episodes; pretraining lifts OOD completion from 34.9% to 72.2%.
- **[MicroHookACT: Monocular Microscopic Vision Guided Visuomotor Policy for Flexible Microelectrode Hooking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21365)** — Micron-level threading via attention over defocus cues; 96.7% success.
- **[Dense to MoE Adaptation for Compact VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16503)** — Dense→MoE conversion deactivates 40% of LLM params with modest loss.
- **[SkipVLA: Skipping VLA Steps with Classical Planning for Fast Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20648)** — Planner handles free-space motion, VLA only for contact-rich skills; up to 2.5× faster.
- **[CARF: Contrastive Attraction-Repulsion of Failure-Guided Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21982)** — Imitates progressive segments, repels from failure-critical ones.
- **[From Pretraining to Proficiency: Real-World Subtask RL for Long-Horizon Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21788)** — Targeted subtask RL; 32%→61% and 50%→95% on bimanual/single-arm tasks.
- **[VLA-Precision: Asymmetric Co-Bootstrapping for Real-World Online RL of VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04355)** — Streaming architecture with 10.9× throughput gain; 98.3% on high-precision chemistry tasks.
- **[PaCo-VLA: Passivity-Shielded Compliance Prior for Contact-Rich VLA Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00515)** — Treats VLA outputs as compliance proposals behind a passivity shield.
- **[LoCal-RIO: Radar-Inertial Odometry with Loop-Closure IMU Bias Calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14109)** — Worth noting for state estimation in degraded conditions.
- **[Adapting Rigid-Body Dynamics Derivatives for Closed-Chain Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21024)**, **[LOInK: Learned Optimal Inverse Kinematics via Structured Neural Surrogates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21275)**, and **[PRIME: Perception Feedback with Situational Memory Embeddings in VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22040)** round out the VLA-adjacent set.

## 2. Tactile Sensing & Contact-Rich Manipulation

A particularly strong theme today.

- **[ForeTac-VLA: A Forecasting-Based Tactile-Vision-Language-Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20980)** — Predicts future tactile states to condition actions; 95% avg success across four contact-rich tasks.
- **[STAR: Sparse Tactile Representation Learning in Vision-Tactile-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12549)** — 200-hour bimanual dataset with synchronized tactile; sparse token representations for dexterous control.
- **[PSR: Predictive Sensorimotor Representation Learning for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21753)** — Hierarchical predictive representations fed into VLA action stream; +30 over π0.5.
- **[DexTouch-WM: Learning Action-Conditioned Tactile World Models from Human Touch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20649)** — Shared sensing layout across human/robot hands; human touch scales robot world models.
- **[ZeroTouch: Tactile-Supervised Visual Contact Estimation for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21726)** — Distills tactile supervision into wrist vision; 95% on unseen objects vs OpenVLA's 25%.
- **[AVT-Fabric: Active Visuo-Tactile Perception via Adaptive Evidence Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21377)** — RGB-first with tactile queried only when needed; 98% accuracy at 1.6/5 stages on average.
- **[Metallic Ultrasound Waveguides as a Distributed Tactile Sensing Platform](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02827)** — Single proximal transducer for force, localization, and material classification.
- **[Metallic Ultrasound Waveguides...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02827)** (noted above), plus **[Bimodal Aerial-Terrestrial Drone...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20965)** under drones.
