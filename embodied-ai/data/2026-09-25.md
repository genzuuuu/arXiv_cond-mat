### 🦾 Dexterous Manipulation & Robotic Hands

*   **Morphometric Imitation**: A three-stage framework that retargets human hand-object interactions to different robot hands, refines them with RL for dynamic feasibility, and distills them into zero-shot sim-to-real visuomotor policies. Achieves high success across three robot hands. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28660)
*   **FingerViP**: Introduces a vision-enhanced fingertip module for multi-fingered hands. A diffusion policy conditioned on multi-view fingertip vision and proprioception improves success on real-world tasks, especially those with occlusions. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21331)
*   **Real-Time Force Regulation for Whole-Hand Dexterous Grasping**: A framework that estimates contacts across the entire hand using a tracked object model and proprioception. It continuously recomputes contact-force distributions to maintain stable grasps under disturbances. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30082)
*   **Aero Hand Open**: Presents a simulation-ready, tendon-driven anthropomorphic hand. It includes a simulation model of the cable transmission, an identified actuation map, and an RL package for training policies entirely in simulation for zero-shot deployment. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28578)
*   **Outcome-Sensitive Motion Search for Impact-Aware Dexterous Catching**: Proposes a method to generate better demonstrations for imitation learning by identifying and repairing "outcome-sensitive" motions in a teacher policy, leading to better catching and impact mitigation. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29020)
*   **CAMP: Cooperative Arm-Hand Motion Planning in Constrained Spaces**: A planner that jointly optimizes arm and hand motion in constrained environments. It uses a layered search and via-point primitives to achieve high planning success. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29021)
*   **Anthropomimetic Soft Robotic Forearm**: An anatomically accurate soft robotic forearm used to identify morphological factors behind human-like wrist stiffness modulation, highlighting the role of carpal bone motion. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29176)

### ✋ Grasping & Tactile Sensing

*   **DA-GRD**: A method for grasp pose recovery under perception-to-execution mismatch using only sparse tactile interactions. It uses decision-aware probing to find an executable grasp without fully relocalizing the object. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29065)
*   **Simple Torque-Observation Alignment for Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Grasping**: An alignment method that uses motor current to align simulated and real torque observations by correcting scale, offset (via delta), and noise, enabling zero-shot transfer for a direct-drive gripper. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29031)
*   **TactileStep**: A tactile learning framework for humanoid locomotion that uses sole pressure sensing to regulate foot-terrain interaction, resulting in softer touchdowns and more stable support. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28959)
*   **Training Object Permanence in World Models**: Introduces a dataset (WROP) and a world model (PWM-WROP) trained on it to improve object permanence and reasoning in video generation models. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28654)
*   **Learning tactile perception from high-bandwidth single-point sensing**: A framework (SpectRobot) that converts single-point tactile signals into time-frequency spectrograms, allowing standard vision encoders to process them and solve visually occluded tasks. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24621)
*   **TacSushi**: A tactile-grounded world-action model for dexterous sushi manipulation. It uses gated fusion of fingertip tactile data to inform actions and is trained with predictive supervision from both successful and failed trials. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19613)
*   **Self-Supervised Anchoring of Fingertip Sensing to Proprioception and Proactive Actions**: A pretraining method (PROPRA) that aligns fingertip sensor histories (tactile, proximity) with proprioceptive and action segments, enabling more effective use of these complementary signals in imitation learning. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29822)
*   **PolyUMI**: An open-source platform for collecting visual-tactile-audio demonstrations. Its handheld gripper records synchronized data, and a token-level multimodal policy, VisTA, effectively uses this data for contact-rich tasks. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29760)

### 🤖 Visuomotor Policies & VLA Models

*   **Know Your Body**: Introduces a "harness" (KnowBody) that makes a robot's action-relevant body relations explicit for a VLM, allowing it to select actions and interpret interactions without changing model weights. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28530)
*   **ActGaze**: A training approach that guides VLA policies to focus on task-relevant image regions by using counterfactual visual interventions to derive gaze supervision from the action objective. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28955)
*   **Streaming-WAM**: An action-conditioned world-action model for asynchronous robot manipulation that accounts for already-scheduled actions in future visual prediction, significantly reducing episode times. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28927)
*   **OCC4M**: An object-centric 4D memory that maintains persistent tracks in a shared world frame for long-horizon manipulation, enabling a VLM to reason about absent objects and container contents. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28798)
*   **Uncertainty-Gated Exploration Noise Suppresses Task Collapse in Online RL Fine-Tuning**: Studies "task collapse" during online RL of VLA policies and finds that an uncertainty-gated exploration controller prevents collapse better than fixed or learned noise schedules. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28838)
*   **MorphIK**: A flow-matching model for neural inverse kinematics that generalizes to unseen robots. It uses a transformer to encode robot morphology and a flow-matching head to generate solutions. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29908)
*   **AdaHVLA**: An adaptive harness for long-horizon VLA execution that refines code-based coordination policies through robot experience, improving success rates in simulation and real-world settings. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29204)
*   **Decoupled Early Exits for Task-Dependent Compute Allocation in Flow-Matching VLAs**: A framework that allows for dynamic computation allocation in VLAs by allowing early exits in both the vision backbone and action expert, reducing latency and computation. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29382)
*   **Self-Adaptive VLA**: A post-training recipe that enables a VLA policy to iteratively adapt to deployment-time hardware shifts (e.g., actuation bias) by using its own rollouts as context. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30092)
*   **Riemannian MeanFlow**: A flow-based policy that learns the conditional flow map on the robot action manifold, enabling on-manifold action generation with as few as one network function evaluation. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30127)
*   **Training-free Behavior Cloning**: Introduces Behavior Predictive Control (BPC), a method that synthesizes policies without end-to-end training by combining an action-aware retrieval metric, a Hankel-based prior, and a closed-form residual correction. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30134)

### 🌍 Sim-to-Real & World Models

*   **Online Sim-to-Real Adaptation via Closed-Loop System Modeling**: Proposes OSRAM, a framework that adapts the reference commands for an existing controller by learning the task-level command-response behavior of the robot-policy closed loop. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28878)
*   **Rolling-WAM**: A world-action model that distributes joint video-action denoising across replanning cycles. It maintains a sliding window of chunks at staggered noise levels, leading to faster replanning. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30247)
*   **DeltaWAM**: A world-action model that predicts visual deltas and actions using dense-anchor, sparse-delta, and action streams, reducing training and inference costs by focusing on changed content. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28811)
*   **Coordinated Robot Agents**: Investigates whether VLMs can pilot robots via an "action workspace" where they can rehearse actions and correct in-view. The World Action Agent (WAA) framework achieves strong results on LIBERO-Pro and RoboTwin. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29964)
*   **RAPID**: A framework that automatically generates, verifies, and refines robot programs from a single visual human demonstration using coding agents. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30249)
*   **AD-WM**: An action-discriminative world model for counterfactual MPC. It uses predictor-level action-recovery regularization to ensure the model can distinguish candidate actions, improving planning success. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30264)
*   **World Action Agent: Harnessing VLMs for Robot Manipulation via World Action Rehearsal**: Introduces a multi-agent harness (WAA) where VLMs pilot robots by previewing and revising actions in a visual workspace. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29964)
*   **Robo-Harness K1**: A robot-use agent framework that exposes perception (depth, anchors, grasp hypotheses) as tools for a VLM, achieving high accuracy on LIBERO-PRO with a Gemini model. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29389)
*   **HarnessPAI**: A model- and embodiment-agnostic harness for Physical AI that treats code as an evolvable interface for action primitives, separating execution from adaptation. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29166)
*   **Do World Models Make Better Robots? A Survey of Evaluation Benchmarks for Predictive Embodied Intelligence**: A survey that argues the field lacks benchmarks to measure the closed-loop advantage of world models over direct VLA policies, cataloging 160 benchmarks. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29669)

*  **BeyondRetarget**: An end-to-end framework that maps monocular RGB videos directly to executable humanoid motions, bypassing explicit motion representation and retargeting. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29850)

*  **Direction-Scale Decomposition (DSD) in Action Representation**: Introduces an action representation for discrete-token VLA models that decomposes increments into direction and scale, improving performance and mitigating degradation in mixed-dataset training. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28865)
*  **RotVLA**: A VLA framework built on a continuous rotational latent action representation (SO(n)), pretrained on cross-embodiment data and achieving high scores on LIBERO and RoboTwin2.0. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13403)
