# arXiv Robotics & AI Digest — Focus: Embodied Intelligence, Manipulation, and Tactile Sensing

## 1. Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models & Adaptive Inference

**[Fast and Accurate: An Adaptive VLA Inference Framework through Environment-aware Model Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06434)** — Proposes EMS, an adaptive VLA framework with fully decoupled dual-system architecture (large deliberative + lightweight reactive). RL-based switching policy dynamically selects the system, achieving 93.4 Hz effective action frequency on LIBERO with comparable success rates.

**[AtlasVLA: Persistent World-Ego State Modeling for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06729)** — Introduces a dual-memory architecture (4D Persistent World State Memory + Ego-Working State Memory) with DiT-based policy. Using only a wrist camera, outperforms multi-view baselines by +9.4% on LIBERO-Long and +17.5% on real-world long-horizon tasks.

**[EchoVLA: Robotic Vision-Language-Action Model with Synergistic Declarative Memory for Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18112)** — Memory-aware VLA with scene memory (spatial-semantic maps) and episodic memory (task experiences), fused via coarse/fine attention. Exceeds pi0.5 by +0.20/+0.11 on manipulation and mobile manipulation tasks.

**[TEMPO: Semantic-Action Decoupled RL Post-Training for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07314)** — Decoupled two-timescale RL: freezes vision-language backbone, updates semantic projection layer infrequently and action expert frequently. Consistent gains on CALVIN and real-world tasks.

**[Cross-View Action Consistency for Camera-Robust Vision-Language-Action Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06965)** — Regularizes action-flow velocity fields across action-equivalent view pairs. Improves held-out-camera success from 53.3% to 74.4% on real robot, +12.5pp over naive mixed-camera SFT on LIBERO-Plus.

**[STRONG-VLA: Decoupled Robustness Learning for Vision-Language-Action Models under Multimodal Perturbations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10055)** — Two-stage decoupled fine-tuning: curriculum-based robustness learning followed by task alignment. Gains up to +16.49% on pi0 and +14.48% on OpenVLA-OFT across 28 perturbation types.

## 2. World Models & Predictive Learning

**[Is Forward Prediction Enough? Physical State Grounding for JEPA World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06799)** — PSG-JEPA adds dual grounding objectives (proprioceptive state + multi-horizon joint-angle changes) to JEPA latent spaces. Consistently outperforms SOTA latent world model baselines across probing, planning, and policy learning.

**[ODEWorld: A Continuous Predictive Architecture via Physical-Time Flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27924)** — Learns continuous latent velocity fields in physical time via ODEs. Supports arbitrary temporal resolution and backward prediction; reconciles planning-conducive dynamics abstraction with visual realism.

**[Robust-WAM: Bridging Generative Pretraining and Semantic Foresight in World-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05903)** — Preserves VAE-based generative path while aligning actions with appearance-invariant semantic foresight. Improves OOD generalization of multiple WAM baselines under illumination shifts.

**[Decoupling Intention from Trajectory: A Representational Deduction Framework for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06994)** — PILOT introduces Representational Deduction with motion chain-of-thought guidance, explicitly modeling state transition tokens. Improves success rates, physical interpretability, and serves as few-shot real-robot fine-tuning strategy.

## 3. Tactile Sensing & Contact-Rich Manipulation

**[Detection and Ranging of Transient Extrinsic Contacts Based on 6D Dynamic Tactile Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07075)** — TECDAR uses a single 2.5×3mm 6D IMU in gripper tips (7 kHz sampling). Fused with EKF, achieves ~7mm localization accuracy within 180ms for line/point contacts, enabling millisecond-scale trajectory rectification.

**[τ: Learning Touch-Augmented Vision-Language-Action Models from Future Visual Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24485)** — Touch-augmented VLA with action-conditioned spatiotemporal tactile representation learned via JEPA-style future visual supervision (training-only). Introduces TacAura dataset with 4 contact-rich tasks; generalizes to unseen objects.

**[C2Dex: Contact-Consistent Reconstruction and Retargeting for Dexterous Manipulation from Monocular Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07045)** — Shared interaction representation via stable object-side contacts aggregated in canonical object space. End-to-end success rates of 57.78% (DexYCB) and 26.67% (TACO), outperforming strongest baselines by >3x.

**[A Haptic Robot Finger Designed for Guqin Instrument Playing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07002)** — Biomimetic multimodal haptic fingertip with high-precision tactile sensing. Validated on guqin string-contact tasks including open-string comparisons and harmonic-tuning.

## 4. Dexterous Grasping & Hand Manipulation

**[FirmGrasp: A Friction-Informed Risk Margin for Robust Grasp Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25049)** — Grasp quality metrics incorporating friction uncertainty via CVaR. Across 1,599 LEAP/Allegro grasps, identifies 53% of nominally force-closed grasps that lose closure under adverse friction. 95% retention rate in MuJoCo adverse-friction trials.

**[Grasp Execution Without a Planner: Configuration-Space Grasp Distance Fields with Certified Safety & Guaranteed Quality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00600)** — Grasp Distance Fields with CBF-CLF QP enforcement. Fixed-base manipulator + Unitree G1 grasp/lift 46/50 objects amid clutter; retains median 94% of synthesized quality margin.

**[FirmGrasp Companion: Grasp Execution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00600)** — (Seen above; execution-focused companion to FIRMGrasp with certified safety guarantees.)

## 5. Sim-to-Real & Reinforcement Learning for Manipulation

**[LyEvO: Lyapunov-Guided Evolutionary Optimization for Safe and Robust Sim-to-Real Policy Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06481)** — Combines constrained evolutionary optimization with SMC-based verification and Lyapunov stability analysis. Iteratively expands verified stability regions; validated on Cartpole and 3D Quadrotor with real-world experiments.

**[SoRoMoX: Fast, Differentiable, and Parallelizable Soft Robot Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06650)** — First JAX-based soft robot modeling framework with GPU-parallel, end-to-end differentiable Cosserat-rod models. 234.6x throughput improvement, enables RL training 7x faster than CPU baselines.

**[Spatiotemporal Agility: Time-Constrained RL for Vision-Guided Dynamic Quadrupedal Interception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06907)** — Direct position/time-conditioned RL policy (instead of velocity commands) with multi-camera perception. Higher success rates in dynamic ball-catching with improved sim-to-real behavior.

## 6. Teleoperation & Human-Robot Interaction

**[A Master-Slave Robot Manipulator for Needle-Based Teleoperation in MRI Chamber](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06354)** — MR-safe master-slave manipulator with fluid transmission. Supports manual, digital, hybrid, and collaborative control modes; validated in in-vivo pig experiment.

**[Automated Terminal-to-Housing Assembly System for Flat Ribbon Cable Harness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06996)** — Sensor-minimal automated assembly for multi-terminal insertion under flexible constraints. Achieves 83.75% end-to-end success over 80 trials using purely mechanical sequences.

**[Hoverflie: Rotor Shrouds for Multi-Modal Micro Air Vehicles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06707)** — (Briefly noted: UAV hovercraft hybrid; outside core manipulation theme.)

## 7. Navigation & Embodied Reasoning

**[CrossTracer: Cross-Embodiment Navigation via VLA Model Reasoning and Trace Residuals Adapting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06688)** — Hierarchical framework with pixel-space waypoint interface, embodiment-conditioned residual corrections. Outperforms Gemini-2.5-Pro by 28.1% on NaviTrace; validated on wheeled and legged robots.

**[WMN3D: A World Navigation Model with 3D Scene Conditioning for Closed-Loop VLN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07267)** — Generative world-action model with 3D Scene-to-Token Adapter conditioning future video-action blocks. Outperforms strong VLM navigation policies on GN-Bench.

**[Unordered Landmark Visual Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06833)** — RGB-only navigation framework free from temporal/odometric priors. Uses topological maps from unstructured images with graph-based belief propagation. Significantly outperforms SOTA methods.

## 8. Policy Improvement & Intervention

**[AutoIntervene: Calibrated Intervention for Action-Chunking Imitation Learning Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07065)** — Online framework selectively transferring control between policy and operator using visual-action support memory. Calibrated thresholds from empirical quantiles; higher post-adaptation success with lower operator time.

**[Retrieve in Time, Correct in Frequency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04527)** — Training-free test-time correction: progressive memory alignment + frequency-domain residual transfer. Raises LIBERO-Long from 61.6% to 68.6% with no parameter updates, client-CPU only (10.99ms overhead).
