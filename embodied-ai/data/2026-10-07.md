## 1. Vision-Language-Action Policies & Action Representations

The dominant cluster today. Several papers attack the same core problem — action chunking is a blunt instrument — from different angles.

- **VLA-ZO** — zeroth-order adaptation of VLAs to deployment shifts, freezing the vision-language prefix. 25-33× faster adaptation than naive ZO. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06271)
- **Vela** — replaces fixed-rate action chunks with continuous spline trajectories, decoupling temporal resolution from output budget. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05230)
- **GeoAlign** — post-trains an RGB geometry branch offline, discards the depth head, and feeds compact geometry tokens into action prediction. 99.0% on LIBERO. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03240)
- **ViDAL** — anchors continuous action latents in *future visual dynamics* rather than just reconstructing action chunks. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08150)
- **StairVLA** — exploits the observation that denoising stages shift focus (language early, vision late); high-level VLA does early denoising, a lightweight refiner handles the rest. 115 ms → 44.2 ms. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07756)
- **VLA-ACL** — learns visual token pruning under action-level supervision with the base VLA frozen; prunes 87.5% of tokens. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08133)
- **PermVLA** — factorization *order* as a regularizer; samples action reveal orders instead of fixed left-to-right. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04659)
- **ESP** — one-step multimodal action generation via energy-score training, avoiding iterative sampling. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07696)
- **Adapting VLAs to Unknown Visual Disruptions** (SALT) — uses the *leftover* unexecuted portion of the previous action chunk as self-supervision at test time. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07946)
- **Guided Action Flow** — trains a small value critic and applies its gradient to steer reverse-time flow sampling, keeping the VLA frozen. 60% → 82.5%. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02092)
- **DiVeR** — reweights verifier learning toward *decision-critical* states, identified by dispersion of sampled action representations. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04933)
- **Commit While Futures Agree** (CA³C) — execution horizon determined by agreement among imagined futures, not action similarity. Up to 71.8% relative failure reduction. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07949)

## 2. Dexterous Manipulation & Robotic Hands

Strong day for hands. Two papers explicitly bridge sim-to-real via tactile/force.

- **ReDex** — humans physically correct *individual fingers* under compliant control during real rollouts while a frozen sim-trained base policy drives the rest; force-conditioned policy then behavior-cloned. Object flipping 14% → 86%. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07525)
- **EigenDEXplore** — argues human motion priors work best as *exploration structure* rather than action representation; adds perturbations along human-derived eigenvectors to joint-space noise. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07681)
- **GOTT** — a reusable, cross-embodiment reach-acquire-move primitive for contact establishment across seen and unseen hand morphologies. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03861)
- **FingerEye** — continuous vision-tactile sensing: fingertip cameras for pre-contact localization, marker-tracked compliant ring deformation as post-contact wrench proxy. +30 pp over wrist-only. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20689)
- **SimToolReal** — object-centric RL policy for zero-shot tool manipulation; procedurally generated tool primitives, random goal poses, no task-specific training. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16863)
- **DexPIE** — post-training dexterous policies from real-world deployment experience with dexterous-hand-adapted intervention. +37.3%. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09615)
- **HRDexDB** — 4D dexterous grasping dataset across five embodiments (human + robots), 3.2K trials, 100 objects. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14944)
- **MIM-VLA** — encodes gripper motor current/position/velocity as an interaction token; distinguishes visually similar real vs. replica objects. 75% vs. 48.8% baseline. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08425)
- **ExoBridge** — uses human limb coupling (one bare hand + one exoskeleton hand) to learn a bridge from bare-hand video to motion and tactile state. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07692)

## 3. Tactile & Force-Aware Manipulation

- **TacZero** — training-free peg insertion using a general-purpose VLM with three-axis tactile responses overlaid on images. 15/20 vs. 10/20 without tactile. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07621)
- **VT-MUSE** — two-stage visuotactile representation learning with masked-view consistency and tactile depth-change prediction. +11 pp. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21290)
- **PEARS** — VLM-based physics reasoning diagnoses failures and updates contact-force bounds; tactile-conditioned diffusion steering corrects the frozen policy. 95%/90% on real tasks. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08784)
- **Robot Learning with Visual Predicted Force** — infers force from Fin Ray gripper deformation; no force/tactile sensor at deployment. Berry picking, in-hand reorientation, plug insertion. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04741)
- **Learning Force-Regulated Manipulation** (TF-Gripper + RETAF) — ~$150 force-controlled gripper, decouples high-frequency force regulation from arm pose prediction. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10013)

## 4. World Action Models

A rapidly consolidating subfield — joint video-action prediction for manipulation.

- **FLEX-WAM** — block-causal, KV-cacheable WAM supporting variable-length contexts and infinite autoregressive rollout; solves PushT and Puzzle-4x4 entirely in imagination. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05483)
- **Rolling-WAM** — distributes joint video-action denoising across successive replanning cycles via a rolling noise schedule. 4.5× steady-state replanning speedup. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30247)
- **ACG-WAM** — adds an Action-Conditioned Geometric JEPA objective predicting geometric features at multiple horizons from frozen VGGT. 93.46% on RoboTwin 2.0. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06965)
- **IronMan** — information bottleneck constrains visual detail to preserve action-relevant dynamics. 79.1% on LIBERO-Plus under OOD shifts. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07961)
- **OpenWAM** — open framework built on Wan2.2-5B with configurable video-action interaction. Counterfactual supervision for inverse/forward dynamics. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07922)
- **AutodidactWAM** — exposes a video-action asymmetry: generated video looks right but co-generated actions are mis-targeted. Fixes via self-distillation from generated video through a hand-pose estimator + IK. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08119)
- **Mask2Real-WM** — decouples dynamics (mask prediction) from rendering to improve per-DoF controllability under a 2.5 h real-data budget. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04546)
- **Completion Aware Guidance** — training-free sampling that counters "task-incomplete imagination." 64% → 70% on RoboTwin 2.0. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01559)
- **FAVOR** — keeps WAM-predicted frames as anchors for verification and VLM-guided recovery when execution drifts. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06280)
- **CSWAM** — adds a V-JEPA 2.1 causal semantic expert with sparse history. RoboTwin Clean→Randomized 10.16% → 45.18%. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18462)
- **WAMJET** — agentic harness for accelerating WAM inference, up to 9.95× lossless speedup. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03797)
- **Mind the Execution Gap** — asynchronous execution breaks action semantics in world-model controllers; TD-MPC2 needs future action sequences, DreamerV3 needs timely attribution. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06582)
