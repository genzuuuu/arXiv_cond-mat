# arXiv Robotics & AI Papers — Focused Summary

## 1. Tactile Feedback & Dexterous Manipulation

**[The Missing Touch: Spatially Distributed Tactile Feedback Brings Teleoperation Closer to Human Dexterity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19372)**
Demonstrates that spatially distributed tactile feedback significantly improves teleoperated manipulation performance. Reproducing localized deformations on the operator's fingertip accelerated task performance and reduced deviation between teleoperated and natural trajectories by 29–79%. Higher-resolution tactile feedback also compressed state-space distribution, potentially improving autonomous policy training outcomes.

**[HiTac-WAM: A Hierarchical Tactile World Action Model for Contact-Rich Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19574)**
Presents a hierarchical tactile world action model that forecasts contact state, 3D deformation fields, and slip risk before execution. Achieves mean contact F1 of 0.921 and improves real-robot success rates from 31.1% to 61.1% (72.2% full system) across chip grasping, blackboard erasing, and USB insertion tasks.

**[SoftVTBench: A Safety-Aware Visuo-Tactile Benchmark for Physically Constrained Robotic Manipulation of Deformable Objects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04234)**
Introduces a benchmark separating Goal Success from Safety Success (no drop, no excessive deformation). Shows success-only evaluation overstates policy performance; tactile sensing improves Safety Success from 21.4% to 35.6% on deformable object tasks.

**[HT-Bench: Benchmarking and Learning Dexterous Full-Hand Tactile Representations with Egocentric Vision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19161)**
Large-scale benchmark with 10M RGB frames and 7.8M tactile frames across 226 tasks. Proposes HandTouch encoder using progressive spatial, cross-modal, and temporal training, outperforming baselines on tactile retrieval, inpainting, and vision-to-tactile synthesis.

## 2. Dexterous Grasping & Manipulation Planning

**[GOAG: Generative and Object-Agnostic Grasp Planner for Dexterous Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19759)**
Trains on gripper contact surface distribution without object-specific data; achieves 86.93% average success on MultiDex objects with faster inference than object-specific approaches.

**[CoToGrasp: Contact-Topology-Conditioned Dexterous Grasp Synthesis via Canonical Workspace Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19776)**
Generates grasps conditioned on specific contact topologies without object-annotated datasets. Zero-shot generalization to unseen objects via feature-based canonical workspace; validated on physical robot.

**[Where to Touch, How to Contact: A Hierarchical RL-MPC Framework for Geometry-Aware Sim-to-Real Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10930)**
Hierarchical framework: high-level RL predicts contact intentions (surface location + subgoal pose), low-level contact-implicit MPC executes. Achieves high success with 40× fewer RL decisions, zero-shot sim-to-real transfer on pushing, pivoting, and reorientation tasks.

**[ADEPT: Accelerating Dexterity via Pre-Training and Post-Training using Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19182)**
Large-scale RL framework for multi-fingered hands. Pretrains on object reposing, then post-trains with behavior-cloning distillation and conservative on-policy updates. Zero-shot sim-to-real on 23-DoF Kuka-Allegro and 29-DoF Flexiv-Sharpa with visuo-tactile perception.

## 3. Visuomotor Policies & VLA Models

**[Fine-Tuning VLAs with Self-Demonstrated Generative Control for Multi-Task Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19490)**
Self-supervised method generating online interaction rollouts from zero-shot VLA as additional training data. Preserves prior instruction-following while learning new skills; validated on ALOHA robot and RoboTwin simulation.

**[What Matters for Latent Actions in Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19613)**
First comprehensive empirical study of latent action models: 41 design choices across modeling paradigms, learning objectives, and integration strategies. Finds VLM backbone fine-tuning with latent actions provides stronger initialization for downstream policy learning.

**[OrthoSkillVLA: Continual Skill Learning via Gradient-Informed Skill Subspace Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19589)**
Addresses catastrophic forgetting in VLA models with separate subspace constraints for VLM vs ActionHead, plus a feature-aware MoE decoder with training-free router. Preserves prior skills while learning new ones without demonstration replay.

**[GS-VLA: Plug-and-Play Viewpoint Canonicalization for Frozen VLA Policies via Gaussian Splatting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19066)**
Lightweight 4M-parameter 3D-Gaussian canonicalizer prepended to frozen VLA policy. Recovers performance lost under camera viewpoint shifts (from ~10% back toward ~90% on LIBERO) without policy retraining.

**[MPCoT: Reward-Guided Multi-Path Latent Reasoning for Test-Time Scalable Vision-Language-Action](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06245)**
Multi-path latent reasoning with M hypotheses refined over K steps, softly aggregated before action decoding. Zero reasoning tokens, improves long-horizon performance on LIBERO and CALVIN.

## 4. Teleoperation & Human-Robot Interaction

**[Wave-Based Bilateral Teleoperation between Nonlinear Manipulators with Direct Contact Force Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20043)**
Addresses stability of bilateral teleoperation with direct contact force feedback under communication delays. Uses passivity-shortage characterization with LMI approach and upper strictly passive communication law.

**[Keeping the Franka Emika Panda alive: a ROS 2 stack with a reliable position interface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19740)**
Open-source ROS 2 stack resolving unstable position control for Franka Panda. Root cause identified as timing/jitter rather than robot limitations; asynchronous hardware interface decouples real-time communication from control loop.

**[HOST: Robots Acquire Manipulation Skills in Seconds from a Single Human Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20033)**
Cascade of self-grounded prediction: estimates robot progress, translates to robot's future observations, derives actions. Acquires skills in ~29 seconds from single human video with 62% success, retaining previously mastered skills.

## 5. Whole-Body Control & Humanoids

**[GigaBrain-WBC-0.5: A Behavior World Model for Robust Whole-Body Control with Environment Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18234)**
First Behavior World Model for humanoid control: predicts next action, state, and latent behavior command distribution. Detects implausible commands online and retracts them. 81.3% terrain interaction success (4.3× baseline), 99.3% fall recovery.

**[Learning Vision-Driven Reactive Soccer Skills for Humanoid Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03996)**
Unified RL controller coupling visual perception with locomotion control. Virtual perception system during training exposes policy to perceptual noise. Reduces ball position error by 46%, time-to-kick by up to 64%; ~90% kicking success.

**[Towards Professional Tennis Styles for Humanoid Robots with Adaptive Motion Planning and Tracking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20087)**
AdaPT framework learns professional tennis styles from broadcast videos. Hierarchical planner-tracker design with motion-speed adapter to bridge sim-to-real gap. Deployed on Unitree G1 and full-size Dobot Atom.

**[DECOWAM: Decoupled Whole-Body World-Action Model for Legged Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20114)**
Separates camera ego-motion from base and arm actions via dedicated conditional interfaces. Reduces action MSE by 21.7% over baseline with 25.95M trainable parameters. Introduces ARMDOG real-robot dataset.

## 6. Contact-Rich Manipulation & Sim-to-Real

**[Hybrid Feedback Sampling for Sample-Efficient Model Predictive Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19443)**
FS-MPC uses optimized feedback policies for sampling in MPC. Hybrid sampling balances local/global search based on system stability. Handles contact-rich tasks like humanoid loco-manipulation and dexterous manipulation where standard approaches struggle.

**[MILD: Tractable Terrain Modeling for Learning Improved Bipedal Locomotion on Deformable Surfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19955)**
Physics-grounded discrete-element contact solver for deformable terrain. Terrain-aware RL controller with latent modulation and proprioceptive estimation. Hardware validation on real deformable surfaces.

**[Learning Highly Dynamic Skills Transition for Quadruped Jumping Through Constrained Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19977)**
Hierarchical RL: imitation-learned low-level skills from animal behaviors + high-level controller selecting maneuvers for narrow-gate traversal. Extended to aerial gate traversal tasks.

## 7. Robot Programming & Planning with LLMs

**[APPROVE: Visual End-User-in-the-Loop Robot Programming with LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19281)**
Multi-modal programming framework combining natural language, block-based interface, and explicit user confirmation. Generated programs visualized in Blockly; confirmed functions stored for reuse.

**[Evidence-Gated Task and Motion Planning with Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20084)**
EAFG acquires visual evidence through VLM-generated exploratory subgoals, then applies feasibility gate before task planning. Improves recipe completion in ambiguous cooking tasks and promotes appropriate halts when objects are absent.

**[Neurosymbolic Embodied Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16794)**
Factors long-horizon tasks into visual exploration and constrained symbolic planning. PDDL transition model restricts decoding to applicable actions; MCTS evaluates continuations. Exceeds 90% success on VirtualHome and ALFWorld with 4B-27B models.

## 8. World Models & Manipulation

**[Mask2Real-WM: Segmentation Masks as a Sim-to-Real Bridge for Controllable Dexterous World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04546)**
Two-stage world model: dynamics model predicts future segmentation masks, rendering model converts to photorealistic RGB. Pretrained on 50h synthetic data, fine-tuned on 2.5h real demonstrations. Per-DoF controllability across all 23 degrees of freedom.

**[Video2DoorTraversal: Push Door Traversal via Simulated Door Twins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20251)**
Single-video real-to-sim-to-real framework for wheel-legged manipulators. DoorTwin reconstructs instance-aligned articulated door twin; ArticuACT policy achieves 96.57% success on five real doors, 80.95% zero-shot on unseen doors.

**[Learning the Right Abstraction: Neural Reduced Dynamics for Complex Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19375)**
NRD framework learns reduced-state dynamics models enabling high-throughput policy learning. Policies transfer to high-fidelity simulators (100/100 goals tracked vehicle, 97/100 arm) with ~4 orders of magnitude speedup.

## 9. Task & Motion Planning

**[When Automata Meet Streams: Temporal Logic Compilation for Stream-Based Robotics Task and Motion Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19453)**
SAM-TD enables LTLf temporal constraints in stream-based TAMP with dynamically generated objects. First demonstration of stream-based TAMP under temporal constraints in PDDLStream environments.

**[PVRA: A Pointwise Key-point Voting Framework for Robotic Assembly](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19968)**
3D keypoint-based modular learning framework predicting actionable outputs for progressive assembly manipulation, learning assembly dependencies from RGB-D input.

## 10. Manipulation & Locomotion Infrastructure

**[Learning Hierarchical Skill Policies with Offline Quality-Diversity Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19684)**
QDOS incorporates advantage-weighted quality-diversity pretraining for robust offline-to-online skill learning with dual dataset reuse. Outperforms baselines in structured manipulation and locomotion tasks.

**[Model-Less Feedback Control of Space-based Continuum Manipulators using Backbone Tension Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06754)**
Complete model-less control for continuum manipulators using empirically initialized Jacobian refined online. Real-time QP computes actuator increments with tendon slack avoidance; sub-millimeter steady-state accuracy.
