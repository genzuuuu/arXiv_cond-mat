## World Models & Action Models

**[Selective Cross-View Consistency for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21402)**  
Shows that applying consistency loss to view-covariant coordinates in world action models (WAMs) is provably harmful, shrinking legitimate view-specific content. Proposes Selective Cross-View Consistency (SCVC) that constrains only view-invariant blocks, improving held-out viewpoint success by 12.2 points on LIBERO-Plus without test-time camera information.

**[Inferring Action from Future Latent State for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22067)**  
Argues video generation is an unnecessary intermediate objective for WAMs. DELE-w0.5 infers action sequences from compact future latent states, removing visual redundancy. Across 480 real-robot trials on four long-horizon tasks, outperforms the strongest baseline by 47.5 percentage points on full-task success.

**[LD4WAM: Learning Latent Dynamics from Human Videos for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22403)**  
Proposes motion-aligned latent dynamics as an embodiment-agnostic representation bridging video priors and low-level actions. Trained on 5,000+ hours of human and robot data, generalizes to unseen objects/backgrounds on RoboTwin and real robots with grippers and dexterous hands.

**[τ₀-WM: A Unified Video-Action World Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01027)**  
Integrates policy learning, video prediction, and action evaluation in a single future-predictive framework. Uses test-time computation to sample and rank action candidates with simulator-based rectification on long-horizon manipulation tasks.

**[WAM-OPD: On-Policy Distillation for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22364)**  
Studies whether on-policy distillation can repair accelerated WAM students without sparse-reward RL. Shows dense teacher supervision on student-induced histories is a promising post-training interface, improving Flash-WAM from 0% to 58.3% success on one task.

**[GeoWAM: Visual Geometry World Action Models for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23486)**  
Predicts future scene geometry (point clouds) instead of images, arguing geometry is a more natural state space for driving. Substantially stronger driving policies than image-based alternatives in open- and closed-loop evaluations.

**[TacWAM: Anchor-Guided World Action Model with Mechanics-Aware Tactile Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28391)**  
First tactile WAM: integrates tactile appearance, dense force fields, and deformation flow into latent prediction. Achieves 75% average success on contact-rich tasks (fragile grasping, in-hand manipulation), exceeding the strongest baseline by 37.5 points.

**[WorldToken: Time-First Sequence Modeling for Robotic Imitation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22591)**  
Fuses multiview images, proprioception, and task conditioning into one token per timestep for causal temporal modeling. On 23 RoboCasa tasks, a 85.3M-parameter policy achieves 59.45% mean success with 2,900 demonstrations per task.

## Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models

**[Mamba-based Selective State Space Modeling Improves SmolVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21407)**  
Replaces causal self-attention with Mamba in SmolVLA's action expert. At N=50 execution horizon (real-time feasible), Mamba expert outperforms Transformer baseline by 7.8% on LIBERO while reducing parameter complexity by 24%.

**[CounterAlign: Counterfactual Supervision for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21740)**  
Transforms expert demonstrations into dense corrective supervision via instruction relabeling and adversarial discriminator training. Improves robustness on LIBERO-PRO and outperforms baselines on real-robot TX-G2 experiments.

**[Robust Bimanual VLA via Embarrassingly Simple Modality Masking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22419)**  
M3 stochastically masks modality channels during training for bimanual VLA. Improves average success by 21.7% in Clean setting and 11.4% in Clean2Rand on RoboTwin 2.0, plus 30%+ on real-world full-task success.

**[UniMem: Unifying Multimodal Memory and Control for VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22869)**  
Unifies high-level memory and low-level control under one backbone with keyframe caching. Outperforms fixed-interval image sampling (93.4% vs. 68.2%) and hierarchical baselines (80.0% vs. 43.5%) on hardware.

**[Geo-VLA: Geometry-Aware VLA Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21440)**  
Internalizes geometric map semantics during training—no HD maps at inference. Achieves 92.1 PDMS, a new SOTA among single-camera VLA planners on NAVSIM v1.

**[Pointing-VLA: Typed Spatial Grounding for VLA Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23138)**  
Typed hidden-state spatial readouts (points, heatmaps, trajectories) instead of text coordinates. Achieves 72.9% on Bridge/WidowX, raises π0.5 real-robot success from 52.7% to 80.7%.

**[Think Only When Needed: Prompt-Authority Control for VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23224)**  
Identifies *prompt-form collapse* where appended text breaks VLA policies (92.47%→3.00%). TOWN-VLA restores original prompt when retrieval would alter form: +3.6 points on LIBERO-Plus, 52.7%→78.7% on physical arm.

**[Latent Reward Steering for Reasoning LLMs in Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00726)**  
Optimizes SAE latent states via a latent reward model during inference. State-specific correction directions improve reasoning quality across multiple backbones without explicit behavior-level control.

**[ROS2SmolVLA: VLA for Industrial-Grade Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23320)**  
Adapts Hugging Face SmolVLA for Universal Robots via ROS 2 interface. Validates pick-and-place on UR10e with on-premise computation, addressing compliance and security concerns.

**[UCA-Flow: Unified Condition-Action Modeling for One-Step Action Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16153)**  
Represents conditions and actions in shared token space; +9.3 points over strongest baseline with 45.6× speedup vs. DP3, 4.3× vs. one-step FlowPolicy.

## Learning & Reinforcement Learning for Manipulation

**[CIDER: Continual Interactive Distillation for Embodied RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21899)**  
Freezes accumulated policy as teacher and interleaves new-task learning with distillation. Across six real-world manipulation tasks, maintains high success on prior tasks while acquiring new skills in 10–20 minutes.

**[Privileged Critic Training Enables Sensor-Free Thruster Fault Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22976)**  
Gives PPO value function access to true degradation state while actor sees only standard observations. Achieves 70.2% success with four simultaneous thruster failures (vs. 4.8% naive, 82.4% oracle).

**[Reward-Free Continual Adaptation for Resilient Space Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23452)**  
Pre-trains model-based agent across simulations; freezes encoder/reward predictor, updates only transition dynamics via unsupervised rollouts. Adapts to severe hardware degradation without new rewards.

**[Physics Filtering Favors Generalization of Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22701)**  
Lightweight physics-filtered feedback corrects learning outputs. Enables quadruped generalization to unseen terrains/payloads, drone flight under wind, and centimeter-level aerial manipulation capture.

**[Learning While Deploying: Fleet-Scale RL for Generalist Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00416)**  
Fleet-scale offline-to-online RL for VLA policies using Distributional Implicit Value Learning and Q-learning via Adjoint Matching. A single policy reaches 95% average success across 8 tasks on a 16-robot fleet.

**[Q-VGM: Q-Value-Gradient Matching for Offline-to-Online RL of Flow-Matching VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08015)**  
Formulates policy improvement as optimal control over denoising dynamics. From few-shot SFT π0.5, improves LIBERO success from 86.9% to 99.7% with 6× fewer rollouts than PPO; real-world bimanual: 66.7%→98.3%.

**[Guided Action Flow: Q-Guided Inference for Frozen Flow-Matching VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02092)**  
Steers reverse-time action-flow sampling with gradients from a task-specific critic. On real-robot water-bottle placement: 47.5%→85.0% success without policy fine-tuning; with visual distractor, 0/11→6/12.

**[Act with Intent: Distilling Behavior Intent for VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23478)**  
Distills behavior-level intent into action decoder via frozen teacher VLM. GR00T-N1.7 improves from 64.3% to 84.7% on SimplerEnv-Bridge; π0.5 gains consistently; real-world success 62.0%→68.7%.

**[RARM: Confidence-Gated Progress Reward Modeling for RL in Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22027)**  
Lightweight visual comparator converts a single demonstration into dense progress rewards, rewarding only confident forward progress. Best overall success across 9 simulated and 4 real-world tasks including cloth folding.

**[EDGE: Experience-Distillation for Guided Exploration in Agentic RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21946)**  
Treats retrieved experiences as temporary training-time scaffolds, progressively internalizing benefits into the parametric policy. Improves GRPO by 8.3 and 12.5 points on ALFWorld/WebShop at 7B scale.

**[Agent-G²: Gaussian Guidance for Agentic RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23318)**  
Draws per-task guidance depth from a Gaussian whose parameters are estimated online from existing rollouts. Outperforms hint-based, hint-free, and Aux-RL baselines on ALFWorld at one-third the rollout cost.

**[Privacy-Aware Robot Perception: RoboShape](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21380)**  
Information-theoretic compression head for point cloud encoders. Reduces embeddings by 87.5% while retaining 98.7% of object classification utility; collapses sensitive attribute predictions by 39.3%.

## Tactile Sensing & Proprioception

**[Force/Torque-Based Kinematic Adaptation for Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21592)**  
Estimates tool kinematics online using only joint angles and wrist F/T sensing, with no exteroceptive tool-tip measurement. Provably stable update law; validated on peg-in-hole; first step toward factoring manipulation learning into task policy + adaptive kinematics.

**[An Interpretable Deep Learning Framework for Material Perception from Multisensory Tactile Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21894)**  
Three interconnected models map multisensory touch to material perception with Integrated Gradients interpretability. Thermal cues are the most informative modality across all models; near-perfect classification when unconstrained by intermediate stages.

**[Design of Biomimetic Joint-Covering Skin with Tissue-Like Structure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23304)**  
Joint-covering skin with pressure- and stretch-sensitive elements on musculoskeletal humanoid Musashi-W. Alone achieves ~3° joint angle estimation error; integrates with muscle sensing for improved accuracy.

**[GhostTac: Manipulating Tactile Sensors without Physical Contact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20817)**  
First contactless attack on tactile sensing via electromagnetic interference. Exploits nonlinear rectification to induce DC offsets that bypass filtering; demonstrates impacts on grasping, slip detection, and material classification.

**[Insect-like Distributed Proprioception for Flapping-Wing Aerial Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21699)**  
Piezoelectric sensors integrated into actuator and hinge track stroke (0.44° RMSE) and pitch (2.44° RMSE) angles. Enables collision detection and asynchronous flapping for insect-scale robots.

## Teleoperation & Human-Robot Interaction

**[Whole-Body Bilateral Teleoperation with Multi-Stage Object Parameter Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.09846)**  
Wheeled humanoid loco-manipulation teleoperation with online object inertial parameter estimation (vision + VLM prior + hierarchical sampling). Supports lifting/delivering/releasing payloads ~1/3 of robot body weight with compliant behavior.

**[Safety-Critical Bilateral Teleoperation for Omnidirectional Aerial Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21735)**  
Hierarchical safety filter based on control barrier functions, force-sensorless haptic feedback. Enforces thrust limits while accounting for interaction tracking errors; validated on hexarotor aerial manipulator in diverse tasks.

**[TONAV: Task-Oriented Navigation and Action-Velocity Chunk Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22296)**  
For articulated-object quadrupedal mobile manipulation: position-velocity-coupled teleoperation, VLM task decomposition, and velocity-supervised action chunks. Real-world experiments show higher success with continuous-contact interaction.

**[Human-Assistance Robot System with Contextual Action Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22028)**  
Conceptual design for proactive assistance using contextual HAR and behavior trees on the Spot platform for dynamic decision-making in complex scenarios.

## Whole-Body Control & Humanoids

**[GigaBrain-WBC-0.5: A Behavior World Model for Robust Whole-Body Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18234)**  
First Behavior World Model for humanoid control: jointly predicts action, next state, and latent behavior command distribution. 81.3% on terrain interaction (4.3× strongest baseline), 99.3% fall recovery (16.8× baseline); transfers to Unitree G1 and Maker L01.

**[Athena-WBC: Capability-Aligned Policy Experts for Long-Tail Humanoid WBC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04837)**  
Dynamic and balance experts with routing + DAgger distillation to a single controller. Improves long-tail motion recovery and held-out tracking over SONIC-recipe baseline on a full-size humanoid.

**[DreamMimic: Learning Visuomotor Whole-Body Loco-Manipulation via World Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22278)**  
Distills privileged teacher policies into vision-based humanoid controllers using world-model-assisted distillation with RNN-based latent dynamics and performance-conditioned guidance. Improves on OMOMO and BEHAVE over strong vision baselines.

**[GOLEM: Modular Humanoid Autonomy Towards EV Battery Disassembly](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21550)**  
End-to-end open-source system on Unitree H1-2 for battery disassembly. Modular architecture with MuJoCo/IsaacLab digital twins; grasping degrades from 97% tethered to 37% under navigation-induced pose variance.

**[RPL: Robust Humanoid Perceptive Locomotion on Challenging Terrains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03002)**  
Two-stage training: terrain-specific experts with privileged heights → transformer policy with multiple depth cameras. Robust to payloads on 20° slopes, varied staircases, and 25cm stepping stones with 60cm gaps.

**[DELTA: Deformable Elevation-Based Local Terrain Attention for Quadruped Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22033)**  
State-conditioned sampling locations with fixed-size token set; encoder cost independent of map resolution. Better traversal on fine-grained sparse terrain, successful sim-to-real on RAIBO2.

## Grasping & Dexterous Manipulation

**[Uncertainty-Aware Grasping of Leaf-Occluded Strawberries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14492)**  
UNCLE-Grasp: shape completion with Monte Carlo dropout generates multiple completion samples; completion-level force-closure scores quantify grasp uncertainty. At highest occlusion: 0.870 success among attempts vs. 0.780 baseline.

**[Contact-Rich Robotic Manipulation in Construction via Zero-Shot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22100)**  
Diffusion policy trained on simulated pose + F/T data with L1-inspired adaptive controller. 100% success on timber joinery and pipe fitting; 90–100% on sequential truss assembly; zero-shot sim-to-real.

**[Diffusion Policies for Timber Joinery under Fabrication Uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.17774)**  
Sensory-motor diffusion policies from teleoperated demonstrations on industrial robot. 100% success nominal, 75% under 10mm positional perturbations exceeding joint clearance.

**[Learning Diffusion Policies for Robotic Manipulation of Timber Joinery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.17774)** — covered above.

**[Latency-Aware Framework for Visuomotor Policy Learning on Industrial Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14255)**  
Timestamps policy-predicted actions and schedules only temporally feasible ones. Maintains smooth motion within 13% of demonstration reference across 100–500ms inference latencies, avoiding contact-force overshoot.

## Sim-to-Real & Domain Adaptation

**[Enhancing Sim2Real Transfer for Torque-Controlled Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22629)**  
Real2Sim2Real pipeline: trajectory matching, genetic parameter optimization, and domain randomization for Franka Panda. Smooth policy transfer in Gazebo and MuJoCo to real robot across target-reaching tasks.

**[MorFiC: Fixing Value Miscalibration for Zero-Shot Quadruped Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14554)**  
Multiplicative critic conditioning on morphology latent prevents averaging incompatible value targets. Zero-shot transfer to 7 robots from single source robot; 1.98 m/s on AlienGo vs. additive PPO baselines below 0.65 m/s.

**[Betting for Sim-to-Real Performance Certificates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21572)**  
Sim-to-real betting certificate framework: simulators place bets on real outcomes, accumulated wealth tightens performance certificates. Narrows certificates by 51.6% vs. baselines, anytime-valid with any simulator bank.

## Benchmarking & Evaluation

**[The Imitator Game: Benchmarking Robot Imitative Ability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22301)**  
Four-level benchmark (L0–L3) progressively widening human-demo/robot-scene gaps, with IG-10K dataset (20,000+ paired episodes, 50+ tasks). Functional substitution is the decisive barrier: every model falls below 13% zero-shot on unseen tasks.

**[InstructMove: Text-Indispensable Benchmark for Instruction-Following Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22990)**  
Requires multiple visually plausible actions with only one consistent with language instruction. Enables diagnosing visual shortcuts in VLA policies; InstructMove sim data improves real-world performance.

**[Triplet2Track: Hierarchical System with Object-Centric Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22800)**  
Instance-grounded triplets as subgoals, translated into continuous track priors with online replanning. 74.8% average success on real-world long-horizon tasks with compositional generalization.

**[Guardian: Detecting Robotic Planning and Execution Errors with VLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01946)**  
GuardianFail-36k: large-scale failure reasoning dataset from perturbed trajectories + step-by-step VLM traces. Multi-view Guardian model achieves SOTA on three unseen real-world benchmarks and boosts downstream policy success.

**[Retrieval-Grounded Robot Program Generation with MCP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21417)**  
LLM workflow generating ABB RAPID programs from natural language. Dual-stream RAG grounds code in technical documentation; MCP server links to RobotStudio for simulation-based correction.

## Object Manipulation, Navigation, and Systems

**[Vision Guided Target Conditioned Control for Autonomous Excavation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21778)**  
Image-aligned target mask as visual command; mask-conditioned Action Chunking Transformer. Paired-condition supervision improves target success from 4% (no condition) to 96%; pile removal 76.8% vs. 15.7–27.4% baselines.

**[Vision-Guided Morphing Quadcopter for Multi-Geometry Payload Transport](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21879)**  
Tendon-based morphing arms grasp boxes/cylinders/spheres through narrow passages. Footprint reduction 75–89.7%, drop-zone error below 0.18 m across payload types.

**[Operational Digital Twin Clinics for Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21416)**  
Single photographs → editable simulator-ready environments for 39 ophthalmic clinic scenes. Shows different reachability/contact patterns across robot embodiments under device perturbation.

**[ParallelWorld: Test-Time Scaling for Embodied Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22971)**  
Verifier-guided tree search enabling multi-step trajectory simulation before committing to actions. Consistently improves active perception and reasoning on ESI-Bench.

**[EndoNav: Language-Guided Robotic Endoscopic Examination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22093)**  
Translates surgeon commands into autonomous endoscopic visualization within patient-specific sinonasal anatomy. Achieves 87.04–84.37% visualization IoU vs. resident surgeons (inter-surgeon IoU 87.44%).

**[Macro-Action Topological Navigation under Noisy Localization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23055)**  
Object-centric topological controller with EKF-based pose estimation in photorealistic Habitat. Reaches target objects from vision alone with locally consistent pose.
