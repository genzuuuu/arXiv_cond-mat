## 1. Dexterous Manipulation, Hands & Tactile Sensing

**[OpenDexGrasp: Open-vocabulary Task-Oriented Dexterous Grasping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18117)** — Unified data + generative modeling framework with dual-source supervision (OpenDex-Scale for coverage, OpenDex-Align for embodied alignment); shared perception–action latent couples vision-language context with dexterous action generation, skipping a separate affordance-to-pose stage.

**[TacBPM: A Tactile-conditioned Behavior Prior Model for Dexterous Reorientation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18174)** — Distills multi-scale sphere specialists into a latent controller; downstream policies reuse the fixed tactile prior via residual latent actions, improving training speed and enabling sim-to-real in-hand and arm-hand tasks.

**[LUMO: Designing Luminous Contact Morphology for Repeatable Whole-Finger Contact Observation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18165)** — Embedded LEDs + compliant silicone pad make contact location externally observable; multi-objective Bayesian optimization over cross-section yields 15–59% better separation across contact locations, 1.44 N normal-force MAE.

**[InterMASH: A Unified Geometric Representation for Grasp Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18504)** — Sphere-fixed anchors with low-degree spherical harmonics encode hand geometry, object geometry, and contact as tokens; a conditional Diffusion Transformer generates hand geometry and contact jointly across embodiments.

**[Primitive-Informed Sampling-Based MPC for Multi-Fingered Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14868)** — Biases sampling with low-dimensional manipulation primitives while optimizing joint-level residuals; validated on a 16-DoF Allegro hand with a MuJoCo digital twin, including primitives from human hand-motion data.

**[Real-World Reinforcement Learning with MPC Scaffolding for Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14878)** — Sampling-based MPC provides prior experience for off-policy SAC; reaches 100% in-hand rotation success after 7 minutes of online RL and 1000 consecutive rotations without a drop after 20 minutes.

**[CANTABILE: Learning Expressive Dynamics for Robotic Piano Performance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18213)** — Dynamics-aware framework closes the score-to-contact loop by conditioning on velocity goals and mapping key angular velocity at onset to MIDI velocity; raises Velocity F1 from 0.06 to 0.34 on EXPRESSIVE-51.

## 2. Grasping, Contact-Rich Manipulation & Force Feedback

**[Human-Centric Grasp State Assessment: Toward Transferring Subjective Evaluation to Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17540)** — VLM-based semi-automated supervisor plus lightweight grasp-state predictor transfers tacit human criteria to deformable-object grasping; validated on three objects and 25 human participants.

**[ForceDelta-VLA: Distilling Force-Conditioned Action Corrections for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18242)** — Correction-distillation constructs explicit force- and delay-correction targets; 82.2% mean success on nine contact-rich tasks vs 54.4% for ForceVLA, with ~26% lower peak contact force.

**[TAO-Force: Unifying Force-Aware Perception and Fast-Slow Control for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18497)** — Injects encoded force feedback into a frozen VLM backbone via F-FiLM, with a contact-gated slow position / fast admittance control split.

**[Energy-Regularized Imitation Learning for Force- and Work-Aware Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18164)** — Differentiable energy predictor turns simulator-side mechanical work into a regularizer for RVT-2 fine-tuning; modest but consistent work reduction with no success loss.

**[Calibrated Probabilistic Obstruction Reasoning with Vision-Language Models for Grasping in Clutter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18718)** — Calibrates and fuses VLM/depth/amodal cues into a distribution over obstruction graphs, with a total-variation bound for certified decisions; 77.8% real-world success.

**[TACO: TActile World Model as a Self-COrrector for Scalable Robot Policy Post-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02840)** — Recognize–Imagine–Label loop using a compositional visuo-tactile world model; raises average task score from 0.375 to 0.825 after two post-training iterations.

**[Learning Contact Dynamics through Touching: Action-conditional Graph Neural Networks for Robotic Peg Insertion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12151)** — Graph-structured mesh model predicting pose updates and emergent reaction torque, self-supervised from random touching; 98% MPC success in simulation and clear real-world gains over a sys-ID MuJoCo baseline.

**[Language-Guided Grasping under Partial Observation for Mobile Manipulation in Field Inspection and Maintenance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07866)** — Open-vocabulary detection + promptable segmentation + depth completion for 6-DoF grasp selection on a quadruped; 9/10 vs 3/10 for a view-dependent baseline.

## 3. Vision-Language-Action Models & Policies

**[Not All Layers Need Tuning: Diagnosing and Directing Adaptation in Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18084)** — Shows adaptation cost is region-structured (appearance→vision encoder, instruction→language backbone, novel objects→encoder + action head) across five VLAs, then allocates variable-rank LoRA accordingly; matches full fine-tuning with 0.04% of trainable parameters on a real xArm-7.

**[Reinforcement Learning for Real-Time Vision-Language-Action Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18207)** — Real-Time EXPO-FT decouples slow chunk generation from fast reactive action edits; best in 10/10 Kinetix environments and raises real-world dynamic task performance from 42% to 97% with ≤10 minutes of online data.

**[Decoupling Vision, Language, and Action for Efficient Multi-Task Robot Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18374)** — Controlled study producing DEM (fine-tuned DINOv3 + frozen NeoBERT + MeanFlow head); comparable success to VLM-backbone policies at 8–17× higher inference frequency and 6–15× less energy.

**[ActionPiece: Rethinking Action Tokenization for Autoregressive Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18487)** — Introduces physical rank consistency (PRC) as a tokenization metric and a tokenizer that supervises near-far ordering in both encoder and quantized features; 94.8% LIBERO, 68.8% unseen LIBERO-Plus.

**[M²Tok: Multi-head Multi-codebook Discrete Action Tokenization for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18259)** — Multi-head latent decomposition with independent per-head codebooks reduces the discretization bottleneck and improves VLA success on RoboTwin, Simpler-Env, and three real tasks.

**[DistAL: Distance-based Advantage Learning for VLA Fine-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18392)** — Uses embedding-space distance as a dense reward to build a more informative value function for advantage-conditioned VLA training.

**[M³P-R1: Reinforcement Learning for Large Language Model Guided Multi-Modal Motion Planning via MIP Code Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18669)** — Fine-tunes LLMs to emit executable MIP code for multi-modal motion planning rather than answers, with solver-backed verification.

**[GeomVLA: Unifying Scene, Motion, and Action in 3D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13812)** — Lifts VLM features into a robot-centric 3D frame and conditions a 3D flow action denoiser on intermediate motion tokens from a scene trajectory denoiser.

**[WholeBodyWAM: Learning Whole-Body World Action Models with Scalable Motion Priors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18197)** — Pretrains a language-conditioned Motion Expert on UniMotion-4K (>4K hours of heterogeneous human/humanoid motion), then integrates it with Video and Action Experts via asymmetric MoT attention.

**[Atomic Motion Coordinate for Language-Steerable and Force-Responsive Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15012)** — Thirteen signed translation/rotation/hold atoms grounded from text and forward kinematics, injected into every action-expert block; improves OOD fruit progress from 60.5% to 87.8%.

**[Veo-Act: Enhancing VLA Policies with Frontier Video Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04502)** — Hierarchical split with a video model as high-level planner and a VLA as low-level executor, connected by a multi-head inverse dynamics model and a learned interaction gate.

**[PACT-WAM: Predicting Actions and Visual Foresight with Compact Temporal Encoding for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15882)** — Hierarchical history encoding with 75% fewer tokens and joint action/visual flow sampling; 98.6% LIBERO, 92.3% RoboTwin 2.0, 78.0% real Piper, rising with Proposal Review.

**[Knowing When to Stop: Adaptive Action Chunking via Internal Cross-Attention Dynamics in VLAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00908)** — Training-free truncation that detects high-entropy plateaus in action-to-observation cross-attention to pick the execution horizon online.

**[CSWAM: Better Causal Semantic Representations for Out-of-Distribution Generalization in World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18462)** — Adds a V-JEPA 2.1 causal semantic expert to FastWAM; RoboTwin Clean-to-Randomized success rises from 10.16% to 45.18%, and real-world OOD from 27.5% to 70.0%.

## 4. Imitation Learning, Teleoperation & Data Collection

**[Vision-Language Grounded Task-Context-Aware Imitation Learning for Robotic Disassembly](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17714)** — Hierarchical task selection plus language-grounded spatial targets for long-horizon disassembly; +35pp over a diffusion policy baseline and +75pp over a prior task-context baseline.

**[UMI-Bridge: Action-Anchored Latent Alignment across Human and Robot Manipulation Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18232)** — Uses UMI as an intermediate domain to align latents by action equivalence rather than pixels; 91.7% mean success vs 73.3% for naive co-training, and 25% of robot data suffices on data-efficiency tasks.

**[From Gameplay to Policy: Towards Scalable Robot Data Collection via Gamified Robot-Free Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18650)** — VR gamified egocentric collection platform plus Game2Policy affordance extraction; +10.0 points in simulation and +18.3 on real robots in the few-shot setting.

**[Learning to Stack: Cube-Stacking Imitation Learning from Virtual Reality Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19040)** — VR pipeline (Vive Pro 2 + Manus gloves + OpenXR) with replayable trajectory conversion; 200 virtual demos in 30 minutes vs 45 real-world ones.

**[Missing Bridges: Composition-Aware Active Imitation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18004)** — AALT requests bridge demonstrations maximizing start-goal connectivity gains; 72/72 simulated UR5e retrieval tasks using 3 demonstrations vs 88.6% for the strongest baseline after 20.

**[A Hierarchical Spatiotemporal Action Tokenizer for In-Context Imitation Learning in Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15215)** — Two-level vector quantization over actions and timestamps; reports new SOTA for in-context imitation learning across simulation and real benchmarks.

**[Gated Residual Body-Hand Coordination for Whole-Body Humanoid Teleoperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18763)** — Frozen motion tracker and hand retargeter with bounded gated residual corrections; cuts wrist and fingertip geometry errors by 39.2–56.3% on held-out GRAB motions.

**[In-Context Robot Learning with VLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19138)** — GPT-Policy compiles context, proposes robot-tool actions via a VLM, and verifies execution; human video demos improve completion even without robot action labels.

## 5. Sim-to-Real, Real-to-Sim & Data Generation

**[Function-Preserving Data Generation for Zero-Shot Real-to-Sim-to-Real Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18293)** — Constraint-guided mesh deformation with physically consistent pose/collision transfer; enables zero-shot deployment without real-world fine-tuning.

**[DeformSmith: Physics Harness-Guided Hierarchical Generation of Deformable Assets for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18620)** — Hierarchical agentic construction with a shared physics harness closes the loop via manipulation feedback for deformable assets.

**[Agentic Real2Sim: Physics-based World Modeling with Vision-Language Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19190)** — Converts real recordings into simulatable episodic twins across rigid, deformable, and humanoid scenes; supports policy fine-tuning and surrogate evaluation with open-weight VLM backends.

**[Explicit Language Memory for Long-Horizon Planning in Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04765)** — Converts discrete temporal observations into a textual memory sequence with a high-level VLM / low-level VLA split; validated in simulation and sim-to-real.

**[Proprioception-Anchored Cross-Modal Pretraining for Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Contact-Rich Assembly](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07534)** — PACE supervises visual and F/T representations by predicting proprioceptive transitions; 93.3% real-world success with only a 2.7pp sim-to-real drop and no real-world fine-tuning.

**[Dreaming the Sound of Contact: Leveraging Video and Audio Generation for Zero-Shot Force-Aware Manipulation and Data Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19137)** — Uses generated contact-sound loudness to shape desired-force profiles executed by a closed-loop regulator on a Franka Panda.

**[EmboAlign: Aligning Video Generation with Compositional Constraints for Zero-Shot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05757)** — VLM extracts compositional constraints used for rollout selection and trajectory optimization; +43.3pp over the strongest baseline with no task-specific training data.

**[WeaveRL: Weaving Reconstruction into Scene-Aware Fabrics for Perceptive Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18685)** — GPU-accelerated surfel reconstruction across thousands of parallel sim instances lets geometric-fabric RL policies operate on sensor-derived geometry; unseen-obstacle success rises from 35% to 61%.

**[Latency-Tolerant Cloud-Edge Collaborative Vision-Language-Action Models via Emergent Representational Specialization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00569)** — Cloud VLA provides slowly varying task features, edge head adds state-sensitive corrections; retains 63.8–78.0% success under a 40-step delay window where baselines collapse.

**[AdaGeoVLN: Selective Geometry Across Representation Depth and Navigation Time for Vision-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18789)** — Hierarchical GFM–VLM fusion with navigation-aware memory retention of VGGT global-attention KV states.

**[VLA-ULAP: Interleaving Cloud VLA Calls with Ultra-Lightweight Local Action Prediction at the Edge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18663)** — ~7.4M-parameter local predictor removes 48.8–76.7% of VLA calls while retaining 95.0–97.5% of baseline success, with real SO-101 validation.

**[rMuscle: Robotic Muscle Memory for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Model Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19104)** — Dual-phase cache (context + action) exploiting cross-execution similarity; 1.29–1.42× speedup on RTX 4090 and Jetson Thor with unchanged success rates.

**[Technical Report: One-Step Drifting Action Heads for GR00T N1.7](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18108)** — Cuts action-head forward time from ~45.3 ms to 5.0 ms but documents a systematic success drop — a speed/success trade-off, not a win.

## 6. Humanoids, Loco-Manipulation & Whole-Body Control

**[Learning Multi-Humanoid Pickup and Transport via Decentralized Object-Centric Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17824)** — Each humanoid gets a local attachment region and learns gripperless bimanual pinching; single-robot policies transfer nontrivially to teams, with sim-to-real hardware demos.

**[Adaptive-MHE: A Sampling-Based Adaptive MPC for Legged Loco-Manipulation via Moving Horizon Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17832)** — Online sampling-based system identification of object/terrain parameters (mass, friction) coupled to a sampling MPC; matches a controller with ground-truth parameters.

**[PASSAGE: Scaling Scene-Aligned Motion Learning for Perceptive Humanoid Traversal in Cluttered Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18732)** — 100 h of scene-aligned motion capture across 1,500 cluttered scenes; flow-matching planner + perceptive whole-body tracker scaled 6→100 h lifts contact-free success from 48.1% to 68.9%.

**[KINO: A Keyframe Interface for VLM Planning and Whole-Body Control in Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18869)** — Keyframes as the intermediate representation between VLM planning and RL control; saliency-based keyframe sampling raises end-to-end success from 44% to 92% on a Unitree G1.

**[Learning Holistic Whole-Body Loco-Manipulation with a Bipedal Mobile Manipulator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18930)** — Single RL controller maps 6-DoF end-effector targets directly to coordinated base+arm actions, with reward gating and a temporal context estimator; works under VR teleop, a diffusion policy, and scripted trajectories.

**[RecMorph: Topology-Guided Spatial Recurrence for Generalized Morphology Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18359)** — Depth-first traversal turns the kinematic tree into a sequence for cross-limb communication; generalizes to 30-limb bodies and completes 40 physical Go1/Go2 trials without falls.

## 7. Navigation, SLAM & Perception

**[LEAP: Learning Emergent Active Perception for Quadruped Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17628)** — Gaze control emerges from task pressure alone on a gaze-invariant egocentric belief map; 92.7% success vs 74.2% scripted and 34.5% passive, within 4.6 points of a privileged oracle.

**[ActiveScale: Scaling Active Perception for Robots across Model, Data, and Hardware](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18514)** — VLA augmented with historical video and explicit camera-pose supervision; 1,000 hours of egocentric + robotic mid-training data and an active-perception mobile manipulation platform.

**[I-Perceive: A Foundation Model for Vision-Language Active Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00600)** — Predicts a 6D camera pose fulfilling a natural-language perception intent, with a large-scale language-viewpoint dataset and zero-shot closed-loop refinement.

**[WAVE-Go: World-Model Navigation with Adaptive Execution for Wheel-Legged Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18193)** — Separates world-action prediction from interruptible execution with a conditional-risk prefix selection rule; 74.1% in-distribution and 63.3% dynamic OOD success, collisions down from 4.4 to 2.9 per 100 m.

**[GroundingVLN: Reasoning and Acting with Grounding for Vision-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18581)** — Uses visual grounding as a shared interface between reasoning and action; 69.9% SR on R2R-CE with 0.9% of the baseline's training data.

**[HarnessVLN: Unifying Training-Free Embodied Navigation through an Agent Harness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15195)** — Zero-shot agent harness coordinating perception, memory, and execution tools with a spatiotemporal graph; 60.8%/53.9%/76.0%/59.3% on R2R, RxR, HM3D-v2, HM3D-OVON.

**[CueNav / Visual Cue Guided Video Planning for Generalizable Robot Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16737)** — BEV global cue + body-aware egocentric view with an embodiment-specific IDM; ~2× maze success and 70% success in a narrow passage.

**[HINT-Plan: Human Intention-Aware Robot Task Planning in Context-Rich Environments using Vision Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17771)** — VLM anticipates human intentions from third-person images and feeds hierarchical scene graphs into joint task planning; 69.71% success, up to 35.29pp over baselines.

**[Mem2Ego: Empowering Vision-Language Models with Global-to-Ego Memory for Long-Horizon Embodied Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14254)** — Adaptively retrieves task-relevant cues from global memory and aligns them with egocentric observations.

**[VLEM: Real-Time 3D Vision-Language Embedding Mapping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06291)** — Integrates pixel-aligned 2D VL embeddings into a globally consistent metric 3D map from a raw RGB-D stream; better open-set segmentation and a more compact representation than ConceptFusion/Open-Fusion/RayFronts.

**[DRT&R: Direct Radar Teach & Repeat](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17766)** — Direct spinning-radar navigation with local mapping; 4 cm localization in most on- and off-road conditions, 12 cm in degenerate settings, >10 km closed-loop with MPC.

**[ReRadar: Robust Radar Global Localization via Rotation-Equivariant Descriptor Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18092)** — Steerable CNNs + group pooling + NetVLAD for rotation-invariant radar descriptors; 99.37% Recall@1 on OORD Bellmouth.

**[2Fast-2Lamaa: Large-Scale Lidar-Inertial Localization and Mapping with Continuous Distance Fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05433)** — Prior-less continuous IMU preintegration undistortion plus GP distance-field mapping; 0.22% odometry and 0.06 m localization error over 750 km.

**[AERO-VIS: Asynchronous Event-based Real-time Onboard Visual-Inertial SLAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07885)** — Decouples event preprocessing from estimation, operating at hardware rate; first purely event-based inertial SLAM with closed-loop UAV control on onboard compute.

**[SOL-SLAM: Inverse Compositional Gauss-Newton Direct Registration for Fast Sonar-Only Local SLAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18893)** — Dense direct registration of full acoustic intensity scans to a recursive local map; sub-meter tracking over wide displacement gaps, comparable to FLS+DVL+IMU fusion.

**[SEAM: Submap-Anchored Evidence for Lifelong LiDAR Mapping under Trajectory Deformation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18819)** — Submap-level evidence via reprojection so edits don't require full recomputation, plus DOP-based loop-edge suppression and direction-aware voxel evidence.

**[LiLi: Lie Theory Based 3D LiDAR Scan Alignment Degeneracy Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17145)** — Uses se(3) generators to identify the full set of degenerate transformations; halves alignment error vs a Hessian-based detector and succeeds on a 430 m tunnel where the reference fails.

**[PESTO: Formally Correct Registration of LiDAR Point Clouds with Limited Overlap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18082)** — Tetrahedra as environment-agnostic features for registration, with proven worst-case alignment-error bounds.

**[DefVINS: Visual-Inertial Odometry for Deformable Scenes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.00702)** — Decomposes state into a rigid IMU-anchored component and a non-rigid embedded deformation graph, plus the first real deformable-scene VIO benchmark (VIMandala).

**[Benchmarking Visual-Inertial Odometry in Subterranean Environments Under Sensor Degradation, Miscalibration, and Dynamic Occlusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18628)** — Failure-centric stress test over nine perturbations, exposing paradigm-specific vulnerabilities not visible under nominal conditions.

**[Does Online Gravity Estimation Matter? Revisiting a Silent Design Split in LiDAR-Inertial Odometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13675)** — Finds no consistent benefit from online gravity estimation under continuous LiDAR correction, but does find trajectory-dependent costs during multi-second outages; recommends keeping gravity and accel bias online.

## 9. Safety, Control & Formal Guarantees

**[Timely Activation of Safety Filters via One-Step Reachability Expansion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17904)** — A one-step-expanded backward reachable tube as the filter activation condition fixes the discrete-time jump-into-unsafe-set failure of least-restrictive safety filters.

**[ElastiQP: An Always-Feasible QP Solver for Constrained Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19080)** — Relaxes every inequality with an exact per-constraint ℓ1 penalty while folding slacks analytically; microsecond-level, up to 40× faster than alternatives on infeasible problems.

**[Predictive Varanus: Combining CSP Conformance Monitoring with Predictive LTL Runtime Verification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17625)** — Two-stage pipeline using a CSP model as a conformance gate and as a Büchi automaton constraining a predictive LTL monitor; demonstrated on a nuclear-store inspection rover.

**[CaSCo: Cascade-Aware Soft-Collision Motion Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18910)** — Assigns semantic risk to objects and simulates cascade collisions, searching for minimum total semantic risk with an admissible, consistent heuristic.

**[Indicators of resilience for autonomous control systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18264)** — Derives critical-slowing-down resilience indicators for nonlinear control systems and validates them by progressively damaging a quadrotor's propeller blades.

**[A Non-Linear Neuron Based Detection of Isolated Pixels in Binary and Grayscale Images using Contrast Sensitive Receptive Fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18399)** — Threshold-free detector built on excitatory/inhibitory receptive fields.

## 10. Aerial, Underwater, Field & Soft Robotics

**[An Efficient Algorithm for Minimum-Pressure Growth Planning of Vine Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18070)** — Derives a growth-pressure equation, reduces planning to a time-dependent shortest path, and guarantees global optimality in 2D with an open-source VinePlanner package.

**[CLASP: A Cluster-Level Autonomous Selective Picking Robot with a Soft Rolling-Band Gripper for Fresh-Market Blueberry Harvesting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18051)** — Compliant rolling bands with closed-loop pulling-force regulation keep load below the immature detachment threshold; 23/25 cluster grasps at ~$3,326 per unit.

**[Volumetric Harmonic Field Navigation for Quadrotors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15680)** — Couples a precomputed harmonic field to a constrained predictive planner; first physical quadrotor demonstration, with better clearance and lower jerk than Dijkstra guidance.

**[VLM-MPPI: Grounding Natural Language in Behaviorally Diverse Trajectories for Aerial Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18451)** — Six behavior-conditioned MPPI planners produce intentionally diverse candidates projected to FPV view; a VLM asynchronously selects among them at 20 Hz replanning.

**[Real-Time Bounded Catenary Solver for UAV Tether Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18482)** — Reformulates the catenary BVP into one well-conditioned transcendental equation with proven convergence; ~6.9 µs average solve and a Lua implementation running in ArduPilot.
