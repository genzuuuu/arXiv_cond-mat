# arXiv Robotics & AI Digest — Embodied Intelligence & Manipulation Focus

## Dexterous Manipulation & Grasping

**[MANGO-Grasp: Mahalanobis Fields over Geometry-Oriented 3D Gaussians for Cross-Embodiment Dexterous Grasping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02014)**  
Presents an anisotropic interaction framework for cross-embodiment dexterous grasping. Represents objects as geometry-oriented 3D Gaussian primitives and robot hands via morpho-kinematic surface keypoint descriptors. Mahalanobis fields over keypoint–primitive pairs encode directional contact structure. Achieves up to +16.57 points over zero-shot baselines on unseen hands and 86% real-world success.

**[GraspMeanFlow: SE(3)-Equivariant MeanFlow for Few-Step 6-DoF Grasp Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03295)**  
Introduces an SE(3)-equivariant flow-matching framework for fast 6-DoF grasp generation. Learns average velocity over finite time intervals via time-ordered exponentials, enabling single-step sampling that matches a 5-step iterative flow model on ACRONYM. Improves grasp success by up to 24.3 points in the few-step regime.

**[GraRe: Grasp Candidate Re-Ranking for Frozen 6-DoF Grasp Detectors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00946)**  
Shows that detector confidence is often poorly aligned with actual grasp quality on GraspNet-1Billion. Proposes a re-ranking module (GraRe) that estimates quality from candidate attributes, local geometry, and object context, improving Average AP by up to 13.60 points without changing the detector.

**[DexMani: Human-Derived Manipulability Guidance for Dexterous Rotation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00554)**  
Transfers human demonstrations as contact-conditioned manipulability evolution to guide RL for dexterous object rotation. The prior captures how contact transitions reshape available rotation directions. Achieves highest success rates across Shadow Hand, Allegro Hand, XHand, and LEAP Hand for seen and unseen objects.

**[Grasp Execution Without a Planner: Configuration-Space Grasp Distance Fields with Certified Safety & Guaranteed Quality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00600)**  
Proposes Grasp Distance Fields (GDFs) — smooth softmin distance fields over arm-hand configuration space — allowing direct gradient-following grasp execution without a planner. Integrates CBF-CLF filtering for safety, achieving 46/50 successful grasps with median 94% quality margin retention.

## Tactile Sensing & Haptic Feedback

**[MVP-Tac: A Miniaturized Dual-Modal Vision and Photoelastic Tactile Sensor for Robot-Assisted Minimally Invasive Surgery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18660)**  
Presents a compact vision-based tactile sensor using reflective photoelastic imaging for co-located vision and touch. Achieves 97% accuracy for exposed-tumor and 92% for subdermal-tumor classification via palpation. Includes vision-guided 3D photomapping in simulated colonoscopy.

**[Semantic Haptic Feedback Enhances Dexterous Robotic Teleoperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02780)**  
Introduces semantic haptics — abstract haptic patterns conveying robot state information ("Confirmations" vs. "Exceptions") through pneumatic/vibrotactile wristbands. In bimanual tasks, semantic haptics outperform sensory haptics and visual feedback with reduced workload and increased situational awareness.

**[A Wearable Stiffness-Rendering Haptic Device with a Honeycomb Jamming Mechanism for Bilateral Teleoperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03002)**  
A 20g wearable haptic device (HJ-Haptic) using honeycomb jamming to render object stiffness (1.15–2.64 N/mm) via vacuum pressure. Demonstrates effective stiffness feedback for teleoperated grasping with strong correlations in motion, stiffness rendering, and interaction force.

**[Stiffness Copilot: An Impedance Policy for Contact-Rich Teleoperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14068)**  
A vision-based shared-control policy that adjusts robot impedance online during teleoperation. Infers direction-dependent stiffness matrices in simulation, then transfers zero-shot to real images. Achieves safety comparable to constant low stiffness while matching the efficiency of constant high stiffness.

**[Electrostatic Clutch-Based Mechanical Multiplexer with Increased Force Capability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08469)**  
Presents an electrostatic capstan clutch-based transmission enabling high-force mechanical multiplexing — one motor controlling multiple outputs. Demonstrates 212 N individual output forces and a 4.09× increase in vertical grip strength on a tendon-driven hand.

## Visuomotor Policies & VLA Models

**[How Should Vision-Language-Action Models Use Proprioceptive State?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03052)**  
Systematically evaluates five interfaces for wiring proprioceptive state into VLA models on 45 atomic + 20 composite tasks. Distills design principles for when state history helps, and where state should enter (VLM backbone vs. action head), finding state history matters for tasks with genuine temporal variation.

**[Track4Action: Distilling World-Centric 3D Tracker into Vision-Language-Action Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03727)**  
Distills realized 3D transitions from a frozen world-centric tracker into VLA policies as privileged supervision. Reaches 82.3% on zero-shot LIBERO-Plus (+7.6 over alignment-free baseline) and 67.5% on physical bimanual tasks (+25.0 points), with no tracker needed at deployment.

**[Unified Visuomotor Targets: Supervising VLAs Beyond Physical Actions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03563)**  
Proposes UVT, a unified latent prediction target encoding both motor control and visual scene transition information. Requires no architectural changes and no additional data, improving training efficiency, final performance, and robustness, particularly under limited budgets.

**[When Prompts Control Robots: Prompt Injection Attacks in Multi-Agent Robotic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00747)**  
First systematic study of prompt injection attacks in multi-agent LLM-based robotic systems, showing attacks propagate across agents through shared prompt structures. Reveals architectural choices significantly affect attack success.

**[VLAGuard / Structure-Aware Robust Fine-Tuning Against Physical Attention Hijacking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03231)**  
Introduces AGSD, a printable-patch attack that induces policy-critical attention hijacking in VLA policies, and SARF, a structure-aware defense. SARF reduces OpenVLA's failure rate under attack from 100% to 14.2–56.8% in LIBERO while preserving clean performance.

## Teleoperation & Human-in-the-Loop

**[Freeform Preference Learning for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.32027)**  
Instead of binary trajectory preferences, FPL lets humans define natural-language preference axes (safety, speed, carefulness) with pairwise preferences along each axis. Learns a language-conditioned reward model, improving over sparse and binary-preference methods by 38 points and enabling test-time behavior steering.

**[EvoHIL: Self-Evolving Reward and Flow-Matched Policy Optimization for Robust Human-in-the-Loop RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03872)**  
A unified HIL-RL framework jointly adapting the reward model, action generator, and visual domain. Uses self-evolving success classifiers, flow-matched action stabilization, and retention-aware offline fine-tuning. Improves success, motion smoothness, and completion time across six tasks on Franka and SO-101 arms.

**[Mixed-Initiative Human-Robot Teaming under Suboptimality with Online Bayesian Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.16178)**  
Online Bayesian framework enabling robots to infer human willingness to comply with assistance in sequential decision-making games. Enhances objective team performance (p<.001), trust, and willingness to comply, even when both human and agent act suboptimally.

## World Models & Manipulation Prediction

**[EmbodiedVAE: Disentangled Video VAE for Efficient and Controllable Embodied Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02990)**  
A dual-encoder, single-decoder video VAE with asymmetric spatio-temporal compression that disentangles robot arm motion from background. Introduces optimal-transport-based consistency for temporal coherence. Achieves ~2dB PSNR improvement over SOTA video VAEs with higher compression.

**[LiLa-WAM: Lightweight Latent Reasoning World-Action Model for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03701)**  
A lightweight world-action model with a compact latent reasoning space jointly shaped by future-state prediction and action generation. Uses language-free Visual Transition Tokens for task specification. Trains end-to-end on a single 24GB GPU, achieving 90.48% success across 50 RoboTwin tasks.

**[GEM-4D: Geometry-Enhanced Video World Models for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22882)**  
Injects dense 4D correspondence supervision from a pretrained geometry foundation model into a video generative backbone. Includes an inverse dynamics module converting correspondence-consistent rollouts into executable trajectories. Improves real-world manipulation success from 61% to 81%.

**[Faster-WAM: Do World Action Models Need Deep Action Modules?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02365)**  
Introduces Dock of Transformer (DoT), a video-centric design principle docking a single-layer action head onto a 30-layer video backbone. Achieves competitive performance with 3.2× latency reduction (66.5 ms/inference) and strong out-of-distribution generalization.

## Sim-to-Real & Embodiment Adaptation

**[Rapid Embodiment Adaptation for Quadrupedal Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01506)**  
Online embodiment adaptation framework inferring physical changes (joint-range constraints, trunk mass) from short interaction histories within half a second. Maintains stable locomotion on a real Unitree Go2 under fully locked leg and 5kg payload, where non-adaptive methods fail.

**[KING: Embodiment-Aware Kinematic Graph Neural Network for Unified Motion Representation of Legged and Wheeled Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01015)**  
A GNN-based kinematic model explicitly incorporating robot embodiments as a unified graph. Trained across diverse embodiments, achieves high-accuracy odometry using only URDF + proprioception, and adapts to new robots via few-shot learning with one minute of data.

**[RobotDancing: Residual-Action RL for Robust Long-Horizon Humanoid Motion Tracking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20717)**  
Single-stage RL recipe predicting reference-conditioned residual joint targets for humanoid motion tracking. Executes long-horizon LAFAN1 dance motions on Unitree G1 hardware without test-time modifications, with cross-platform feasibility shown on H1/H1-2.

**[Contact-Implicit Multiple Shooting for Dynamic Motion Retargeting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03116)**  
Transforms kinematically feasible references into dynamically feasible whole-body trajectories via contact-implicit, direct simulation-based multiple shooting. Accelerates motion-imitation RL training and demonstrates zero-shot sim-to-real transfer on Unitree G1 for crawling and 180° jump-turn.

## Skill Learning & Hierarchical Manipulation

**[RoboReact: Agentic Skill Distillation from Generated Egocentric Videos for Generalizable Whole-Body Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03387)**  
Automatically synthesizes whole-body manipulation skills from a single egocentric RGB-D observation. Generates human manipulation videos, extracts geometry-preserving keyframes via depth-aware 3D reconstruction, and retargets them to high-DoF humanoids. Generalizes across object configurations without teleoperation or human demonstrations.

**[Hierarchical Approach to Imitation Learning for Manipulation Tasks Requiring Time-Varying Forces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03103)**  
DPA-FTG decouples low-frequency planning (5 Hz, diffusion policy over task primitives) from high-frequency force regulation (60 Hz, neural impedance controller). Outperforms Reactive Diffusion Policy on bimanual battery disassembly with compliant sheet separation.

**[GORDON: Graph-based Object-centric Rewards for Decomposition of Long-Horizon Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03753)**  
Learns dense rewards from action-free video demonstrations by representing scenes as object-relation graphs embedded in a task-aligned latent space. Enables automatic subtask discovery without manual segmentation, averaging +35pp improvement over learned baselines on long-horizon tasks.

## Motion Planning & Control

**[Biconvex Optimization for Smooth Minimum-Time Trajectories around Convex Obstacles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02834)**  
Guaranteed-convergent, anytime biconvex approach for minimum-time motion planning with derivative constraints to arbitrary order. Alternates between maximum-margin separating planes and trajectory optimization. Handles drone navigation and dual-arm bin unloading robustly.

**[Control Barrier Functions via Minkowski Operations for Safe Navigation among Polytopes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02886)**  
Exact Signed Distance Function formulation for polytopic robot-obstacle pairs with nonsmooth CBFs via Minkowski operations. Derives unified analytical gradient expressions, enabling non-conservative maneuvers and safety recovery from unsafe initialization.

**[CUDA MPC: A GPU-Native Solver for Model Predictive Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03051)**  
GPU-native MPC framework with parallel-in-horizon ADMM and fused CUDA kernels. Sustains real-time control at horizons 1–2 orders of magnitude longer than CPU solvers; up to 965× faster than tensor-framework implementations.
