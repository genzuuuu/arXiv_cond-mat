# Robotics & Embodied AI Papers — Daily Digest

## Tactile Sensing & Haptic Systems

**[The Potential of Haptic Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28664)**  
Wang et al. — Discusses the path toward Haptic Foundation Models (HFMs), covering hardware heterogeneity, data collection challenges, and benchmarks comparing UniTouch, AnyTouch, T3, and Sparsh on TacBench for force estimation and slip detection.

**[SpectraTac: A Compact Camera-Free Optical Tactile Sensor with Distributed Color Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30368)**  
Wu et al. — A 19mm diameter, camera-free tactile sensor using RGB illumination and distributed color sensing. Achieves ~0.16–0.43 N force MAEs and 99.9% contact classification accuracy, offering a low-cost (<$5) alternative to camera-based tactile sensing.

**[\(\mathcal{N}_0\)-Foundation: Towards the Age of Tactile Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29601)**  
NeoteAI Team — Large-scale tactile framework with 30,000+ hours of visuo-tactile data (NeoData), a tactile representation model (NeoForce), and the NeoReal/NeoSim benchmarking suites for tactile-enabled manipulation.

**[Blind Dexterity: Whole-Body Humanoid Manipulation via Pure Proprioception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29487)**  
Bhatt et al. — Demonstrates whole-body manipulation on a Unitree G1 using only joint encoders (no cameras or tactile sensors), arguing joint-encoder readouts under compliant contact function as a whole-body tactile channel.

**[GhostTac: Manipulating Tactile Sensors without Physical Contact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20817)**  
Wang et al. — First contactless attack on tactile sensors via electromagnetic interference, demonstrating that crafted EMI can induce false force readings in 15 tactile sensors across dexterous hands, potentially causing unsafe robot behavior.

## Dexterous Manipulation & Hands

**[Motus2: A Self-Evolving General World Model for Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30237)**  
Bi et al. — A single model exposes policy, simulator, and evaluator interfaces for closed-loop dexterous manipulation, trained on large-scale egocentric data with tactile feedback and dual dexterous hands.

**[DREAM: Deployment-Time Demonstration Generation via Real-to-Sim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29078)**  
Sato et al. — Generates fine-tuning data for pretrained VLAs from a captured workspace without human demonstration, using real-to-sim reconstruction plus task-and-motion planning; studied for scalable adaptation to new workspaces.

**[A Kinematic Framework for Screening Candidate Pinch Configurations in Robotic Hand Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20692)**  
Kang et al. — Design-stage evaluation of robotic hand pinch capability using only fingertip workspace relationships, requiring no object or contact models.

**[Cross-Modal Visuo-Tactile Representation Learning with ACT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00514)**  
Liu et al. — CLIP-style visuo-tactile alignment improves ACT policy performance from 30% (vision-only) to 54% on contact-rich tasks, with a low-cost visuo-tactile gripper (LVTG).

**[AnyWorld: Factorized Egocentric World Models for Cross-Embodiment Generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29242)**  
Chen et al. — Expands single human interactions into diverse robot-native rollouts via factorized action/camera/embodiment modeling. Improves RoboCasa GR1 benchmark performance and enables targeted policy-gap correction from human video.

## Visuomotor Policies, VLA Models & Action Generation

**[SMILE: Smooth Motion for Improved Long-Horizon VLA Execution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29432)**  
Park et al. — Predicts B-spline coefficients instead of raw action chunks; improves accuracy and inference efficiency across SmolVLA, Evo1, VPP, and DAWN on LIBERO and CALVIN.

**[DriftingVLA: Native One-Step VLA Generation via Per-Dimension Temporal Drifting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29749)**  
Gao et al. — One-step action chunk generation with a per-dimension drifting objective; 98.32% on LIBERO and 3.36× speedup in action generation versus multi-step flow policies.

**[AdaVLA: Adaptive Step Flow Matching for Training-Free Acceleration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29208)**  
Han et al. — Dynamically reduces flow-matching inference steps and prunes MLPs using a curvature-derived confidence metric. Achieves 1.87–2.24× speedups on Jetson AGX Orin with negligible success-rate loss.

**[CometVLA: Co-Training on an Embodied Data Pyramid](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30289)**  
Wan et al. — Introduces embodied physical VQA data (CometData/CometBench) aligned with robot action domains, plus Global Action Prior (GAP) tokens that let action heads consume physical commonsense without corrupting the VLM backbone.

**[Temporal Forcing: 4D Representation Alignment for VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30643)**  
Ding et al. — Aligns VLA representations with a pretrained 4D foundation model to capture evolving 3D scenes over time. Reaches 98.8% on LIBERO and improves physical hidden-placement tasks from 20% to 43.3%.

**[Zeva: In-Context Causal Learning for Generalizable Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30880)**  
Chen et al. — Enables frozen policies to learn from their own physical interactions during deployment by storing causal interaction signals in dual-timescale memory and injecting retrieved signal as context.

**[CorrectVLA: Training-Free Action Correction via Language Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29967)**  
Kwon et al. — Translates human corrections into additive action-magnitude adjustments without retraining. Corrects execution-misalignment failures while remaining ineffective when semantic comprehension itself fails.

**[PredVLA: Predictive Sensorimotor Modeling for Sub-Million-Parameter Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26673)**  
Sawada & Kasahara — A 0.68M-parameter predictive-coding policy with hierarchical recurrent dynamics reaches 86.9% on three LIBERO suites—3.7–7.4× better than parameter-matched behavior-cloning baselines.

## Teleoperation & HRI

**[GHOST in the Robots: Real-Time Exocentric Dual-Robot VR Teleoperation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29080)**  
Wei et al. — Single-operator VR control of two mobile manipulators via onboard cameras with point-cloud completion. Novice success rates are 1.6–4× higher than a tablet interface across nine tasks.

**[RoboGesture: Semantic-aligned Co-Speech Gestures for Humanoid Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28693)**  
Wang et al. — Co-designed data, modeling, and control pipeline for real-time gesture generation on a Unitree G1, with collision-free MPC-based safety filtering.

## Sim-to-Real & World Models

**[Task-Relevant Feature-Dynamics Fidelity Enables Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Transfer for Robotic Ultrasound](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29516)**  
Qian et al. — Formalizes task-relevant feature-dynamics fidelity (TR-FDF) and builds a simulator that reliably transfers to physical systems: 390/400 zero-shot successes across four target planes.

**[Hydra: Navigation World Action Model with Discrete Latent Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28995)**  
Nazeri et al. — Unifies visual states, poses, and actions in a shared latent manifold compressed through VQ bottlenecks. Discrete latent planning plus conditional flow-matching execution outperforms leading world models on physical robots.

**[DELE-w0.5: Inferring Action from Future Latent State](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22067)**  
Lei et al. — Removes video generation from world-action modeling entirely: predicts the future latent state directly and infers actions. Gains +32.5 pp full-task success over the strongest baseline.

**[PAVE: Predictive Alignment and Value-Guided Evolution for World-Action Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30378)**  
Zhao et al. — Combines JEPA-style multi-horizon predictive targets with value-critic-gated flow-matching training so that deployment trajectories teach dynamics while only better actions are distilled.

## Long-Horizon, Skill-Level Benchmarks & Evaluation

**[Behavior-Skill: Fine-Grained Long-Horizon Evaluation via Constituent Skills](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30536)**  
Ma et al. — Benchmarks VLA policies across 235K skill instances from 50 household tasks; reveals contact-rich manipulation skills as persistent failure bottlenecks that full-task metrics mask.

**[RoboTrustBench: Trustworthiness of Video World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01600)**  
Li et al. — Evaluates world models on normal, constraint-sensitive, counterfactual, and adversarial instruction scenarios. Models often produce visually coherent but physically/constraint-invalid rollouts.

**[RoboPhys-3D: Comprehensive Embodied World Model Evaluation via 3D Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28718)**  
Wang et al. — 50 manipulation tasks with 50 metrics across 4 levels (pixel → state → task). Cosmos 3 leads, while state- and execution-grounded metrics reveal failures hidden by perceptual similarity scores.

Sun et al. — Combines a Fourier-encoded query reconstructor with trajectory-aware distillation for perceptive humanoid walking; 97.5% traversal success on stress tests and zero-shot 1.5 km outdoor deployment.

**[Learning Agile Perceptive Traversal of Sparse 3D Structures for Humanoids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29769)**  
Ongan et al. — RL-based monkey-bar brachiation on a humanoid using raw lidar with attention-based encoding, achieving 14/15 trials and brachiation speeds up to 0.5 m/s.
