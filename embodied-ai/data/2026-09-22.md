### **Dexterous Hands, Grasping, and In-Hand Manipulation**

This group of papers focuses on the hardware and control of robotic hands, from new mechanical designs for in-hand assembly to learning-based methods for robust grasping.

- **A Reconfigurable Dual-Opposition Architecture for Single-Hand Assembly and Manipulation** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22871)): Introduces a reconfigurable hand with a palm and four fingers that can form two "virtual oppositions," enabling it to grasp and manipulate two separate objects for in-hand assembly.
- **GraspTune: Tactile-Driven Execution Refinement for Robust Grasping** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24180)): Presents a framework that uses tactile feedback to refine a visually proposed grasp. It applies small corrections during the approach and contact phases, significantly improving the success rate of stable grasps on a real UR5e robot.
- **Steerable and Reactive Grasping Through Modular Design with a Three-Point Interface** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24896)): Proposes a modular system where a heatmap suggests contact points for a grasp, a controller guides the hand to them, and a final RL policy uses only proprioception to stabilize the grasp, avoiding the need for object-specific training.
- **A Direct Rigid Transmission 2-DoF Wrist Extension for Tendon-Driven Hand** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22681)): Details the design of a 2-DoF wrist extension for tendon-driven hands, improving dexterous manipulation in confined spaces by providing local control of hand orientation.
- **Connectivity-Aware Exploration of Robotic Grasp Spaces** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22983)): Explores the structure of successful grasp poses in SE(3), finding that they form distinct clusters. It introduces a sampling strategy that exploits this connectivity for more efficient grasp exploration than random sampling.
- **GraRe: Grasp Candidate Re-Ranking for Frozen 6-DoF Grasp Detectors** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00946)): Addresses the issue that a grasp detector's confidence is often poorly aligned with actual success. It proposes a method to re-rank candidates from a frozen detector using a learned quality score, improving performance by up to 13.56 points in Average AP.
- **2nd Place Solution to the HANDS 2026 Workshop Challenge-Dexterous Grasp Motion Track: Single-Shot Trajectory Warping for Grasp Motion Generation** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21511)): Describes a winning entry for a grasp motion challenge. Instead of generating a trajectory step-by-step, it learns a one-shot warp of a single successful demonstration, achieving a 94.61% success rate on the easy track.

### **Tactile Sensing and Visuo-Tactile Policies**

These papers highlight the increasing importance of tactile sensing for contact-rich manipulation, covering representation learning, new sensor designs, and policies that fuse touch with vision.

- **Tactile-JEPA: Topology-Aware Self-Supervised Representation Learning for Distributed Tactile Sensors** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24385)): Introduces a self-supervised method for pre-training encoders for distributed tactile skins. By predicting masked sensor readings based on their spatial arrangement, it learns robust representations that reduce force and orientation estimation errors.
- **When Does Touch Matter? Charting the Vision-Interaction Gap in Cluttered Dexterous Grasping** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24068)): A controlled real-world study showing that in cluttered scenes, policies using tactile and wrench feedback greatly outperform vision-only policies (24/25 vs. 14/25 success), as touch helps reject inadequate contacts and prevents premature lifting.
- **TACIT: Tactile Contact Supervision for Spatial Attention in Dexterous Manipulation** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24507)): Uses tactile contact signals from demonstrations to supervise a spatial attention mechanism in a visuotactile policy. This guides the robot's "gaze" to the right place, significantly improving success on placement and insertion tasks.
- **Layered e-skin for Shear Sensing** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22493)): Presents a two-layer force-sensing resistor (FSR) array for fingertips that can estimate three-axis forces, including shear. It can track contact motion and detect slip events, which is crucial for stable manipulation.
- **VT-Bridge: Bridging Pretrained Foundation VLAs to VTLAs via Lightweight Residual Adaptation** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22606)): Proposes a method to efficiently add tactile sensing to a pre-trained VLA model. By training a small residual adapter, it raises the task completion rate from 11.7% to 62.9% on contact-rich tasks with only 50 demonstrations per task.
- **Learning tactile perception from high-bandwidth single-point sensing** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24621)): Introduces SpectRobot, which converts single-point, high-bandwidth vibration signals into spectrograms. This allows standard vision encoders to process them, enabling a robot to solve an occluded manipulation task using only touch.
- **BiView-Touch: Learning Bimanual Tactile Representations by Cross-Hand Completion** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23352)): Explores the relationship between tactile sensing on two hands. It proposes a framework to learn a representation by masking one hand's tactile data and reconstructing it from the other, showing improved performance in low-label settings.

### **Robotic Manipulation Policies (VLA & World Models)**

This section covers new architectures and methods for learning and deploying robotic manipulation policies, including Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and World-Action Models (WAMs).

- **AffordanceWAM: Affordance-Aware Joint World-Action Modeling for Robot Manipulation** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22332)): Introduces a World-Action Model that predicts future scenes and actions using an "affordance" representation (scalar and heatmap). This allows it to learn from diverse human videos without action labels, improving generalization and human-to-robot transfer.
- **DexTacWAM: A Visuo-Tactile World-Action Model for Dexterous Manipulation** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24976)): Presents a WAM that integrates per-fingertip tactile encoding into a video diffusion world model. It predicts future visual and tactile states, improving performance on contact-rich tasks by 70.6 vs. 38.0 for the strongest baseline.
- **Bridge3D: Enabling Vision-Language-Action Models to See and Act in 3D** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24525)): Enhances 2D VLAs with explicit 3D geometry guidance. It fuses features from 3D foundation models (implicit) and uses a 3D semantic field for action denoising (explicit), improving performance on spatial manipulation tasks.
- **ReVeal: A Reconstruction-Aware Real-to-Sim Framework for VLA Policy Evaluation** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23910)): Presents a framework for evaluating VLA policies in a realistic simulated environment. It measures the fidelity of the real-to-sim reconstruction and shows that higher fidelity correlates with a stronger agreement between simulated and real-world policy performance.
- **HapticWAM: Distilling Imagined Touch into a World-Action Model without Inference-Time Tactile Sensing** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23888)): Introduces a teacher-student framework where a teacher model with tactile sensing guides a student that does not use touch at inference. The student learns to "imagine" contact mechanics, achieving a 77% mean success rate on contact-rich tasks.
- **ForceRFT: Refining VLA Actions through Force-Guided Residual Reinforcement Learning** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22840)): Presents a framework where a frozen VLA generates a base action, and a lightweight residual actor refines it using real-time wrist force feedback. This is trained with human corrections and autonomous rollouts, improving success on contact-rich tasks like plug insertion.

### **Human-to-Robot Transfer and Learning from Human Videos**

This theme focuses on leveraging human videos and motion for scalable robot learning.

- **EgoWild2Dex: Learning Dexterous Robotic Manipulation from In-the-Wild Human Experience** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23755)): Introduces a method to transfer skills from hours of in-the-wild egocentric video to a dexterous robot. It uses a geometric transformer to align viewpoints and a human-to-robot training scheme, achieving a 96.7% success rate on long-horizon bimanual tasks.
- **Hand-centric Human-to-Robot Trajectory Transfer from Video Demonstrations via Object Category-agnostic Temporal Localization** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10743)): Presents a framework for hand-centric retargeting. It identifies task-relevant contact intervals from stereo videos, using them as anchors to transfer human grasps into executable robot trajectories.
- **3PoinTr: From Human Videos to Robot Policies with 3D Point-Track Plans** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08485)): Introduces a method that predicts dense 3D point tracks from a human video to create a global plan. A robot policy then uses this plan to generate actions, transferring well even when human motions are infeasible for the robot.
- **Scaling Sim-to-Real VLA Reinforcement Learning with Generative 3D Worlds** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18532)): Demonstrates that VLAs can be RL fine-tuned in simulation using diverse scenes generated by 3D world models. This approach improves simulation success from 9.7% to 79.8% and transfers well to the real world, boosting success from 21.7% to 75%.
- **Dexterous Robot Manipulation from Human Demonstrations via Contact-Anchored Retargeting and Residual Policy Learning** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24093)): Proposes a pipeline to convert human motion capture to dexterous robot policies without real-robot training data. It uses contact-anchored retargeting to transfer contact structure and a residual RL policy to adapt to the robot's actuation, achieving significant success rate boosts on physical hardware.

### **Sim-to-Real and World Models**

This section highlights papers focused on bridging the simulation-to-reality gap and using world models for planning and control.

- **InsertAnything: Generalizable Contact-Rich Precision Insertion from Simulation to Reality** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24511)): Presents a reinforcement learning framework that trains an insertion policy entirely in simulation. The policy, using force feedback, generalizes to real-world tasks with tight clearances (0.02 mm), achieving a perfect score on a benchmark and a 95% success rate on unseen tasks.
- **Sim2Real Diffusion: Leveraging Foundation Vision Language Models for Adaptive Automated Driving** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00236)): Presents a framework for sim-to-real transfer in autonomous driving. It uses conditional latent diffusion with a VLM to bridge the perceptual gap (by over 40%) between simulated and real-world driving scenes.
- **ARSTAG: An Agentic Real2Sim2Real System for Task-Specific Robot Data Generation** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24563)): Describes an agentic system that takes a single RGB image and a language instruction to generate task-specific data in simulation. This data is then used to train a real-world policy, achieving a 74.6% average success rate for a dual-arm robot on tasks like grasping and stacking.
- **RopeFormer: Cross-Trial Adaptation from Interaction History for Dynamic Rope Manipulation** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23432)): Introduces a history-conditioned policy for dynamic rope manipulation. By using prior interactions as context, the policy adapts to different rope dynamics without explicit online estimation, significantly reducing target-acquisition time on a Unitree H1-2.
- **EmbodiedDiffusion: End-to-End Traversability-Guided Visual Diffusion for Heterogeneous Robot Navigation** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02851)): Proposes a diffusion-based framework that predicts traversability maps and generates trajectories from RGB images. It achieves 80-100% navigation success in indoor environments with real-time inference (90 ms) and adapts to new platforms with only 10 minutes of new data.
- **ReVeal: A Reconstruction-Aware Real-to-Sim Framework for VLA Policy Evaluation** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23910)): This paper, also listed under VLA policies, is highly relevant here. It provides a framework to assess the fidelity of real-to-sim reconstruction and shows that higher fidelity is linked to better real-sim performance agreement for VLA policies.
- **EmbodiedDiffusion: End-to-End Traversability-Guided Visual Diffusion for Heterogeneous Robot Navigation** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02851)): (Note: This was also listed under general robotics) This paper presents a diffusion model to predict traversability and generate trajectories, achieving high success rates in sim and adapting to new robots with minimal data.

### **Other Relevant Themes (Humanoids, Teleoperation, Force Control)**

- **CHOREO: Every Humanoid Skill as a Trajectory** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22274)): Introduces a framework for training-free composition of humanoid skills. It represents any skill as a unified executable motion trajectory (SkillMotion), enabling the combination of pre-trained skills for complex, multi-action tasks with a 95.4% success rate.
- **Whole-Body UMI: Transferring UMI Manipulation Skills to Humanoid Whole-Body Manipulation via Real-Time Motion Generation** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22829)): Presents a framework to transfer manipulation skills from handheld UMI data to a humanoid's whole-body control. It uses a real-time motion generator to decouple coordination learning from task semantics, achieving success on tasks like drawer closing and shelf pick-and-place.
- **Simultaneous Forward and Inverse Human-in-the-Loop Optimization** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22630)): Proposes a method for human-in-the-loop optimization that efficiently infers an individual's reward function from preferences and finds a control policy maximizing it. This is applicable to assistive robotics and teleoperation.
- **Opt2VLA: Force-Aware Vision-Language-Action for Contact-Rich Humanoid Whole-Body Manipulation** ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23968)): Introduces a force-aware VLA framework for humanoids that predicts both geometric motion goals and continuous contact-force references. It uses trajectory optimization to generate physically grounded training data and improves force regulation on hardware.
