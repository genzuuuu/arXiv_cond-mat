# arXiv Robotics & AI Papers — Embodied Intelligence Focus

## Dexterous Manipulation & Multi-Finger Grasping

**[DemoMimic: One Demonstration, Many Objects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01938)** — Satvik Sharma et al. — Proposes DemoMimic (Dexterous Motion Mimic), a policy that focuses on object geometry local to contact points. Contact-centric rewards improve sim-to-real transfer, achieving 71% success on 16 objects across 4 tasks and 2 robot-hand embodiments. Demonstrates the smallest sim-to-real drop vs baselines.

**[RoboTok: Internet-Scale Data Engine for Human Demonstration Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03199)** — Howard Qian et al. — Retrieves manipulation-relevant human demonstrations from web videos for dexterous robot policies. Learns a latent motion space from 3D hand trajectories in actor-centered frames; retrieval improves downstream task success, making web video a scalable supervision source.

**[ARTiS: Adaptive Robotic Gripper for Tool Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03362)** — Roman Mykhailyshyn et al. — Novel gripper combining soft-gripper adaptability, anthropomorphic hand dexterity, and rigid mechanism robustness for disassembly applications. Uses active jamming in palm + fin-ray fingertips with 7-DOF dexterity.

**[AdaRoboVLG: Adaptive Vision-Language Grasping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04096)** — Sixu Yan et al. — Framework decoupling task-dependent understanding from grasp synthesis. Base policy learns generalizable grasp candidates via kinematic mapping and force-closure stability; composable foundation-model priors enable task-adaptive grasping without retraining.

## Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models & Policy Learning

**[Evidence-Gated Regularization for Robust VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03142)** — Yue Yang et al. — Addresses "modality entanglement"—spurious inter-sensor correlations in VLA policies. EGR gates consistency objectives by per-sensor task relevance. Improves success rates on BEHAVIOR-1K (+31%) and real-robot setups (up to +183%), including bimanual Kinova arms with tactile sensing.

**[MINERVA: Small Manipulation Policy on LIBERO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03715)** — Kohei Sendai et al. — A 0.54M-parameter policy achieves 95.1% on LIBERO (only 2.4 pts below π₀.₅ with 7,700× fewer params). Task-ID permutation probe shows LIBERO instruction conditioning primarily selects among memorized tasks (execution in 5–9ms on laptop CPU).

**[GIFT: Guided Intermediate Feature Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04193)** — Yupeng Zheng et al. — Trains intermediate features to preserve geometry, affordance, and goal-region structure via alignment objectives. Improves VLA and world-action models on LIBERO-Plus (up to +12.6 pts) and RoboCasa, with large gains on articulated objects and high-precision real manipulation.

**[FWBC-VLA: Force-Aware Whole-Body Compensation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03889)** — Yutian Zhang et al. — Bridges VLA action generation with whole-body control for wheeled-legged loco-manipulation. Uses sensorless residual-torque estimation for contact state, injected into VLA action decoding. Validated on whiteboard wiping and door-opening with door closer.

## World Models & Planning

**[WISE: World-model-guided Imagination Scheduling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03681)** — Chenhao Zhang et al. — Schedules world-model imagination only at interaction-relevant states with bounded multi-view rollouts. Coordinates when and how imagination is used during VLA post-training; consistent improvements with π₀ and π₀.₅ while cutting GPU time ~80%.

**[Physically Grounded JEPA World Models for Robotic Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03565)** — Muyuan Liu et al. — Augments latent prediction with inverse dynamics + state alignment to ground representations in physical configurations. Top success rates on TwoRoom (100%), PushT (98%), OGBench-Cube (87%), with state alignment improving planning over IDM alone.

**[TrAct: Visual Tracks as World Model Interface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24101)** — Zhi Cao et al. — Uses visual tracks (embodiment-agnostic point trajectories) as intermediate interface between control and prediction. Improves success from 27%→55% in simulation and 49%→76% on real Franka tasks vs π₀.₅, plus better video prediction quality.

**[Rethinking World Models for Safety-Critical Embodied Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03774)** — Kailang Ma et al. — Proposes Risk-Informed World Model (RIWM): prediction vs intervention, finite-horizon prediction vs accumulated consequences. Defines decision-centric direction with representation, counterfactual reasoning, episodic memory, and runtime assurance.

## Bimanual & Contact-Rich Manipulation

**[Scaling Bimanual Manipulation: 1,500 Hours of Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03591)** — Jiafeng Xu et al. — Releases 1,500 hours of bimanual household demonstrations; trains XR-2 VLA model with multi-stage paradigm. Shows scaling trends for both demonstration data volume and DAgger correction data, with dataset open-sourced.

**[FailBench: Reliability of VLMs at Judging Task Success](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03611)** — Zaruhi Navasardyan et al. — 2,197 manipulation attempts across 14 sources; best model achieves only 0.77 balanced accuracy. Models show systematic success-bias under ambiguity. Spatial localization/cropping of outcome regions improves detection by 2.4 pts.

## Tactile Sensing & Perception

**[Vision-Based Tactile Sensing for Compliance/Hardness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12528)** — Muxing Huang et al. — Fuses temporal force sequences with deformation fields from vision-based tactile sensors. Force prediction error 0.06 N within 12 N range; 98.0% Shore hardness classification accuracy (10–80 HA); >98.5% in fruit detection and soft-matter identification.

**[Highly Deformable Proprioceptive Membrane for Real-Time 3D Shape Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13574)** — Guanyu Xu et al. — Optical-waveguide membrane reconstructs 3D geometry from its own deformation at 90 Hz with 1.307 mm average error (up to 25 mm deformation). Robust under occlusion/low illumination, extends to 75% in-plane strain.

## Teleoperation & Human-Robot Interaction

**[R2S-Eval: Robot Evaluation with Real-to-Sim Calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03276)** — Yidi Wang et al. — Combines real-to-sim calibration with VLM preference evaluation to assess execution quality. Reduces repeated hardware trials while producing stable policy rankings that agree with human preferences; reveals quality differences beyond binary success labels.

**[MoMaStage: Skill-State Graph Guided Planning for Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08383)** — Chenxu Li et al. — Long-horizon mobile manipulation with a Skill-State Graph (SSG) constraining VLM planning. Propagates scene-region and gripper-occupancy states to reject infeasible plans; event-driven monitor triggers graph-grounded repair.

## Sim-to-Real & Datasets

**[XR-2 Bimanual Dataset: 1,500 hours](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03591)** — *(see above under Bimanual)*

**[DARP: Dual-Arm RGB-D-IR Dataset for Multi-View Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31002)** — Manish Kansana et al. — Two independently moving eye-in-hand manipulators; 10 objects with calibrated multi-view recordings. Median point-to-mesh distance 2.13 mm, demonstrating geometric consistency for multi-view robotic perception.

## Quadruped & Legged Locomotion

**[SPARC: Spine for Faster Quadrupedal Bounding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01984)** — Yue Wang, Yanran Xu — Sagittal spine with revolute+prismatic compliance and task-space tunable stiffness/damping. Impedance-controlled SPARC reaches 1.029 m/s vs 0.673 m/s for rigid spine across 97 bounding trials; reveals energetic trade-offs.

**[QLAUN: 3D-Printed Torque-Controlled Quadruped](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03623)** — Mohamad S. Moudallal, Noel J. Maalouf — Research-oriented 15 kg quad with 12-DOF, fully 3D-printed quasi-direct-drive actuators, modular interchangeable legs. Addresses cost barriers for robotics research in Lebanon/MENA region.

**[DogLegs: Leg-Mounted IMUs for Legged State Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04580)** — Yibin Wu et al. — EKF fusing body-IMU, joint encoders, and multiple leg-mounted IMUs. Leg-IMUs detect foot contact for zero-velocity updates; relative position constraints from leg kinematics reduce drift across terrains.

**[MulDP: Multimodal Diffusion Policy for Quadruped Parkour](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03984)** — Kangmai Hu et al. — Diffusion policy integrating vision + proprioception + goal for autonomous parkour navigation. Introduces first Quadruped Parkour Navigation Dataset (QPND). Validated in simulation and real-world complex terrains.

## Robot Design & Body Morphology

**[BRIDGE: Morphology-Control Co-Design for Humanoids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03497)** — Jianren Wang et al. — Data-driven co-design framework optimizing humanoid morphology for human-like movement. Realized in Bridge: an 88cm open-source humanoid with superior motion-capture fidelity, locomotion, balance, and dynamic maneuvers.
