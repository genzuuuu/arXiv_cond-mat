# arXiv Robotics & AI Papers — Robotics and AI Feeds Summary

## Dexterous Manipulation & Human Demonstrations

**[AdvDex: Learning Dexterous Manipulation from Human Demonstrations via Joint-Aligned Actions and Adversarial Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14028)**  
Introduces AdvDex, a unified VLA framework for dexterous manipulation trained on human and robot demonstrations. Proposes OmniShare (multimodal human demo dataset), JAAS (canonical SE(3)+15-joint action space aligning human hands and robot grippers), and domain-adversarial learning to reduce embodiment bias. Demonstrates zero-shot human-to-robot transfer, cross-embodiment generalization, and few-shot adaptation.

**[Learning Dexterous Manipulation Using Contact Wrench Guidance From Human Demonstration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00033)**  
Presents CHORD, a RL framework for long-horizon dexterous manipulation guided by object-centric contact wrench space from human demos. Scales to 4,739 bimanual tasks (82% average success on 1,831 evaluated tasks, 90.77% for whole-body), with sim-to-real transfer in both open-loop and closed-loop settings.

## Visuomotor Policies & VLA Models

**[BICPO-VLA: Behavior-Identified Continuation Preference Optimization for Smooth Asynchronous Vision-Language-Action Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13924)**  
Addresses the request-to-handoff gap in VLA control with three components: instruction-aware causal history encoding, sequential Haar subspace action generation, and behavior-matched Flow-DPO for smooth continuation.

**[Reflex: Enabling Fast and Predictive Vision-Language-Action Models for Reaction-Critical Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14379)**  
Introduces ReflexBench for dynamic manipulation evaluation (6 tasks with configurable latency) and ReflexVLA, an efficient VLA with latent future prediction and CUDA Graph optimization. Demonstrates improved dynamic manipulation performance without large-scale robot-data pretraining.

**[Evolve Vision-Language-Action Model into an Agent with On-the-fly Tool-use](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14047)**  
ART is a tool-injection framework for VLA models enabling on-the-fly tool use for perception, affordance, and embodiment enhancement. Achieves 20% higher success than mainstream baselines on pick-and-place tasks with 30K trajectories (much smaller than typical VLA datasets).

**[AtomBridge: Agentic VLA Inference Plugin for Long-Horizon Tasks in Scientific Experiments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09430)**  
Plug-and-play inference-time plugin for VLAs that inserts LLM-generated transitional actions between atomic tasks, mitigating skill-chaining gaps from robot-state mismatch. Improves full-sequence success by 10–25% on 8-step composed scientific manipulation tasks.

## Tactile Sensing & Precision Assembly

**[From Reach to Insert: Tactile-Augmented Precision Assembly under Sub-Millimeter Tolerances](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04649)**  
Two-stage IL+RL method for tight-tolerance insertion using tactile group sampling and a tactile critic. Under 0.05mm clearance achieves 67% success while reducing max interaction force by 60% and torque by 44%.

**[Effect of Twisted-Yarn Architecture on Pressure and Proximity Sensing Characteristics of Textile Capacitive Sensors for Robotic Skin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14406)**  
Systematic study of yarn-level architecture effects in textile capacitive sensors for robotic skin. Four-layer twisted design achieves 0.1331 MPa⁻¹ sensitivity, proximity detection to 60mm, and integration into a 4×4 sensing array with robotic-arm real-time detection (403ms latency).

## World Models & Planning

**[hint²: Hierarchical World Models for Inference-Time Temporal Logic Guidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13678)**  
Guides short-horizon policies toward LTL satisfaction using hierarchical world models. High-level models track progress through LTL automata; low-level models provide local safety. Outperforms existing LTL-guided diffusion and steering methods on CALVIN, validated on real UR5e.

**[Ontology-Grounded World Models for Failure Diagnosis and Closed-Loop Repair in Physical AI Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13901)**  
Onto-EV-WM layers ontology-based diagnosis and verification-gated correction above a world model. Evaluated on LIBERO benchmarks with 93.8% corrected-window success; addresses unmet task predicates with typed records.

**[Traj-LeWM: Path-Aware World-Model Planning via Latent Trajectory Cost](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14125)**  
Extends LeWM with goal-conditioned latent trajectory cost (LTC) for better candidate action ranking. Improves Push-T, OGBench-Cube, Reacher, and Two-Room by 3–14 percentage points via trajectory-level representation shaping.

**[Event-Conditioned Diagnostics of Kinematic, Contact, and Object-Permanence Fields in Passive Object-State World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28455)**  
Diagnostic protocol for analyzing event-dependent latent structure in object-state world models. Shows event-conditioned reweighting of kinematic/contact/object-permanence fields with causal effects on prediction.

**[Marionette: Predicting World States, Rendering Geometry, Painting Appearance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14530)**  
World model decomposing into explicit world-state prediction, zero-parameter geometric rendering, and neural appearance synthesis. Enables direct state-space control and repair (e.g., terrain colliders reduce ground penetration by 66%).

## Locomotion & Humanoids

**[A Change of Frame Makes Balance Observable: Distillation-Free Humanoid Single-Leg Stance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00500)**  
DDC policy for humanoid single-leg balance using a foot-relative dynamic CoM observation reconstructed from encoders and IMU (no external sensors). Holds clean balance on 89/90 held-out motions, transfers to real Unitree G1; dynamic-CoM observation is the single largest driver (+43 points).

**[Pose Flow Matching for Humanoid Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03227)**  
PFM-HR trains a reusable flow-matching pose prior on large-scale unordered pose data, using a Pose Geometry Score to modulate tracking rewards. Improves single-motion and general tracking for dynamic motions.

**[Genie Sim 3.0: A High-Fidelity Comprehensive Simulation Platform for Humanoid Robot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02078)**  
Unified simulation platform with LLM-powered scene generator, automated VLM-based evaluation, and 10,000+ hours of synthetic data over 200 tasks. Demonstrates zero-shot sim-to-real transfer for policy training, plus open-source dataset release.

## Teleoperation & Control

**[Demonstration of Space Robot Teleoperation over a Lossy and Delayed Network using ATMOS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14031)**  
ATMOS planar spacecraft-analog for hardware-in-the-loop teleoperation testing. Demonstrates docking control combining state prediction and trajectory tracking under random round-trip delays, validated in Seoul-Stockholm intercontinental experiment.

## Sim-to-Real Transfer

**[FlatLab: A Unified Methodology Framework and Simulation-Based Benchmark for Robotic Manipulation of Flat Objects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14049)**  
Benchmark for flat-object manipulation (ungraspable configurations) with strategy generation + action primitive execution. Provides high-fidelity simulation of rigid/deformable objects and standardized evaluation protocols.

**[Simulation-to-real transfer learning for infrared spectroscopic chemical sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13341)**  
UltraIR is a 100M-parameter foundation model pretrained on 60M simulated IR spectra with spectral reconstruction, fingerprint alignment, and functional-group prediction objectives. Strong zero-shot performance across spectrometers and laboratories in chemical sensing applications.

## Tactile & Contact-Rich Learning

**[Learning Dexterous Manipulation Using Contact Wrench Guidance From Human Demonstration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00033)**  
CHORD's object-centric contact wrench space guidance enables scalable RL for contact-rich dexterous manipulation. Key innovation is representing human and robot motions by induced forces/torques on objects, enabling direct cross-embodiment similarity measurement.

## Evaluation & Benchmarking

**[PRM-as-a-Judge 1.5: A Toolkit for Robot Process Assessment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14284)**  
Toolkit for fine-grained robot process evaluation that converts rollout videos into dense progress curves with metrics for failure-side progress, post-drawdown recovery, and execution quality. Includes RoboPulse++ for PRM reliability testing.
