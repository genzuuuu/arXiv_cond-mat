Looking at this large batch of arXiv papers, the majority fall outside the core themes of embodied intelligence, dexterous manipulation, and robotic manipulation. However, I've identified and summarized the most relevant papers, grouping them by topic.

### Robot Learning & Manipulation Policies

1.  **[SSTG-Nav: Metric-Grounded Spatial-Semantic Topological Graphs for Reusable Object Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00527)** - Introduces a reusable metric-semantic memory system for object navigation, allowing service robots to build a topological map from a one-time survey and use it for repeated tasks, improving success and efficiency.

2.  **[Push-Wiper: Toward General-Purpose Robotic Cleaning across Varied Stains and Surfaces with Segmented Pushing Trajectories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00730)** - Presents a framework for robotic cleaning of viscous stains by using a sponge and a policy to progressively gather the stain into a ball, rather than smearing it, with strong generalization to new surfaces and stains.

3.  **[DynamicManip: Enabling Dynamic Manipulation from a Single Static Demonstration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01452)** - Proposes a data pipeline to synthesize dynamic manipulation demonstrations from a single static one and a dynamic-aware policy that adjusts its inference frequency for responsive control, significantly improving data efficiency and success rates.

4.  **[Grasp Execution Without a Planner: Configuration-Space Grasp Distance Fields with Certified Safety & Guaranteed Quality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00600)** - Presents a method for grasp execution that follows the gradient of a smooth distance field over grasp configurations, eliminating the need for a separate planner and providing formal safety guarantees via Control Barrier Functions.

### Dexterous Manipulation & Tactile Sensing

5.  **[Learning to Predict Contact Force Distributions from Vision Leveraging Object Geometry Priors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00464)** - Aims to predict 3D contact force distributions from a single RGB image by learning smooth force distributions from a simulator, which improves downstream manipulation performance and generalizes well to real-world scenes.

6.  **[Disentangling Visuo-Tactile Foresight: Oracle-Guided Interface Discovery for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00547)** - Addresses the structure of future visual and tactile predictions for world-action models in manipulation. It introduces a controlled framework to study the interface between predicted futures and the action expert, finding that joint visual-tactile routing is superior to modality isolation.

7.  **[DexMani: Human-Derived Manipulability Guidance for Dexterous Rotation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00554)** - A framework that transfers human demonstrations as a "manipulability evolution" prior to guide reinforcement learning for dexterous object rotation, enabling skills to be learned across different robotic hands.

8.  **[MANGO-Grasp: Mahalanobis Fields over Geometry-Oriented 3D Gaussians for Cross-Embodiment Dexterous Grasping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02014)** - Proposes a cross-embodiment dexterous grasping framework using Mahalanobis fields over 3D Gaussian primitives for objects, enabling grasp synthesis for different multi-fingered hands without embodiment-specific tuning.

9.  **[ReTouch: Empowering Contact-Rich Dexterous Manipulation with Online-Refined Tactile Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01824)** - Presents a vision-language-action model for dexterous manipulation that can predict future tactile states and refine its actions online using incoming tactile feedback during execution, improving robustness in contact-rich tasks.

10. **[CAAT: Contact-Aware Attention Scaling and Tactile Masking for Data-Efficient Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01102)** - Proposes a lightweight framework to explicitly incorporate contact priors into visuo-tactile policies by scaling attention toward visual information before contact and tactile information during contact, boosting learning efficiency.

11. **[TWINS: A Tactile Wearable Isomorphic Arm Networked System for Contact-Rich Manipulation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01733)** - Introduces a teleoperation and learning system for manipulation tasks that require contact with the robot's body surface (arms/chest), featuring a wearable operator device and an isomorphic robot embedded with tactile sensors.

### World-Action Models (WAMs) & Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models

12. **[SelfWAM: A Self-Grounded Unified World Action Model for Fast Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00725)** - Introduces a world action model that conditions future prediction on the robot's own body masks and action-specific consequences, grounding the model in the visible effects of its actions for more accurate predictions.

13. **[DynamicWAM: Dual-Path Motion Conditioning for World-Action Models in Dynamic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00793)** - Proposes a compact WAM with dual-path motion conditioning, using optical flow for history and kinematic descriptors for magnitude, which improves performance in dynamic manipulation tasks like following moving targets.

14. **[SG-WAM: Self-Guided World Modeling in Geometry-Aware Policy Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01397)** - Introduces a WAM that learns future dynamics directly in the policy's representation space with a self-guided predictor, making predictions more action-relevant and geometry-aware.

15. **[Demystifying When and Why VLAs Fail in Contact-Rich Tasks and How to Fix Them](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01402)** - Analyzes failure modes in Vision-Language-Action models for contact-rich tasks, identifying "precision" and "force" failures with distinct root causes, and proposes a combined mechanism (FACT) that significantly improves success rates.

16. **[DreamTrajectory: Trajectory-Guided Action Generation with World Model Alignment for Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01381)** - Introduces a VLA policy that jointly predicts an end-effector trajectory guiding whole-body action generation and uses a world model for test-time alignment between planned and actual motion, improving precision.

17. **[Faster-WAM: Do World Action Models Need Deep Action Modules?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02365)** - Asks whether deep action modules are necessary in WAMs, proposing a "Dock of Transformer" (DoT) design principle that docks a single-layer action head onto a pre-trained video backbone, achieving high speed and strong performance.

18. **[When Replanning Becomes the Bottleneck: Budgeted Replanning for Embodied Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01428)** - Presents BRACE, a controller that budgets LLM-based replanning calls by deciding when to replan and allocating an explicit token budget, preventing latency from becoming a bottleneck in long-horizon embodied tasks.

### Humanoid & Legged Locomotion

19. **[LooperMuscle: Fast and Stable Learning of Humanoid Whole-Body Tracking via Structured Mixture-of-Experts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00820)** - A framework to train humanoid whole-body tracking policies faster and more accurately by using a structured mixture-of-experts actor and a contribution-routed replay strategy, addressing the speed-performance gap of prior methods.

20. **[GenTrack: Physical Alignment for Robot-Native Motion Generation and Zero-Shot Humanoid Tracking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01410)** - Introduces a framework that alternates between aligning a text-to-motion generator with a humanoid tracker to produce more executable motions and training the tracker on these new references, improving zero-shot tracking performance.

21. **[Developing Combined Manipulation and Locomotion Skills with Interaction Representation and Skill Composition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00208)** - Presents a method for a humanoid to learn whole-body reaching/grasping and locomotion policies separately, then compose them to perform complex sequences like grasping, standing up, and walking.

### Sim-to-Real & Control

22. **[Bridging the Sim-to-Real Gap in Parallel-Link Leg Mechanisms via Simulator-Side Dynamics Normalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01697)** - Addresses the sim-to-real gap for robots with parallel-link mechanisms by proposing a simulator-side method to normalize the serial-tree dynamics to better match the physical hardware, improving both motion and force-level consistency.

23. **[Open-DiffLoco: Open-Source Differentiable Learning for Deployable Blind Quadruped Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02069)** - Introduces an open-source framework for training deployable quadruped locomotion policies using differentiable simulation, which trains a policy that transfers to real hardware without privileged observations and with simplified rewards.
