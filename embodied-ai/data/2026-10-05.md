# arXiv Robotics/AI Digest

Most of today's batch sits outside embodied manipulation (LLM agents, NLP, medical ML, physics surrogates), so I've kept those brief at the end. The embodied core is unusually rich today: **tactile sensing**, **sim-to-real co-training**, and **world-action models** dominate.

## 🖐️ Tactile Sensing & Visuotactile Policies

**SoTa** ($10 capacitive skins with 202 taxels shared across human and robot hands) enables human-robot co-training and lifts mean success from 22.8% → 45.9% with a fixed robot budget. [SoTa: Soft Tactile Skins for Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02338)

**SimpleTouch** asks whether tactile policy pretraining is really necessary — a single-stage tactile expert on π₀.₅ hits 77.5% average across six UniVTAC tasks (vs 45.2% for FTP-π₀.₅). [SimpleTouch: Can Vision-Language-Action Models Master Contact-Rich Manipulation Without Tactile Policy Pretraining?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02784)

**VISTA** brings SE(3)/spherical equivariance to visuotactile diffusion policies for sample-efficient contact-rich imitation. [Equivariant Visual-Tactile Diffusion Policy for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03333)

**RATE** encodes task-conditioned *risk* into tactile representations, distinguishing sensor noise from genuinely dangerous contact. [RATE: Risk-Aware Tactile Encoding for Contact-rich Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03538)

**GlowTact** visualizes pressure directly with single-color illumination — no geometry reconstruction, compact enough for humanoid fingertips (resolves M1 screw threads). [GlowTact: Simple and Compact Vision-Based Tactile Sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32471)

**TacEx** uses tactile-driven curiosity for RL exploration, steering uncertainty toward the touch channel and enabling offline pick-and-place plus VLA post-training without task rewards. [Tactile Curiosity Drives Robot Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40134)

Two supporting pieces: a learning-free resilience/sensitivity benchmark for polyurethane vs. silicone VBTS surfaces [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07797), and **TouchTherm**, which builds visuo-tactile-*thermal* digital twins from real objects [TouchTherm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01943).

## 🦾 Dexterous & Multi-Finger Manipulation

**DexJoCo-X** is a controlled 7-hand benchmark (2,100 demonstrations) isolating action representation from architecture. Key finding: cross-embodiment learning depends on the *whole* system — function-aligned action slots reach 47.7% vs 33.1% for DexLatent. [DexJoCo-X: Benchmarking Action Representations for Multi-Hand Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03278)

**PointWAM** decomposes the world into scene and hands as 3D point trajectories, pretraining on human video with no keypoint selection. Human-video pretraining adds 56.9 points on DexJoCo. [PointWAM: 3D World Action Modeling for Dexterous Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02840)

**EyeRobot 2.0** replaces wrist cameras with active gaze — swiveling stereo viewpoints plus foveated token allocation. Closes the wrist-camera gap (40% over passive stereo) and more than doubles success when grasped objects occlude wrist views. [EyeRobot 2.0: Active Gaze for Precise Manipulation without Wrist Cameras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03710)

**S2M-Trek** studies permutation symmetry when balancing multiple free-rolling spheres on a wheel-legged quadruped, with Per-Frame Deep Sets to avoid slot-index shortcuts. [S2M-Trek: From Single to Multi-Sphere Transport](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01332)

## 🎬 World-Action Models (WAMs)

A big cluster today. The recurring theme: **language is a poor interface; visual/spatial conditioning works better.**

**ProAct** replaces the task-agnostic Gaussian source in flow-based VLAs with a world-calibrated, proposal-centered anisotropic source — 50% fewer denoising steps, up to 25.8% lower latency. [World-Calibrated Proposal-to-Action Flow for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02323)

**ProWAM** predicts an ordered sequence of *sparse visual subgoals* (not dense video), setting SOTA on LIBERO-Plus (85.8%) and randomized RoboTwin (75.7%), with 70% zero-shot real-world success. [World Action Modeling with Progressive Visual Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02508)

**GlanceWAM** decouples imagination from control asynchronously — a background proposer imagines one lookahead frame while the action head decodes at 48 ms, cutting latency 24× vs synchronous WAMs. [GlanceWAM: Sparse Test-Time Imagination for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23927)

**PEWAM** (71.5M params) compiles demonstrations into editable *effect programs* — 3D object keypoint trajectories with the demonstrator removed. Programs from single human videos complete 36/40 Franka trials. [Keep the Effect, Drop the Actor: Programmable Effect-to-Execution World-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02398)

**XGenAct** renders depth, normals, and functional segmentation as RGB videos through deterministic codecs, training one diffusion transformer — 52% vs 26% on RLBench. [XGenAct: Geometry-Enhanced World Action Models through Cross-Task Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03516)

**DeltaWorld** predicts action-induced *latent increments* rather than full next states, reducing FVD by 46.6% on a cross-robot dataset. [DeltaWorld: Physically Consistent Interactive World Simulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02691)

**NAVA-WAM** pretrains the action policy directly from observation-only video via transition-structured attention, avoiding separate latent-action grounding. [Native Action-Prior Learning from Videos for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03391)

Also: **WAM-OPD** (on-policy distillation with joint video-action targets, 33.8%→65.7% on RoboTwin) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22364); **AED** (action experience dictionary for cross-task skill reuse) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40219); **AR-WAM** (language-free visual + operation-token conditioning, 14.1 ms inference) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23578); **ViGAR** (hierarchical visual subgoal planner + executor, 82% on RoboTwin Clean) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02368). For theoretical grounding on why JEPA-style models can lose action-relevant information: [AVL-JEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03587) and [World Action Learning via Interaction-Centric Spectral Latent Guidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03607). On the planning side, [World Action Planner](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27599) and [Closing the Train-Test Gap in World Models for Gradient-Based Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09929).

## 🔄 Sim-to-Real & Data Generation

**Awomo-SimDataEngine** is an agentic scene/asset/demonstration generator. Co-training with its Isaac Sim data lifts a World-Action Model on LIBERO-Plus from 77.17% → 89.43% (goal success +31.66 pts). [Awomo-SimDataEngine: Agentic Simulation-Ready World Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02274)

**SARI** splits phases by domain: free-space approaches from a photorealistic twin, contact interactions from a few real demos. Cuts real-data collection 34.3% and achieves 27.5% at unseen placements where full-task baselines get 0%. [SARI: Phase-Split Sim-Real Co-Training for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02804)

**NEEDLE** stitches offline robot data by inserting short, verified action bridges in *high-dimensional* space (RGB + proprioception only, no privileged state) — +21 points average on real tasks. [NEEDLEWORK: Offline Rewriting of Robot Data with Verified Local Stitches](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02339)

**Skill2Real** learns skills through a shared API with a Proposer-Verifier-Governor loop; frozen checkpoints transfer to a real robot with no task-policy fine-tuning. [Skill2Real: Agentic Skill Learning for Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02788)

**RoboBridge** treats sim-to-real as continued adaptation of executable procedural skills, targeting LIBERO-PRO plus physical tasks. [RoboBridge: A Self-Evolving Embodied Agent Framework for Sim-to-Real Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02717)

Also: **I2CD** predicts convex collision geometry directly from a single image (0.5 s/image, 6–37× faster than reconstruct-then-decompose) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03453); **RoboChemGym** generates long-horizon chemistry protocols [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02708); **Safe and Robust Neural Policy Learning** offers statistically verified sim-to-real regions [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06481).

## 🧠 VLA Training, Generalization & Efficiency

**MixVLA** improves OOD generalization by stochastically mixing *non-invariant* representations while preserving invariant ones — no extra data or architecture changes. [MixVLA: Adaptive Mixing of Non-Invariant Information for Generalizable Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02898)

**APT** factorizes the policy into a language-agnostic Vision-Action prior and a language-conditioned likelihood, pretraining the action expert first — addressing VLA language-imbalance shortcuts. [APT: Action Expert Pretraining Improves Instruction Generalization of VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12366)

**FastOPD** distills large VLAs to lightweight students via flow-map + self-consistency, retaining 84% of π₀.₅ with 2 inference steps (−78.1% latency). [FastOPD: On-Policy Distillation for Lightweight VLA Deployment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02832)

**LayerRoute** adds action-conditioned mixture-of-layers routing over cached VLM representations — up to +7.2 on LIBERO Long with 0.31% extra parameters. [LayerRoute: Action-Conditioned Mixture-of-Layers Routing for VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06079)

**W2-VLA** forecasts future *wrist* latents conditioned on a task-level interface, with a W2-CoT synthesis pipeline for supervision. [World-to-Wrist: Task-Conditioned Future Wrist Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05369)

Also: **OpenRUA** (off-the-shelf coding agents as zero-shot visuomotor policies via ROS 2, 99.0% on CaP-Bench) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02459); **RRDF** on cross-modal fusion topology in visuomotor imitation [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02813); **Uncertainty Quantification for Flow-Based Generalist Robot Policies** via velocity-field disagreement [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18043).

## ⚠️ Safety, Benchmarks & Failure Modes

**CriticHack** shows optimizing a learned visual reward can raise task success *and* wrong-object failures simultaneously — the monitored signals look healthy. A frozen outcome verifier redirects optimization. [CriticHack: Evaluating Visual Rewards Under Robot Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02527)

**SafeVLA-Bench** post-hoc evaluates safety via STL invariants. Sobering: 15 tabletop policies above 90% success still show 18–28% unsafe-episode rates. [SafeVLA-Bench: A Benchmark for the Success-Safety Gap in Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00773)

**ManiPhysicsBench** consolidates material properties and meshes to assess *object preservation* — revealing a substantial gap between task success and "safe success." [ManiPhysicsBench: Physics-Based Assessment of Object Preservation in VLA Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02802)

**SocialVLA** converts spontaneous human vocal/facial reactions into runtime VLA interruption signals (91.7% precision prospectively, 1.02 s reaction-to-hold latency). [SocialVLA: A Social Perception Gateway](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02360)

**Detect and Suppress** finds a sparse-autoencoder feature correlated with adversarial patches and suppresses it only when an attack is detected — emphasizing *when* to intervene matters. [Detect and Suppress: A Mechanistic Defense against Adversarial Patches in VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03498)

Also: **EvoRiskBench** (450 adversarial workspace-agent tasks) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03153); **SafeCoEvo** (test-time harness-guard co-evolution) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36580); **LOCUS** for container/object search via spatial scene graphs [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02803).

## 🏗️ Manipulation Control, Planning & Teleoperation

**Learning Reflexive Behavior** trains a proprioceptive reflex policy (no force or geometry sensing) as a frozen layer beneath higher-level controllers — peg insertion success rises from ≤22% to 36–58%. [Learning Reflexive Behavior for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02811)

**Screw Attention** embeds rigid-body algebra *inside* a transformer layer — messages transported along relative poses, scores frame-invariant. A 16k-parameter policy reaches 97.3% on LIBERO-Spatial and resists frame-convention changes that collapse other networks. [Screw Attention: Rigid-Body Algebra Inside a Transformer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00904)

**FAME** conditions a humanoid standing policy on a latent encoding of arm configuration *and* bimanual contact forces (estimated via inverse dynamics, no wrist F/T sensing) — 38.9% vs 16.6% task success. [FAME: Force-Adaptive RL for Expanding the Manipulation Envelope of a Full-Scale Humanoid](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08961)

**MobiAgent** separates deployment (receding-horizon planning with composable atomic skills) from lifelong learning (autonomous rollout recycling). Success improves 32.5%→57.5% on the real Astribot S1. [MobiAgent: Dual-Loop Recursive Policy Self-Improvement for Long-Horizon Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03476)

**RIFAR** combines reliability screening with drift-aware replay for continual robot learning, retaining ~4.9% of the steps of standard replay. [RIFAR: Reliability and Forgetting-Aware Replay for Continual Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03079)

Also relevant: **AdaTempo** (up to 3.57× speedup by learning shared relative tempo from demos) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02706); **PAM** (timing-free proprioceptive sketches as long-horizon intent, 47.5%→75.0% on bimanual tasks) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02759); **UniIntervene++** (adaptive intervention, 89.67% success with 0.77% human intervention) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03620); **TimelyDAgger** (timing-aware expert querying) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33157); **ROVE** (RL post-training for humanoid manipulation from imperfect interventions) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17011); **QueryArt** (articulation estimation from a single image + query point, 70.2% on 57 real trials) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01726). On the humanoid side: **CoFiT** for filter-aware safe whole-body tracking [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02341), **WB-WAM** for heterogeneous body-hand pretraining [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34199), **EgoAlign** for human→humanoid data conversion [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38046), and **DDC** for distillation-free single-leg balance via support-relative capture point [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00500).

## 🚗 Navigation, Perception & Locomotion (Brief)

**GeoScaffold** internalizes geometry via training-time reconstruction targets, then discards all scaffolding — leaving the deployed policy unchanged. [GeoScaffold: Learning Compact Geometric Latents via Reconstruction for Efficient Vision-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02697)

**CORNAV** grounds navigation in 2D CAD blueprints + project schedules, raising task success from 13.0% to 72.2% on active construction sites. [CORNAV: Construction-Aware Reasoning for Robot Navigation on Active Worksites](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03622)

**HexVIO** offloads the visual frontend of stereo-inertial odometry to a commodity Hexagon DSP — 67% lower power, sustaining 30 fps at 0.83 W (~18 h). [HexVIO: Towards All-Day Stereo-Inertial Tracking Through Commodity DSPs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03283)

**SABER** adds a semantic *contact permission* signal to legged locomotion attention; removing the semantic bias alone increases forbidden contacts 55%. [SABER: Learning Attention-based Semantic Affordance for Legged Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21572)

Also: **Safe Streaming Flow Planning** (aligns sampling dynamics with execution dynamics, enforcing CBF safety only on the executed step) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03132); **Real-time Event-camera Stereo VO** via keytime GP regression [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02601); **DR-IPC** for disturbance-resilient LiDAR quadrotor navigation [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03530). On the benchmark/eval side: **Toward Controlling Biology with Language** (offline VLM-as-reward for xenobot intervention) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02247) and **AgenticNav** (tool-calling harness for zero-shot VLN-CE, 76% SR / 66.50% SPL on R2R-CE) [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10577).
