# Summary of Today's arXiv Papers (Robotics & AI Feeds)

## Embodied Intelligence & Manipulation Policies

**[VLCP: Vision Language Control Policy Closed-Loop Code Replanning for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16978)**  
Keeps a VLM frozen and uses it to write Python control functions as policies, with closed-loop code rewriting every K steps. Achieves 35.1% pooled success on 57 MuJoCo/RoboVerse tasks (vs. 3.5% open-loop), with a 27.3% within-episode recovery rate on failed grasps.

**[FetchMan: Learning Visual Humanoid Loco-Manipulation Policies from Simulated Experiences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17027)**  
Sim-to-real pipeline for humanoid loco-manipulation combining behavior cloning with Flow-GRPO reinforcement learning. Deploys zero-shot on a Unitree G1 with 73.3% reach-and-pick success across unseen scenes.

**[EXPO-FT: Sample-Efficient Reinforcement Learning Finetuning for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25477)**  
System for stable RL finetuning of pretrained VLAs. Achieves perfect 30/30 task success on challenging manipulation tasks (string lights, pool ball, flower insertion) within ~19 minutes of online robot data.

**[UniReflex: Plug-and-Play Force Control for Pretrained Generative Policies via Fast-Slow Reflex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17432)**  
Adds closed-loop force regulation to frozen generative policies via variable impedance control. A fast reflex network predicts stiffness directions from latent features, achieving 25-66x lower per-step latency vs. joint training.

**[ORPA: Online Residual Policy Adaptation for Robot Manipulation Control with Human Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17323)**  
Lightweight feedback-conditioned module predicts residual joint-space adjustments on top of ACT policies, enabling runtime correction without retraining. Evaluated on precision-sensitive tasks with ALOHA platform.

**[LoopVLA: Learning Sufficiency in Recurrent Refinement for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09948)**  
Recurrent VLA architecture that iteratively refines representations with a learned sufficiency score for early exit. Reduces parameters 45%, improves throughput 1.7x while matching or beating baselines on LIBERO.

**[SAFE-Pruner: Semantic Attention-Guided Future-Aware Token Pruning for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29662)**  
Prunes visual tokens using attention cues from future layers, achieving up to 1.89x speedup with <1.5% success-rate degradation on VLA models.

**[Prism-GRPO: Faster VLA Policy Optimization via Splitting Same-outcome Groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17423)**  
Augments binary success rewards with weighted trajectory-level quality scores to recover training signal from same-outcome groups. Reaches target success with up to 56% fewer rollouts on RoboTwin tasks.

## Tactile Sensing & In-Hand Manipulation

**[ReTouch: Empowering Contact-Rich Dexterous Manipulation with Online-Refined Tactile Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01824)**  
VLA model for contact-rich dexterous manipulation with a Tactile-Patch Encoder that preserves finger identity. Jointly predicts future tactile states and actions with closed-loop refinement. Surpasses baselines by 18.4-23.8 percentage points on real XHand-UR7e tasks.

**[Physics-Informed Sliding-Window Particle Filtering for Tactile-Only In-Hand 6-DoF Object Pose Refinement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17601)**  
SE(3) particle filter for tactile-only pose refinement using dense whole-hand measurements. Combines contact signed-distance consistency, force-normal alignment, friction-cone feasibility, and symmetry-aware resampling on an Allegro Hand.

**[PRISM: Precision and contact-rich Real-world Industrial Skill dataset with Multimodal sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17962)**  
Large-scale multimodal dataset: 25+ contact-rich industrial tasks, 5,000+ trajectories (45 hours), synchronized RGB-D, force/torque, tactile, and robot states. Open-sourced for contact-rich manipulation research.

## Grasping & Assembly

**[Iterative Grasp Pose Refinement: A Deep Reinforcement Learning Approach for 2D Vision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17628)**  
DQN-based grasp refinement using keypoint representations. Transforms failed grasps into successes, achieving 100% success on previously ungraspable Dex-Net objects in simulation with sim-to-real transfer on a Delta robot.

**[Force-Based Offset Estimation for Keyed Peg-in-Hole Assembly Using Local Gaussian Process Regression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17691)**  
Estimates radial misalignment from wrist force/torque using KNN-Gaussian Process hybrid regressors with separate models for hard collision vs. guided chamfer insertion regimes. Increases insertion success from 67% to 87%.

**[Hydra-0: Action Flow for Generalist World Modeling and Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18077)**  
Represents robot actions as pixel motion in a shared visual interface, enabling world modeling across embodiments. Achieves 90.4% lower robot-motion error and supports zero-shot composition. Emergent inverse mode predicts robot actions from human demonstration object flow.

## Skill Learning, Composition & Transfer

**[Teach and Grow: An Agent-Centered Architecture for General Robot Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17209)**  
Agentic framework where multimodal agents turn demonstrations into reusable Skill Blocks with Experience Memory. State-of-the-art on LIBERO with skill induction, persistent reuse, and task adaptation without task-specific retraining. Proposes a scaling-law hypothesis for teach-and-grow learning.

**[VOLT: Vision and Language Trajectory Segmentation for Faster-than-Demonstration Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06323)**  
Uses VLMs to segment demonstrations and identify which parts can be safely sped up vs. requiring careful precision. Selectively downsamples unconstrained segments for fast yet reliable imitation learning with diffusion policies.

**[ManiCM: Real-time 3D Diffusion Policy via Consistency Model for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01586)**  
Imposes consistency constraints on diffusion policies for one-step inference. 10x faster than state-of-the-art diffusion methods while maintaining competitive success on 31 Adroit and Metaworld tasks.

**[KAN We Flow? Advancing Robotic Manipulation with 3D Flow Matching via KAN & RWKV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01115)**  
Flow-matching policy with RWKV-KAN backbone for 3D manipulation. Reduces parameters by 86.8% while achieving SOTA success rates on Adroit, Meta-World, and DexArt.

**[Action-Effect Memory Pretraining for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12499)**  
Pretraining framework learning compact temporal representations from vision-action history via masked modeling. Improves Diffusion/Flow Policy performance in simulation and real-world settings, including non-Markovian and cluttered tasks.

**[AEM / AFRO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.00074)**  
AFRO is a self-supervised framework for dynamics-aware 3D representations without action supervision. Casts state prediction as diffusion, jointly models forward/inverse dynamics. When combined with Diffusion Policy, substantially improves success across 16 simulated and 4 real manipulation tasks.

## World Models & Navigation

**[QWM / Q-Learning With World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17163)**  
Uses world models for test-time search over imagined trajectories on top of standard Q-learning. Trains only on real transitions to avoid compounding bias while gaining sample efficiency. Outperforms prior SOTA on Robomimic and LIBERO.

**[No Gaussian Required: Contrastive Inverse Dynamics for JEPA World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17542)**  
Replaces Gaussian anti-collapse regularizers with an action-contrastive inverse dynamics head. Trains stably from scratch and improves success on OGBench Visual Scene from 58% to 80% vs. SIGReg.

**[EmbodiedGen V2: An Agentic, Simulation-Ready 3D World Engine for Embodied AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07459)**  
Generative 3D world engine assembling sim-ready assets into policy-ready task environments. Online RL with generated environments improves simulation success (9.7% to 79.8%) with real-robot transfer (21.7% to 75.0%).

## Humanoid & Locomotion

**[Robust Brachiation on a Life-Sized Dual-Arm Robot Using Waypoint-Guided Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17320)**  
Waypoint-guided RL for brachiation (arm-based locomotion). Sparsely specifies end-effector waypoints while RL generates whole-body motion. Demonstrates robust Sim-to-Sim and hardware performance including failure recovery.

**[EATR-Stereo: Embodiment-Aware Routing of Paired Stereo Evidence for Humanoid Vision-Language-Action Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17453)**  
Embodiment-aware token routing for stereo vision in humanoid VLA control. Retains primary-view tokens and constructs aligned Cross-View Auxiliary Tokens. 60% full-task success on 33-DoF physical humanoid with 100% grasp success.

**[HODAgent: Towards On-Demand, Responsive Humanoids for Physical World Human Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17584)**  
System-2 embodied agent for humanoid service robots with semi-duplex architecture for handling new requests during motion, progress retention, and task revision. 84.8% Joint Success in simulation, 63.3% complete-task on Unitree G1.

## High-Level Planning & Reasoning

**[PDDL-ART: Autonomous Symbolic Abstraction From Demonstration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17146)**  
VLM-based framework autonomously generates PDDL domain/problem from a single demonstration with multi-stage syntactic, semantic, and execution-level correction. Achieves 93.3% success vs. 78.3% for baseline VLM planner.

**[CompCPZ: Preserving Multi-Modal Intent in Language-Guided Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17717)**  
Algebraic layer composing constrained polynomial zonotopes along language parse trees to preserve disjunctive intent (e.g., "near the red OR blue plate"). Outperforms convex baselines and zero-shot VLAs on ManiSkill3 with 1,900/1,918 paired wins.

## Benchmarks & Safety

**[LIBERO-VIFO: Benchmarking the Capability and Safety of Visual Cue Following in Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17600)**  
Evaluates whether VLAs follow authorized visual cues while disregarding unauthorized ones. 8 cue families, 4 protocols, 7 VLA models. Reveals emerging risk that current VLAs execute cue-indicated tasks without language instruction.

**[MANIGUARD: A Benchmark and Data Suite for Specification-Grounded Safety Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17386)**  
200 base tasks × 5 perturbations with LTLf-grounded safety monitors. Finds 6-21% of successful rollouts violate safety specs; releases 8,000 safety-annotated demonstrations. Fine-tuning improves safe completion but gaps remain.

**[Reuse Before You Retrieve: Diagnosing Headroom and Complementarity for Test-Time Augmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17484)**  
Analyzes when test-time sampling vs. retrieval helps frozen VLAs. Episode-level retry selector recovers up to 21.0 success-rate points on LIBERO. Retrieval helps most when action-prior gap is measurable.
