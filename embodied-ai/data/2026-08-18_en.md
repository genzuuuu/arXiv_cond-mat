# Robotics & AI Papers Summary (arXiv)

This digest summarizes papers from today's arXiv listing, focusing on themes relevant to embodied intelligence, manipulation, tactile sensing, and robot learning. Papers outside these core themes are briefly noted or omitted.

## 1. Tactile Sensing & Contact-Rich Manipulation

**[MISTac: A Vision-Based Tactile Sensor for Minimally Invasive Surgery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14772)**
A high-resolution vision-based tactile sensor for palpation in minimally invasive surgery. Features an 8mm replaceable tip, 250μm tactile resolution, and detects forces as small as 24.3mN. An in vivo study with a tissue classification task achieved ~84% accuracy. Open-sourced at github.com/lasr-lab/mistac.

**[ReForce: Learning Force-aware Retargeting for Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15560)**
Presents a force-aware retargeting method that converts human motion and forces into robot actions, predicting residuals on kinematically retargeted actions to reach desired forces. Supports online teleoperation and offline data translation, achieving lower force-tracking error on contact-rich tasks like paper-cup grasping.

**[Tactile Sim2Real without Tactile Simulation via Bottlenecked Latent Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15897)**
Introduces SBLR (Sim2Real via Bottlenecked Latent Reconstruction), a framework that avoids sensor-specific simulation entirely by training policies on an oracle sensor and aligning real sensor latent embeddings to the oracle. Hardware experiments on Peg Insertion and Gear Meshing demonstrate 85–97.5% zero-shot success.

**[Vision-Based Tactile Intelligence for Robotics: Sensing, Learning, and Embodied Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15490)**
A comprehensive survey of vision-based tactile sensors (VBTSs), covering the full pipeline as an integrated sensing-and-learning system: hardware taxonomy, learning methods, simulation platforms, and sim-to-real transfer.

**[ViTaR: Visuo-Tactile Residual Adaptation for Foundation VLA Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15816)**
Frames tactile feedback as an execution modulator that selects and scales bounded residual corrections atop a frozen VLA, preserving pretrained capabilities. Achieves 61.3% average success on UniVTAC (7 contact-rich tasks), a 30.6-point improvement over its frozen VLA base.

## 2. Dexterous Manipulation & Robotic Hands

**[H-PAC Hand: Control-Oriented Modeling and Tendon-Elasticity Compensation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16712)**
A modular 6-actuator, 15-DoF underactuated robotic hand with a control-oriented modeling framework. Introduces a mechanics-based compensation model for tendon-elasticity-induced joint errors, reducing index DIP joint MAE from 1.15° to 0.18°.

**[DECO: Decoupled Multimodal Diffusion Transformer for Bimanual Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05513)**
A decoupled multimodal diffusion transformer that disentangles vision, proprioception, and tactile signals through specialized conditioning pathways. Released with DECO-50 dataset (50 hours, 5M+ frames) for bimanual dexterous manipulation, achieving 72.25% average success with a tactile adapter adding 10.25%.

**[Arm-Aware Guided Dexterous Grasp Generation with Arm-Agnostic Grasp Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16351)**
A framework for arm-aware dexterous grasp generation that leverages pretrained arm-agnostic models, formulating constrained generation as joint optimization of hand pose and arm configuration. Uses gradient-based guided diffusion sampling for feasible grasps in highly constrained settings.

**[Pre-training Visual Dexterity in Simulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15917)**
Introduces Simulation Pre-training for Dexterity (SPD), using 75 hours of multi-task dexterous manipulation collected via VR teleoperation to pre-train a causal transformer. Fine-tuning on 1-2 hours of physical demonstrations on a 56-DoF bimanual setup outperforms training from scratch.

**[Learning Versatile Humanoid Manipulation with Touch Dreaming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13015)**
Introduces HTD (Humanoid Transformer with Touch Dreaming), a multimodal encoder-decoder that models touch as a core modality. Achieves 90.9% relative improvement in average success rate over baselines across five real-world contact-rich tasks.

## 3. Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models & Policies

**[PACE: Phase-Progress-Aware Credit for Long-Horizon Embodied Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15026)**
A credit-assignment framework for post-training VLA models on long-horizon manipulation, centering on a phase-progress-aware critic. Includes Global-Local Cooperative Value-Correction Critic and Progressive Policy Distillation, achieving consistent improvements over baselines in both simulation and real-world.

**[StructRL: Structured Action-Space Exploration for Flow-Based VLAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15139)**
Addresses RL for flow-based VLA models, proposing three coupled choices to avoid "Structured Noise Dilution": deterministic ODE decoder, structured noise in action space, and last-step replay. Improves exploration efficiency and OOD performance across benchmarks.

**[PhaseLoRA: Control-Regime-Conditioned Low-Rank Adaptation for VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15285)**
A lightweight LoRA parameterization that conditions adaptation on control regimes (approach, contact, grasp, etc.) via fine-control tendency and event intensity descriptors. Improves average success rate by 12.2 points over matched high-rank LoRA baseline on LIBERO.

**[SparkVLA: Stop-Aware Hierarchical VLA with Adaptive Action Chunking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16172)**
Resolves inter-dependence between subtask termination and action-chunk execution length by formulating both as a single ranking problem. On RoboCerebra, achieves 47.12% success, surpassing hierarchical baselines by over 30%.

**[Flow Motion Policy: Manipulator Motion Planning with Flow Matching Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07084)**
An open-loop neural motion planner using flow matching to generate batches of motion plan proposals, enabling efficient best-of-N inference without iterative collision checking.

**[NebulaVLA: A Dual-Frequency VLA Model With Guide Action](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16503)**
An asynchronous dual-frequency architecture decoupling high-level reasoning from low-level control. Introduces GESTURE-7 unified action representation and Guide Action algorithm for kinematic continuity, achieving 85.5% success on LIBERO-Plus with ~2.7x faster action generation.

**[TurboVLA: Real-Time VLA Model at 32 Hz on RTX 4090 with <1 GB VRAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27205)**
Reformulates the V→L→A pathway as direct V+L→A mapping, avoiding LLM-centric architecture. Achieves 97.7% average success on LIBERO with only 0.2B parameters and 31.2ms inference latency.

**[τ₀-VLA: Hierarchical Robot Foundation Model with World-Model-Guided Test-Time Computation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16885)**
Formulates high-level subtask generation as compute-scalable inference with world-model-guided search. Trained on 40,115 hours of heterogeneous data, shows gains in long-horizon closed-loop success.

## 4. Teleoperation & Data Collection

**[ViHaTeleop: A Low-Cost, Lightweight Visual-Haptic Teleoperation System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16572)**
A 0.7kg, $550 visual-haptic teleoperation system with SLAM-based wrist tracking and finger-wise vibrotactile feedback. Haptics improved success across all six contact-critical tasks (+2.2 to +15.6pp) with nine participants.

**[SurgVIL: Scaling Surgical Robot Imitation Learning with Open-source Surgical Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16058)**
Combines kinematically labeled phantom robot demonstrations with open-source surgical videos for policy learning. Across ACT, π₀, and GR00T-H backbones, adding surgical videos substantially improves generalization to real-tissue settings.

## 5. Long-Horizon Manipulation & Memory

**[GaussMemory: Task-Driven 3D Gaussian Scene Memory for Long-Horizon Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14986)**
Proposes a paradigm shift from passive storage to active, task-driven spatial memory using 3D Gaussian Splatting. Outperforms MemoryVLA on LIBERO Goal and Long-10, and π₀-FAST on VLABench.

**[Don't Drop the BATON: Long-Horizon Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16889)**
Freezes VLA and uses an LLM agent with subtask-level exploration, making long-horizon cost additive rather than multiplicative. Improves RoboMemArena task success by 11.6% over state-of-the-art.

**[Remember Smarter: Visual History Compressor and Hyperbolic Experience Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15269)**
A plug-and-play module with visual-history compression (spatial/causal Mamba) and hyperbolic experience memory. When adapted to pi0, increases LIBERO-Plus success from 53.6% to 70.6%.

## 6. Sim-to-Real & World Models

**[SADP: Subgoal-Aware Diffusion Policy for Long-Horizon Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16871)**
Uses foundation models to generate subgoal-annotated demonstrations, conditioning diffusion policies on explicit natural-language subgoals with a continuation score for online subgoal switching.

**[EvoScene-VLA: Evolving Scene Beliefs Inside the Action Decoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21862)**
Maintains persistent action-updated scene state across chunks with recurrent scene prefix. On 31 RoboTwin tasks, raises average success from 87.2% to 89.1% in fixed evaluation.
