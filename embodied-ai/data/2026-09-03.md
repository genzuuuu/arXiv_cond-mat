# arXiv Robotics & AI Papers Summary

## Dexterous Manipulation & Contact-Rich Policy Learning

**[One Demonstration, Many Objects: Generalizing Manipulation via Local Contact Geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01938)** — Proposes DemoMimic, a policy that uses contact-centric rewards with local geometry features for dexterous manipulation. Achieves 71% success across 16 objects, four tasks, and two robot-hand embodiments, demonstrating strong sim-to-real transfer on unseen object instances.

**[From Proxy Learning to Driving Decisions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02688)** — Proposes the Proxy-to-Decision Transfer (PDT) framework for evaluating future-aware autonomous driving planners, localizing value loss through score margins and switch-conditioned utility. None of 432 screened configurations passes the robustness gate, showing that improved proxy objectives do not guarantee planning gains.

## Surgical Robotics & Medical Manipulation

**[MACAW: Reliable And Efficient Surgical Debridement Using Monocular Adaptive Compact Attention Windows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01961)** — An augmented dexterity system combining visual servoing with monocular depth control for surgical debridement on the da Vinci Research Kit. Achieves 93% success rate at 304 fragments/hour, extending to 473 fragments/hour with bimanual setup.

**[MV-dVRK: A Multi-Viewpoint Benchmark for Spatial Surgical Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02717)** — First ex-vivo surgical dataset combining synchronized stereo viewpoints with accurate surface geometry and camera poses for evaluating multi-view 3D reconstruction in endoscopic settings.

**[A Physics-Consistent Benchmark for Contact-Rich Human-Robot Interaction in Assistive Care](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02402)** — Introduces a physics-consistent benchmark for robot-assisted bathing with a deformable, passively responding human model. Shows task completion alone doesn't imply physically valid contact—an LLM-augmented state machine drops from 72.9% to 56.4% after correct-region and force-safety screening.

## Humanoid Locomotion & Control

**[Unified Motion Retargeting for Humanoids with Learned Point Cloud Correspondence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02134)** — Presents UMR, which learns dense point cloud correspondence as an interface between human motion and humanoid robots, avoiding hand-crafted keypoint mappings. Enables scalable retargeting across heterogeneous motion sources without manual semantic design.

**[FOCUS: Foot Observation Confidence for Robust Humanoid Proprioceptive Odometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02222)** — Predicts continuous foot FK reliability weights instead of binary contact for proprioceptive odometry. Reduces ATE by 83.7% in simulation and 70.8% across 19 real walking segments.

**[Humanoid Safe Stop via Learned Stoppability Value](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02358)** — Casts emergency stopping as a reach-avoid problem using complementary stoppability estimators, committing to stop only when both estimators indicate feasibility.

**[World-Model-Augmented Visual Locomotion for Humanoids on Foothold-Constrained Terrain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02542)** — Jointly trains a recurrent world model with PPO for foothold-constrained terrain. Deploys on a physical Unitree G1 with 93.3% average success across gaps, stepping stones, and stairs.

**[Contact-Constrained Lower-Limb Joint-Offset Calibration for Humanoid Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02306)** — Self-contained calibration using only onboard joint encoders and IMU during static double-support contact; reduces foot-height RMS residuals from 4.26 to 2.20 mm.

## World Models & Predictive Modeling

**[Modeling What Changes: Sparse, Residual World Models for Object-Centric Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02046)** — Explicitly models change via per-object gates and residual delta heads rather than predicting full states. Predicts 2.5–4.6× more accurately using 8.6–11.1× fewer parameters than dense baselines; transfers across object counts.

**[Spatially Aware World Action Model via Geometric Latent Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02531)** — Repurposes pretrained video diffusion for joint action, RGB, and depth prediction. Achieves SOTA on RoboCasa and LIBERO-Plus, with strong real-world gains on a UR5 arm.

**[Do Better Imagined Rollouts Mean Better Robot Control?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02811)** — Controlled study showing offline rollouts are most informative for control when sensing/correction patterns reflect closed-loop operation. Replay metrics correlate better with closed-loop performance than prediction error alone.

## Policy Learning & VLA Models

**[ZETA: Zero-Shot Cross-Embodiment VLA Transfer for Tabletop Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02546)** — Controlled benchmark spanning 14 held-out embodiments. Finds local end-effector representations, embodiment diversity, and auxiliary co-training improve transfer by 15/18/7 percentage points respectively.

**[HINT: Human-Intent Inception for Long-Horizon Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02653)** — Agentic framework that invokes semantic reasoning only at manipulation-pattern transitions. Maintains commitments via multi-view grounding to prevent visual-shortcut following.

**[Latent Cluster Analysis for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02634)** — Studies GR00T N1.5's latent representations, showing clusters progressively disentangle spatiotemporal and kinematic features with refinement in middle layers.

**[FineVLA: Fine-Grained Instruction Alignment for Steerable VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27284)** — Framework for action-aligned fine-grained VLA supervision. Mixed fine-grained/raw instructions peak at 86.8%/82.5% on RoboTwin and 62.7 on real dual-arm manipulation.

## Grasping & Multi-Gripper Systems

**[MultiGraspNet: A Multitask 3D Vision Model for Multi-gripper Robotic Grasping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06504)** — Unified framework predicting grasp poses for parallel and vacuum grippers simultaneously with 15.75M parameters. Outperforms normalization-based multi-gripper approaches in real-world validation.

## Safe & Compliant Control

**[Real-Time Dynamics-Based Torque-Sampling MPPI for Compliant and Force Aware Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02020)** — MPPI-based task-space control solving rigid-body dynamics at 166 Hz with GPU-parallelized torque sampling, enabling accurate motion and force control for physical interaction.

**[Passivity-Centric Safe Reinforcement Learning for Contact-Rich Robotic Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00287)** — Shows standard RL policies violate stability in contact-rich scenarios; introduces passivity-aware training with energy constraints plus passivity filter at deployment.

## Manipulation & Active Perception

**[Multi-Skill Manipulation-Enhanced Mapping via Uncertainty- and Disturbance-Aware Action Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02493)** — Integrates viewpoint selection, pushing, and grasping for uncertainty-aware scene mapping. Introduces a full-evidential grasp estimator modeling both affordance and orientation uncertainty.
