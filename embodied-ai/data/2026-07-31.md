# Summary of Today's arXiv Papers (Robotics & AI Focus)

## Tactile Sensing & Contact-Rich Manipulation

**[TacWAM: Anchor-Guided World Action Model with Mechanics-Aware Tactile Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28391)** — Introduces a tactile world action model that integrates force fields and deformation flow into future-state prediction. Achieves 75% success on contact-rich tasks (fragile grasping, in-hand manipulation), exceeding strongest baselines by 37.5 pp. Key insight: separating tactile prediction tokens prevents privileged cue leakage into action generation.

**[FasTac: A Curved Multispectral Vision-Based Tactile Sensor for High-Speed High-Precision 3D Shape and Force Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28416)** — Curved tactile fingertip combining multispectral photometric stereo, dynamic-convolution force estimation, and FPGA acceleration. Reduces depth MAE from 0.273mm to 0.042mm using NIR illumination; achieves 1.09ms processing latency with three-axis force estimation.

**(τ): Learning Touch-Augmented Vision-Language-Action Models from Future Visual Supervision**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24485) — Touch-augmented VLA framework using JEPA-style future visual supervision for spatiotemporal tactile representation learning. Training-only supervision adds no deployment overhead; introduces TacAura dataset with synchronized vision, proprioception, and tactile signals across four contact-rich tasks.

## Dexterous Manipulation & Hand Control

**[DexDirect: Direct Kinesthetic Arm Guidance for Efficient Dexterous Demonstration Collection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27784)** — Kinesthetic arm guidance with vision-based hand retargeting for dexterous demo collection. Collects 17.2x more successful demos than pure vision baselines; diffusion policy trained on these demos achieves 90% success on cube pick-and-place.

**[UniCross: Unified Cross-Skill Dexterous Manipulation Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28198)** — Unified framework modeling grasping, relocation, in-hand rotation, and in-hand translation in a single formulation with shared state/action spaces. Enables seamless skill chaining for long-horizon manipulation and transfers across hand morphologies.

## Imitation Learning & Policy Robustness

**[It's Not Just More Demos: Counterfactual Action Sensitivity Coverage for Data-Efficient Robust Robot Imitation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27261)** — CFNBC framework selects repair demonstrations by measuring *action drift* — policy prediction changes under task-preserving visual nuisances. With only 20-30 selected candidates, outperforms random selection substantially and approaches much larger repair budgets.

**[RedFlow: Redirect Failure into Action-Level Corrections for Flow-matching VLA Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27782)** — Offline RL framework converting failure experiences into action-level corrective supervision for flow-matching VLAs. Improves real-world success from 56.7% to 74.7%, matching on-policy methods with ~10x fewer samples.

**[FA-RDP: A Frequency-Adaptive Reactive Diffusion Policy for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28596)** — Adaptively switches between multi-step low-frequency sampling (pre-contact, preserving multimodality) and one-step high-frequency sampling (post-contact, for force feedback reactivity). Introduces Manifold Consistency Distillation for action manifold prediction.

**[Static In, Dynamic Out: Counterfactual Action Augmentation for Moving Object Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27890)** — SIDO factorizes moving-object manipulation into pose prediction and goal-conditioned reaching. Policies trained only on static object demos adapt to unseen object motion via counterfactual displacement of demonstrated action chunks.

## Cross-Embodiment & Transfer Learning

**[Cross-Embodiment Transfer via Behavior-Aligned Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27549)** — Studies how behavior-aligned representations (object boxes, end-effector traces) in VLAs promote cross-embodiment transfer. End-effector traces particularly beneficial; improves sim-to-real task completion progress by 28% over baselines.

**[SemAnCorr: Semantic Anchored Correspondence for Zero-Shot Manipulation Skill Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28382)** — Training-free correspondence framework using semantically consistent anchor regions and functional maps. Achieves 90.8% semantic accuracy on PartNet-Mobility benchmark; enables substantially more reliable skill transfer to unseen objects with single demonstrations.

## World Models & Planning

**[World Action Planner: Generalizable Decision-Making with Action-Conditioned World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27599)** — Combines VLM reasoning with multi-task pose-image conditioned world model rollouts. Iteratively refines action plans via optimization/search over imagined futures; outperforms end-to-end VLAs and WAMs on compositional tasks and zero-shot generalization.

**[LeapBot-WA: World-Anchor Action Models via Predictive Latent Alignments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23969)** — JEPA-as-World-Anchor paradigm shifting WAMs from pixel-level synthesis to predictive semantic alignment. Introduces Isotropic Semantic Autoencoder for diffusion-friendly latent space; achieves SOTA among predictive models on LIBERO without large-scale trajectory pretraining.

**[ODEWorld: A Continuous Predictive Architecture via Physical-Time Flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27924)** — Learns continuous latent velocity fields parameterized by ODEs; enables arbitrary temporal resolution and backward prediction. Addresses representation collapse in latent world models while supporting planning-oriented downstream policy learning.

## Surgical Robotics

**[Simulation of Surgical Suturing Using Position-Based Dynamics and the Material Point Method](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27494)** — Novel suturing simulation combining PBD sutures with MPM soft tissue including two-way contact. RL agents achieve 80% and 68% success on needle insertion/extraction with strict distance thresholds.

**[Failure Detection for Surgical Robot Imitation Policies via Flow-Matching World Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27511)** — FoMo-FD learns nominal visual dynamics with action-conditioned flow matching, detecting visual-action inconsistencies without failure demonstrations. Achieves 96.6% failure detection at 1.3% false alarm rate on dVRK.

## Human-Robot Interaction & Social Robotics

**[Learning Social Robot Navigation By Sensing Human Legs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27922)** — CALF architecture combining convolution, attention, and MLP to interpret leg motion from 2D LiDAR for safe navigation. Zero-shot deployment on TurtleBot 4 yields smooth, socially compliant trajectories.

## Robot Learning & Data Efficiency

**[SeedPolicy: Horizon Scaling via Self-Evolving Diffusion Policy for Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05117)** — SEGA temporal module with gated attention accumulates long-term context into compact latent state. 36.8% relative improvement over DP in clean settings, 169% in randomized settings; outperforms 1.2B-param VLAs with ~10-100x fewer parameters.

**[CLAM: Continuous Latent Action Models for Robot Learning from Unlabeled Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04999)** — Infers continuous latent actions from unlabeled observation sequences; jointly trains action decoder with task-agnostic play data. Improves success rates 2-3x over prior latent-action baselines, approaching BC trained with privileged action labels.

**[SharedAssembly: A Data Collection Approach via Shared Tele-Assembly](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12287)** — Shared-autonomy bilateral teleoperation for sub-millimeter assembly tasks. Achieves 97% assembly success with novices matching experts; eliminates expertise gap for scalable contact-rich data collection.

## Humanoid & Loco-Manipulation

**[REFINE-DP: Diffusion Policy Fine-tuning for Humanoid Loco-manipulation via Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13707)** — Hierarchical framework jointly optimizing DP motion planner and RL-based loco-manipulation controller. Achieves >90% success in simulation including OOD cases; enables real-world execution without privileged state.

**[Critic Architecture Matters: Dual vs. Unified Critics for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11891)** — Dual critics with disjoint reward signals reach targets 3.5x faster with 2x higher throughput than unified critic on Unitree G1. Suggests unified critics may suppress pretrained arm behavior through competing locomotion gradients.
