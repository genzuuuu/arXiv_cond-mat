### Dexterous Manipulation & Robotic Hands

- **[A Support-Enhanced Granular-Jamming Gripper for RL-based Grasping with Continuum Manipulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29093)** — A compliant granular-jamming gripper for continuum arms that locks into a rigid load path after jamming, paired with an RL reaching controller in a sim-to-real grasp-and-release loop.
- **[See to Reach, Feel to Grasp: Learning A Blind Grasp Reflex for Anthropomorphic Robotic Hands](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31323)** — A modular architecture separating arm motion from a proprioception-only RL "blind grasp reflex," making grasping a reusable hand-level skill with a learned stability score.
- **[VisTacAlign: Co-Training Dexterous Policies on Tactile Human and Robot Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30959)** — Retargets glove-tracked human hands onto a 17-DoF tactile hand, erases and replaces the hand in stereo views, and aligns a capacitive glove to robot sensors for visuo-tactile co-training.
- **[Cloak: Zero-Shot Cross-Embodiment Manipulation by Masking the End-Effector from the VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22836)** — Masks the robot's own end-effector from the wrist camera during training so a VLA transfers zero-shot to grippers, arms, and a five-fingered hand unseen in the data.
- **[Vision-Based 6-DoF Grasp Pose Estimation for Robot Cloth Unfolding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31452)** — CeDiRNet-6DoF jointly predicts grasp points and full 6-DoF poses for regrasping-in-the-air cloth unfolding, with state-of-the-art results in the ICRA 2024 Cloth Competition.
- **[GraspTwin: Zero-Shot Task-Oriented Grasp Optimization via a Digital Twin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30543)** — Uses a foundation model for task-relevant grasp proposals as semantic priors, then refines them with Bayesian optimization over domain-randomized physics rollouts for a real-to-sim-to-real pipeline.

### Tactile Sensing

- **[Tactile Sensing Array for Multi-Phalanx Sensing in Humanoid Hands](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30506)** — A low-cost Velostat/conductive-tape array covering fingertip and proximal/middle phalanges; a compliant 3D-printed contact structure cuts recovery time by 74% over a flush baseline.
- **[TACTIC: Understanding Tactile Encoders and Conditioning for Contact-rich Robot Manipulation Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30969)** — A controlled study of vision-based tactile encoders and fusion schemes across 2000+ real-world rollouts, finding no universally optimal representation.
- **[PHASE: Compliance-Enabled Tactile Phase Retrieval for Few-Shot Insertion Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30889)** — Uses a compliant wrist's sustained contact signals to segment insertion into phases and retrieve phase-consistent experience, +13 points over the strongest non-phase-aware baseline.
- **[Can a Robot Read Braille? - Learning to Adapt Contact via Imitation Learning for Tactile Braille Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30676)** — An adaptive-contact framework where multi-head policy learning assesses and corrects contact before recognition, reaching 88.6% tactile reconstruction.
- **[Frequency-Modulated Piezoelectric Haptic Display](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30626)** — Encodes haptic intensity via vibration frequency with a shared-source FM structure, enabling denser wearable displays.

### Grasping, Cross-Embodiment Transfer & Policy Learning

- **[POIL: Point-based One-Shot Imitation Learning with Stable Dynamical Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30404)** — Uses 3D points on an object's functional part as a shared representation for both one-shot trajectory transfer and closed-loop execution with stable dynamics.
- **[Enabling a Unified Cross-Domain Representation for Two-Finger Gripper Manipulation via Interaction-Centric Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31207)** — A canonical gripper-frame representation with a VLM-inferred interaction triplet, enabling zero-shot sim-to-real transfer to heterogeneous two-finger platforms.
- **[Latent Policy Steering: An Efficient and Flexible Framework for Cross-Embodiment Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22521)** — An embodiment-agnostic world model pretrained on optical flow, finetuned per target robot, then used to steer any base policy at test time with only 50 demonstrations.
- **[Policy-Calibrated DAgger: Offline Calibrated Noise Injection for Imitation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30462)** — Estimates a diffusion policy's own noise distribution offline via partial denoising, matching the best hindsight noise level without sweeping.
- **[Impedance Cloning: Learning Equilibrium Point Parameters for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30842)** — Imitates stiffness and equilibrium point rather than trajectory, extracting them from bilateral teleoperation via a particle filter without force sensors.
- **[Imp-ACT: Adaptive Impedance Control and Action Chunking with Transformers to Learn Contact-Rich Manipulation from Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31225)** — Records direction-dependent Cartesian stiffness during teleoperation and predicts stiffness alongside pose and gripper actions, cutting contact-force vibration by ~29x.
- **[Learning to Leverage Compliance: A Policy-Admittance Learning Framework for Robotic Insertion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31439)** — Adds conflict and high-force-tail costs to discourage unproductive policy-loading/controller-unloading opposition, reaching 94% success on connector assembly.
- **[HIRE: History-Conditioned Interaction Reasoning and High-Rate Execution for Visually Aliased Precision Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30828)** — A cross-rate framework pairing a history-conditioned interaction-state reasoner with a high-rate execution module for contact-critical tasks with visual aliasing.
- **[Learning Robot Policies from Sparse Success Signals via STL-Guided Stein Variational Policy Gradient](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31606)** — A population-based method using smooth STL robustness as a trajectory-level objective, best on five of six quadcopter and manipulator benchmarks.

### Aerial & Whole-Body Manipulation

- **[Aerial Manipulation in the Wild with Onboard Perception, Policy Learning, and Whole-Body Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30521)** — Integrates a Diffusion Policy with onboard LiDAR-inertial odometry and whole-body MPC for outdoor aerial manipulation without motion-capture infrastructure.
- **[HuGo: LLMs as Whole-Body Policy Code Designers for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30594)** — An LLM generates closed-loop high-level policy code over a frozen low-level whole-body policy, refined from rollouts without task-specific reward design or demonstrations.
- **[Praxis: Distilling Physical Interaction Priors from Egocentric Videos for Generalizable Whole-Body Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30735)** — Combines one-shot egocentric video priors with closed-loop posture calibration and tactile feedback for long-horizon mobile humanoid manipulation.
- **[Generate, Track, Improve: Perceptive Multi-Skill Humanoid Locomotion with RL-Fine-Tuned Motion Generators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31577)** — Optimizes multimodal motion generators via an off-policy RL loop, reporting unified designs and training strategies for multi-skill humanoid locomotion.
- **[TAGA: Terrain-aware Active Gaze Learning for Generalizable Agile Humanoid Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05880)** — RL alone lets a humanoid learn to attend to informative regions of height scans, enabling a reported 1.2 m real-world gap traversal.
- **[Critic Architecture Matters: Dual vs. Unified Critics for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11891)** — Compares dual and unified critics on a Unitree G1, and honestly flags that the comparison is confounded with the PPO update rule.
- **[Grounded Action Model: 3D Grounding as a Foundation for Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23863)** — Builds robot foundation models around an object-centric 3D grounding representation, with strong gains under scene randomization and visual shift.

A quick observation across today's robotics papers: the strongest recurring theme is **failure recovery and runtime monitoring** (Kintsugi-VLA, RoboMonitor, Hide-and-Seek, Causeway, EffectMatch), and a second is **cross-embodiment transfer via masking or latent alignment** (Cloak, VLaRL, Latent Policy Steering, the two-finger gripper representation). Tactile work also clusters around **phase-aware, contact-conditioned retrieval** rather than raw recognition.
