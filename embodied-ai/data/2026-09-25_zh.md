# 今日 arXiv 机器人学与人工智能论文总结

## 一、具身智能与世界模型

- **KnowBody**：让冻结权重的 VLM 显式建模机器人身体结构与动作关系，四真实任务成功率从 25% 提升至 75%。[Know Your Body: A Harness for Direct and Self-Improving Robot Control with VLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28530)

- **OCC4M**：面向长时程操作的物体中心 4D 记忆，显式建模时序、运动与包含关系，仿真端到端成功率 88.9%（基线仅 57.7%）。[OCC4M: Object-Centric 4D Memory for Spatiotemporal Reasoning in Long-Horizon Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28798)

- **HarnessPAI**：模型与本体无关的 Physical AI Harness 框架，将代码作为可执行可演化的接口，LIBERO-PRO 上较 π₀.₅ 提升 61.6 分。[HarnessPAI: An Evolving Harness for Physical AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29166)

- **RWM**：在表征空间直接学习状态、转移与可执行规划，无需递归 rollout 或动作空间搜索。[Representation World Model: Learning States, Transition and Executable Plans in Representation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29171)

- **WAA（World Action Agent）**：多智能体 harness，让 VLM 在"视觉动作工作区"中预演并修正动作，LIBERO-Pro 达 75.6%。[World Action Agent: Harnessing VLMs for Robot Manipulation via World Action Rehearsal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29964)

- **世界模型评估综述**：梳理 160 个基准，指出仅 7% 构建了 VLA vs 世界模型的显式对比，反事实能力几乎未被测量。[Do World Models Make Better Robots? A Survey of Evaluation Benchmarks for Predictive Embodied Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29669)

- **训练物体永久性**：构建 WROP 数据集（150 个认知科学任务）训练视频世界模型，发布 16B 模型 PWM-WROP。[Training Object Permanence in World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28654)

- **Causal-Plan-Bench**：指出当前具身规划基准偏爱语言 next-token 预测而非物理因果推理，GPT-6-astra 仅得 43.04。[Planning Takes More Than Token Prediction: Causal Plan for Benchmarking and Building Physically Grounded Embodied Reasoners](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01810)

- **Omni-Decision**：以"证据账本"替代膨胀的对话历史做全模态规划，OmniGAIA 达 81.4% 且成本约为 Gemini-3.1-Pro 的 43%。[Omni-Decision: Evidence-Ledger Planning for Omni-Modal Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11433)

- **HumanSystem（BEHAVE）**：把交互人群建模为可观测的复杂动力系统对象，位于感知与动作之间的"人侧对象"。[BEHAVE: Real-Time Modeling of Human Systems as Observable Complex Dynamical Systems and Operational Objects for Physical AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12730)

- **ContactWorld**：系统性研究视觉-触觉世界模型的表征选择，点云 + 结构化触觉力场表现最佳。[ContactWorld: What Representations Matter for Vision-Tactile Latent World Models in Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13877)

**动作模型（WAM/VLA 架构）**

- **Streaming-WAM**：把视频-动作联合去噪与异步控制耦合，LIBERO 达 98.35% 且平均时延降至 1/2.93。[Streaming-WAM: Action-Conditioned World-Action Model for Asynchronous Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28927)

- **Rolling-WAM**：滑动窗口交错噪声调度，稳态重规划提速 4.5 倍。[Rolling-WAM: World Action Models with Rolling Imagination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30247)

- **DeltaWAM**：预测视觉增量而非稠密未来帧，训练 FLOPs 降 17.8-23.8%，推理时延降 36.6%。[DeltaWAM: Delta World Action Models for Bimanual Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28811)

- **LeWAM**：用 JEPA 嵌入替代视频扩散骨干，仅 0.4B 可训练参数，RoboTwin 2.0 达 92.28%。[Latent evolving World Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27455)

- **AD-WM**：动作判别性世界模型，用逆向动力学正则提升反事实 MPC，OGBench-Cube 硬启动成功率 3.7%→52.0%。[AD-WM: Action-Discriminative World Models for Counterfactual Model Predictive Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30264)

## 二、灵巧手、灵巧操作与多指抓取

- **Morphometric Imitation**：人类手-物交互经形态优化 + 残差 RL + 蒸馏，得到零样本 sim-to-real 视运动策略，300 次真机试验成功率 89.3%。[Morphometric Imitation: From Morphology and Contact Aware Hand Retargeting to Sim-to-Real Visuomotor Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28660)

- **全手力调节**：无触觉传感，仅靠物体模型与本体感受几何估计接触，实时重算接触力分配，27-DoF 臂手系统在扰动下维持并恢复抓取。[Real-Time Force Regulation for Whole-Hand Dexterous Grasping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30082)

- **FingerViP**：指尖微型相机模块提供多视角近距视觉，扩散式全身视运动策略，遮挡场景平均成功率相对基线提升 56.6%。[FingerViP: Learning Real-World Dexterous Manipulation with Fingertip Visual Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21331)

- **Res-HIL**：人在回路残差 RL，在冻结模仿策略上学习修正动作，20 条演示 + 10 分钟在线训练即超越用 5 倍演示训练的模仿策略。[Res-HIL: Human-Guided Residual Reinforcement Learning for Sample-Efficient Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30023)

- **冲击感知抓取**：提出"结果敏感窗口"（OSW）以构造演示，IL 策略在抓取成功率与冲击缓解上超过特权 RL 教师。[Outcome-Sensitive Motion Search for Impact-Aware Dexterous Catching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29020)

- **Aero Hand Open**：仿真就绪的腱驱仿人手，含线缆传动仿真模型、辨识驱动映射与 RL 训练包，可仿真直接部署无需微调。[Aero Hand Open: A Simulation-Ready Tendon-Driven Hand for Dexterous Manipulation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28578)

- **仿生前臂**：八块独立腕骨 + 22 条驱动肌肉的软体前臂，复现人类腕关节自适应刚度调制，指出腕骨形态的主导作用。[Anthropomimetic Soft Robotic Forearm with Independently Articulated Carpal Bones Enabling Human-Like Adaptive Stiffness Modulability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29176)

## 三、触觉感知与接触反馈

- **DA-GRD**：在视觉失效时仅用稀疏触觉探测恢复抓取姿态，MuJoCo 物理抓取成功率 84.7%（陈旧 AnyGrasp 仅 9.1%），触觉探测数减少 72.5%。[DA-GRD: Decision-Aware Grasp-Relevant Disambiguation for tactile recovery under perception-to-execution mismatches](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29065)

- **PolyUMI**：开源视觉-触觉-听觉演示采集平台（无线手持夹爪），配套 VisTA 令牌级多模态策略，验证触觉与音频提供视觉之外的信息。[PolyUMI: Accessible Visual-Tactile-Audio Data Collection for Object Inference and Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29760)

- **PROPRA**：以本体感受为锚点自监督预训练指尖触觉与接近觉编码器，解决稀疏相位依赖信号难以利用的问题。[Self-Supervised Anchoring of Fingertip Sensing to Proprioception and Proactive Actions for Robot Imitation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29822)

- **TacSushi**：触觉接地的世界-动作模型用于寿司操作，特征级门控融合指尖触觉，同分布成功率 68.3%。[TacSushi: Tactile-Grounded World-Action Modeling for Dexterous Sushi Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19613)

- **SpectRobot**：将单点高带宽触觉信号转为时频谱图，可复用视觉编码器，在视觉被遮挡任务中仅靠单点振动完成操作。[Learning tactile perception from high-bandwidth single-point sensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24621)

- **TactileStep**：足底压力传感引入人形运动控制，峰值落地力降低 48.8%。[TactileStep: Sole Tactile Learning for Regulating Foot-Terrain Interaction in Humanoid Locomotion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28959)

## 四、视觉-运动策略与 VLA

- **Self-Adaptive VLA**：用自身 rollout 作为上下文自适应硬件偏移，在四类双臂/灵巧任务中恢复 80% 以上基础策略性能。[Self-Adaptive VLA for Robust Robot Deployment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30092)

- **Riemannian MeanFlow Policy**：在机器人动作流形上学习条件流映射，单次函数评估即生成在流形上的动作序列。[Faster Visuomotor Policy Learning on Action Manifolds via Riemannian MeanFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30127)

- **Training-free Behavior Cloning**：提出 Behavior Predictive Control，结合动作感知检索、Hankel 动作延续先验与闭式一步残差修正，无需端到端训练，Jetson Orin Nano 上闭环 75+ Hz。[Training-free Behavior Cloning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30134)

- **ActGaze**：用反事实视觉干预从 VLA 自身动作目标导出注视监督，四类精密操作任务上优于基础 VLA 与其他视觉接地方法。[ActGaze: Learning Action-Grounded Gaze through Counterfactual Visual Interventions for High-Precision Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28955)

- **KeyGen**：从点云学习规范化语义 3D 关键点作为物体中心表征，提升类别级策略泛化。[KeyGen: Unsupervised Keypoint based Object-Centric Representations for Category-Level Policy Generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28818)

- **DSD（方向-尺度分解）**：将平移/旋转增量分解为方向与尺度后再 tokenize，混合数据集训练下 SimplerEnv 提升 10.3 分。[Direction-Scale Decomposition in Action Representation: Rethinking What to Tokenize for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28865)

- **Decoupled Early Exits**：将 VLA 骨干深度 V、动作专家深度 A、去噪步数 D 作为三条可联合配置的计算轴，时延降低 79.2% 且成功率提升 5.6%。[Decoupled Early Exits for Task-Dependent Compute Allocation in Flow-Matching VLAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29382)

- **AdaHVLA**：自适应 harness，通过机器人经验精炼代码化协调策略，NaVILA-LH 成功率 22.5%→57.5%。[AdaHVLA: Adaptive Harnesses for Long-Horizon Vision-Language-Action Execution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29204)

- **Harness VLA**：把冻结 VLA 作为可重试的接触丰富原语，配合小型解析原语库，LIBERO-Pro 提升 38.6 分。[Harness VLA: Steering Frozen VLAs into Reliable Manipulation Primitives via Memory-Guided Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08448)

- **StageCraft**：免训练方法，用 VLM 上下文推理在初始状态中预先操纵干扰物/障碍物，真实任务绝对提升 40%。[StageCraft: Execution Aware Mitigation of Distractor and Obstruction Failures in VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20659)

- **RotVLA**：连续旋转潜动作（SO(n) 元素）作为 VLA 的统一动作空间，仅 1.7B 参数，LIBERO 98.2%。[RotVLA: Rotational Latent Action for Vision-Language-Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13403)

- **VLANeXt**：系统梳理 VLA 设计空间，提炼 12 条关键发现构建强 VLA 模型。[VLANeXt: Recipes for Building Strong VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18532)

- **VLA 在线微调中的任务坍缩**：不确定性门控探索噪声可抑制流匹配 VLA 在线 RL 微调中的任务坍缩。[Uncertainty-Gated Exploration Noise Suppresses Task Collapse in Online RL Fine-Tuning of a Flow-Matching Vision-Language-Action Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28838)

- **多任务策略蒸馏**：从单任务 CFM 专家蒸馏速度场到共享多任务策略，提升 RLBench 多任务性能且模型尺寸不变。[Distillation for Efficient Multitask Manipulation Policies via Conditional Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28107)

## 五、Sim-to-Real 与策略迁移

- **OSRAM**：把部署机器人与策略视为统一闭环动力系统，学习任务级命令-响应行为并在线优化参考指令，无需微调策略。[Online Sim-to-Real Adaptation via Closed-Loop System Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28878)

- **拖动观测对齐**：直驱夹爪上的简单力矩观测对齐（力矩常数标定 + Δτ 消除偏置 + 高斯噪声注入），零样本抓取成功率 100%。[Simple Torque-Observation Alignment for Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Grasping with a Direct-Drive Gripper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29031)

- **CALM**：以电机电流作为关节负载反馈学习长时程超大物体举升，仿真成功率 76.2%、真机 73.3%。[CALM: Current Aligned Link Manipulation for Single Arm Oversized Object Lifting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29017)

- **MPC-Injection**：将 MPC 生成的转移注入 off-policy RL 回放，把策略导向可部署行为盆地，真机 Go2 验证。[MPC-Injection: Biasing Off-Policy Locomotion RL Toward Controller-Induced Behavior Basins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26392)

- **SCoCaT**：识别终止式约束 RL 在终端导航任务中的"可行性坍缩"，加入稠密成功信号辅助价值评论家解决。[SCoCaT: Success Conditioned Constrained Reinforcement Learning for Spacecraft Docking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06061)

- **流式深度 RL 持续学习**：首次系统分析流式深度 RL 用于机器人自适应持续学习，四足运动成功率最高提升 90%。[An Analysis of Streaming Deep Reinforcement Learning for Adaptive Continual Learning in Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28807)

- **DSC-Gate / 动作信用更新**：提出"分支敏感性"判断动作信用何时需更新，平均减少 39.4% 新工具调用步数。[When Does Action Credit Need Updating?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29007)

**腿足与运动**

- **DAWN**：通过去噪 + 对比对齐构建噪声鲁棒世界模型，在 Unitree Go1 上实现零样本跑酷（18cm 台阶、70cm 间隙）。[DAWN: Noise-Robust Quadruped Parkour via Depth-Denoising World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29092)

- **Gated Memory 人形跑酷**：显著性引导时序感知 + 门控记忆，仅用机载深度穿越稀疏落脚点。[Echo in the Steps: Learning Perceptive Humanoid Parkour with Gated Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28960)

- **物理引导残差 RL**：两阶段训练框架结合模板落脚规划器与 RL 落脚追踪/修正，Unitree G1 成功穿越 0.2m 宽 3m 长横梁 20 次无失败。[Physics-Guided Residual Reinforcement Learning for Humanoid Narrow-Path Traversal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20661)

## 七、抓取与操作规划

- **CAMP**：面向受限空间的臂-手协同运动规划，提出可行手纤维表示 + VMP 粗到细联合优化，六任务规划成功率 84.2-98.5%。[CAMP: Cooperative Arm-Hand Motion Planning in Constrained Spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29021)

- **ReVAMP**：通过解析 IK 重参数化实现向量加速运动规划，高维系统（20 维）微秒至毫秒级，比 SOTA 快 10 倍。[ReVAMP: Vector-Accelerated Motion Planning for Kinematically-Constrained Systems via Reparameterization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30213)

- **Wrap**：无夹具多机器人装配规划，线性规划推理抓取受力，贪心 + 启发式搜索。[WRAP: Fixtureless Wrench-aware Multi-Robot Assembly Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29407)

- **Coding Agents for TAMP**：编码智能体自动合成跨实例泛化的 TAMP 程序，平均成功率 56-95%（对比规划器 47%）。[Coding Agents for Generalized Task and Motion Planning Problems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30233)

- **TapeSim**：胶带分发的仿真器，刚性簇 + 可形变领口实现解卷与脱离，物理步提速 3.2-8.4 倍。[TAPESIM: Efficient Simulation of Adhesive Tape Dispensing for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28766)

- **布料抓取接口**：仿真器无关的布料抓取模型，仅需离散布料位置与位置约束机制即可实现。[A Simple Gripper Interface for Simulator-Agnostic Cloth Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29340)

- **TRACE**：双向电缆追踪 + 交互感知原语（分歧推挤、簇膨胀），复杂场景（4 线 40 交叉）正确追踪长度从 ~60% 升至 ~90%。[TRACE: Interactive Bi-Directional Tracing of Monochrome Cables Amid Clutter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29103)

- **RoboRecover**：机器人策略恢复基准，包含 RoboTwin 与 LIBERO 共 2000 场景，专门评估执行偏差下的恢复能力。[RoboRecover: Benchmarking Robot Policy Recovery under Execution Deviations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28952)

- **AquaMend**：在探测-信念-动作图上比较重探、回滚与继续，32 个配对场景中恢复 28 个，平均损失较重启减少 21.6%。[AquaMend: Minimal Re-probing and Conditional Rollback for Latent-Belief Failures in Embodied Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28973)

- **Body-Grounded Replanning**：用内部物理状态（关节负载、移动性）触发高层策略重规划，保持任务目标与底层控制器不变。[Body-Grounded Replanning for Physically Adaptive Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30024)

- **接触选择作为决策变量**：提出 CTCS，在残差力旋量、末端可达性与基座移动性三能力间权衡选择支撑接触，Unitree Go2 + AgileX NERO 验证约 3 倍加速。[Contact as a Decision Variable: Capability-Tradeoff Contact Selection for Legged Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30140)

- **受限空间布料操作**：推理时仿真器在环细化，FLASH 仿真器 + 实到仿模块 + 先验引导 MPPI，支持中途折叠扰动闭环修正。[Enabling Robust Cloth Manipulation via Inference-Time Simulator-in-the-Loop Refinement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24552)

- **Flow as Flow**：将机器人流建模为概率速度场（流匹配），生成速度比标准流匹配快约 24 倍，13 个操作任务 260 次试验优于基线。[Flow as Flow: Modeling Robot Velocity Fields as Probability Velocity Fields for Flow-Based Object Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23090)

- **Koopman 加速 MBD**：双线性 Koopman 提升实现实时基于模型的扩散，50ms 控制周期内 14.7ms 完成规划更新，真机机械臂唯一满足跟踪与截止时间。[Koopman-Accelerated Model-Based Diffusion for Real-Time Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28920)
