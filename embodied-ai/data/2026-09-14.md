### **World Models & Simulators for Embodied AI**

These papers present new simulators and world models designed to improve policy learning, evaluation, and sim-to-real transfer by predicting future states and consequences of actions.

- **[Pelican-Sim 1.0: A General World Model Simulator for Embodied Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12036)**: Introduces a general-purpose world model that predicts future observations from visual context and robot actions. It uses a unified action space, action-visual injection, and a sparse MoE architecture to improve controllability across different robot embodiments.
- **[IMPLY: Physically Anchored Consistency for World-Model Rollouts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12441)**: Argues that self-consistency in world models is insufficient for physical realism. It proposes a method that reads the implied physics from a rollout and scores it based on how well a single object model explains all observed outcomes, anchored to calibration data.
- **[GigaBrain-WBC-0.5: A Behavior World Model for Robust Whole-Body Control with Environment Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18234)**: Presents a Behavior World Model for humanoid control that predicts actions, states, and future behavior commands. This allows the robot to interact robustly with its environment and retract implausible commands, achieving high success rates in terrain interaction and fall recovery.
- **[CLAP: Cross-Embodiment Video World Models are Zero-Shot Physical Simulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27406)**: Introduces a framework for cross-embodiment video generation. It uses a curriculum-based learning recipe with latent and end-effector actions to train a model on diverse human and robot videos, enabling zero-shot deployment for real-world tasks.
- **[EVIS: Real-Time Event Camera Simulation with Multimodal Supervision in NVIDIA Isaac Sim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08098)**: An open-source event camera simulator for Isaac Sim. It uses renderer-provided motion vectors and depth maps for high-throughput, real-time event generation, aiding sim-to-real transfer for event-based perception models.
- **[LettuceVisSim: A Simulator That Generates Lettuce Image Time-series for Vision-Based Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12505)**: A lettuce growth simulator that generates labeled time-series of crop images to address the scarcity of labeled data for vision-based RL in controlled environment agriculture.
- **[MPT: Missing Prototype Tracking via Barycentric Reconstruction in Vehicular Federated Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12771)**: A cross-vehicle federated learning framework that maintains rare-class recognition by reconstructing class prototypes, addressing the issue of transient vehicle participation in federated learning.

### **Dexterous & Multi-Finger Manipulation**

This section includes papers on dexterous hands, in-hand manipulation, and grasp synthesis.

- **[ArtManip: Category-Level Articulated In-Hand Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12498)**: The first method for category-level in-hand manipulation of articulated objects. It uses an automated pipeline to generate diverse grasping configurations and a two-stage training strategy to handle complex contact dynamics, achieving zero-shot transfer to real-world objects.
- **[STAR: Sparse Tactile Representation Learning in Vision-Tactile-Language-Action Models for Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12549)**: Introduces a large-scale dataset with 200 hours of bimanual dexterous manipulation and a training recipe for Vision-Tactile-Language-Action models. It addresses tactile sparsity through joint pre-training and sparse token representation, achieving a 61% success rate on real-world tasks.
- **[Adversarial Game-Theoretic Algorithm for Dexterous Grasp Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05809)**: Proposes a grasp synthesis approach that formulates the problem as a two-player game. One player controls the robot hand, while an adversary controls the object to seek motions that attempt to escape the grasp, leading to more stable grasps.
- **[Control Architecture for Safe Grasping of Fragile Objects Using a Coarse Position-Controlled Gripper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12737)**: Presents a geometry-aware force/torque-based contact estimation method with an adaptive admittance controller for safe grasping of fragile objects with unknown properties.
- **[Robust Underwater Grasping of Sloped Objects with a Waterproof Passive Adaptive Gripper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12927)**: Introduces a waterproof gripper with a passive rotational joint for global self-alignment and variable-stiffness pads for local adaptation, enabling robust grasping of sloped objects in submerged environments.
- **[From Transportation to Manipulation: Enabling Grasping in Magnetic Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12883)**: Presents the Gripper MagBot, a low-cost parallel 6-DoF manipulator with an integrated gripper for magnetic levitation (MagLev) systems, enabling grasping and manipulation directly on these systems.
- **[Before the Tipping Point: Force-Guided Active Perception for Shape-Agnostic Estimation of 3D Centers of Mass](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12894)**: A force-based method for estimating the 3D center of mass of unknown objects by applying a sub-critical tipping experiment with a robot manipulator, enabling reliable non-prehensile manipulation.

### **Visuomotor Policies & VLA Models**

Papers in this group focus on learning policies that map visual and other sensory inputs to actions, including new architectures, training methods, and evaluation techniques.

- **[RodForesight: A World Model Enhanced Diffusion Policy for Slender and Material Agnostic Rod Insertion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12103)**: A learning framework for slender rod insertion that uses a diffusion policy and an action-conditioned world model to predict the effects of candidate actions, improving success rates for this precise manufacturing task.
- **[DIA: Denoising Intermediate Advantage for Diffusion Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12245)**: A policy-gradient method for fine-tuning diffusion policies. It learns a value function over partially denoised actions to provide credit assignment at each step of the generative process, outperforming existing methods.
- **[DATAFARM: Distribution-Aligned Task and Motion Planning for Fine-Tuning Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12316)**: Addresses the distribution mismatch between planner-generated trajectories and VLA pretraining data. It aligns generated trajectories with the pretraining data in joint configurations, motion style, and temporal profiles, making TAMP an effective source of data for VLA fine-tuning.
- **[Breaking the Vision-Action Shortcut: Latent Interface Training for Generalizable Robotics Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12641)**: Proposes a two-stage training strategy to prevent robot foundation models from exploiting task-irrelevant visual cues. It first learns a goal-conditioned action prior without images, then constrains visual conditioning through a pose-supervised latent interface, improving generalization.
- **[Online Material Estimation for Conditioned Diffusion Policy in Shaping Deformable Linear Objects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12634)**: A diffusion policy for shaping deformable linear objects that is conditioned on material labels estimated online from images and joint states. This allows a single policy to adapt its actions based on material properties.
- **[Dynin-Robotics: Omnimodal Unified Diffusion Vision-Language-Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13053)**: An omnimodal masked-diffusion backbone that represents language, visual observations, goals, and actions as discrete tokens. It supports test-time scaling through goal prediction and joint action-next-state refinement, achieving competitive performance on LIBERO and other benchmarks.
- **[Improving Imitation Learning Efficiency for Manipulation through Geometric Prior Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12721)**: Investigates using a geometric visual pretraining dataset (planes, objects, and a cube-shaped hand) to provide a useful initialization for imitation learning policies, improving sample efficiency for new tasks.
- **[Modular Sensory Stream for Integrating Physical Feedback in Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23272)**: Introduces a framework that adapts VLAs to leverage multiple physical signals (e.g., tactile, force, torque) through decoupled modality streams and a two-stage training scheme, improving contact-rich manipulation.
- **[AdaVLA: Adaptive Step Flow Matching for Training-free Acceleration of Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29208)**: A training-free framework for accelerating flow-matching-based VLA models. It uses a metric based on trajectory curvature to dynamically reduce inference steps and prune MLPs, achieving significant speedups with negligible performance degradation.
- **[NS-VLA: Towards Neuro-Symbolic Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09542)**: Proposes a neuro-symbolic VLA framework with a plan-constrained primitive inference encoder and a backbone-agnostic policy solver, improving one-shot training, generalization, and exploration.
- **[HuRo: Robotizing Human Videos for Scalable VLA Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10706)**: Develops a pipeline to convert heterogeneous human videos into robot-aligned observations and action trajectories. Pretraining on this robotized data improves real-world manipulation task completion and out-of-distribution generalization.
- **[SIDE: Sensor Impersonation Detection at the Edge via Sequence Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06271)**: A sequence-prediction model using LSTMs to detect sensor impersonation in IoT deployments by flagging data streams with high prediction errors.

### **Teleoperation & Latent Space**

This section covers teleoperation strategies and methods for learning abstract representations.

- **[Understanding Whole-Body Robot Teleoperation Strategies Under Diverse Task Objectives and Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12384)**: Investigates control strategies for complex whole-body teleoperation (perception, bimanual manipulation, navigation) of a mobile manipulator, finding that coordinated control improves task efficiency.
- **[Breaking the Token Ceiling: Distilling Smaller, Stronger Byte Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12303)**: A large-scale study comparing token and byte-based models for distillation. It finds that byte models eventually surpass token models with more compute and are more data-efficient, though they perform worse in the low-FLOP regime.
- **[Expert-Level Crisis Detection in Mental Health Conversations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10380)**: Introduces a clinician-annotated benchmark for turn-level crisis detection in multi-turn dialogues, showing that identifying when risk emerges is much harder than recognizing its existence.

### **Sim-to-Real & Real-World Robotics**

These papers focus on transferring policies from simulation to the real world or improving performance in real-world robotic systems.

- **[A Physics-Based Closed-Loop Robotic Bioprinting Framework Towards Volumetric Muscle Loss Treatment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12159)**: A closed-loop robotic bioprinting system that uses a 3D point cloud camera for quantitative evaluation and a controller to adapt printing parameters in near real-time, achieving convergence in all trials.
- **[An Automated Thickness Evaluation Procedure Using an Integrated Structured Light 3D Camera in a Robotic Bioprinting Framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12206)**: A fully automated vision-based method for measuring the thickness of bioprinted constructs with complex geometries, using a structured light 3D camera and novel geometry-based measurement algorithms.
- **[Size Doesn't Matter: Material-State Reinforcement Learning for Excavator Transferable Soil Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12677)**: Uses RL in a GPU-parallelized Material Point Method simulation to learn controllers for soil manipulation that are conditioned on material state. The policies transfer across machines, from a tabletop robot to an 11.5t excavator, for autonomous construction tasks.
- **[FoldNet++: a Large-Scale Synthetic Dataset for Robotic T-Shirt Folding and Unfolding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12433)**: Presents a large-scale synthetic dataset for robotic T-shirt folding and unfolding, covering 6 embodiments and 120K episodes. Policies trained solely on this synthetic data achieve over 90% success in unseen real-world environments.
- **[DWMP: Leveraging Dual World Models for Humanoid Obstacle Traversal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12347)**: A framework for humanoid obstacle traversal that uses separate world models for proprioceptive (Koopman-based) and visual (RSSM-based) observations, improving performance in simulation and on a Unitree G1 robot.
- **[DropVLA: An Action-Level Backdoor Attack on Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10932)**: An action-level backdoor attack that forces a reusable action primitive to execute at attacker-chosen decision points, achieving high attack success rates with minimal data poisoning and without degrading nominal task performance.
- **[Driving Context-guided Model Predictive Planning and Control for Autonomous Car Racing at the Limit and Beyond](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12660)**: Presents a Model Predictive Control pipeline for autonomous car racing that adapts to different driving contexts (overtaking, nominal, countersteering) and is validated on a fully autonomous racecar.
- **[OA-NBV: Occlusion-Aware Next-Best-View Planning for Human-Centered Active Perception on Mobile Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11072)**: An occlusion-aware next-best-view planning pipeline that autonomously selects the next traversable viewpoint to obtain a more complete view of an occluded human, improving success rates over baselines in simulation and real-world trials.
