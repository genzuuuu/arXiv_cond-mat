# arXiv Robotics & AI Papers — Embodied Intelligence Focus

## Vision-Language-Action Models & Spatial Reasoning

**[GaussVLA: Geometry-Aware Spatial Reasoning for Vision-Language-Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24959)** — Introduces a Mamba-based VLA with a Gaussian Spatial Tokenizer that lifts semantic/depth features into 3D Gaussian tokens, plus Depth-Aware Chain-of-Thought. Achieves 93.5% on LIBERO with only 200M parameters, improving 19.7% over SpatialVLA.

**[GaussianDream++: Efficient 3D Gaussian World Modeling for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25659)** — Inserts World State/Prediction Tokens into a VLA backbone with a training-only world representation head over shared Gaussian primitives. Achieves 98.6% on LIBERO and 87.8% on LIBERO-Plus, with only 20 world tokens at inference.

**[VISTA: Visually Inferred Spatial ConTact Attention for Contact-Rich Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25872)** — Uses a Visual Deformation Field of a compliant gripper as visuo-physical feedback for imitation learning, outperforming 3D Diffusion Policy and tactile baselines on grasping, cap unscrewing, and calligraphy.

**[RA-VLA: Retrieval-Augmented VLA for Test-Time Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25585)** — Integrates behavior-aligned context retrieval with a grounded execution pipeline for training-free adaptation, outperforming baselines on LIBERO and a real UR5e.

**[Learning to Act While Waiting: RL Finetuning of Generalist Policies Under Inference Latency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23831)** — Introduces Asynchronous RL with Intermediate Information (ARLI) to enable RL-based improvement of VLA policies under severe inference delays, restoring near-Markovian structure via state augmentation.

**[Hierarchical Skill Retrieval for Data-Efficient Adaptation of VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24042)** — Decomposes target tasks into skill sequences, uses hybrid retrieval combining subtask-level language matching with behavior-feature reranking, improving average success by 10.3% on LIBERO and 21.3% on real-world tasks.

**[PonderPounce: A Pretrained MLLM as an Episode Context Engine for Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24115)** — Reuses an MLLM's native causal context as robot memory, with a System2/System1 architecture achieving 60.83% on RoboMME and p50 latencies of 78ms/25ms for cognition/action.

**[LM-X: Explainable Action Modeling with Progress, Event, and Uncertainty Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25757)** — Emits return-to-go, event-to-go, and heteroscedastic action flow estimates that condition action generation. Achieves 74.1% across 50 RoboTwin2.0 tasks vs 55.4% for GR00T.

**[PonderPounce / RoboMME-Interference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22338)** — Benchmarks robot memory under interference using a cross-session benchmark built on RoboMME, showing perceptual memory variants decay strongly as unrelated sessions accumulate.

## Tactile Sensing & Contact-Rich Manipulation

**[TacForcing: Streaming Action Generation with Execution-Time Tactile Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25798)** — Replaces standard action experts with a streaming variant conditioned on evolving tactile observations during execution, with Execution-Aware Tactile Attention reducing temporal mismatch. Achieves 65% on simulated UniVTAC tasks, 69% on real tasks.

**[A Tendon-Driven Five-Fingered Hand with Distributed Tactile Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25547)** — Soft-rigid hybrid hand with dual-modality tactile sensing on distal/middle phalanges; demonstrates counting gestures, pinching, grasping, and bottle-grasp tactile recording.

**[Multi-Touch and Bending Sensing Using Electrical Impedance Tomography for Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13048)** — Framework combining deep neural networks for state classification with adaptive reference to decouple touch and deformation signals in EIT-based tactile sensors, validated on a magnetic hydrogel composite.

**[ForceFlow: Learning to Feel and Act via Contact-Driven Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11048)** — Asymmetric multimodal fusion treating force as a global regulatory signal with joint prediction, achieving 37% success improvement over ForceVLA across six real-world contact-rich tasks.

## Dexterous Manipulation & Grasping

**[Fast Generative Grasping via Lie Group-Constrained MeanFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26076)** — Samples reliable grasps in ≤5 network evaluations using MeanFlow on product Lie group SO(3)×ℝ³, achieving up to 39× speedup over diffusion/flow baselines on ACRONYM.

**[Sequential Object Placement Optimization with Convex Decomposition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25162)** — Frames object placement as differentiable nonlinear optimization with closed-form constraint derivatives (~200ns), achieving 100× speedup over grid search; demonstrated on Tangram with Allegro Hand.

**[MA-VLA: Multi-Arm Vision-Language-Action Model for Collaboration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25864)** — Decomposes cooperative behavior into atomic prompts allocated per arm, with Arm Shuffle permutation enabling compositional generalization to unseen collaboration patterns.

**[PRISM: Projection-Integrated Sampling-Based MPC for Bimanual Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25666)** — Uses QP-guided control sampling with GPU-accelerated physics simulation as online world model, with successful sim-to-real transfer on dual UR5e.

## Teleoperation & Human-Robot Collaboration

**[Generative Action-Chunk Sampling for Adaptive Stiffness Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25284)** — Samples action chunks from an observation-conditioned prior; variation among samples drives adaptive joint stiffness. Achieves 0.95 success rate on collaborative transport.

**[ROS2 Connect: A New ROS2 over WAN Solution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25102)** — WebSocket-based framework enabling transparent ROS2 interaction across routed networks for teleoperation, with lower latency than DDS Router, rosbridge, and Zenoh.

## Sim-to-Real & World Models

**[SANTS: A State-Adaptive Scheduler for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27947)** — Selects state-dependent noise trajectory points for video-to-action diffusion, reaching 94.4% on RoboTwin 2.0 while reducing latency by ~80%.

**[DELE-w0.5: Inferring Action from Future Latent State](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22067)** — Models physical state changes rather than visual appearance evolution, outperforming baselines by 47.5 percentage points on long-horizon tasks.

**[One Policy, Many Embodiments: Unified Camera-Centric Action Geometry Pre-training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26058)** — Represents manipulation through camera-observable anchor motion, treating robots/humanoids/hands as different embodiments of a common action schema. Single checkpoint reaches 98.3% on LIBERO.

**[Zero-WAM: In-Context World-Action Modeling from Human Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26103)** — Uses human videos as task specification for zero-shot generalization, achieving 47.0% on seven unseen RoboTwin 2.0 tasks.

## Simulation & Data Generation

**[CRESSim-Neo: A Batched GPU Simulation Engine for Surgical Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25192)** — Batched position-based simulation with GPU-resident data pipeline, achieving 2.03M environment steps/sec on RTX 4090.

**[Scene2Demo: Self-Evolving Embodied Data Generation via Object-Action Graph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12065)** — Constructs interactive simulated scenes from single RGB images, generating executable task configurations and robot-learning datasets.

**[Performance-guided Task-specific Optimization for Multirotor Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04724)** — Uses RL, Bayesian optimization, and CMA-ES to optimize multirotor designs guided purely by closed-loop task performance, with sim-to-real validation.

## Embodied Reasoning & Planning

**[R³: Training Robots to Reason in Natural Language via Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26053)** — Trains VLMs to reason in free-form language to guide low-level policies, using single-step rubric-based RL from offline data. Improves exploration and generalization on Language Table and bimanual grocery packing.

**[Meta-Ctrl: Guaranteed Plan Generation by Decoupling Syntactic and Semantic Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22149)** — Uses meta-tokens for constrained decoding that enforces syntax at token level and semantics at action level, making a small open-weight LM competitive with GPT-4 on WAH-NL.
