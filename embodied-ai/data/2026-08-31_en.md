# arXiv Robotics & AI Feed Summary (Embodied Intelligence Focus)

## Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models & Policy Learning

**[PHR-VLA: Planning Horizon Reasoning for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27609)** — Introduces a lightweight auxiliary future head that aligns VLA representations with latent dynamics from future observations. Patch-level supervision from wrist cameras improves LIBERO success from 84.1%→88.4% and real-world disassembly from 63.3%→82.5%, demonstrating that privileged latent dynamics alignment improves anticipatory reasoning.

**[Beyond Data Scaling: Representation-Centric Continued Pre-training for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27550)** — Proposes VLAct, a VLA-oriented VLM backbone trained on heterogeneous multi-embodiment robot data. Achieves 82.6% on LIBERO-Plus and 92.5% on RoboTwin with only 16 GPUs, and notably outperforms a full-data GR00T-N1.6 baseline on unseen humanoid embodiment using just 20% of downstream trajectories.

**[SCALE: Self-uncertainty Conditioned Adaptive Looking and Execution for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04208)** — A single-pass inference strategy that jointly modulates perception and action based on self-uncertainty, requiring no additional training or verifiers. Broadens exploration under high uncertainty while exploiting when confident, improving SOTA VLAs across simulated and real benchmarks.

**[Mind-VLA: Instruction-Aware Spatial Representation Alignment for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04633)** — Addresses the instruction-agnostic limitation of existing 3D alignment methods by aligning latent representations with target-object-specific features. Reaches 94.4% on LIBERO and improves real-world target-occlusion success by 26 percentage points over scene-level alignment.

**[DeicticVLA: Unifying Instruction Modes Based on Language and Deictic Gestures in a Single VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28108)** — Canonicalizes language, vision-language, and visual instructions into text prompts plus deictic masks, enabling a single VLA to handle all three modes. Vision-based modes achieve 100% success on unseen categories versus 16.7% for jointly trained language-only.

**[FlowCorrect: Efficient Interactive Correction of Generative Flow Policies for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22056)** — Deployment-time adaptation of flow-matching policies from sparse human corrections via VR without retraining. Achieves 80% success on previously failed cases while preserving performance on solved scenarios.

**[GRAFT: Grounded and Efficient Online Reinforcement Adaptation for Fine-Grained Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27079)** — Region-level supervision learns view-specific visual anchors for fine-grained tasks. Improves success by 32.5 percentage points across four biomedical manipulation tasks while reducing online update overhead via cached visual-language prefixes.

## Dexterous Manipulation & Robotic Hands

**[Aero Hand Open: A Simulation-Ready Tendon-Driven Hand for Dexterous Manipulation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28578)** — Releases a tendon-driven anthropomorphic hand with a complete simulation stack: cable transmission model, identified actuation map (including thumb coupling), and RL training suite. Policies train entirely in simulation and deploy with zero fine-tuning, with full mechanical design released.

**[Contact-Guided Exploration for Non-Prehensile Locomanipulation with Multi-Critic RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28140)** — Uses a dedicated exploration critic with dense contact-seeking rewards that progressively decays to recover task-optimal policies. Generalizes across box pushing, chair transport, and dishwasher opening; validates chair transport on a quadrupedal mobile manipulator in the real world.

**[Beyond Relative Geometry: Metric-Aware Geometry Perception for Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27497)** — Metric-Scale Equivariant Augmentation enables reconstruction with real-world absolute scale rather than arbitrary relative geometry. Reduces absolute error from 2.01m to 0.07m and consistently improves LIBERO, RoboTwin, and zero-shot LIBERO-Plus policies.

**[Picking Bins Empty: A Hierarchical Hybrid Approach with Online Self-Learning of Grasp Points](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28175)** — Four-tiered hierarchy combining model-based reliability with model-free deadlock resolution. Online self-learning via gripper-stroke feedback and Wilson score intervals raises bin clearance from 50.9% to 100% across three automotive parts.

**[Training-free Suction Grasp Detection for Deformed Aseptic Cartons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28246)** — Open-vocabulary VLM detection with SAM2 instance masks and geometric surface scoring for suction-point selection. Achieves 88.2% single-object grasp success and 72.6% end-to-end retrieval in clutter across three deformation levels.

## Teleoperation & Data Collection

**[RoboEdit: Turning Human Manipulation Videos into Scalable Robot Experience](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18948)** — A human-to-robot video editing suite with automatic 3D reconstruction/retargeting (RoboEdit-ADC) generating RoboEdit-14M (174K aligned pairs, 14M frames across 7 embodiments). Core engine RoboEdit-Trans preserves temporal coherence with a 3D Robot-State Decoder; supports downstream real-world control policies.

**[Remote Human and Robot Interaction for Greenhouse Gardening Using Virtual Reality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27545)** — VR teleoperation for leaf inspection achieves 88% disease detection accuracy, but soil moisture assessment is limited by plant canopy occlusion (100% success for broad-leaf vs. 16.7% for dense compound canopies), showing sensing constraints rather than operator skill dominate.

## Sim-to-Real & World Models

**[Stay Seated: Learning Omnidirectional Humanoid Locomotion on a Passive Mobile Chair](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28090)** — Extends standing velocity-tracking with passive-chair model, achieving zero-shot sim-to-real transfer on Unitree G1. Policies use only proprioception and velocity commands; foot-slip regularization combined with symmetry or command curriculum prevents degenerate convergence.

**[ChainSplat: A Physics-Inspired Screw-Theoretic Model for Learning DLO Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28570)** — Represents deformable linear objects (cables, ropes) as open-chain rigid links with revolute joints, integrated with Gaussian splatting. Jointly learns 3D geometry, appearance, kinematics, and dynamics from multi-view RGB only; enables real-time state/force estimation and model-based trajectory optimization.

**[Describe-Then-Act: Proactive Agent Steering via Distilled Language-Action World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23149)** — DILLO uses privileged VLM teachers to annotate trajectories and trains a latent-conditioned LLM to predict action outcomes in text, bypassing visual simulation. Achieves 14× speedup and improves LIBERO success by up to 15 percentage points.

## Motion Planning & Multi-Agent Coordination

**[Coordinated Motion Planning for Multi-Arm Systems via Iterative LQ Games](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27726)** — Game-theoretic framework with Riccati backward recursions yielding feedback Nash strategies. Differentiable self-collision and inter-arm penalties enable coordinated, collision-aware trajectories in high-dimensional articulated systems.

**[SIMPNet: Spatial-Informed Motion Planning Network](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12831)** — Graph neural network-based sampling heuristic embedding workspace information into configuration space via cross-attention. Evaluated on UR5e across simple and complex workspaces, outperforming state-of-the-art sampling-based planners.

## Task & Motion Planning (TAMP)

**[Plan Along the Way: Event-Triggered Foundation-Model Planning for TAMP Execution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28075)** — Modular LLM/VLM-guided reactive TAMP that treats object discovery as a distinct replanning event. Evaluated on six RLBench kitchen/grill variants with hidden objects, comparing text-only and VLM planners under identical validation and execution pipelines.

**[Macro-Operator Generation and Predicate Selection for TAMP Operator Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23629)** — Automatically discovers causally linked action pairs to compress multi-step manipulation into single planning steps. Achieves up to 4.6× planning speedup and solves long sequential tasks the baseline cannot.

## Robot Learning & Evaluation Frameworks

**[ROBUST TAMP / CoCoBench: A Cooperative Coordination Benchmark for Embodied Multi-Agent Task Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28266)** — 897 oracle-validated instances around four coordination constructs (task allocation, sequential ordering, mutual exclusion, handoff). Evaluates 11 MLLMs; coordination ability is highly construct-specific with balanced competence across types being rare.

**[AFFORDANCE20Q: Evaluating Affordance Reasoning from Physical Properties](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14240)** — 20-Questions game format hiding object identity (1,009 games, 454 objects, 59 affordances). LLMs lag humans by ~20 points; KB-anchored rule induction improves open-source models by up to 15.2 points.
