## Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Policies

Several papers push VLA pretraining, efficiency, and robustness.

- **[HuRo: Robotizing Human Videos for Scalable VLA Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10706)** — Turns heterogeneous human videos into robot-aligned observations/actions (~630K episodes), lifting real-world task completion from 51.5% to 80.3% with scale.
- **[IMLE-VLA: Fast Single-Step Action Generation for VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10915)** — Replaces iterative diffusion/flow heads with a single-step conditional generator, reaching 3.67× higher inference frequency (55 Hz) and 98.0% on LIBERO.
- **[Time-Frequency Geometric Cross-Attention for Chunked VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09925)** — A drop-in module for action-chunk decoding; improves OOD LIBERO-Plus by +6.3 and real-robot tasks by +11.67 points.
- **[VLA-Precision: Asymmetric Co-Bootstrapping for Real-World Online RL of VLAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04355)** — Online RL framework for VLA post-training; 98.3% mean success on nine high-precision chemistry tasks.
- **[FiberTune: Preserving Action-Fiber Visual Residuals in VLA Fine-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08653)** — Training-time objective to prevent visual residual collapse; improves CALVIN and physical SO-101 success.
- **[Bit-Flip Attacks on VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15475)** — Shows quantized VLAs are highly vulnerable; a few gradient-selected bit flips can drop closed-loop success to 0%.

## Generative & Diffusion Policies

- **[Beyond Noise Steering: Dual-Latent Space RL for Generative Robot Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11270)** — Adds representation-level control inside a frozen generator to accelerate online adaptation.
- **[ObstaDiff: Generalizable Diffusion Policy via Obstacle-aware Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10918)** — Target-obstacle-background decomposition for cluttered scenes; 75.4% success in greenhouse trials.
- **[LTLDiff: LTL-Guided Data Generation and Diffusion Policies for Multi-agent Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11043)** — Uses finite LTL to structure demonstration generation and condition diffusion policies.
- **[MIMIC-D: Multi-modal Imitation for Multi-agent Coordination with Decentralized Diffusion Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14159)** — Joint training with decentralized execution for multimodal multi-agent coordination.
- **[ActSafeGuard: Differentiable Constraint Enforcement for Flow-Matching Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11697)** — Integrates hard action feasibility into policy learning; achieves 100% step safety rate.
- **[HeteroPROMPT: Real-time Privacy-Preserving Heterogeneous Collaborative Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26283)** — Prompt-based feature alignment for heterogeneous collaborative perception.

## Memory, World Models & Long-Horizon Manipulation

- **[2AM: Grounding Agent-Side Memory for Steerable Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11308)** — Multimodal agent holds task memory while a single RGB action model executes; 76.3% on LIBERO-Mem.
- **[Memory as Plans: World-Action Modeling with Memory-Grounded Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11561)** — Memory-as-plans framework, SOTA 83.3% on RMBench and 78.0% on real-robot tasks.
- **[UniMPA: Unified Memory-Prediction-Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11875)** — Action-grounded transition modeling with dual memory banks.
- **[Motus2: A Self-Evolving General World Model for Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30237)** — Shared-weight policy/simulator/evaluator world model with tactile feedback.
- **[FARM: Reading Failure Signals from Frozen Robotic World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11445)** — Lightweight readout over frozen VLA-JEPA states for online failure monitoring.
- **[Compact Visuotactile World Models for Lifting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09597)** — Visuotactile world model; raises force-budgeted success from 73.3% to 93.3% in ID.

## Dexterous Manipulation, Hands & Tactile Sensing

- **[Rapid Learning of Dexterous In-Hand Pen Writing via Real-Time Jacobian Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11775)** — Embodied control via task Jacobian; sub-mm in-hand writing, no RL/IL or simulation.
- **[SEED-UMI: Sharing the Exoskeleton between Human and Robot for One-to-One Dexterous Demonstration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11753)** — Both human and robot wear the same exoskeleton; 3× data efficiency and 70% rollout success.
- **[Quasi-static Analysis of Passive Stability in an Underactuated Multi-Finger Hand](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11579)** — Analytical approach for grasping stability in a novel three-finger differential hand.
- **[Tactile Memory with Soft Robot: Robust Object Insertion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19275)** — Soft wrist plus masked tactile trajectory transformer for peg-in-hole tasks.
- **[MuJoCable: Reduced-Order Surface-Routed Cable Transmission for Tendon-Driven Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09612)** — Adds configuration-dependent cable routing to MuJoCo, closing sim-to-real gap for tendon robots.
- **[Expressive Robotic Pianist: Mastering Complex Piano Repertoire](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10844)** — Graph-mimic RL plus acoustic modeling for human-level expressive piano performance.
