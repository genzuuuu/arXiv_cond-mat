# Condensed Matter Physics Preprints — Thematic Summary

## Superconductivity: Pairing, Pair-Breaking & Hybrid Devices

Several works probe unconventional pairing. [Singlet-triplet superconductivity from repulsive interactions in a p-wave magnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14609) uses parquet RG to show superconductivity is the leading repulsive instability in p-wave magnets, generically mixing singlet/triplet. [Symmetry-Enabled Rolling of Composite Bosons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14615) proposes "rollable" 3e1h composites that keep finite mass at infinite binding, permitting Tc proportional to bandwidth. [Sign changing pairing in single layer FeSe/SrTiO3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16679) reports impurity bound states consistent with a sign-changing order parameter. [Interplay of Pair Density Wave and Uniform Superconductivity on the Kagome Lattice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15354) shows uniform pairing reorganizes PDW amplitudes and phases. [Band geometry, Anderson's theorem, and vortex nucleation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15872) generalizes Abrikosov-Gorkov physics to nontrivial Bloch geometry, constraining BCS pairing in twisted MoTe2. [Quantum geometry drives orbital paramagnetism in superconducting fluctuations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16387) identifies pair quantum geometry as a source of fluctuation paramagnetism. [Theory of critical behavior in superconducting systems] is complemented by [Evidence for time-reversal symmetry breaking and glassy dynamics in (La,Pr,Sm)3Ni2O7 films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16412), reporting TRS breaking and glassy order in nickelates.

Heterostructure and junction device papers include [Quantum-Metric-Induced Distinct Optoelectronic Responses in Triple M Valleys](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15145), [A superconducting quantum point contact in Ge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16705) (conductance-quantization-tracked supercurrent), [Nitride crystalline barrier epitaxial SIS Josephson junctions by MBE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14968), [Gatemon qubits based on in-situ ternary InAsSb/Al nanowire Josephson junctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16595), and [Emergence of Bogoliubov Fermi Surfaces in hybrid Al/InAs heterostructures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06553). [Microscopic evidence] from [55Mn-NMR/NQR on Sc3Mn3Al7Si5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15693) finds no spin-singlet pairing signature.

## Quantum Hall, Topology & Altermagnetism

[Quantum Hall Fabry-Pérot Interferometry in Monolayer WSe2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14633) realizes the first gate-defined QH interferometer in WSe2, extracting edge velocity. [Superlattice Stabilization of the Topological Quantum Hall Ferromagnet in Graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14679) shows superlattices stabilize the QHFM without large fields. [Integer and Fractional Chern Plateaus Shaped by Layer-Dependent Disorder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17217) and [Optimizing Chern Bands for Moore-Read States](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17188) address plateau structure and non-Abelian parent Hamiltonians.

Altermagnetism features prominently: [Higher-order topology induced by altermagnetism in quasicrystals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15139), [Kitaev Exchange and Altermagnetism in Sr2CoTeO6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15152), [Geometry-Selective HOTIs induced by d_xy altermagnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13438), [Odd-Parity Altermagnetism Originating from Orbital Order](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18361), [Giant spontaneous Kerr effect in altermagnetic MnTe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21021), and [Magnon equilibrium spin current in collinear antiferromagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16184).

Other topology: [Non-Bloch Topology of Frustrated Non-Hermitian Flat Bands](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15863), [Intrinsic Chiral HOTI Stabilized by a Single Diagonal Reflection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16261), [Topologically protected zero-reflection nodes for perfect crossed Andreev reflection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26702), [Reflection Zeros Set the Scattering Invariant of Finite Majorana Wires](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17001), and [Momentum-Space Mismatch Enables Non-Hermitian Valley Filtering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15036).

## Strong Correlations, Quantum Criticality & Strange Metals

[Universal Theory of Strange and Bad Metals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15851) derives T-linear resistivity and ω/T scaling from spatially random interactions with underdamped bosons. [Mott transitions in Dirac nodal-line semimetals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16033) combines DMFT/QMC with RG, finding nodal-line reconstruction. [Discovery of Kondo-Kramers Nodal Line Topological State in CeAuAl3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10368) reports a new correlated topological state. [Multi-reference variational states resolve stripe selection and local d-wave pairing in the Hubbard model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16430) finds local pairing intrinsic to half-filled walls. [Fractionalized Fermi liquids with the ghost-Gutzwiller Ansatz](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12410) reproduces cuprate phenomenology. Also [Near itinerancy and slow singlet formation in NaRuO2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05287).
