# Today's arXiv Condensed Matter Physics — Highlights

## Quantum Geometry, Topology & Light-Matter Control

The interplay between quantum geometry and electromagnetic response features prominently. A new hierarchy of electromagnetic gauge-field quantum geometries unifies electric-dipole, magnetic, and multipole responses, with connections to nonlocal Hall effects ([Hierarchy of Electromagnetic Gauge Field Quantum Geometries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41610)). A fluctuation-dissipation structure is introduced for mixed-state quantum geometry, identifying distance with fluctuations and curvature with linear response ([Fluctuation-dissipation structure of quantum geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25006)).

A major experimental milestone: the long-predicted light-induced Haldane gap in graphene has been directly observed via tr-ARPES, confirming dynamical time-reversal symmetry breaking and Floquet Chern topology ([Observation of light-induced Haldane gap in graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42115)). In cavity settings, ultrastrong coupling between the SrTiO₃ soft mode and THz photonic-crystal cavities produces cavity-enhanced ferroelectricity (>400% SHG enhancement) ([Cavity-engineered ferroelectricity in SrTiO₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40756)), while exact solutions clarify when chiral cavities can generate synthetic magnetic fields—only at the "soft-partner" point, not by single-helicity projection alone ([Exact Electron Dynamics in Linear and Chiral Cavities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40986)).

## Altermagnetism

Altermagnets are being pushed in several directions. Cr-doped MnTe shows the homogeneous coexistence of ferromagnetic and altermagnetic order with symmetry-selective coupling—FM moment biases the Z₂ and Z₃ domain sectors ([Intertwined ferromagnetic and altermagnetic orders in Cr-doped MnTe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40870)). Transport theory shows that spin-flip critical fluctuations can either help or harm the spin-splitter ratio, while energy-conserving band-shift contributions are protected ([Spin-conserving and spin-flip magnetic scattering in the altermagnetic spin-splitter effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41414); [Spin conservation protects the altermagnetic spin-splitter current from critical scattering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41419)). A Berry-curvature quadrupole mechanism drives third-order anomalous Hall response in KV₂Se₂O ([Berry curvature quadrupole induced third-order anomalous Hall effect in altermagnetic KV₂Se₂O](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41274)), while subgap SHG in PT-symmetric antiferromagnets probes the Riemannian connection ([Quantum-Geometric Subgap Second-Harmonic Generation in PT-Symmetric Antiferromagnetic Insulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41167)). A mirror-symmetry design principle proposes realizing altermagnetism in monolayer hexagonal lattices, exemplified by holey graphyne ([Mirror-symmetry engineering of altermagnetism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41589)). Experimentally, α-MnTe epilayers on different substrates exhibit robust ~300 meV anisotropic spin splitting ([Anisotropic spin splitting in α-MnTe epilayers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40850)), and surface defects—not intrinsic altermagnetism—appear to underlie the anomalous Hall hysteresis in bulk MnTe ([Spontaneous Circular Dichroism and Memory Effect from Surface Defects in Altermagnetic MnTe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41797)).

## Moiré Systems & Strongly Correlated Electrons

Neural quantum states map two-electron moiré atoms, revealing a field-tunable singlet-triplet transition with rotor-like circulating currents ([Singlet-Triplet Transition of Rotating Moiré Atoms in a Magnetic Field](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40786)).
