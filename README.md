# Condensed Matter Physics — arXiv Roundup

## Multiferroics, Ferroelectrics & 2D Electronics

A review argues that intrinsic 2D multiferroicity (e.g., few-layer NiI₂) cannot be reliably established by any single probe, and calls for magneto-opto-electric cross-correlative measurements ([Cross-probing van der Waals multiferroics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23337)). Room-temperature antiferroelectricity is reported in layered NbOCl₂, with strain- and field-driven (≈20 kV/cm) reversible AFE-to-FE switching traced by SHG and Raman ([room-temperature intrinsic antiferroelectricity in van der Waals NbOCl2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24092)). Rhombohedral bilayer WSe₂ is shown to transduce ferroelectric polarization into a valley-selective shift of the K edge (up to 41.7 meV) with a testable transport signature ([ferroelectric valley transduction in rhombohedral bilayer WSe2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15494)). On the device side, NEGF modeling of Hf₀.₅Zr₀.₅O₂ tunnel junctions with TiO₂/IGZO quantum wells improves the TER–RA trade-off, reaching TER ≈ 3×10¹¹ % at 1.25×10³ Ω·cm² ([electrostatic and quantum transport engineering of ferroelectric tunnel junctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24103)). Cryogenic HZO ferroelectric capacitors deliver >99% precision in-memory computing at 4 K with 3-bit operation and >10⁹-cycle endurance ([cryogenic AI hardware with Hf0.5Zr0.5O2 capacitors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23489)). A 4-tier stacked-transistor DTCO study, however, finds only 12.8% block-level area savings and ~11% slower operation at iso-power ([breaking the myth of four-tier stacked transistors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23806)).

## Topology, Superconductivity & Quantum Matter

A 2-band interacting tight-binding model yields exact fractional Chern insulator ground states at 1/3 filling at any finite size and boundary condition, plus zero-energy quasihole states ([analytical interacting tight-binding model for fractional Chern insulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23409)). Trapped fermions in odd-parity Josephson trijunctions induce phase frustration that maps onto a Kagome XY antiferromagnet, producing 6e phase coherence visible as a third harmonic in the current–phase relation ([trapped fermions frustrate multiterminal Josephson networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23390)). For La₃Ni₂O₇ films on LaSrAlO₄, RPA and DCA predict s± pairing in all three bilayers, with an incipient γ band near E_F in the interfacial layer ([layer-dependent s± pairing in La3Ni2O7 films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23595)). DFT+DMFT comparison of nickelate models finds that a single-band Hubbard description suffices for superconductivity, but optical responses require the interstitial-s band ([reconciling models for infinite-layer nickelate superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24288)). A 2D ferromagnetic Weyl half-semimetal is predicted in monolayer BaMn₄O₈, with a Monte Carlo T_c estimate near 1446 K and SOC-robust Fermi arcs ([two-dimensional ferromagnetic Weyl half-semimetal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23688)). Interface Weyl electrons in sputtered kagome bilayers show anomalous Hall conductivity ~2000 Ω⁻¹cm⁻¹ from an intrinsic mechanism ([interface Weyl electrons in the kagome-bilayers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23787)). A quasi-1D Kitaev plaquette chain is mapped to a uniform Majorana chain, giving a c=1/2 Ising transition, an exactly marginal defect line verified by entanglement scaling, and a "relevant" vacancy ([conformal defects in a quasi-one-dimensional Kitaev chain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24312)). Atomically assembled sulfur-vacancy complexes in monolayer WS₂ produce tunable in-gap states, with two geometries flagged as single-photon-emitter candidates ([atomic-scale assembly of sulfur vacancy complexes in monolayer WS2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24754)). Microscopic parent Hamiltonians are constructed for partially magnetized chiral spin liquids realizing Abelian and non-Abelian bosonic FQH states ([exact parent Hamiltonians for partially magnetized chiral spin liquids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24935)). Stabilizer-code constructions realize every achiral 2D abelian topological order from signature-zero K-matrices ([stabilizer models of achiral abelian topological orders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24105)).

## Correlated Electrons: Experiment

Spin- and angle-resolved photoemission with circular dichroism on Ni₁/₄TaSe₂ reveals spin-polarized states at E_F and a helicity-dependent response consistent with finite spin Berry curvature ([hidden topological signatures in a van der Waals ferromagnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24468)). Site-selective ¹³C NMR shows that the insulating phase of α-(BETS)₂I₃ preserves inversion and time-reversal symmetry — a gapped Dirac state, unlike the gapless tilted-cone case in the BEDT-TTF analog ([symmetry-preserving gapped Dirac state in α-(BETS)2I3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16939)). Dual neural networks applied to atomically resolved DOS in Bi₂Sr₂CaCu₂O₈ visualize supercurrent loop networks that obey Kirchhoff's law, bearing on time-reversal symmetry breaking in cuprates ([atomic-scale imaging of time reversal breaking supercurrents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23060)). Synthesis route is shown to select the ground state in the frustrated spinel ZnV₂O₄: microwave-assisted synthesis stabilizes the cubic structure with spin-liquid-like dynamics, suppressing the usual tetragonal transition ([synthesis-route-dependent state selection in ZnV2O4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23500)). In 1T-TaS₂, uniaxial tensile strain splits the CCDW–NCCDW transition into two temperatures separated by over 10 K ([strain-induced splitting of the CCDW-NCCDW transition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19503)).

## Magnetism, Spins & Quantum Devices

Optically detected magnetic resonance resolves millisecond electron spin lifetimes (T₁ up to 0.9 ms at 1.6 K) in CsPbI₃ nanocrystals, explained by a two-LO-phonon Raman mechanism and a measured 56 μs nuclear correlation time ([millisecond-long electron spin lifetime in CsPbI3 nanocrystals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03038)). Mixed-cation MAₓFA₁₋ₓPbI₃ crystals show multiple distinct electron and hole spin subensembles with T₁ reaching 2 ms ([millisecond spin relaxation times in MAxFA1−xPbI3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06887)). Room-temperature valley relaxation near 10 ps is measured in Ge(001), with sub-picosecond optical reversal of valley polarization ([ultrafast control and electrical detection of valley polarization in germanium](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23998)). A molecular Kondo "ball" is predicted for TiSc₂N@C₈₀ on Al(111), where the closed cage acts as an itinerant reservoir and screens a Ti S=1/2 moment (T_K ≈ 60 K) ([molecular Kondo ball based on an endohedral fullerene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23918)). Hierarchical anti-crossings in a tripartite CrCl₃ van der Waals cavity-magnonic device cut magnon loss by an order of magnitude while preserving strong coupling ([hierarchical coupling in tripartite van der Waals cavity magnonics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23756)). An Fe²⁺:forsterite qubit proposal reports 99.9% Rabi fidelity up to 10 K, potentially avoiding dilution refrigeration ([Fe2+:forsterite qubit system](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23550)). Spin-splitting in planar Ge Josephson junctions is shown to be independent of junction width — unlike InAs wires — though charge traps spoil the benefit ([gate tunable spin splitting for Andreev spin qubits in Ge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18888)). A generalized Schrieffer–Wolff transformation extends the method to strong coupling, with a closed-form generator and a geodesic gauge principle ([generalized Schrieffer-Wolff transformation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22014)). Pump–probe work on a Co/Ir/Co trilayer links native top oxide to layer asymmetry, two-step switching, and low-temperature exchange bias ([native-oxide-driven layer asymmetry in Co/Ir/Co](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33595)). Also notable: two-color coherent control generates phase-tunable pure spin photocurrents in centrosymmetric crystals ([two-color coherent control of the spin photovoltaic effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24045)), and light is predicted to induce odd-parity h-wave magnetism in a Penrose quasicrystal ([light-induced odd-parity h-wave magnetism in Penrose quasicrystals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23992)).

## Light–Matter, Nonlinear Optics & Nonequilibrium Dynamics

A velocity-gauge, relaxation-time-approximation framework computes nonlinear optical conductivity without momentum derivatives of Bloch states, and shows that adiabatic switching (an imaginary frequency shift) does not reproduce true relaxation — leading above-gap injection terms differ by a factor of two ([nonlinear optical response with relaxation in the velocity gauge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23400)). A real-space linear-response theory for field-induced orbital magnetization is developed for inhomogeneous solids and implemented with Chebyshev expansions ([real-space theory of nonequilibrium orbital magnetization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23404)). Its follow-up shows point defects create nanoscale orbital-magnetization hotspots that can reverse the sign of the net orbital moment ([defect engineering of orbital magnetoelectric effects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23405)). Phase-resolved THz spectroscopy shows vacuum Rabi oscillations of Landau polaritons starting only after one cavity round-trip, i.e., strong coupling is established after the cavity field forms ([delayed formation of Landau polaritons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29501)). Ultrafast x-ray dynamical diffraction reveals transverse, not just longitudinal, lattice distortions in bulk Si below the melting threshold ([coherent shear wave in crystalline Si](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10420)). A mid-infrared (7.5–10 μm) GaAs/AlGaAs acousto-optic phase modulator at 460 MHz is demonstrated monolithically ([mid-infrared integrated acousto-optic phase modulation on GaAs/AlGaAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24530)).

## Quantum Information & Quantum Algorithms

Under a uniform subfield product expansion assumption, qLDPC codes are constructed with individually addressable transversal logical CCZ gates, enabling magic-state distillation with overhead exponent γ→0 and a CCZ magic-state fountain ([addressable transversal CCZ gates on almost good qLDPC codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23762)). Window propagation gives the first provably polynomial-time classical algorithm for first-order OTOCs in 1D Haar-random brickwork circuits ([provably efficient classical algorithm for OTOC(1) in 1D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24640)). Virtual autopurification builds virtual copies from spatially separated subregions of a single translationally invariant state, avoiding multi-copy overhead ([virtual autopurification in translationally invariant quantum states](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24448)). Topological reservoir computing exploits long-lived Majorana edge modes for memory while the fast bulk processes information ([topological quantum reservoir computing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24013)). A quantum annealing network of width W=2 solves search problems with exponentially small error at depth L=1 after parameter tuning ([quantum annealing with a quantum neural network](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24089)). QAOA samples on the Sherrington–Kirkpatrick model are typically macroscopically uncorrelated even as the low-energy tail develops Parisi-like structure — approaching the Parisi energy before Parisi geometry ([Parisi energy before Parisi geometry in QAOA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23645)). Gated enumeration extends the anyon modular-data search to rank 42 (150,679 new datasets), finds counterexamples to a Geiko–Moore conjecture, and identifies rank-100 prime modular data not realized by Chern–Simons theory ([learning the anyon atlas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24752)); a companion work extracts F-symbols from modular data, including Z(Haagerup₃) ([computing F-symbols from modular data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24753)). A nonsecular quantum master equation with time-reversal KMS detailed balance achieves a time-uniform O(λ²) error bound beyond the secular approximation ([nonsecular quantum master equation with detailed balance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24911)). Also: irreversibility is shown to enhance quantum-enhanced MCMC with a polynomial spectral-gap advantage ([irreversibility enhances quantum-enhanced MCMC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23350)), and a time-dependent Gutzwiller quantum-classical embedding hybridizes classical lattice propagation with quantum embedding solvers on IBM and Quantinuum hardware ([Gutzwiller quantum-classical embedding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24592)).

## Statistical, Soft & Nonlinear Physics

A spin-1/2 Kitaev-chain analysis gives an exact density-matrix organization by gauge string-operator classes, proving the block-diagonal structure behind extensive entanglement-spectrum degeneracy and the gauge/matter split of entanglement entropy ([exact organization of density matrices in the Kitaev spin liquid](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18002)). Finite-time stochastic resetting via a linear trapping potential yields analytical first-passage and splitting statistics, a critical trapping strength for accelerated search, and an exact work–return-time relation ([finite-time stochastic resetting in bounded domains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23790)). Work-fluctuation distributions in correlated N-particle resets partition parameter space into N-dependent "phases" with discontinuously changing variance ([phase transitions in work fluctuations in correlated N-particle resets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24318)). A Galilean-covariant theory shows curvature gradients convert lattice shear into superflow in supersolids on curved surfaces ([curvature gradients turn shear into superflow in supersolids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24354)). A Bose–Hubbard Otto cycle shows that compressing at strong repulsion into a Mott insulator maximizes work output near Carnot efficiency ([a Bose-Hubbard quantum thermal machine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24483)). Blanket theory constructs cavity functionals for dense spin networks, recovering the Parisi functional for SK and deriving fullRSB functionals for restricted and deep Boltzmann machines ([blanket theory for dense networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23960)). A kinetic theory of sheared dry granular particles uses Enskog plus Grad closure to capture steady viscosity, normal-stress trends, and relaxation after a volume-fraction quench ([kinetic theory of dry granular particles under simple shear](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23635)). Synchrotron XPCS with a Fibonacci bunch-mode acquisition scheme measures full autocorrelations over long times with orders of magnitude fewer frames ([XPCS-Echo and broad relaxation measurements](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02645)). The role of fixed, non-rewirable links in the coevolving voter model is analyzed, revealing a new localized active phase beyond the standard pair approximation ([localized active phase in the coevolving voter model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23870)). Also: a review argues that synthesis route is an underused experimental variable for correlated materials generally ([synthesis-route-dependent state selection in ZnV2O4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23500)), and a broad tutorial review collects statistical-physics and sociophysics models of fracture and earthquakes ([models and measures for fracture mechanics and earthquake dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12109)).

## Machine Learning & Computational Methods

Matlantis PFP v9 extends r2SCAN training data to 96 elements (lanthanides Pr–Lu, Po–Cm) plus clusters and adsorption, evaluating forces 3–4× faster than SevenNet-Omni and handling 5–10× more atoms ([Matlantis PFP v9](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23873)). Benchmarking ten universal MLIPs on ZrO₂ shows only four reproduce the monoclinic–tetragonal–cubic sequence; two screening diagnostics are proposed — polymorph energies within ~6 meV/atom of DFT and no systematic phonon softening ([pretrained universal MLIPs for phase transitions in ZrO2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23535)). For iron oxides at 140–260 GPa, force-dominated training leaves systematic energy offsets up to 41 meV/atom that stronger energy weighting removes, but an untrained NiAs-type FeO phase still shows 57 meV/atom free-energy error ([machine-learned potentials for iron oxides at extreme conditions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24525)). JAXRENS provides GPU-native nested sampling, completing a ZnS phase-diagram simulation (~10¹⁰ model evaluations) in six days on four GPUs ([GPU-native nested sampling with MLIPs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24088)). XCAPE combines symmetry-aware evolutionary search with a universal MLIP to solve PXRD structures: 92.2% recovery on simulated patterns, all 12 experimental references, and 12 new ICSD-absent candidates ([physics-guided crystal structure elucidation from PXRD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23760)). A neural classical DFT framework computes ab initio CO₂ adsorption isotherms for >5,000 MOFs without grand-canonical simulation, sorting them into four isotherm-shape classes that the Henry constant cannot distinguish ([adsorption thermodynamics in carbon capture materials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24759)). A universal E(3)-equivariant diffusion model predicts Wannier centres and localization, generalizing to 100 unseen crystals and enabling IR spectra and Born-charge/piezoelectric finite differences ([universal diffusion model for the quantum position operator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24130)). A vision-LLM + classical CV pipeline digitizes 11,445 calibrated PXRD curves from 3,954 COF papers (median peak error 0.07°) ([structured extraction of diffraction data from COFs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23680)). Typed Decision over pretrained VLMs gives 0.13–0.21 s closed-loop STEM navigation decisions with up to 91% success and natural-language target specification ([fast closed-loop STEM navigation using vision-language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23698)). 4D-TEMPO fits atoms, microscope, and detector directly to 4D-STEM counts under a Poisson likelihood, providing per-atom Fisher-information error bars and faster reconstruction than published codes ([4D-TEMPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24776)). A simulation-free training pipeline reduces effective interseismic time-stepping cost by mapping earthquake rupture jumps with a Fourier neural operator ([ML acceleration of earthquake sequence simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23561)). Also relevant: orbit-diffusion crystal generation ([GEODE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01898)), automated discovery of crystal plausibility rules ([PRIS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01209)), hierarchical Bayesian grain-size analysis from single-shot XRD ([hierarchical Bayesian multi-emitter analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24755)), and a data-quality confidence framework for materials databases ([confidence-based materials data quality assessment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23342)).
