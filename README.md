# arXiv Condensed Matter — Thematic Summary

## Frustrated Magnetism, Spin Liquids & Altermagnetism
Theoretical work maps how frustration and symmetry produce unconventional magnetic order. An orbital-field interaction on the honeycomb lattice generates a cascade of collinear phases and a Lifshitz-type transition to a unidirectional spiral ([A cascade of orbital-field-driven phases...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36214)). Surface altermagnetism is shown to be topologically protected on conventional antiferromagnets, with CuMnAs as a candidate ([Topologically Protected Surface Altermagnetism on Antiferromagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10108)). A slave-rotor treatment of the Lieb-lattice altermagnetic Hubbard model finds a cascade from metal to altermagnetic Mott insulator ([Slave-rotor theory of correlated altermagnets on the Lieb lattice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30061)). Neutron scattering on Er₂Be₂GeO₇ reveals stripe Ising order and fractional magnetization plateaus ([Stripe antiferromagnetic order and spin dynamics...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36799)), while Schwinger-boson theory shows thermal Hall conductivity can distinguish flux sectors on the honeycomb lattice ([Thermal Hall Signatures of Distinct Schwinger-Boson Flux Sectors...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29792)).

## Superconductivity: Unconventional Pairing, Vortices & Diode Effects
Several papers probe broken-symmetry superconducting states. A large superconducting diode effect in UTe₂ at 33.6 T is attributed to an odd-parity FFLO state ([Large superconducting diode effect indicates nonuniform triplet pairing in UTe2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36332)), and pulsed-field work maps "metamagnetic ripples" in its high-field phase diagram ([Metamagnetic ripples in the UTe2 high magnetic field phase diagram](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11362)). Ginzburg-Landau calculations resolve the apparent nematic-chiral vortex conundrum in 4Hb-TaS₂ ([Vortex anisotropy in chiral and nematic superconductors with strain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36358)), and a tensorial framework classifies symmetry-resolved diode responses ([Symmetry-resolved superconducting diode response](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04928)). Point-contact spectroscopy finds anisotropic multi-gap structure in 2H-NbSe₂ ([Point-contact spectroscopy...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37304)). On the theory side, a bootstrap/SDP approach proves a Laughlin gap above 1/100 in the thermodynamic limit ([A Hierarchy of Exact Lower Bounds on the Laughlin Spectral Gap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18854)), and neural tensor-network simulations find extreme stripe–superconductivity competition in the doped Hubbard model ([Extreme competition between stripes and superconductivity in the hole-doped Hubbard model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30321)).

## Topological Order, Anyons & Quantum Codes
