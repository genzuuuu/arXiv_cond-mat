# Today's arXiv Condensed Matter Physics — Key Advances

## Thermal Transport & Phonons

New theoretical work maps where heat actually flows in disordered solids, replacing gauge-dependent atom projections with an exact response of κ to atomic masses and bond stiffnesses ([Where heat flows and where to change it: gauge-free sensitivity maps of thermal conductivity in disordered solids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06958)). In MOF-5, mode-projected MD plus spectral Wigner transport resolves particle-like phonon propagation (falling sharply from 200–500 K) from wave-like coherence that supplies ~half the conductivity at 500 K ([Heat Transport Mechanisms in MOF-5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08024)). A thermodynamically consistent nonlocal, frequency-dependent conductivity kernel reproduces the spot-size and frequency dependence of thermoreflectance in Ge and SiGe without fitting ([Generalized Fourier's law in mesoscopic systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23909)). TGS on epitaxial NiTi reveals a 4.5× change in thermal diffusivity between R-phase and austenite, with no hysteresis — promising for thermal switches ([Anomalous thermal and elastic properties of an epitaxial NiTi film exhibiting R-phase](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17553)).

## Superconductivity

Strong-coupling Migdal-Eliashberg kinetics is shown to be stable once non-Markovian effects are included, with Planckian-slope relaxation and strongly oscillatory decay; the phonon frequency is argued not to bound T_c ([Stability and Relaxation of Electron-Phonon Systems at Strong Coupling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07014)). A universal negative correlation between nonreciprocity and transition width ΔT is established across MBE-grown FeTe heterostructures, identifying interfacial disorder as the unifying knob ([Universal Negative Correlation between Nonreciprocity and Superconducting Transition Width in FeTe-Based Heterostructures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07116)). Twisted cuprate junctions show Shapiro steps at nhf/3e, a signature of three-electron tunneling ([Signature of charge-3e tunneling in twisted cuprate Josephson junctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07708)). AC susceptibility, specific-heat and hysteresis data reclassify OsB₂ as a rare nonelemental Type-I superconductor ([Experimental Evidence for Type-I Superconductivity in OsB₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08239)). Multiorbital theory links Fe d_xy character in monolayer FeSe to nodeless d-wave pairing, with symmetry-based Josephson interference selection rules ([Microscopic Origin and Josephson Signatures of d-Wave Superconductivity in Monolayer FeSe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08037)). First-principles work predicts a pressure-induced transition in LiPdH₂ near 220 GPa with T_c up to 46 K (harmonic) ([Pressure-Induced Phase Transition and Superconducting Properties of LiPdH₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07989)).
