## Superconductivity & Josephson Physics

**Shapiro physics without charge.** Simulations of a potential-biased atomic Josephson junction reproduce Shapiro-spike resonances (Bessel-function scaling) in a neutral quantum fluid, tracing them to quantized-vortex phase slips; locking plateaus even permit *negative-mobility* transport. [Shapiro spikes in driven quantum fluids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05477)

**Anyon superconductivity, controlled.** A microscopic theory maps dilute charge-2e/3 anyons (doped Laughlin state) onto a resonantly interacting anyon gas; a solvable Aharonov-Casher fixed point exhibits Cooper-pair ODLRO directly in electron coordinates. **Hidden weak-pairing anyon superfluid.** A non-interacting gas of θ = −π/3 anyons with three degenerate pockets can pair via statistical gauge fluctuations into a chiral state with c₋ = −1/2, reconciling theory with numerics near fractional Chern insulators. [Hidden weak-pairing superconductivity of non-interacting anyons obeying 1/3 statistics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19036)

**FQH–SC junctions explained.** Dissipation to sub-gap modes accounts for non-quantized crossed-Andreev conductance, with a phase diagram predicting temperature-independent conductance only when an integer mode couples to the dissipative channel. [Classification and Signatures of Dissipative Quantum Hall-Superconductor Junctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05555)

**TI Josephson junctions.** Tunneling spectroscopy of topological-insulator junctions finds highly transmissive, spin-nondegenerate Andreev modes whose phase dependence suggests gap closing/reopening, persisting across chemical potentials. **Tc from chemical bonding.** A perspective consolidates the "networking value" (electron-localization-function) descriptor and proposes a real-space framework for predicting Tc across conventional superconductors. **Nickelates.** DMRG-inspired VMC on a five-layer Hubbard model favors d_x²−y² pairing for La₆Ni₅O₁₂, with nodal signatures (V-shaped DOS, T-linear superfluid density, 1/T₁T ∝ T²). [Toric codes all the way down](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40592)

**Lattice Chern–Simons.** Quantized on a cylinder, level-k Abelian lattice Chern–Simons theory reduces to exactly localized chiral edge modes carrying commuting Kac–Moody algebras. [Chiral edge modes from lattice Chern-Simons theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05663)

**Gyromorphs.** Disordered systems with average rotational symmetry can host higher-order topological phases; average-symmetry indicators, spectral localizers, and scattering invariants agree on the phase diagram. [Topological Gyromorphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.02167)

**Altermagnet switching.** Surface antisymmetry groups yield design rules for deterministic Néel-vector switching in d-wave altermagnets, including all-order symmetry obstructions to bipolar switching. [Deterministic Electrical Switching in Altermagnets via Surface Antisymmetry Groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06537)
