## 1. Graphene, Moiré, and Correlated Flat Bands

## 2. Topological Phases: Theory and Materials

## 4. Quantum Matter Out of Equilibrium: Chaos, Scrambling, Entanglement

- **TQFT entanglement.** Naive path-integral cuts are non-isometric; dressing with edge operators yields isometric cuts, non-integer Affleck–Ludwig degeneracies, and symmetry-resolved phase detection. [More physics at the entangling surface: boundary conditions and entanglement in 2d TQFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13078)
- **Tripartite nonlocal magic.** Flat marginal/reflected/negativity spectra characterize stabilizer states; anti-flatness gives a measurable tripartite magic diagnostic. [Tripartite nonlocal magic from reflected and negativity spectra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13071)
- **Kinematic quantum geometry.** Fast DOFs following states parametrized by slow coordinates *and* velocities generate mixed Berry curvatures, velocity-dependent mass tensors, and nonlinear Hall currents. [Kinematic Quantum Geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13089)
- **Krylov refinement.** logK complexity, defined via a replica approach, separates genuine from saddle-dominated scrambling and extends to classical dynamics. [Toward a Refinement of Krylov Complexity: Scrambling, Classical Operator Growth and Replicas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19359)
- **Yang–Baxter operator entanglement.** Proves constant Schmidt rank for qubit YB gates and polynomial growth for involutive dual-unitary YB gates; constructs an exponential counterexample.
