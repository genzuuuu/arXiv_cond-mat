### 🧊 Superconductivity & Superfluidity

*   **[Stripe-tuned superconductivity in single-flavor metals with nontrivial quantum geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24905):** Demonstrates that a stripe potential can stabilize an exotic transverse p_x-wave superconducting order, controlled by quantum geometry and electron density.
*   **[Probing nonlocal superconducting fluctuations with covariance noise magnetometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24906):** Calculates nonlocal fluctuation corrections to conductivity and works out how they can be observed via two-sensor covariance noise magnetometry.
*   **[Charge-6e superconductivity from doping SU(3) spin liquids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25909):** Proposes a route to charge-6e superconductivity by doping SU(3) spin liquids, leading to exotic paired states of charge-3e trions.
*   **[Superfluidity without charge order in the attractive Hubbard model on the kagome lattice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25983):** Using QMC simulations, finds a superfluid transition at 2/3 filling that belongs to the chiral-XY universality class, occurring without charge order.
*   **[Quantum Geometry, Anomalous Scaling, and Strong Pseudogap Superfluidity in a Flat-Band Lieb Lattice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02990):** Shows that quantum geometry in a flat band leads to an anomalous power-law scaling of the superconducting gap and T_c, and a strong pseudogap regime.
*   **[Stripe-tuned superconductivity in altermagnetic metals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12897):** Analyzes pairing instability in altermagnets, showing finite-momentum FFLO pairing with singlet-triplet mixing due to altermagnetic spin-splitting.
*   **[Intrinsic Nernst Effect from Berry Curvature in Superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26638):** Identifies a mechanism for an intrinsic Nernst effect in clean superconductors, arising from the Berry curvature of Bogoliubov quasiparticles.
*   **[Theory of ballistic SNS junctions revisited](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.02967):** Revises the theory to account for phase gradients in superconducting leads, resulting in a backward-skewed current-phase relation for short junctions.
*   **[Mechanism for Nodal Topological Superconductivity on PtBi₂ Surface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09994):** Proposes anisotropic electron-phonon coupling as the microscopic mechanism for the observed nodal topological superconductivity on the PtBi₂ surface.
