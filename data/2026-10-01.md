## Quantum Many-Body Theory & Methods

A controlled benchmark of the **$GW$+BSE** workflow shows that its frequently accurate optical gaps arise from *accidental cancellation* between an underestimated quasiparticle gap and an oversized exchange kernel, not hidden consistency — with a taxonomy of where standard workflows can be trusted ([Accidental accuracy and formal consistency in $GW$+BSE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38247)).
