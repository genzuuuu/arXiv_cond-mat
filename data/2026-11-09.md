**Kuramoto on networks.** Finite-size and degree effects alter the synchronization transition relative to infinite globally coupled theory for networks with mixed-order interactions. [Synchronization transitions in Kuramoto oscillators with mixed interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09564)

**Non-neutrality across scales in a forest.** Random-matrix filtering combined with a neutral model reveals a crossover at 20–35 m beyond which neutral theory accurately describes collective organization at Barro Colorado Island. [Resolving non-neutrality across scales in a hyperdiverse tropical forest](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09479)

**Morphology of chess openings.** Separating irreversible pawn structure from fast piece degrees of freedom yields a connected, modular network of 435 pawn structures covering ~98% of positions. [Slow structures, fast degrees of freedom, and the morphology of chess openings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09710)

**Neural symmetry via dimensionality scaling.** Splitting participation-ratio slopes into a sampling floor and an axis-resolved shift separates even and odd sectors of V1's O(2) symmetry and, in language models, token content from depth-built construction. [Structure is in the zoom](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08506)

**Gauging continuous symmetries on the lattice.** Dual models for compact Lie group symmetries, with MPO intertwiners and twisted-boundary spectra, generalize fusion-category dualities; NISSB underlies symmetric tensor-network algorithms. [Dualities in one-dimensional quantum lattice models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08293)

**Classifying free-scalar boundaries and lines.** A remote-detectability criterion gives complete classifications of unitary conformal boundaries and topological lines for the 2D free scalar, uncovering a 4D moduli space of line defects, with explicit Lagrangians and fusion rules. [All Symmetries and Boundaries of the Two-Dimensional Free Scalar from Continuous Flat-Gauging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40818)

**Framing anomaly on the lattice.** The U(1) Chern–Simons–Maxwell lattice theory reproduces the universal −2π/12 phase of ⟨T⟩, isolating 2π/24 framing anomaly via ⟨Tᵐ⟩—the final property needed for a valid lattice CS definition. [Framing Anomaly in Lattice Chern-Simons-Maxwell Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04318)
