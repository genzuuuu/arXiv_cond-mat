# Today's arXiv Condensed Matter Physics — Key Advances

## Quantum Geometry, Topology & Flat Bands
Several papers push quantum geometry beyond single-particle band theory. [Quantum Geometry Effects in Lattice Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30210) shows that beyond the molecular Berry curvature that splits degenerate optical phonons, the quantum metric produces an additional *nonchiral* splitting, demonstrated first-principles in EuB₆. [Quantum-Metric-Driven Hollow Wigner Crystals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29317) finds that a strong quantum metric can reconstruct an s-wave Wigner crystal into a "hollow" charge distribution and boost exchange interactions by over an order of magnitude even at zero Berry curvature. On the topology side, [Exact Fractional Chern Insulators from Hubbard Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25010) construct exact Laughlin/Read–Rezayi/Halperin parent states for Hubbard-interacting Chern bands, and [A Fractional Chern Semimetal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31234) builds an exact tensor-network state with a quantized 1/3 Chern number whose IR physics is governed by an unexpected U(3)₁×SU(3)₁ CFT. [Quantum-geometry-tuned Mott transition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31242) develops DMFT on coherent orbitals of a topological flat band, finding the Mott transition tuned by the Berry-curvature fraction s², and proposes a pseudogap metal as the parent state of TBG superconductivity. [Floquet State Quantum Geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13649) bounds how micromotion and Floquet topology constrain the intra-period evolution of the quantum metric.

## Altermagnetism and Odd-Parity Magnets
Altermagnetism appears in several guises. [Interstitial Magnetoelectricity in Altermagnetic Electrides](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28504) shows electric-field-induced net magnetization in Zr₂N arising from interstitial anionic electrons, enabling electrical control of spin conductivity. [Coupled Altermagnetic and Valley Physics in Monolayer VBiSe₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30649) finds i-wave altermagnetic splitting plus ~112 meV valley splitting, with strain driving a quasi-half-valley-metal. [Pseudo-Altermagnetism in Non-Hermitian Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29976) maps balanced gain/loss onto altermagnetic spin, revealing symmetry-enforced nodal structures. [Singlet-triplet superconductivity in a p-wave magnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14609) uses parquet RG to show repulsive interactions generically give mixed singlet-triplet pairing, possibly a finite-momentum pair-density wave.

## Superconductivity: Nickelates, Cuprates, and Unconventional Pairing
Nickelate theory advances on two fronts. [Minimal Three-Band Model and d-Wave Superconductivity in La₄Ni₃O₈](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28747) combines DFT+DMFT+RPA+FLEX to find dominant d_{x²−y²}-wave pairing with T_c ≈ 10 K, suggesting the Γ/A pockets of infinite-layer nickelates are not essential. [Pair-density-wave superconductivity and Anderson's theorem in bilayer nickelates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15023) shows a displacement field can stabilize a zero-field PDW, and that mirror-symmetry-breaking disorder weakens pairing even with time-reversal symmetry intact. [High-temperature superconductivity from kinetic energy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07292) demonstrates DMRG pairing with gaps >1.5t arising purely from kinetic energy in a constrained hopping model, with a smooth connection to realistic parameters. Experimentally, [2e-ARPES Cooper-pair emission](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09857) directly observes zero-momentum, zero-binding-energy Cooper pairs in an optimally doped cuprate, with anomalous broadening along relative energy. [Neutron Spin Resonance in CeCoIn₅](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30395) discovers a third high-field resonance mode and interprets the branches as condensate collective modes, including singlet-triplet excitations. [Charge-fluctuation hot lines in Ba₁₋ₓKₓBiO₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28879) proposes an overdamped Q=(π,π,π) boson accounting for the missing λ ≈ 0.7 and reproducing T_c ≈ 25–30 K. [Exact PDW in moiré flat bands](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26245) proves an exact PDW ground state from quantum geometric nesting and derives a universal superfluid stiffness relation.
