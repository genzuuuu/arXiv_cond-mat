### Quantum Information & Topological Phases

Research highlights a strong focus on topological states of matter, quantum information processing, and the design of novel qubits.

- **Topological Superconductivity:** A theoretical proposal suggests that interfacing an altermagnet (V₂Se₂O) with a conventional superconductor can create a fully gapped $p$-wave state hosting chiral Majorana modes, providing a new route to topological superconductivity [Topological superconductivity in an altermagnet-superconductor heterostructure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13408). Another work identifies a mechanism for strain-induced helical superconductivity and a zero-field diode effect in materials like PbTaSe₂, linking it to a Lifshitz coupling that drives a transition to a helical state with broken time-reversal symmetry [Strain-Induced Helical Superconductivity and the Zero-Field Diode Effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13380).
- **Non-Abelian Anyons:** A theoretical study provides strong evidence for a non-Abelian chiral spin liquid (the Moore-Read state) in a spin-1 square-lattice model, using DMRG calculations to confirm its topological properties and anyonic excitations [Non-Abelian chiral spin liquid in a spin-$1$ antiferromagnet on the square lattice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15061).
- **Quantum Computing Platforms:** The first demonstration of an all-van-der-Waals superconducting qubit based on an NbSe₂-hBN-NbSe₂ junction is reported, showing coherence times comparable to conventional qubits but with a smaller footprint [An All-van-der-Waals Qubit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14230). A separate work proposes a scalable quantum register design using electron space states in field-defined double quantum dots within semiconductor nanowires [Quantum register based on double quantum dots in semiconductor nanowires](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08485). Additionally, a new numerical framework for variational real-time evolution on quantum hardware is benchmarked on a 24-qubit superconducting processor for simulating the Anderson impurity model [Variational Real-Time Dynamics on Reduced Operator Manifolds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15272).
- **Topological Classification:** A new framework using "knotted graphs" is proposed to classify the entire band dispersion of materials, including complex Fermi surface structures and their evolution through Lifshitz transitions, going beyond traditional knot theory [Topological classification through knotted graphs: Fermi surface dispersions and Lifshitz transitions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13390).
- **Hyperbolic Quantum Geometry:** A theoretical work introduces "holonomy shaking" to dynamically extract the quantum metric on hyperbolic lattices, bridging hyperbolic band theory and quantum geometry with a proposed experimental technique using periodic lattice driving [Quantum Geometry in Hyperbolic Band Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14876).

### Magnetism & Spintronics

The exploration of novel magnetic materials and their potential for spintronic applications is a prominent theme, with a strong focus on altermagnetism.

- **Altermagnetism:** The B-type quadratic planar Hall effect is introduced as a sensitive probe of altermagnetic order, a response that is symmetry-forbidden in conventional antiferromagnetic phases [B-type Quadratic Planar Hall Effect as a Probe of Altermagnetic Order](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13826). A machine-learning-guided search has identified new metallic $d$-wave altermagnets in the stable materials Co₂AlSc and Fe₂AlGe, predicting a tunneling magnetoresistance of up to 203% [Large spin splitting metallic altermagnets from machine-learned design rules](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14051). A new mechanism for "vestigial altermagnetism" is proposed, where a composite order with zero net momentum can emerge from the thermal melting of antiferromagnetic and orbital orders [Vestigial altermagnetism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14228). A separate work predicts that bilayer rhombohedral graphene in an in-plane magnetic field can act as a tunable $p$-wave magnet, with the effect strongly enhanced by increasing the number of layers [Rhombohedral Multilayer Graphene as a $p$-Wave Magnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14705). A theoretical study explores altermagnetism across the BCS-BEC crossover, showing a universal suppression of phase stiffness in the fully gapped regime [Altermagnetism across the BCS-BEC crossover](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14613).
- **Chiral Magnetism:** A family of metallic altermagnets, XCoB₂ (X = Ta, Zr, Hf), is predicted to host both chiral magnons and Weyl points, with Néel temperatures above room temperature for those containing Zr and Hf [Exchange-Driven Chiral Magnons and Weyl States in Room-Temperature Metallic XCoB$_2$ (X= Ta, Zr and Hf) Altermagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14094).
- **2D Magnetism:** The hidden magnetic complexity of the van der Waals ferromagnet Ce₂Te₅ is revealed through muon spin relaxation and neutron diffraction, showing a single magnetic transition where only one of two inequivalent Ce sites carries an ordered moment [Hidden Magnetic Complexity Within a Simple van der Waals Ferromagnet Ce$_2$Te$_5$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15815). In another work, a chiral triple-$q$ antiferromagnetic state was discovered in the 2D semiconductor GdGaI, which spontaneously exhibits a large anomalous Hall effect despite being a semiconductor with negligible net magnetization [Chiral Antiferromagnetism from Momentum-Space Resonance in a 2D Semiconductor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15854).
- **Spin-Orbit Torques:** Two strategies for measuring spin-orbit-torque efficiency acting on an insulating magnet (lithium aluminum ferrite) are analyzed, finding a value comparable to metallic ferromagnets via spin-torque FMR, while optical measurements via Sagnac interferometry yielded a much weaker efficiency [Two Strategies to Measure Spin-Orbit-Torque Efficiency Acting on the Insulating Magnet Li$_{0.5}$Al$_{0.7}$Fe$_{1.8}$O$_4$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15474).
- **Magneto-Transport:** A colossal magnetostriction effect, with lattice strains exceeding 60-100%, is reported in rare-earth orthoferrites (DyFeO₃, HoFeO₃) at low temperatures, driven by a magnetic-field-induced structural phase transition [Colossal magnetostriction effect in rare-earth orthoferrites](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14731). The thermal Hall effect in elemental niobium is observed to exhibit a crossover, switching dominant carriers from hole-like to electron-like upon cooling, providing unambiguous evidence for its long-proposed two-gap superconducting nature [Thermal Hall effect in elemental niobium, a two-gap superconductor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15663).

### Superconductivity & Strange Metals

New experimental and theoretical insights into superconducting mechanisms and related correlated phenomena are presented.

- **Cuprates and Nickelates:** A mean-field description of the monitored Bose-Hubbard model provides a local order parameter to characterize strong-to-weak symmetry breaking, linking it to the charge-sharpening transition in a potential model for cuprates [A mean-field description of strong-to-weak symmetry breaking in the monitored Bose-Hubbard model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02713). The superconducting state of an infinite-layer nickelate is found to be highly non-uniform, exhibiting a stripe-like enhancement pattern coexisting with a weak magnetic texture, which may explain the broadened transitions observed in these materials [Stripe-Like Superconducting Enhancement and Coexisting Magnetic Texture in an Infinite Layer Nickelate](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15958).
- **Josephson Junctions:** The superconducting diode effect is theoretically demonstrated in a Josephson $\varphi_0$ junction with a ferromagnetic weak link, where the interplay between a linear gradient coupling and higher-order gradient terms breaks the symmetry of the critical current [Superconducting Diode Effect in Josephson $\varphi_0$ Junction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13399). A separate experimental work demonstrates both in-plane and out-of-plane driven Josephson diode effects in a magic-angle twisted four-layer graphene junction, pointing to strong in-plane orbital coupling [In-plane and out-of-plane magnetic field driven Josephson diode effect in magic-angle twisted four-layer graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21229).
- **Multigap and Unconventional Pairing:** Scanning tunneling spectroscopy reveals a strongly anisotropic multigap state in misfit layer compounds (LaSe)₁.₁₄(NbSe₂)ₘ, with a fragile gap on one Fermi pocket and a robust gap on others, suggesting unconventional pairing beyond simple $s$-wave [Multigap superconductivity in Ising superconductors: The case of (LaSe)1.14(NbSe2)m misfit layer compounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15446).
- **Theoretical Frameworks:** A review of the "Extremely Correlated Fermi Liquids" (ECFL) theory is presented, a framework for understanding the $t$-$J$ model near the Mott limit, which captures the quasilinear T-dependent resistivity and small quasiparticle weight seen in single-layer high-Tc systems [Overview of the Theory of Extremely Correlated Fermi Liquids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13541).

### Soft & Active Matter

Studies in soft and active matter focus on the collective behavior of particles, from colloidal suspensions to biological systems.

- **Active Matter:** A mechanism is identified where "active pistons" can extract work from a system through periodic compression alone, without changing bulk properties, by coupling an external perturbation to the internal degrees of freedom of active particles [Active pistons extract work by periodic compression alone](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15380). A Floquet-type linear stability analysis shows that a "vortex state" in active nematics can emerge from a secondary, zigzag instability of a uniformly flowing state, without needing nonlinear dynamics [Spontaneous Vortex Instability in Active Nematics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15093). A separate study establishes that curvature alone can generate and protect topological sound in a polar active flock on a torus, creating chiral edge modes that are protected by an index theorem [Geometry-induced flocking and topological sound on a defect-free curved surface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15402).
- **Colloidal Systems:** A cylindrical sintering method is introduced for creating more realistic nanocrystalline thin-film samples in molecular dynamics simulations, producing wider, more disordered grain boundaries that lead to lower mechanical properties compared to standard Voronoi-based models [A cylindrical sintering method for more realistic grain boundaries in nanocrystalline thin films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15246).
- **Granular Physics:** An experimental study finds that avalanches in a compressed granular fault can both dilate the layer and increase the elastic energy transmitted to the boundary, demonstrating that failure can lead to a more highly loaded configuration rather than relaxation [Avalanches can increase stored energy in a granular fault](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14406).

### Computational & Data-Driven Methods

Machine learning and advanced computational techniques are increasingly central to materials discovery and simulation.

- **Materials Discovery:** A symmetry- and property-aware reinforcement learning framework (SPARC) is introduced for the inverse design of crystalline materials, ensuring that generated candidates have the appropriate crystallographic constraints for a desired physical property [Symmetry- and Property-Aware Crystal Generation with Reinforcement Learning for Inverse Materials Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13468). An interpretable, closed-loop multi-agent LLM framework (LLM4MOF) is shown to accelerate the inverse design of metal-organic frameworks, outperforming standard search algorithms without any prior training on property-labeled data [Interpretable Inverse Design of Metal-Organic Frameworks with Large Language Model Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29459).
- **Neural Network Potentials & Simulations:** An end-to-end neural network, Multi4D, is developed for automated structural determination at complex material interfaces using 4D-STEM, achieving high accuracy and generating maps down to single-nanometer resolution [Multi4D: an end-to-end neural network for structural determination at complex material interfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14348). A physics-constrained neural network surrogate model is shown to accurately predict long-time microstructure evolution in systems governed by the Cahn-Hilliard equation by imposing conservation laws as a hard constraint on the network output [Physics-Constrained Neural Surrogate for Domain Growth Prediction in Systems with Conserved Kinetics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26128). Carbon nanotubes are being cleaned with a sublimable dispersant to reduce gate-induced drain leakage in BEOL-compatible transistors [Reducing the Gate-Induced Drain-Leakage Current of Carbon-Nanotube Transistors by Subliming Dispersants with Back-End-of-Line Compatible Temperature](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13583).
- **AI for Quantum Chemistry:** An autonomous LLM agent is shown to be capable of executing complex multireference quantum chemistry calculations, reproducing published results with a small mean absolute error when guided by a structured decision ladder [Can Autonomous LLM Agents Execute Multireference Quantum Chemistry Calculations?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13357).
