### Strongly Correlated and Topological Systems

This section covers advances in fractional quantum Hall physics, topological phases, and strongly correlated electron systems.

- **Twisted MoTe₂ Phase Diagram:** A comprehensive exact-diagonalization study maps the phase diagram of twisted bilayer MoTe₂, reproducing experimental fractional Chern insulator (FCI) hierarchies and identifying where multi-band physics is essential. Notably, they find band mixing destabilizes the composite Fermi liquid state at half-filling and find no evidence for a non-abelian Pfaffian state at ν=-3/2. [Large scale theoretical investigation of the phase diagram of twisted bilayer MoTe₂ at fractional fillings: agreements and contradictions with current experiments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23675)
- **Fractional Chern Insulator Stabilization:** A center-decorated kagome model is proposed where the quantum geometry (Berry curvature and Fubini-Study metric) can be tuned via a hopping parameter. This "quantum-geometry engineering" suppresses competing charge order and stabilizes dilute FCIs at ν=1/3 and the more fragile ν=1/5 filling. [Quantum-geometry stabilization of dilute fractional Chern insulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24013)
- **Composite Fermions in Quantum Spin Hall:** This work classifies all two-component composite-fermion states for the fractional quantum spin Hall effect at ν=3, including new classes. It proposes a combined probe of shot noise, thermal conductance, and a new transport measurement between two quantum point contacts to identify the experimentally relevant state. [Composite fermions in the ν=3 fractional quantum spin Hall effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24059)
- **Non-Abelian Anyon Fusion Probes:** Two interferometer configurations are proposed to probe the fusion structure of parafermion zero modes (PZMs) in fractional quantum Hall-superconductor heterostructures. The interferometer signal vanishes exactly in the topological limit, revealing the defects' non-Abelian nature. [Interferometric Signatures of Zero Modes in Fractional Quantum Hall-Superconductor Heterostructures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19312)
- **Composite Boson Condensation:** Tensor-network methods provide direct numerical confirmation of long-range order and composite boson condensation in lattice quantum Hall states, defining a nonlocal order parameter for the topological transition. [Long-Range Order and Composite Boson Condensation in Lattice Quantum Hall States](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24628)
- **Hartree-Fock Stability in tBG:** A rigorous study proves the strict coercivity of the Hartree-Fock Hessian for the minimizers in the chiral model of twisted bilayer graphene, confirming the stability of the flat-band ground states. [Hartree–Fock coercivity for twisted bilayer graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23884)

### Spintronics, Magnetism, and 2D Materials

This section highlights new magnetic phenomena, spin transport, and advances in van der Waals heterostructures.

- **Spin-Position Coupled Interactions:** A new, universal electron spin-spin interaction between Rydberg atoms is predicted, originating from fine-structure splitting and leading to spin-position coupling. This natively realizes the Kitaev-Heisenberg model and is experimentally observed in a companion paper. [Universal Spin-Position Coupled Rydberg Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23694) & [Observation of electron spin interactions between Rydberg atoms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23711)
- **Raman Circular Dichroism of Magnons:** A gauge-invariant theory of two-magnon Raman circular dichroism is developed, establishing it as a spectroscopic probe of higher-order magneto-optical quantum geometry, beyond the standard Berry curvature and quantum metric. [Raman Circular Dichroism Reveals Higher-Order Quantum Geometry of Magnons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23683)
- **Spectrally Programmable Spin Currents:** WSe₂-NiPS₃ magnetic van der Waals heterostructures demonstrate spectrally tunable spin-polarized photocurrents, achieving 80% spin polarization near the Néel temperature without requiring magnetic fields. [Spectrally Programmable Spin-Polarized Photocurrents in WSe₂-NiPS₃ Magnetic van der Waals Heterostructures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23832)
- **Intertwined Spin-Charge Order:** First-principles calculations on La₃Ni₂O₇ reveal that spin, charge, and lattice degrees of freedom are strongly intertwined in its density-wave state, with the low-energy state spontaneously relaxing into a polar structure. [Intertwined spin-charge stripe order and polar lattice distortion in La₃Ni₂O₇](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23823)
- **Piezomagnetism Mechanisms:** First-principles calculations disentangle two distinct microscopic mechanisms for piezomagnetism in CoF₂: one driven by sublattice moment imbalance and another by Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya-induced spin canting. [Two Microscopic Mechanisms of Piezomagnetism in CoF₂ from First-Principles Calculations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24227)
- **Altermagnetic Hall Effect:** A modified Kane-Mele-Hubbard model shows that hole doping drives a spin-flop transition that enables a correlation-driven altermagnetic anomalous Hall effect with spin-edge-locked chiral modes, avoiding the need for Ising-like interactions. [Altermagnetic Anomalous Hall Effect and Spin–Edge-Locked Chiral Modes in a Modified Kane–Mele–Hubbard Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24408)
- **Magnon Layer-Hall Effect:** A new mechanism for the magnon layer Hall effect is predicted, originating from non-reciprocal dipolar scattering at heterointerfaces rather than band geometry, leading to a large Hall angle in conventional magnetic heterostructures. [Scattering-Induced Magnon Layer-Hall Transport beyond Band Geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24023)

### Quantum Simulation and Cold Atoms

This section covers new platforms and phenomena in quantum simulation and ultracold atom physics.

- **Stable Quantum Droplet Vortices:** Stable rotating 3D vortex clusters in binary Bose-Einstein condensates are predicted, existing as interacting vortex lines with identical charges within a common envelope. [Stable rotating vortex clusters in three-dimensional quantum droplets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24036)
- **Tunable Mediated Interactions:** A proposal shows how Nambu-Goldstone modes near spontaneous symmetry breaking can generate tunable long-range interactions between impurities, ranging from a Coulomb-like law to Yukawa-type and even Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya-like couplings. [Tunable Mediated Interactions Near Spontaneous Symmetry Breaking: From Yukawa to Coulomb and Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23954)
- **Long-Lived Hybrid Ion-Atom Trap:** A hybrid trap achieves multi-hour stable trapping of non-laser-coolable ions via resonant charge-exchange cooling with ultracold atoms, overcoming a critical limitation of previous systems and enabling extended studies of ultracold chemistry. [Multi-hour stable trapping and threshold steady state of non-laser-coolable ions with ultracold atoms in a hybrid trap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23768)
- **Quantum Simulator of Curved Space:** A proposal uses a multimode cavity and a tweezer-trapped atom to create a momentum-selective Unruh-DeWitt detector. This allows for controllable filtering of non-phononic excitations, enhancing the simulated relativistic field phenomena signal. [Tunable-Size Unruh-DeWitt Detector in a Multimode Cavity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24451)

### Superconductivity and Josephson Junctions

This section focuses on new superconducting phenomena, Josephson effects, and material advances.

- **Spin and Charge Diodes:** A four-terminal Josephson junction with p-wave magnets is predicted to exhibit perfect, phase-controlled nonlocal spin and charge diode effects, robust against disorder and parameter variations. [Phase-controlled perfect nonlocal spin and charge diode effects in a four-terminal Josephson junction with p-wave magnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24147)
- **Nonlocal Josephson Diode:** Spin-polarized Andreev molecules in altermagnetic Josephson junctions induce an anomalous nonlocal Josephson effect with tunable nonreciprocal currents, enabling a nonlocal Josephson diode effect. [Spin-polarized Andreev molecules and anomalous nonlocal Josephson effects in altermagnetic junctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04954)
- **Confined Thin-Film Superconductors:** A Ginzburg-Landau theory is developed for superconducting thin films under quantum confinement, showing confinement renormalizes the coherence length and drives systems toward stronger type-II behavior. [Ginzburg-Landau Theory for Confined Thin-Film Superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10686)
- **Spontaneous Vortex Formation:** Scanning SQUID microscopy reveals the spontaneous nucleation of vortices in an intrinsically inhomogeneous, proximity-induced superconductor at slow cooling rates, with unusual extended morphologies. [Imaging and characterization of spontaneous vortices in a proximity-induced superconductor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14697)
- **Enhanced FeSe Films:** A simplified molecular beam epitaxy protocol is reported for growing superconducting multi-unit-cell β-FeSe films on SrTiO₃ with a T_c ~ 20 K, eliminating the need for careful substrate pre-treatment and post-growth annealing. [Enhanced Superconductivity in Multilayer FeSe Films by Simplified Molecular Beam Epitaxy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24710)
- **Bipolar Magnetic Semiconductor Josephson Junctions:** It is shown that the exchange splitting in a bipolar magnetic semiconductor enables purely electrical control of the 0-π transition in a Josephson junction. While an anomalous Josephson effect occurs with Rashba coupling, no diode effect is observed, highlighting a distinction between the two. [Josephson effect in bipolar magnetic semiconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24322)

### Computational Methods and Machine Learning

This section highlights new computational tools, algorithms, and machine-learning approaches for materials research.

- **Exact Sampling of Spin Glasses:** A novel algorithm generates exact, independent samples from the Boltzmann distribution of planar Ising spin glasses using the Kac-Ward theory, providing a rigorous benchmark for neural autoregressive samplers. [Exact autoregressive sampling of planar Ising spin glasses via the Kac–Ward theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24382)
- **LLM-Driven Materials Discovery:** An autonomous LLM research loop was used to optimize a crystal graph network, achieving state-of-the-art accuracy on the MatBench band-gap benchmark without external pretraining. [Optimizing Expert-Designed Crystal Graph Networks for Band-Gap Prediction with an Autonomous LLM Research Loop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29717)
- **Machine-Learning Nuclear Quantum Effects:** Machine-learning-enhanced path-integral simulations reveal that nuclear quantum effects significantly lower free-energy barriers for hydrogen diffusion in layered MoS₂, leading to a pronounced kinetic isotope effect. [Capturing Nuclear Quantum Effects in Hydrogen Diffusion through MoS₂ via Machine-Learning-Enhanced Path-Integral Simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25991)
- **Randomized Eigensolvers for DFT:** A new randomized block Davidson eigensolver is presented that replaces expensive Euclidean orthogonalization with a sketched inner product, improving performance for large plane-wave density-functional theory calculations. [Randomized Block Davidson Eigensolvers for Plane-Wave Density-Functional Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24529)
- **Overcoming Slow Mixing with ML:** A new sampling algorithm uses the transfer operator of a local Monte Carlo dynamics to construct eigenfunctions that resolve slow modes, allowing efficient importance sampling of complex systems like the 1D spin-glass. [Learning metastable states and overcoming slow dynamics with a state-dependent damping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.05736)

### Optical, Magnetic, and Quantum Devices

This section covers advances in experimental and theoretical device physics, from classical photonics to quantum computing hardware.

- **Oxide/Graphene Plasmon Engineering:** Ultrathin oxide overlayers are shown to enable systematic engineering of graphene plasmons via interfacial charge transfer, with MoOx strongly enhancing the plasmonic response. [Engineering Plasmons in Oxide/Graphene Heterostructures via Interfacial Charge Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24619)
- **Topological Spin Texture Writing:** Micromagnetic simulations reveal that structured light pulses with orbital angular momentum can deterministically nucleate asymmetric bimerons and antibimerons, enabling a route to topology-selective writing. [Quantum encoding of structured light into in-plane topological spin textures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23856)
- **Ferrimagnetic Spin Hall Oscillators:** Spin Hall nano-oscillators based on near-compensated CoGd ferrimagnets demonstrate an order-of-magnitude improvement in efficiency (low current density, small magnetic field, narrow linewidth) over ferromagnetic counterparts. [Energy-efficient spin Hall nano-oscillators using near-compensated CoGd ferrimagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09021)
- **Multi-hour Stable Ion Trapping:** A hybrid ion-atom trap successfully trapped non-laser-coolable ions for over 6 hours using resonant charge-exchange cooling, overcoming a critical limitation in hybrid systems. [Multi-hour stable trapping and threshold steady state of non-laser-coolable ions with ultracold atoms in a hybrid trap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23768)
- **Cryogenic Qubit Readout:** A flip-chip integrated platform successfully combines SiMOS quantum dot devices with a superconducting microwave resonator, enabling dispersive readout of charge transitions at cryogenic temperatures. [Dispersive Readout of a SiMOS Quantum Dot Using a Flip-Chip Integrated Microwave Resonator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14559)
- **Low-Noise QD Single-Photon Sources:** Temperature-dependent studies on GaAs quantum dots show that Purcell enhancement can significantly improve photon indistinguishability at elevated temperatures, reaching a record value of 0.80 at 32 K. [Towards Stirling cooler operable single-photon sources based on low-noise GaAs quantum dots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24409)
