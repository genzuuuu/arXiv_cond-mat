# Today's arXiv Condensed Matter Digest

## Quantum Matter & Topology

The interface between symmetry, disorder, and topology dominates today's theory contributions. Burkov's [Anderson localization in topological metals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16124) answers a sharp question: does topology protect a metal from localization? The answer is yes for 3D Weyl semimetals, but no for 2D/3D Dirac or nodal-line semimetals—established by tying the unquantized anomaly to a decorated-domain-wall construction. Ezawa's study of [topological superconductors coupled to X-wave magnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16084) finds altermagnetic order stabilizes chiral (p_x+ip_y)-wave class-D states (Chern number up to 2 in the i-wave case), while odd-parity magnets yield Majorana flat bands robust against s-wave mixing. Read together, these papers reinforce that the *type* of band-touching node—not mere gaplessness—determines whether topology survives.

Three further works push topological engineering into driven and defect regimes. [Algebras of order parameters in 1D spin systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27193) formalizes when string operators acquire nonzero expectation values, organizing them into Lagrangian algebras in the Drinfel'd center—a tensor-network-based classification that extends to mixed states. [Designs without disorder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16140) shows a single O(1) bulk defect suffices to generate approximate unitary k-designs from an otherwise integrable XXZ chain via two-Pauli-kick protocols, deriving an analytic selection rule from domain-wall counting. [Exceptional topology survives strong Hermitian fields in radiative atomic arrays](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17313) reports the rare case where a strong Hermitian magnetic field does *not* destroy exceptional points, instead driving them toward the light cone owing to singular dipolar couplings.

## Computational Methods

This is a standout day for method development, with the theme of *scaling exact techniques to real materials*. White's [Hartree-Fock DMRG for very long chains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16587) is the headline: by compressing the occupied state and long-range interaction separately while retaining exchange, a 100,000-electron hydrogen chain in 1.8 million basis functions converges in about two hours on a 64-GB Mac mini, with work nearly linear in chain length. This plausibly reshapes what "routine" HF looks like.

Complementary advances: [Nonlinear electron-phonon interactions from first principles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17433) provides a general computational framework for arbitrary-order coupling matrix elements (with Wannier-Fourier interpolation), showing second-order couplings are essential for quantitative polaron formation energies and hopping barriers. [Highly parallel real-space RPA using Lanczos quadrature and interpolation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16408) reaches chemical accuracy for 256-electron silicon in under 30 minutes on 4,096 cores, with 8× prefactor reduction from coarse-grid interpolation. [Full minimal coupling all-electron RT-TDDFT for X-ray-matter interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16441) removes the dipole approximation where it fails, retaining photon momentum in both paramagnetic and diamagnetic terms. [ZStar](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16802) automates polarization, Born charges, dielectric response, and IR/Raman spectra across all dimensionalities, and [svPITE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07559) packages probabilistic imaginary-time evolution for ground-state preparation. Finally, [SpectraFit XPS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16789) delivers a free browser-based XPS fitting tool whose quantification pipeline exactly reproduces reference values in a 30-case regression suite—spectra never leave the user's machine.

## Superconductivity & Pairing

A strong cluster spans cuprates, nickelates, iron pnictides, and moiré systems. [Optical and magnetic signatures of drive-enhanced coherence in phase-disordered superconducting bilayers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16137) draws an experimentally actionable distinction: both symmetric and antisymmetric drives enhance 1/ω optical conductivity, but *only* the layer-symmetric drive strengthens counterflow screening. The lesson for driven YBCO: enhanced optical conductivity alone does not imply enhanced diamagnetism—a symmetry diagnostic for the phase-synchronization mechanism. [Mode-selective phonon effects in trilayer nickelates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16143) identifies out-of-plane oxygen breathing modes whose interlayer phase relations selectively enhance or suppress spin-density-wave and s_± pairing, with selected modes yielding negative isotope coefficients consistent with recent observations. [NaFeP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17028) is a predicted—not yet synthesized—111 iron phosphide; the "physics judge" returns a near-nodal s_± gap (inner hole cylinder at the node threshold), and the author transparently reports base-dependence disagreement rather than resolving it by choice.

On the experimental and device side, [La substitution in CeRh₂As₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17080) finds T_c and T_0 both suppressed below 0.1 K for x > 0.05—far too rapid for the lattice-expansion effect alone, implicating substitution disorder and pair breaking in an unconventional superconductor. [Proximity-induced superconductivity in bilayer graphene quantum dots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16320) demonstrates a proximity gap up to 80 μeV persisting beyond 1 μm, consistent with ballistic transport. [Interplay of dimerization and quasiperiodicity in a hybrid ring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17069) shows SSH dimerization sets the oscillation and penetration length of proximity pairing while AAH quasiperiodicity controls its magnitude. [Phase transitions in disordered altermagnetic Josephson junctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23607) finds the anomalous φ phase highly fragile—disorder drives it to π or 0 nonreciprocally. A voltage-driven [Bloch transistor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14080) is proposed as an alternative quantum resistance standard operating at the fundamental conductance 1/R_Q without strong magnetic fields.

## Machine Learning & AI for Science

The field increasingly reports *quantified* gains over single-agent baselines. [HiMatGen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16466) scales LLM agents through hierarchical collective reasoning—100 investigators in discussion pods across ten domains—yielding 1.9× more candidates and 1.7× more stable-unique-novel structures than a tool-enabled single-agent baseline, with a 22× token reduction via hierarchical discussion in paired comparisons. [AI for Science with GPT-6 Astra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17123) applies agent workflows to 2D CFET thermal design, reducing peak temperature rise by 1.67 K at fixed metal volume and quantifying the accompanying 2% drive-current cost.

Methodological rigor is the through-line. [Unsupervised ML of the contact process](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16506) surfaces a genuinely instructive pitfall: positive-definite configurations break standard PCA centering and fixed-Gaussian VAE priors, and both require physics-aware fixes (sign-reversal augmentation, heteroscedastic priors) to recover finite-size scaling. [Symbolic ensemble learning of interatomic potentials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16526) combines Equation Learner networks with MCTS and transfer learning to obtain sub-10 meV/atom aluminum potentials; the ensemble beats each constituent across phonon, surface, and elastic benchmarks. [MEOWN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17063) predicts muon stopping sites in minutes rather than DFT timescales with physically interpretable electrostatics. [A probabilistic deep learning framework for phase transformation forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16940) unifies image compression, temporal fusion transformers, and Gaussian processes for in situ microscopy of Widmanstätten ferrite. [Molecular representation shapes target fidelity and exploration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16028) makes a clean point: different representations can show similar surrogate fidelity yet diverge sharply in validity and training-set replay.

## Quantum Simulation & Dynamics

A hardware milestone: [Large-scale quantum simulations of dissipative spin-1/2 Heisenberg chains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16108) runs 50-site Lindblad dynamics on ibm_kingston (100 active qubits, up to 1,700 entangling-gate depths), mapping the J_x–J_y phase diagram with 117 data points. The key enabling insight is that dissipation is self-correcting, so noise enters only as a weak competing dissipator. The work resolves a long-standing disagreement over this benchmark's phase diagram and finds an incipient Trotter-induced spin-density-wave phase. [Investigating interacting fermionic models with locality-preserving qubit encodings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16142) benchmarks Derby-Klassen against Jordan-Wigner at nonzero chemical potential, where the half-filled sign-free condition is absent, and identifies the mode-placement tradeoff between hopping and interaction locality.

On the relaxation side, [Initial-slip dynamics enables the quantum Mpemba effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16781) provides a mechanistic explanation: neglecting the sudden switch-on of system-bath coupling completely suppresses the anomalous relaxation, identifying transient correlations as the key ingredient. [Preparation-protocol-dependent quantum Mpemba dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17176) then shows the effect is itself a control knob—bare Gibbs preparation gives strong anomalous relaxation, whereas driven steady-state preparation almost eliminates it, traced to modal weights rather than the decay-rate hierarchy. [Nonlocal magic across the MBL crossover](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16935) introduces min-relative nonlocal magic, which resolves Schmidt-spectrum reorganization invisible to entanglement entropy during the ergodic-to-MBL crossover.

## Quantum Sensing & Noise

[Noise spectroscopy using quantum sensing ensembles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16643) tackles a practical barrier to scaling NV ensembles: variable coupling across the ensemble makes output hard to interpret. The authors show that sequence timing sets both frequency selectivity *and* ensemble weighting, and incorporating the coupling distribution recovers consistent surface-noise parameters from two depth-distinct ensembles. [Direct observation of dipolar-driven anisotropic quantum projection noise](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16106) achieves projection-noise-resolved readout of a 2D NV ensemble via repetitive ¹⁵N nuclear readout at ~0.3 T, improving readout fidelity by nearly an order of magnitude and directly watching dipolar interactions shear projection noise into an anisotropic profile—opening the door to solid-state spin squeezing. [Room-temperature 1/3 suppression of diffusive shot noise](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16783) realizes the universal diffusive shot-noise reduction in ferrocene redox cycling at room temperature, bridging mesoscopic physics and electrochemistry. [Integrated quantum-secured cryptographic memory in single nanodiamonds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17329) fuses base-36 data writing via focused-electron-beam defect engineering with ODMR-extracted atomic-scale quantum fingerprints.

## Soft, Active & Statistical Matter

[Odd diffusion and power-law correlations in chiral mass-transport processes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16821) identifies a mechanism distinct from anisotropic hopping: chirality alone generates scale-invariant correlations C(r) ~ r⁻⁴ even under lattice rotation symmetry, with a cusp singularity in the structure factor beyond a threshold odd-diffusion strength. [Odd slip at chiral active surfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17005) introduces transverse momentum transfer at boundaries—dissipationless in effect but energetically costly to maintain—and shows strikingly that suspensions of odd-slip particles embedded in ordinary Newtonian fluid *exhibit odd viscosity*, a new minimal mechanism. [Trajectory statistics govern mechanical power transfer in active baths](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17047) proves positive power transfer is excluded for active OU particles in all dimensions and complete-reset RTP in d ≥ 2, separating bath dynamics from probe coupling so neither needs recalculation per probe.

[Glass and jamming transitions in a random organization model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15519) disentangles distinct amorphous transitions with a binary mixture, finding a protocol-dependent absorbing transition and marginal stability reminiscent of Gardner phases. [Adsorbing boundaries stratify confined colloidal phase separation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16583) shows boundaries don't slow coarsening uniformly but split it into regimes—central poroelastic L ~ t¹ᐟ² growth coexisting with L ~ t¹ᐟ³ near walls—precluding a single dynamic-scaling length. [1/f frequency noise scales inversely with volume, not quality factor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16668) is a provocative meta-analysis: across 13 decades, h₋₁V is invariant, volume-independent noise is excluded at 9σ, and reported Q⁻ⁿ laws are artifacts of Q-volume co-variation. [Shannon entropy as an order parameter for confined Coulomb systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15428) proves an exact entropy balance law and topological charge sum rule for the 2D Thomson problem, verified to machine precision for N = 12 to 10⁵.

## Electronic Structure & Materials Properties

[Breaking the Wiedemann-Franz limit via separated flat bands](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16261) proposes flat-band systems (demonstrated for monolayer Ni₃In with two bands near E_F) for scattering phase-space engineering, achieving large Seebeck and conductivity simultaneously while suppressing electronic heat conduction; the accompanying ~5×10⁴-dataset "Wiedemann-Franz plot" of zT vs. S² is a useful community tool for flagging L ≪ L₀ reports. [Nonlinear photocurrent spectroscopy in CuScP₂S₆](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16577) establishes a 2.35 eV gap and zero-bias photocurrent whose polarity depends on both photon energy and polarization orientation, with power-law scaling consistent with shift-current origin. [Optical properties of single-crystal As₂Se₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15340) combines G₀W₀ with BSE to resolve a 2.31 eV indirect gap and identify bound excitons, settling controversy about the optical gap's nature.

[Fractional-quantum ferroelectrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16799) offers a compelling design principle: in α-In₂Se₃, polarization arises from discrete lattice-scale displacements rather than soft-mode condensation, so ferroelectric-mode scattering is *absent*, lifting room-temperature mobility above 70 cm²/Vs (and beyond 300 with doping)—decoupling robust polarization from strong carrier scattering. [Origin and reduction of coercive fields in ZnO-based wurtzite ferroelectrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16570) traces E_c to an under-coordinated, field-aligned polar filament head; charge redistribution lowers E_c more effectively than strain, with combined effects giving ~50% reduction. [Electric-field-switchable altermagnetism via ligand rotation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16749) is notable for establishing ligand p orbitals—not transition-metal d orbitals—as the origin of altermagnetic order, with barrierless on/off switching at ~0.22 V/Å. [Floquet spin-antiferroelectric phase in collinear antiferromagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16758) predicts symmetry-forbidden spin-resolved in-plane polarizations in driven monolayer MnPS₃, and [Nonlinear Hall effect in altermagnetic warped topological insulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17001) shows the quantum metric dipole becomes the sole driver of the response at charge neutrality, tunable by altermagnet orientation angle.

## Superexchange, Phonons & Magnetism

[Orbital-resolved superexchange and topological magnon bands in MXene Fe₂C](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16530) builds a refined perturbation-plus-tight-binding model showing intralayer coupling is governed by in-plane p_x/p_y orbitals while interlayer coupling comes from p_z—a direct orbital-level explanation for the magnon Dirac point and valley Chern number protected by C₃ᵥ. [Topological spin textures in 2D altermagnetic chromium chalcogenides](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16780) distinguishes *synergistic* frustration-DMI effects (frustration lowers critical DMI) from *competitive* ones (frustration favors high topological charge, DMI favors low). [Weak ferromagnetism in the J₁-J₂-J₂′ model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16593) finds a temperature-dependent net magnetization whose absolute value first rises then falls, with J₂ ≠ J₂′ as the primary driver—a mechanism distinct from Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya. [Quantum skyrmions in the antiferromagnetic triangular lattice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18737) uses DMRG to stabilize three-sublattice quantum AF skyrmion textures over a wide field range. [Interplay of spin-lattice and electronic coupling far above Néel ordering in CrPS₄](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11203) identifies a coupled spin-phonon-electronic state persisting to T* ≈ 120 K (well above T_N ≈ 38 K), with the anomaly propagating into an adjacent In₂Se₃ layer in a heterostructure.
