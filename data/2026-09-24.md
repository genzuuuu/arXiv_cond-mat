# Today's arXiv Condensed Matter Physics — Key Advances

## Kinetics and Transport Theory
Exact analytical treatments of the Boltzmann equation headline this section. Karlin derives an exact nonlinear generating vertex for collisional stress and heat-flux production in hard-sphere gases, validated against DSMC simulations including a Mach-5 shock. [Exact Collision Vertex for Stress and Heat Flux](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26807) Wei and collaborators extend exact solutions to the relativistic Boltzmann equation for multicomponent massless systems, uncovering novel hybrid Maxwell-Jüttner distributions. [Analytical Solution of the Nonlinear Boltzmann Equation For Multicomponent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26845)

On the fluctuation side, Levit and Reuveni identify a phase transition in first-passage times: sufficiently broad jump-rate fluctuations regularize the diverging mean time to make it finite. [First Passage in the Presence of Jump Rate Fluctuations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26925) Tasinkevych et al. show how self-propulsion breaks the uphill/downhill first-passage symmetry for active Brownian particles, yet establish an exact path-reweighting between the two ensembles. [Uphill and downhill first passage of an active Brownian particle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27425)

## Correlated Electrons and Quantum Materials
Two independent DQMC studies sharpen our picture of strange metallicity. Ma, Zhang, and Devereaux find that linear-in-T resistivity survives in both attractive and repulsive Hubbard models at strong coupling, tracing it to Curie-like compressibility and weakly T-dependent diffusivity. [Robust Strange Metallicity across Attractive and Repulsive Hubbard Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26971) Atambo benchmarks GW against exact diagonalization for the extended Hubbard model, showing that vertex corrections in the polarizability channel worsen gap errors—implying that the Mott gap demands dynamical self-energy structure, not improved screening. [Accidental accuracy and vertex corrections in GW](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24838)

## Machine Learning for Many-Body Simulation
A notable cluster of ML-assisted methods appears today. Jang, Bakshi, and Chern show that ordinary MLPs with finite-group averaging yield exactly equivariant surrogates for lattice dynamics, recovering charge ordering and CDW coarsening in Falicov-Kimball and Holstein models. [Exact Equivariance from Ordinary Neural Networks for Lattice Many-Body Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27132) Hu et al. introduce the hyperdeterminant wavefunction, a phase-agnostic variational ansatz achieving >99.9% overlap with exact ground states across fractional Chern insulator phases. [HyperDet Wavefunction: A Phase-Agnostic Ansatz for Strongly Correlated Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04146) For solid-liquid interfaces, the SoLiD26 dataset provides 15.4 million DFT structures for training interatomic potentials. [SoLiD26: A First Principles Solid-Liquid Interface Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28013)

## Topological States and Quantum Hall Systems
Experimental progress on fractional quantum Hall states in multilayers is striking. Wang et al. observe a $\nu=5/7$ FQHS in a GaAs trilayer, naturally interpreted as the long-predicted $\Psi_{33311}$ generalized Laughlin state extending interlayer coherence to three layers. [Interlayer-correlated fractional quantum Hall state in a trilayer electron system](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26916) In acoustic metamaterials, Li et al. realize concurrent $\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Z}_2$ topology in a 2D semimetal, manifesting helical anomaly bulk states absent in single-invariant topologies. [Helical Anomaly from Concurrent $\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Z}_2$ Topology in an Acoustic Semimetal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28125)

On the theory front, Leung and Prodan show that the noncommutative second Chern number of a disordered axion pump remains quantized deep into the mobility-gap regime, with the Středa-Hall response serving as an internal control that distinguishes bulk protection from finite-size artifacts. [Mobility-Gap Robustness and Středa-Hall Separation in Disordered Axion Pumping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27324) Galviz proves that torus Berry data determine all-genus Abelian topological orders, establishing a direct link between genus-one measurements and the full extended TQFT. [Torus Berry Data Determine All-Genus Abelian Topological Orders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20330)

## Magnetism, Spin Dynamics, and Altermagnetism
The spin structure of UTe₂ comes into sharp focus. Wang et al. use polarized INS to demonstrate that antiferromagnetic fluctuations at the Y point are isotropic within the ac-plane but vanish along b, ruling out Ising anisotropy and favoring a d-vector along b for spin-triplet pairing. [Easy-plane anisotropy of magnetic fluctuations in UTe2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26869) Radaelli develops a representation-theoretic framework for time-reversal-odd tensorial properties based on exchange multiplets, applicable to altermagnets and conventional antiferromagnets. [Altermagnetism and generalised tensorial properties in the exchange multiplet framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28453)

A cautionary result on spin nutation: Weißenhofer et al. demonstrate that the inertial LLG equation's predicted nutation mode is a truncation-induced high-frequency artifact arising from eliminating dynamical degrees of freedom, questioning whether observed high-frequency spectral features constitute physical evidence for spin inertia. [Spurious spin nutation modes from truncated memory effects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27487)

## Ultrafast and THz-Driven Phenomena
Two complementary studies probe light-driven symmetry breaking in SrTiO₃. Choi et al. report THz-driven quantum ionic magnetism—an oscillatory ionic magnetization without corresponding polarization oscillation—arising from beating between quantum ionic eigenstates. [THz-Driven Quantum Ionic Magnetism in a Quantum Paraelectric SrTiO3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27347) Their companion work demonstrates that elliptically polarized THz pulses break time-reversal symmetry in cubic SrTiO₃, revealed by emergent SHG circular dichroism and a linear scaling with pulse angular momentum. [Light-Driven Ultrafast Control of Time-Reversal Symmetry in SrTiO3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09497) Zhang et al. predict chiral dynamical multiferroicity in BaTiO₃ moiré lattices driven by achiral pulses, where dipolar mass enables the effect. [Emergence of Chiral Dynamical Multiferroicity in a Ferroelectric Lattice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27592)

## Superconductivity
The orbital Nernst effect is observed for the first time. Masuda et al. detect a thermally induced transverse voltage in Ti that depends on magnetization direction and scales linearly with temperature gradient, establishing orbital caloritronics. [Observation of the orbital Nernst effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27604) In quasicrystal superconductors, Kasai et al. report a sharp transition at 1.35 K in (Ta₀.₇Nb₀.₃)₁.₆Te with strongly anisotropic upper critical field exceeding the Pauli limit by 2.5×. [Anisotropic upper critical field in the van der Waals superconducting quasicrystal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27352)

Paulescu et al. uncover field-induced incipient spin-density wave order within the nematic phase of FeSe₁₋ₓSₓ at fields up to 68 T, linking chemical substitution and pressure as parallel routes to SDW stabilization. [Field-induced incipient spin-density phase stabilized inside the nematic phase of FeSe₁₋ₓSₓ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28446)

## Quantum Information and Simulation
Kim et al. identify transparent domain walls through entanglement-bootstrap methods using information convex sets, showing that maximal entanglement asymmetry equals log GSD. [Transparent Domain Walls through Information Convex Sets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26892) Mackay et al. determine optimal low-rank compression limits for local quantum dynamics: time-independent evolution requires rank $\log D = \tilde{O}(t + \sqrt{\log(1/\epsilon)})$, while driven systems need a different exponent. [Optimal low-rank compression of quantum dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27497)

Experimentally, Ozawa et al. achieve strong coupling of a ¹⁷¹Yb atom array to a telecom-band nanofiber cavity with single-atom cooperativity C = 5.6, projecting 40 kHz remote entanglement generation. [Strong coupling of a reconfigurable ¹⁷¹Yb atom array to a tunable telecom-band nanofiber cavity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25232) Sun et al. demonstrate a photonic integrated comb engine at 780 nm for coherent control of a Bose-Einstein condensate. [A photonic integrated comb engine for ultracold quantum gases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28294)

[Control of filament network rigidity by the condensation of crowding molecules](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26983) Viscous dissipation governs bubble morphology in soft matter, as shown by SP et al.: elastic gels form ellipsoidal cavities before rupture, while viscoelastic gels sustain spherical bubbles. [Viscous Dissipation Governs Bubble Morphology and Failure in Soft Matter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26943)

[An interdimensional funnel enhances topologically protected oscillations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26966) Huang and Tang develop active self-consistent field theory for Ornstein-Uhlenbeck polymers, finding that activity suppresses microphase separation with density modulation decaying as τ⁻¹ᐟ². [Active Self-Consistent Field Theory for Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Polymers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28124)

## Quantum Computing Hardware

[Programmable digital quantum simulation of 2D Fermi-Hubbard dynamics using 72 superconducting qubits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26845)

[Constant-Depth Clifford-Hierarchy Gates via Non-Abelian Surface Codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13777) Garcia-Wetten et al. establish heteroepitaxial γ-Al₂O₃ on TiN as a low-loss dielectric platform for superconducting qubits with TLS loss below 3×10⁻⁵. [Oxide-Nitride Heteroepitaxy for Low-Loss Dielectrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29065)

## Computational Methods
Two methodological advances stand out. Vidarte et al. develop a real-space spectral approach to orbital magnetization based on Chebyshev expansions, applicable to large disordered systems and yielding the bulk Chern number directly from magnetization spectral density. [Real-Space Spectral Approach to Orbital Magnetization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01575) Zarrouk and Caro introduce molecular augmented dynamics, which identifies atomistic structures matching multiple experimental observables while maintaining low MLIP energies—a computational "microscope" for disordered materials. [Molecular augmented dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17132)

For long-range interactions, Buchheit and Busse derive stably computable representations of anisotropic Epstein zeta functions from wave-vector derivatives, enabling machine-precision evaluation across arbitrary lattices and anisotropy orders. [Computation of anisotropic singular sums from high-order derivatives of Epstein zeta functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28282)
