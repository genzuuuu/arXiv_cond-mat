# arXiv Condensed Matter Highlights

## Quantum Hall, Topological Order & Fractionalization

Several papers probe how topology manifests in spectra and transport. A Haldane-sphere study shows that **spectral crossings can diagnose whether a multiply quantized vortex remains fused or splits** into unit-charge zeros, with distinctive scaling laws `|τ_c| ~ N^-(r+1)` versus `N^-2` — [Spectral Crossings Diagnose Multiply Quantized Vortex Splitting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02234). Shot noise in complex fractional quantum Hall edges can be disentangled from equilibration heating to recover quasiparticle charge — [Extracting the anyon charge from shot noise in complex fractional quantum Hall edges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02818). A projective symmetry group analysis extends the classification of **fermionic Z₂ spin liquids to dipolar-octupolar pyrochlores**, connecting gapless spinon thermodynamics to experiments like Nd₂ScNbO₇ — [Projective symmetry group classification of fermionic Z₂ spin liquids on the dipolar-octupolar pyrochlore magnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02409).

On the tensor-network and stabilizer side: Majorana-Pauli stabilizer codes give exact lattice realizations of **intrinsically fermionic topological order** and a duality web linking bosonic and fermionic phases — [Majorana-Pauli stabilizer codes and duality webs of fermionic topological phases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25048). A holographic (Walker-Wang) approach tackles **anyon decoherence** in Kitaev's sixteenfold way, showing decohering the fermion always yields intrinsically mixed-state topological order — [Decohering Kitaev's Sixteenfold Way: A Holographic Approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03610). Measurement-induced thresholds in stabilizer codes split into **geometric versus homological percolation classes**, benchmarked on over a million qubits — [Homological Thresholds in Randomly Monitored Quantum Error-Correcting Codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02310).

## Moiré Systems & Correlated Electrons

Neural quantum states (message-passing networks + variational Monte Carlo) resolve a rich **phase diagram for ν=2 moiré electrons**, predicting a correlated metal and spin-dependent molecular insulators with metamagnetic transitions — [Correlated Metals, Metamagnetism, and Orbital Nematic Order in moiré Materials with Neural Quantum States](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03145). Magnetotransport in twisted MoTe₂ identifies **generalized Wigner crystals** at 1/3 and 2/3 filling with contrasting field responses, plus a stripe phase near half filling — [Magnetic-field response of generalized Wigner crystals in twisted MoTe₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02493). On the device side, electrostatic doping of WSe₂ on twisted bilayer graphene produces **satellite excitonic resonances tunable via the moiré potential** — [Electrostatic Doping of Moiré Superlattices Controls the Optical Fingerprint of a WSe₂/Twisted Bilayer Graphene heterostructure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03532).

## Superconductivity & Pairing

A sign-problem-free route via the **Holstein model** enables determinant QMC access to **FFLO (finite-momentum) pairing** in spin-imbalanced systems — [Finite-momentum pairing and magnetic halos in a spin-imbalanced Holstein model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03678). Theory predicts that **THz 2D coherent spectroscopy fingerprints the Leggett mode** in multiband superconductors through sidebands at `ω_L ± Ω` — [Sideband fingerprint of the Leggett mode in terahertz two-dimensional coherent spectroscopy of multiband superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02978). Bilayer nickelates emerge as platforms for **mirror-protected topological superconductivity** with Majorana Kramers pairs — [Layer-Asymmetry-Induced Topological Superconductivity in High-Tc Bilayer Nickelates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03601). A dense Fermi–dilute Bose mixture near mechanical instability yields **parametrically enhanced `T_c ∝ g`** rather than the BCS exponential — [Quantum critical superconductivity in a dense fermi dilute bose mixture close to mechanical instability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03094). Finally, symmetry fractionalization offers a route to **simultaneous superconductivity and time-reversal breaking at a single transition** in Z₂ Kondo lattices — [Fractionalization-Induced Time-Reversal Symmetry Breaking at Continuous Superconducting Transitions in Low-Symmetry Kondo Lattices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02635).

## Light–Matter Coupling & Cavity QED

**Chiral cavities distinguish resonant vs. antiresonant coupling** to a 2D electron gas, producing handedness-dependent corrections to Hall resistivity and a two-mode squeezed ground state — [Chiral Vacuum Engineering of Quantum Hall Matter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02400). Van der Waals **graphene/hBN multilayer cavities** enable magnetically tunable, multimode strong coupling between inter-Landau-level transitions and hBN phonons — [Quantum materials QED with van der Waals crystals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02514). Reconfigurable dielectric-resonator arrays demonstrate **site-selective magnon-photon coupling governed by collective-mode profiles**, validating a local-node selection rule — [Highly Tunable Photon-Magnon Coupling Governed by Collective-Mode Profiles in Reconfigurable Dielectric-Resonator Arrays](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02422).

## Glasses, Soft & Active Matter

A landmark negative result: **material-time scaling breaks down at the microscopic level** in metallic glasses, where compressed-exponential relaxation emerges even after 4 K jumps — [Breakdown of material-time scaling in the microscopic dynamics of metallic glasses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02426). In amorphous carbon, **shear transformations reduce to single bond breaking/formation** with a universal barrier shape and cubic law — [Universal energy barrier for plastic flow in a network glass](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02454). Random-organization models exhibit **glass and jamming transitions** with protocol-dependent critical points and Gardner-like marginal stability — [Glass and jamming transitions in a random organization model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15519). For active systems, a stepping-stone model reveals **traveling waves and pattern formation**, with excessive activity disrupting invasion waves — [Traveling waves, phase separation and pattern formation in the active stepping stone model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02346). Migrating neural crest clusters behave as **active droplets with asymmetric contact angles**, enabling tissue-viscosity estimation — [Migrating cell clusters as active droplets with asymmetric shape and contact angles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03536).

## Computational Methods & Machine Learning

An **AI Theorist** multi-agent system autonomously developed a physical model for excitonic structure in α-RuCl₃ from optical and photocurrent data — [The AI Theorist reveals excitonic structure in α-RuCl₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02417). A tensor-network finite-element PDE solver achieves **polynomial scaling with bounded bond dimension** — [A Polynomial-Scaling PDE Solver with Entanglement-Basis Tensor Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02316). Finally, complexity results show that **computing stabilizer entropy of 2D tensor networks is #P-hard**, even at constant bond dimension — [Nonstabilizerness of quantum tensor network states is intractable in two dimensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31459).
