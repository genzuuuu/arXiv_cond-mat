### Topology and Topological Phases
- A new theorem proves that wavefunction zeros in a crystal can directly reveal band topology, offering a novel way to connect scanning tunneling microscopy (STM) to group representation theory. This is applied to detect topological invariants in models like the Haldane model and BHZ model. [Real-Space Imaging of Band Topology via Wavefunction Zeros](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21699)
- A new integer topological order parameter for quantum spin chains is introduced, the higher Berry curvature (HBC), which counts gapless edge modes. It is shown to signal phase transitions by a change in its integer value. [Counting Edge Modes with the Higher Berry Curvature: A Bulk Topological Order Parameter for Quantum Spin Chains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21705)
- A theoretical study reveals that the XY toric code on a kagome lattice hosts an unconventional \(\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2\) topologically ordered phase with 64 anyon types, richer than the standard toric or color codes. [Unconventional \(\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2\) topological order in the kagome XY toric code](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22296)
- A rigorous mathematical technique is presented for bounding error-correction thresholds in topological codes under noise, proving that for noise strengths up to a constant, logical information can be recovered. [Mixed-state topological order and error-correction thresholds in non-Abelian codes: rigorous results](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21706)
- On NISQ hardware, signatures of topological crossovers in the Wen-plaquette model are shown to be resolvable despite noise, using a scalable two-stage strategy. [Resolving topological order crossovers on NISQ hardware](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21707)
- Quantum cellular automata (QCAs) are constructed from invertible topological quantum field theories, unifying known constructions and yielding new infinite families of non-Clifford QCAs in higher dimensions. [Non-Clifford quantum cellular automata from invertible topological quantum field theories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21697)

### Altermagnetism and Spintronics
- High-quality single crystals of the altermagnet MnTe are synthesized, revealing an Anderson insulating state below 150 K and robust altermagnetic hallmarks. [Intrinsic single crystals of MnTe altermagnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21803)
- A new mechanism for switchable altermagnetism is proposed in nonpolar materials via spin-induced improper polarization, demonstrated with DFT calculations on DyFeO\(_3\). [Switchable Altermagnetism via Spin-Induced Improper Polarization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21930)
- A comprehensive real- and momentum-space analysis of topological phases in 2D d-wave altermagnets reveals giant conductivity anisotropy and spin-"steering" effects, with a proposed altermagnetic field-effect transistor. [Real and momentum space analysis of topological phases in 2D d-wave altermagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04854)
- In heterostructures of monolayer ferromagnets and altermagnets, the magnetic proximity effect from the ferromagnet induces pronounced non-relativistic valley polarization in the altermagnet. [Magnetic proximity-induced non-relativistic valley polarization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22360)
- A heterostructure of BaMnO\(_3\)|KTaO\(_3\) is theoretically designed to proximity-induce strong Rashba spin-orbit interaction in an antiferromagnet, a key step for antiferromagnetic spintronics. [Proximity-induced Rashba spin-orbit interaction in BaMnO\(_\text{3}|\)KTaO\(_\text{3}\) heterostructure for antiferromagnetic spintronics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01861)
- Non-linear (second-order) in-plane spin currents are predicted in 2D hole gases with \(k^3\) Rashba spin-orbit coupling, including collinearly polarized spin currents. [Non-linear in-plane spin current in spin-orbit coupled 2D hole gases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14890)

### Skyrmions
- Current-induced creation and manipulation of magnetic skyrmion bags (tunable topological charge Q) is demonstrated in a FeGe nanoplate, including signatures of magnetic monopoles. [Current-induced creation and dynamics of embedded magnetic skyrmion bags](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21889)
- A high-pressure synthesis technique significantly elevates the Curie temperature of Mn\(_5\)Ge\(_{3+x}\) crystals to 350 K, stabilizing 50 nm room-temperature skyrmions. [Enhanced Curie temperature and room-temperature 50-nm skyrmions achieved in hexagonal ferromagnet Mn5Ge3+x synthesized via a high-pressure method](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21891)
- Room-temperature ferrimagnetic skyrmions are discovered in a tetragonal Mn\(_{1.9}\)Co\(_{0.1}\)Sb single crystal, with dynamics potentially reaching the THz regime. [Ferrimagnetic Skyrmions in a Tetragonal Mn1.9Co0.1Sb Single Crystal at Room Temperature](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21894)

### Superconductivity
- A theory of thermal phase slips in disordered superconducting films near the critical current is developed, valid for arbitrary temperatures, generalizing recent results near \(T_c\). [Thermal phase slips in superconducting films near the critical current at arbitrary temperatures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21801)
- A first-principles study of the double infinite-layer oxyfluoride La\(_3\)Ni\(_2\)O\(_5\)F reveals a cuprate-like Fermi surface and rich magnetic physics, identifying it as a promising cuprate analogue. [Structural stability, electronic structure, and magnetism of the \(d^9\) double infinite-layer La\(_3\)Ni\(_2\)O\(_5\)F under chemical pressure and epitaxial strain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22029)
- Hierarchical equations of motion (HEOM) are used to model quantum transport in Cooper pair splitters, capturing finite-bias effects beyond Markovian limits and explaining observed thermoelectric effects. [Quantum transport in Cooper pair splitters using hierarchical equations of motion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22235)
- A microscopic calculation demonstrates the equivalence of the vortex-entry current and the depairing current in a superconducting thin-film strip, removing ambiguity from the phenomenological Pearl-London theory. [Microscopic equivalence of the vortex-entry current and the depairing current in a superconducting thin-film strip](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11518)
- BCS states in the 2D Hubbard model are systematically studied, finding clear d-wave condensates in underdoped and overdoped regions. [BCS states and D-wave condensates in the 2D Hubbard model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01280)
- Quantum shot noise and temperature-bias noise are shown to be powerful probes for distinguishing \(S_{++}\) and \(S_{+-}\) pairing symmetries in iron pnictides. [Temperature-Bias Noise and Quantum Shot Noise as Probes of Pairing Symmetry in Iron Pnictides](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20069)
- A new approach for designing superconducting nanowire single-photon detectors (SNSPDs) reaches their intrinsic performance limit for the first time, dramatically reducing dark count rates and enabling near-unity efficiency for wider devices. [Reaching the intrinsic performance limits of superconducting nanowire single-photon detectors up to 0.1 mm wide](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15971)
- A hybrid ferromagnet-SNSPD concept is proposed to raise the operating temperature of infrared single-photon detectors to 3.75 K, enabling simpler cryogenics. [Hybrid Ferromagnet-SNSPDs: Single photon induced order-to-disorder transition in ferromagnets coupled to thin film superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17177)

### Machine Learning for Materials Science
- A comprehensive review discusses the progress, challenges, and perspectives of generative and multimodal AI for materials prediction and design, introducing a materials property hierarchy. [Generative and multimodal AI for materials prediction and design: Progress, challenges, and perspectives](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21660)
- A property-guided diffusion model for inverse materials design is developed, enabling generation of materials with user-specified properties. Success rates for finding stable magnetic and hard materials are 12.3% and 3.9%. [Property-Guided Diffusion for Inverse Design of Crystalline Materials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21849)
- A machine-learned interatomic potential (CHGNet) is used to study oxygen diffusion in rare-earth high-entropy oxides, revealing that conductivity is governed by local chemical environments and Ce content. [Ionic Diffusion Properties of Rare-Earth High-Entropy Oxides from a Machine-Learned Interatomic Potential](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21726)
- A new multimodal framework, SAGE-Net, injects crystallographic semantics directly into geometric message passing, achieving state-of-the-art performance on materials property prediction benchmarks. [SAGE-Net: Semantics-Augmented Geometric Encoder for Material Property Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22271)
- A novel invariant lattice representation (bispectrum) is used to learn unit cell parameters from powder X-ray diffraction data, significantly outperforming direct prediction methods. [Learning Lattice Parameters from Powder X-Ray Diffraction Data Using Invariants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21829)

### Quantum Magnetism and Spin Liquids
- In frustrated magnets like spin ice, a monopole's motion is shown to be a self-interacting random walk with memory, leading to ultra-slow, algebraic relaxation (long-time tails). [Hydrodynamic Memory and Long-Time Tails in Clean Frustrated Magnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21827)
- The compound KCuIn(PO\(_4\))\(_2\) is established as a quasi-two-dimensional quantum antiferromagnet composed of coupled spin chains, with magnetic properties explained by a first-principles-based model. [Quasi-Two-Dimensional Quantum Antiferromagnetism in the Distorted Honeycomb Compound KCuIn(PO4)2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21868)
- An effective low-energy Hamiltonian for Landau quasiparticles in Fermi fluids is derived, which unifies the Landau function, pair interaction, and collision amplitude. [A low-energy effective Hamiltonian for Landau quasiparticles: I. A unified theory of transport and superfluidity in Fermi liquids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15938)

[Towards on-chip nascent all-van-der-Waals polarization optical components for nanoscale photonic applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21748)
- Charge-density-wave phase transitions in monolayer 1T-TaS\(_2\) are accurately modeled using universal machine-learning interatomic potentials. [Charge-Density-Wave Phase Transitions in Monolayer 1T-TaS2 from Universal Machine Learning Molecular Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22316)
