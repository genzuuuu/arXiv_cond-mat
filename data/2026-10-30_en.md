# arXiv Condensed Matter Physics — Thematic Summary

## Superconductivity: Pairing Mechanisms & Unconventional Order
**Theory.** A $B_{1g}$ phonon coupled to the doped repulsive Hubbard model generates genuine $d$-wave superconductivity with $T_c$ exceeding either interaction alone ([A $B_{1g}$ phonon unlocks $d$-wave superconductivity in the repulsive Hubbard model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37746)). In a three-band cuprate model, the same phonon helps or hurts pairing depending on whether it is adiabatic (suppresses via lost quasiparticle coherence) or antiadiabatic (enhances pairing vertex) ([Phonon Fluctuations Enhanced Superconductivity in a Three-Band Cuprate Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39710)). Spin-imbalanced BCS functionals are proven to host a first-order transition where the normal state is globally unstable yet locally stable ([A First-Order Phase Transition in Spin-Imbalanced BCS Theory of Superconductivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38961)). Chiral $p$-wave pairing is found proximate to a fractional Chern insulator and topological CDW in a moiré Chern band ([Chiral $p$-Wave Superconductivity Proximate to a Fractional Chern Insulator and a Topological Charge Density Wave in a Moiré Chern Band](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40469)). Novel routes include a doped charge-transfer insulator giving $p+ip$ order ([Topological Chiral Superconductivity from Doping Correlated Charge Transfer Insulator with Orbital Magnetism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16674)) and Kivelson bilayer-type stiffness enhancement ([Enhancement of superconducting stiffness in hybrid superconducting-metallic bilayers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28187)). A resonator can raise $T_c$ by ~1% via phase fluctuations ([Stabilizing fluctuating Cooper pairs with a terahertz resonator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40222)); a Klein-bottle model yields chirality-wall Majorana seams ([Chiral superconductivity on a closed Klein bottle: a chirality wall and its Majorana seam spectrum](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40251)).
**Experiment/materials.** Sign-reversal nodeless $d$-wave is imaged by scanning Josephson tunneling in monolayer Fe(Te,Se) ([Direct real-space imaging of sign-reversal superconducting state by scanning Josephson tunneling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38421)). Nickellate La$_4$Ni$_3$O$_{10}$ reaches zero resistance at 60% lower pressure, and density-wave order is separated from superconductivity ([Separating the Density Wave Phase from Zero-Resistance Superconductivity in La$_4$Ni$_3$O$_{10}$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38959)); BaFe$_2$S$_3$ shows SC coexisting with robust stripe AFM via orbital-selective delocalization ([Coexistence of superconductivity and magnetic order in BaFe$_2$S$_3$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38966)). Twisted rhombohedral graphene shows SC coexisting with Chern insulators ($|C|\ge1$) ([Coexistence of Superconductivity and Chern insulators with |C| ≥ 1 in twisted rhombohedral bilayer-trilayer graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40170)). A15-phase MoRe contact origin is resolved ([Exceptional Superconducting Interfaces between Graphene and Atomic Chains in A15-MoRe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39694)); Nb films by thermal laser evaporation hit $T_c$ within 10 mK of bulk ([Thermal laser evaporation of superconducting Nb films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38070)); TiN growth/ dimensionality studied ([Influences of N2 flow rate on the superconducting properties and dimensionality of epitaxial TiN thin films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38250)).

## Magnetism, Altermagnetism & Spin Transport
Altermagnetism expands in several directions: **meta-altermagnetism** from first-order band reconstruction ([Meta-Altermagnetism and Enhanced Spin-Splitting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37754)); symmetry-guided light-induced chiral altermagnets via nonlinear phononics ([Light-induced chiral altermagnets from conventional antiferromagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40410)); $i$-wave altermagnetism in graphene antidot superlattices ([Intrinsic $i$-wave altermagnetism in 2D graphene superlattices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19661)); quasicrystal altermagnetism on the Penrose lattice ([Collinear and Coplanar Altermagnetism in a Penrose Quasicrystal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35855)); and a time-reversal-invariant acoustic analogue ([Time-Reversal-Invariant Altermagnetic Acoustic Crystals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08827)). Magnon transport theory includes nodal spin current at AFM/altermagnet junctions ([Nodal magnon spin current at an antiferromagnet/altermagnet junction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39206)) and hybrid cavity-magnon normal-mode formulations ([Micromagnetic Lagrangian and Hamiltonian Normal-Mode Formulation of Cavity Magnon-Photon Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40416)). Experiments report giant piezomagnetism in TbMn$_6$Sn$_6$ from spin reorientation ([Giant piezomagnetic response driven by spin reorientation in the topological Kagome magnet TbMn6Sn6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38528)), room-temperature magnetodielectric coupling in GaFeO$_3$ ([Pulse Laser Deposited GaFeO3 Thin Film Grown on FTO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39696)), and electric-field control of anisotropic spin traffic in CrSBr ([Electric field control of anisotropic spin traffic between spin-filtering and magnon-scattering in a layered antiferromagnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40423)). Disordered magnets can host topological textures without crystalline chirality ([Disorder as a route to topological spin textures in amorphous magnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07725)); spin-space-symmetry constraints are reformulated representation-theoretically ([Spin Space Symmetry Without Spin Space Groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37770)) and a SOC response theory is developed in oriented spin space groups ([Spin-Orbit Coupling Response Theory within the Oriented Spin Space Group Framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40149)).
