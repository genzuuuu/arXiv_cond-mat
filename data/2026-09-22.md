## 1. Computational Methods & Numerical Algorithms

**Exact nonperturbative equilibrium mode statistics.** Closed-form finite-size modal occupations for MMT, FPUT-β, and DNLS, accurately spanning weak to strong nonlinearity and exposing failures of perturbative schemes near quasicondensation.

**Low-cost MLIP training for defects.** Only four DFT relaxations in small (<100-atom) supercells suffice to train potentials predicting defect formation energies in >200-atom cells within ~0.3 eV, across GaN, SiO₂, and CZTS.

**Phonopy axial thermal expansion.** A free-energy-minimization procedure with temperature-dependent force constants, worked through α- and ω-Ti, quantifying the sensitivity of axial coefficients.

**GRAINSMITH.** An open-source Python generator of statistically controlled polycrystalline models with tunable grain-boundary morphology, supporting all 230 space groups and byte-reproducible output.

**LDSTOP for large deviations.** A stochastic-optimization method computing large deviation functions by guiding single trajectories, demonstrated across discrete chains, jump processes, and diffusions.
[Stochastic optimisation method for estimating large deviations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24473)
