# Condensed Matter Physics Research Summary (arXiv)

## Superconductivity & Quantum Materials

- [A Superconducting Peierls Instability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04164): Predicts an edge pair-density wave in 2D superconductors driven by dispersing Andreev bound states producing a "superconducting Kohn anomaly" at 2k_c.
- [Direct Validation of Superconductivity through Contact-Free Detection of Persistent Supercurrents Using Room-Temperature Quantum Magnetometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02995): Demonstrates rapid, contact-free detection of superconductivity via atomic magnetometry, enabling pico-Tesla supercurrent detection in microcrystals.
- [Tunable Chern superconductivity of PtBi2 in slab geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03867): Maps competing gaps for Majorana cones in PtBi2 thin films, finding Zeeman-field-driven nontrivial Chern phases.
- [Valley polarization dependence of candidate even-denominator fractional quantum Hall states in monolayer graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02976): Constructs SU(4) Chern-Simons trial states; identifies multi-component correlated liquids as favored at ν = 1/2 and 1/4.
- [Wavefunctions for Anyon Superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04187): Develops a hierarchy wavefunction construction for superconducting states from parent topological orders, showing semion superconductors equal two effective Landau levels.

## Magnetism & Altermagnetism

- [Moiré-induced altermagnetism from nonmagnetic constituents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03271): Proposes structural relaxation in moiré square lattices as a route to altermagnetic order from nonmagnetic materials.
- [Resolving the Magnetic Ground-State Controversy in RuO2 through A Flat Magnetic Energy Landscape](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03274): New spin-lattice framework showing near-degenerate magnetic configurations in RuO2—reconciling conflicting experiments via perturbation-selected ground states.
- [Hidden Magnetic Octupolar Order driven by Spin-Orbit coupling in KFeF3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03684): Identifies zone-centered magnetic octupole order at 40 K; shows SO coupling suppresses Jahn-Teller instability, favoring multipolar degrees of freedom.
- [A Strategy Toward Room Temperature Topological Hall Effect via Local Moment Magnetism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03223): Demonstrates topological Hall effect in Gd5Pb3 between 100–200 K, an order of magnitude higher in temperature than prior rare-earth magnets.
- [Orbital current rectifier and linear magnon Edelstein effect in p-wave antialtermagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03075): Shows p-wave magnets generate magnetization through thermally driven orbital-momentum Edelstein effects of magnons.
- [Antiferromagnetism-altered plasmon dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03138): First observation of plasmon renormalization at the antiferromagnetic transition in a graphene/NiPS3 hybrid platform.
- [Dipolar order across Bravais lattice space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02936): Complete classification of dipolar ground states for all 14 Bravais lattices, finding only 4 metric-attractor families and an exceptional frustrated case.
- [Classical dipolar Heisenberg models on Archimedean and Laves lattices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02934): Spectral atlas of dipolar Heisenberg systems on all 1-uniform planar tilings; identifies three ground-state classes.
