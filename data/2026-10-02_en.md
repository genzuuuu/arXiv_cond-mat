### Machine Learning for Atomistic Simulation

**Transferable GNN surrogates for MD.** A graph neural network surrogate predicts atomic displacements directly, skipping force evaluation and time integration. The same architecture handles FCC aluminum, BCC iron, and HCP magnesium without symmetry-specific tuning, achieving 10⁻⁴ Å² position errors and stable nanosecond propagation. [Transferable Graph Neural Network Surrogates for Molecular Dynamics Across Crystal Symmetries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00100)

**Reference-free MLIP certification.** A suite of fourteen inexpensive probes grades interatomic potentials against properties the exact Born-Oppenheimer surface must satisfy—symmetry, self-consistency, equilibrium statistics—without any reference calculation. Applied to 64 pretrained models, it resolves two orders of magnitude of quality and flags a fine-tuned potential whose surface degrades as its reported errors improve. [Reference-free certification of machine-learning interatomic potentials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00585)

**Long-range MLIPs for SrTiO₃ defects.** Three long-range potentials (MACE-POLAR, MACE-LES, LOREM) are benchmarked against a short-range baseline for Sr–O Schottky vacancy pairs and oxygen vacancy migration, isolating each model's ability to learn electrostatics from competing contributions like dielectric screening. [Long-Range Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials for Defect Energetics in SrTiO₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00794)

**Scaling equivariant foundation models at inference.** Symmetrix-XL runs pretrained MACE checkpoints without retraining, using streamed-edge execution and tiled memory to cut inference time 3–5× and raise the single-GPU capacity from ~25k to 11.24 million atoms. [Train for Accuracy, Execute at Scale: Architecture-Preserving Inference for Equivariant Atomistic Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01036)

**Energy–force balancing as model selection.** Rather than tuning a scalarization weight, the authors frame energy–force balancing as a primal-dual problem: minimize energy subject to a force-loss bound. This reaches low-error regions 6.7–8.9× faster than full scalarization scans. [From Energy-Force Weighting to Primal-Dual Optimization of Machine-Learned Interatomic Potentials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00876)

**Learning ab initio phase-field models.** A Mori-Zwanzig projection of MD onto density fields yields a mesoscopic phase-field equation with neural-network free energy and mobility, trained on short MLIP trajectories. Applied to Fe–B melts, it explains why FeB₄ is only synthesizable under pressure; for H–He mixtures it captures helium rain at 2.2 million atoms. [Learning ab initio phase-field models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01432)

**Agents for computational materials science.** CompMat-Bench contains 94 tasks derived from published studies, with ground-truth inputs and outputs precomputed so agents can be graded without rerunning expensive simulations. Agents reach 66–90% pass rates on single tasks; failures are mostly scientific rather than software errors. [CompMat-Bench: Benchmarking AI Agents for Computational Materials Science](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00636)

**Generative dynamics for long-time evolution.** Diffusion models learn the finite-time transition kernel from configuration pairs at short lag, then iterate it far beyond training. Reproduces Model B coarsening and predicts driven colloid currents twenty minutes ahead from ten minutes of data. [Generative Modeling of Stochastic Dynamics for Long-Time Evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00546)

**Transferable generative MD for self-assembly.** SupraTITO learns sequence-conditioned implicit transfer operators for peptide self-assembly, generalizing to held-out sequences and dilute concentrations while maintaining molecular integrity over long rollouts. [SupraTITO: Transferable Generative Molecular Dynamics for Supramolecular Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01381)

**Threading detection from writhe.** A 1D CNN trained on the writhe profile of ring polymer pairs classifies threading with >98% accuracy and generalizes without retraining to dense melts across densities and chain lengths up to N=1600, running 3× faster than minimal-surface methods. [Learning to Classify Threading in Melts of Rings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01621)

**Disordered crystal structure prediction.** EP-Flow formulates disordered CSP via an Occupancy Distribution Matrix on a transportation polytope, using marginal-constrained flow matching with a Sinkhorn inverse map. It generates occupancies, coordinates, and lattices jointly, outperforming adapted ordered-crystal generators on formula-conditioned benchmarks. [EP-Flow: Disordered Crystal Structure Prediction without Site-Level Annotations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01315)

**Symmetry-preserving crystal diffusion.** GEODE combines coordinate and lattice diffusion in Cartesian space with a Wyckoff-constrained loss, sampling symmetry templates before generation. It reaches an 11.9% metastable-unique-novel rate, versus 7.7% for the next best symmetry-aware model. [GEODE: Symmetry-Preserving Cartesian Diffusion for Crystal Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01898)

**Avg-atom potentials from cluster expansions.** An exact A-atom potential is derived directly from a linear atomic cluster expansion. Fitted to ~1500 small Mo–Nb DFT structures, it reproduces elastic constants, mixing enthalpy, and surface energies—but only with the right chemical site basis. [Benchmarking average atom potentials derived from atomic cluster expansions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01370)

**Closures for polymer integral equation theory.** A machine-learned closure for PRISM theory, trained on coarse-grained MD of homopolymer melts and solutions, outperforms Percus-Yevick and accurately models small-angle neutron scattering data. [A Machine Learning Closure for Polymer Integral Equation Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.11030)

### Strongly Correlated Electrons and Superconductivity

**Emergent electronic modes in spin-1 insulators.** Two-orbital Kanamori-Hubbard calculations show that doping and temperature generate in-gap electronic modes with momentum-shifted spin-mode dispersion. For small inter-orbital repulsion, onsite spin-triplet pairs form with a pair-breaking gap. [Emergence of electronic modes and triplet pairing from spin-1 antiferromagnetic insulators in the Kanamori-Hubbard model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00110)

**Causality violations in cluster GW+DMFT.** Using exact diagonalization on minimal Hubbard clusters, the authors prove that both GW and GW+DMFT violate Matsubara causality on frustrated clusters. A "dimer anomaly" masks the violation on bipartite two-site systems; local self-energy corrections are shown to be insufficient for causal analytic structure. [Causality violations in cluster GW+DMFT: Exact Lehmann moments and the necessity of non-local vertex corrections](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00324)

**Quadlayer nickelate electronic structure.** DFT and RPA map La₅Ni₄O₁₃ under isotropic and c-axis strain. Uniaxial compression shifts the d_z² bonding band upward and produces a γ hole pocket; the leading spin response moves from (2π/3, 2π/3) to (π, π) under strain. [Electronic structure and two-orbital model of the quadlayer La₅Ni₄O₁₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00489)

**Suppressing electronic orders in bilayer nickelates.** Combining oxygen annealing with A-site substitution suppresses T_SDW from 150 K to 70 K in superconducting bilayer nickelate films. A second charge-like anisotropy order is completely eliminated, consistent with a spin-spinless stripe ground state. [Selective suppression of electronic orders via interlayer coupling in superconducting bilayer nickelate thin films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01757)

**Hubbard parameters: linear response vs. Hartree-Fock.** A systematic comparison across seven oxides finds the two approaches agree for partially filled d states but diverge for nearly empty or full shells, and disagree even more for inter-site V. Pseudopotential choice also matters. [Comparing Hubbard parameters from linear-response theory and a Hartree-Fock-based approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16803)

**Chiral Weyl-Kondo semimetal in CeGaGe.** CeGaGe undergoes a structural transition from achiral to chiral P4₃ symmetry and orders antiferromagnetically below 4.7 K. Anomalous Hall conductivity tracks the Kondo coherence temperature rather than T_N, providing evidence for a Kondo-driven chiral Weyl semimetal. [Chiral Weyl-Kondo semimetallic state through enhanced correlation in CeGaGe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32012)

**Impurity model simulation complexity.** Static properties of quantum impurity models are classically simulable in polynomial time, both for ground-state energy and finite-temperature partition functions. Dynamical properties, such as nonequilibrium Green's functions, capture the full power of quantum computation. [Polynomial-time classical and quantum simulation of quantum impurity models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02167)

**Fermi liquid effective Hamiltonian.** A new renormalization scheme removes resonant couplings via unitary transformation, yielding an effective Hamiltonian unifying the Landau function, the pairing interaction, and the collision amplitude. The Bethe-Salpeter relation is recovered, with an analogue for the frontal limit. [A low-energy effective Hamiltonian for Landau quasiparticles: I. A unified theory of transport and superfluidity in Fermi liquids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15938)

### Topological Matter and Quantum Hall

**Interior contacts in a quantum Hall bar.** Full 2D self-consistent Thomas-Fermi-Poisson calculations reproduce the Kendirlik experiment, identifying three regimes across the ν=2 plateau: galvanic connection, floating, and isolation by closed incompressible rings. The isolation interval's boundaries are robust to regularization but its resistance magnitude is not. [Interior contacts in a narrow quantum Hall bar: a two-dimensional self-consistent screening calculation of the current distribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00326)

**Quantum capacitance as a unifying probe.** A perspective arguing that quantum capacitance is not secondary to quantum Hall and topological insulator physics but one of the clearest windows into boundary density of states, revealing the field-dependent, non-uniformly insulating bulk. [From Quantized Hall Plateaus to Topological Surfaces: Quantum Capacitance as a Unifying Probe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00336)

**Composite fermions in ideal Chern bands.** Composite-fermion wave functions are constructed for Jain states and their quasiparticles in Aharonov-Casher bands. The emergent composite-fermion band structure goes beyond effective Landau levels, and an isolated quasiparticle band carries Berry flux differing from a Landau level. [Composite fermions in ideal Chern bands](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01217)

**Parafermions in FQAH–superconductor junctions.** For spin- and valley-polarized ν=2/3 Jain states, pairing and tunneling bilinears each have exactly two zero-conformal-spin options, both in the neutral-mode channel. Domain walls carry Z₃ parafermions with 3 protected states per pair, and a 6π-periodic Josephson current is expected. [Parafermions in fractional Chern insulator-superconductor heterostructures: the role of spin polarization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01486)

**Lattice model fractional quantum Hall states.** The Z_k Read-Rezayi series is realized on a lattice with exact parent Hamiltonians, with clustering encoded in Macdonald polynomials. Numerical studies of Laughlin and Moore-Read series find expected zero-mode and quasihole counting with positive energy gaps. [Generalized Model Fractional Quantum Hall States on Lattices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13978)

**Many-body Euler topology.** Many-body Euler numbers are introduced as a time-reversal-symmetric counterpart to many-body Chern numbers, with calculations in a topological Hubbard model that hosts Chern and fractional Chern insulating phases, plus a symmetry-indicator classification. [Many-body Euler topology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14217)

**Topological robustness under thermal disorder.** In Bi₂Se₃, the thermally averaged structure is a poor proxy for the ensemble: it misses Rashba-like splitting from instantaneous symmetry breaking and underestimates gap renormalization. Band inversion survives in every configuration up to 600 K, above which individual configurations cross over. [Topological robustness of thermally disordered lattices: From average structures to ensemble electronic properties](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00963)

**Hyperbolic lattices with mass disorder.** Nearest-neighbor tight-binding models on {10,3}, {8,3}, and {8,4} hyperbolic lattices host a Dirac liquid, Fermi liquid, and flat band respectively. The Dirac liquid survives weak mass disorder, then undergoes semimetal-to-metal and metal-to-insulator transitions. [Hyperbolic lattices with mass disorder: Phases and phase transitions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02192)

**No-go for order-two Clifford duality.** Any locality-preserving Clifford realization of electric-magnetic exchange in the Z₂ toric code cannot have order two. For Z_N toric codes, order-two realizations exist for odd N but are impossible for even N. [A No-Go Theorem for Order-Two Clifford Electric-Magnetic Duality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02097)

**Anyon proliferation via conformal embeddings.** Chern-Simons Higgsing transitions based on conformal embeddings can enlarge topological order, taking Abelian to non-Abelian phases. The SO(N)₂ ↔ SU(N)₁ family realizes transitions from fermionic 1/N Laughlin states to charge-2e anyon superconductivity for odd N. [Anyon Proliferation and Anyon Superconductivity in Higgsing Transitions via Conformal Embeddings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00452)

**Boundary-mode localization from quantum geometry.** The quantum metric length of a degenerate manifold sets a lower bound on the spatial spread of flat-band topological boundary modes. Tuning it produces QML-shaped quantum Hall plateaus and anomalous Fraunhofer patterns. [Non-Abelian Quantum Metric Governed Topological Boundary-Mode Localization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.05114)

**Stochastic topological classification.** Markovian constraints alter the matrix space for topological classification, leaving only two robust symmetry classes—no symmetry and pseudo-Hermiticity—with non-Hermiticity required for nontrivial phases. [Classification of topological phases of matter in stochastic systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38171)

**Clifford-hierarchy stabilizers and twisted doubles.** The XC^(k)_d formalism generalizes Pauli stabilizers using level-k Clifford operators. Every 2D twisted quantum double D^ω(Z_p^n) with ω a product of type-III cocycles admits a Clifford-level realization. [Clifford-hierarchy stabilizer formalism with applications to twisted quantum doubles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00464)

### Magnetism, Altermagnetism, and Spintronics

**Stacking-controlled altermagnetism in bilayer CrI₃.** First-principles and spin-wave theory show the interlayer magnetic ground state is highly sensitive to stacking geometry, controlling magnon band topology. Many-body Green's function calculations quantify magnon decay and interaction renormalization. [Stacking-Controlled Altermagnetism and Topological Magnons in Bilayer CrI₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00479)

**Imaging altermagnetic order in MnTe.** Scanning-probe quantum microscopy maps evanescent magnetization and domains in epitaxial α-MnTe, showing field control of altermagnetic order. Thickness-dependent measurements point to an interfacial origin for the weak magnetization and correlate it with the anomalous Hall effect. [Imaging Surface Magnetization in Altermagnetic MnTe Films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25241)

**Exciton-magnon sidebands in an altermagnet.** La₂O₃Mn₂Se₂ hosts a spin-forbidden dark exciton plus magnon sidebands, Stokes-shifted in emission and anti-Stokes in absorption. The sideband energies match the magnon density of states from inelastic neutron scattering, establishing exciton-magnon spectroscopy as an optical probe of altermagnets. [Altermagnetism produces pair emission and absorption from dark excitons and magnons in La₂O₃Mn₂Se₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36128)

**Altermagnetic superconductors with Bogoliubov Fermi surfaces.** In d-wave spin-split superconductors, collective modes inherit d-wave anisotropy and couple to spin-density fluctuations. Applied fields create Bogoliubov Fermi surfaces, producing strongly anisotropic Landau damping, an in-gap Higgs structure, and enhanced spin response. [Highly anisotropic collective modes of altermagnetic superconductors with Bogoliubov Fermi surfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01011)

**Odd-parity altermagnetism in the Haldane-Hubbard model.** Cluster slave-spin calculations show that odd-parity altermagnetic order reconstructs local Berry curvature into spin- and valley-selective form and breaks chiral symmetry at zigzag edges, while the total Chern number and quantized Hall conductivity are unchanged. [The odd-parity altermagnetism induced reconstruction of the Chern-insulating phase in Haldane-Hubbard model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25537)

**Incommensurate order in BaCo₂(AsO₄)₂.** An extended J₁–K–Γ–Γ′–J₂–J₃ model with ab initio constraints shows the observed incommensurate spiral along Γ→M arises from frustration and off-diagonal anisotropy rather than dominant Kitaev coupling. Quantum order-by-disorder stabilizes an out-of-plane FM phase. [Mechanism of Incommensurate Magnetic Order in BaCo₂(AsO₄)₂: Interplay of Frustrated Further-Neighbor Exchanges and Bond-Directional Anisotropy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00626)

**Monoclinic α-RuCl₃ magnetic phase diagrams.** Sub-100 µm crystals that remain monoclinic on cooling show a sharp 14 K transition and reduced rotational symmetry, higher saturation fields, and distinct field-induced phases compared to rhombohedral samples. Minimal magnetic structure models are proposed for all regions. [Magnetic Phase Diagrams and Spin Hamiltonian of Monoclinic α-RuCl₃ from Angle-Dependent Torque Studies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02006)

**Robert** — **Skymeron** in synthetic antiferromagnets: tuning layer anisotropy drives a spin-flop transition where an AFM skyrmion reconfigures into an out-of-plane skyrmion coupled to an in-plane bimeron. Current pulses reveal inertia-like propagation from finite-time reorientation relaxation. [Inertial Dynamics of a Skymeron](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01339)

**Skyrmion thermodynamics in Cr₁₊ₓTe₂.** STM, ARPES, transport, and Lorentz TEM reveal paramagnetic, in-plane spin-fluctuation, out-of-plane FM, and field-induced Néel skyrmion phases, with pronounced thermal hysteresis and coexistence of skyrmions with stripe domains. [Complex Magnetic Phases and Unconventional Skyrmion Thermodynamics in Magnetically Intercalated Cr₁₊ₓTe₂ Compounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00877)

**Single-pulse switching plus current-driven skyrmion motion.** A Pt/Ir/CoB/Gd/Pt multilayer supports both skyrmion stabilization and helicity-independent all-optical switching. A single 30 fs pulse toggles skyrmion polarity without field or current; patterning into a wire combines optical programming with current-driven motion. [Single-Pulse Optical Switching combined with Current-Induced Motion of Skyrmionic Spin Textures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01568)

**Magnetoelastic coupling near room temperature.** UIr₄Al₁₅ shows lattice parameter and bond-distance anomalies near its magnetic transition region without crystallographic symmetry breaking, revealing strong magnetostructural coupling in a bulk intermetallic. [Room-temperature Magnetoelastic Coupling in UIr₄Al₁₅](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01551)

**Complex magnetism in Ir-based aluminogermanides.** Tb₂IrAl₄Ge₂ orders antiferromagnetically at 25 K with field-induced metamagnetic transitions and nonlinear Hall response; Er₂IrAl₄Ge₂ shows three zero-field transitions and a richer low-field phase diagram. [Complex magnetic phase diagrams in Tb₂IrAl₄Ge₂ and Er₂IrAl₄Ge₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01714)

**Frustration-induced multiferroicity in MnS₂.** Pyrite hauerite orders collinearly below 48 K with unit-cell doubling and broken inversion symmetry, producing a ferroelectric polarization up to 360 µC/m² from S₂ dimers polarized by frustrated Mn ordering. Domain switching faces a high magnetic exchange barrier. [Frustration-induced multiferroicity in hauerite MnS₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01990)

**Chiral antiferromagnetic dichroism in SmFe₃(BO₃)₄.** Polarization-resolved spectroscopy below T_N shows magnetic linear dichroism at Sm³⁺ 4f–4f transitions and non-reciprocal directional dichroism in toroidal geometry, providing a route to imaging and time-resolving the AFM order parameter. [Near-infrared magnetic linear and non-reciprocal directional dichroism in the chiral antiferromagnet SmFe₃(BO₃)₄](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00534)

**Nanoscale magnon spectroscopy with MTJs.** A 70-nm magnetic tunnel junction fabricated on a 300-mm line detects magnons in a magnetic vortex via dipolar fields, resolving nonlinear modes with linewidths down to 188 kHz that remain unresolved in Brillouin light scattering. [Electrical magnon spectroscopy with foundry-fabricated nanoscale magnetic tunnel junctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19483)

**Strain-induced spin-phonon coupling in RuO₂.** Anisotropically strained epitaxial RuO₂ films show a sub-THz transverse acoustic phonon from strain-relaxation dislocations, time-varying mode hardening, and an optical phonon emerging in magnetization dynamics that softens near the critical temperature. [Strain-induced Dynamic Spin-Phonon Coupling in Epitaxial RuO2 Films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23969)

**Spin Nernst effect in triplet superconductors.** The intrinsic SNE has two contributions: a quasiparticle term from Berry curvature of triplet Cooper pairs, and an indirect supercurrent term canceling the bulk thermoelectric charge current. The latter is comparable in magnitude in nonunitary superconductors. [Intrinsic spin Nernst effect in spin-triplet superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19971)

**Superconducting orbital diode in SN bilayers.** An orbital mechanism from inhomogeneous Meissner currents produces diode behavior in diffusive SN bilayers. Interface resistance affects the SDE nonmonotonically and can enhance it; near the transition, unidirectional superconductivity appears. [Superconducting orbital diode effect in SN bilayers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09504)

**Josephson nonreciprocity in parallel double dots.** Exact diagonalization of a parallel DQD junction shows orbital flux and tunnel-sign parity control the interference between local and non-local pairing. Diode efficiencies approach 40% near the (1,1) charge sector boundaries, with a broader 10–15% response in the triplet regime. [Flux- and tunnel-parity-controlled Josephson nonreciprocity from singlet-triplet competition in a parallel double quantum dot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01830)

**Nutational spin pumping.** Beyond standard LLG, an inertial regime at THz drive frequencies produces a nutational damping torque with bulk and interfacial terms. The interfacial term generates a dc spin current scaling quadratically with frequency, enhanced in ultrathin films and low-Fermi-energy conductors. [Nutational Spin Pumping and Dissipation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01164)

**CISS from symmetry and from knots.** Two papers: one develops a discrete geometric SOC framework predicting ~120 meV geometric SOC in trefoil-knot molecules, reproducing measured spin polarization and magnetoresistance; the other argues CISS violates no fundamental symmetries (parity, time-reversal), offering a conceptual foundation for interpreting experiments. [Theory of Chirality-Induced Spin Selectivity in Trefoil-knot Molecules](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01732) · [Symmetry considerations in chirality-induced spin selectivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01880)

**Anomalous Hall and goniopolar control.** A structured Laguerre-Gaussian vector potential programmably reconstructs the goniopolar phase space of NaSn₂As₂, with radial scaling r_max ∝ √|ℓ| and a dominant angular harmonic m_dom = 2|ℓ|, enhancing a representative goniopolar window by ~17% at |ℓ|=5. [Structured-Light Control of Goniopolar Thermoelectricity in NaSn₂As₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01755)

**Spintronic THz emitters from TDDFT.** Real-time TDDFT resolves coupled charge, spin, and magnetization dynamics in Co/Pt and Co/W. Despite similar interlayer spin currents, charge transfer differs qualitatively, traced to ground-state electronic structure and the spin- and orbital-resolved phase space. No net orbital angular momentum transfer is found. [Charge and spin dynamics in spintronic THz emitters from Time-Dependent Density Functional Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01089)

**Ferroelectric domain engineering for THz.** Stripe-domain BiFeO₃ films emit THz fields four times stronger than single-domain films. Phase- and polarization-resolved measurements identify ultrafast screening of ferroelectric polarization as the dominant mechanism, enhanced by built-in fields at domain walls. [Domain-engineered ferroelectric BiFeO₃ thin films for efficient bias-free THz emission](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01357)

### Moiré and 2D Materials

**QWRTD probe of twisted bilayer MoS₂.** A quantum well resonant tunneling diode platform measures differential conductance versus filling factor and displacement field in ~57° tMoS₂, finding a robust ν=1 correlated insulator and a displacement-field-induced ν=3/4 state consistent with a generalized Wigner crystal. [Quantum Well Resonant Tunneling Diode Probe of Correlated States in Twisted Bilayer MoS₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39417)

**Quantum optics in graphene slit junctions.** Tight-binding plus NEGF calculations show trigonal warping produces directional electron beams near 2.7 eV, with Aharonov-Bohm oscillations at h/e period and gate-tunable interference phase. Symmetry breaking enables spin filtering; bilayer implementations show analogous beams at 50 meV. [Tunable Electron Quantum Optics in Graphene Slit Junctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00768)

**Metal–semiconductor heterojunctions in single GNRs.** Sequential on-surface synthesis plus STM-induced dehydrogenation writes metallic 7-iGNR segments into semiconducting H₂-7-iGNRs without changing width or backbone. STS and DFT show the valence band edge of adjacent semiconducting segments lies close to E_F, with no depletion region. [Synthesis of Metal-Semiconductor Heterojunctions in Single Graphene Nanoribbons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01194)

**Layer-controlled MoS₂ nanoribbon arrays.** CVD on low-symmetry sapphire grows oriented single-crystalline bilayer MoS₂ nanoribbons below 20 nm width, extendable up to four layers by tuning MoO₃ supply. Bilayer FETs show 2–3× higher mobility and current density than monolayers. [Layer-by-layer growth of highly aligned MoS₂ nanoribbon arrays](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00987)

**Graphite diamagnetism beyond Landau levels.** First-principles calculations of the small contributions to graphite susceptibility find Pauli paramagnetism negligible (2.3×10⁻⁹ emu/g), while the Langevin–Van Vleck diamagnetic term (−2.7×10⁻⁷ emu/g averaged) is comparable to the in-plane experimental diamagnetism and to fullerene response. [Weak non-Landau-type contributions to the diamagnetism of graphite](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01764)

**Anhydrous Na₂IrCl₆ single crystals.** The missing cubic member of the A₂IrCl₆ family is synthesized with fcc structure and antiferromagnetic order at 1.91 K, contrasting with previously reported monoclinic "anhydrous" Na₂IrCl₆. It offers a platform for J_eff = 1/2 physics and Kitaev interactions. [Na₂Ir^IVCl₆: The Missing Member of the Perfectly Cubic Vacancy Ordered A₂IrCl₆ Family, Another Potential J_eff = 1/2 Ground State Candidate](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01371)

### Quantum Information, Entanglement, and Complexity

**Thermal entanglement transitions from SU(2) symmetry.** In Gibbs states with strong SU(2) symmetry, finite-temperature ordering transitions coincide with entanglement transitions. Distillable entanglement and logarithmic negativity grow parametrically faster in ferromagnetic phases than the √N scaling of the paramagnetic phase. [Thermal entanglement transitions from strong SU(2) symmetry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00826)

**Algebraic relaxation of SW-SSB.** Strong-to-weak symmetry breaking decays exponentially under local Z₂ symmetry-breaking, but algebraic relaxation for continuous U(1) symmetry, with a two-stage scaling t⁻¹ then t⁻¹ᐟᶻ. Numerical verification uses fermionic chains with z = 2 and 4. [Algebraic Relaxation of Strong-to-Weak Symmetry-Breaking Order via Charge-Sector Broadening](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00474)

**Power-law channels induce mixed-state phases.** A long-range channel implementing power-law two-body dephasing circumvents the finite-depth obstruction, producing a phase diagram with trivial, mixed SPT, and SW-SSB phases. The mixed SPT phase hosts two logical qubits and nonvanishing Rényi-2 string order. [Mixed-state phases induced by power-law quantum channels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00480)

**Magic in quantum many-body scars.** An exact closed-form nonlocal trace distance magic is derived from the Schmidt spectrum. Scar eigenstates show extensive global magic despite low entanglement, and constrained Hilbert spaces can generate irreducible nonlocal magic. [Global and nonlocal magic of quantum many-body scars](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00129)

**Fermionic Gaussian Scrooge ensembles.** A fermionic Gaussian generalization of the Scrooge ensemble describes deep thermalization in free-fermion systems. Proven analytically for SYK₂ at arbitrary times, and numerically for random Gaussian circuits with charge conservation. [Fermionic Gaussian Scrooge Ensembles in Deep Thermalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01209)

**Walshness: NN representability metric.** A quantum state admits an efficient neural-network representation if and only if it has bounded Walshness. Minimizing the energy of a corresponding classical spin model finds the optimal basis, improving learnability of transverse-field Ising and toric code ground states by orders of magnitude. [Walshness: an intrinsic neural-network representability metric for quantum states](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00505)

**Polynomial-time LU equivalence of graph states.** A deterministic algorithm decides local-unitary equivalence of graph states in Õ(n^6.38) bit operations, replacing subset enumeration with constraints from pairs and triples. It also counts local-Clifford classes and decides LU equivalence for one-logical-qubit stabilizer codes. [Polynomial-time local-unitary equivalence of graph states](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00527)

**Quantum Krylov learning.** A restricted-access framework extracts model-independent spectral information from single-temperature probe autocorrelators, reconstructing the operator Krylov Jacobi matrix without Hamiltonian oracle access, symmetry, parametric models, or controlled gates. Sample complexity is polynomial in 1/ε. [Quantum Krylov Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00537)

**Rapid mixing of Gibbs samplers.** A quasi-local Gibbs sampler using Petz recovery on contiguous blocks mixes to the Gibbs state of any 1D finite-range Hamiltonian in O(log(n/ε)) time at all finite temperatures, with polylog-depth circuits to prepare these states. [Rapid mixing of quantum spin chains at any finite temperature](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01190)

**Non-Gaussianity bound and phase transition.** The magic Rényi entropy has a universal upper bound and Haar mean of ln(4/3) per Majorana. Evolving a Gaussian state under SYK reveals a first-order transition in MRE density with spontaneous breaking of copy-permutation symmetry. [Universal Bound and Phase Transition in Many-Body Fermionic Non-Gaussianity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02075)

**Nonlinear in-context learning.** Comparing kernel and feature learners on single-index targets via the replica method yields phase diagrams for when each architecture is advantageous, with different context-length scalings for the two learners. [In-context Learning of Single-index Targets: Comparing Kernel and Feature Learners](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01712)

**Mask resampling in autoencoders.** Masked linear reconstruction recovers latent features at linear sample complexity where unmasked reconstruction (PCA) fails. Mask diversity lowers sample complexity, but random cropping and flipping can obscure this advantage in standard image pipelines. [The hidden advantage of mask resampling: a theory of masked autoencoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01578)

**Thermodynamics of classifiers.** Error–cost trade-offs for binary classification show that classification error is bounded below by entropy production and dynamical activity. Vanishing thermodynamic cost forces error to 1/2, random guessing. [Thermodynamics of classifiers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24365)

**Stochastic thermodynamics of autoregressive models.** A framework estimates entropy production from sampled trajectories of non-Markovian generative models. Applied to GPT-2, token-level entropy production is dominated by syntactic artifacts while sentence-level production is higher for causally ordered text. [Stochastic Thermodynamics for Autoregressive Generative Models: A Non-Markovian Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07867)

**Schur sampling via SWAP tests.** Unitary Schur sampling of permutation-invariant states on n qudits can be implemented with only pairwise SWAP tests, using O(n min{n,d}³ log²(n/ε)) tests. The algorithm repeatedly extracts the largest antisymmetric subsystem. [Unitary Schur Sampling of Qudits via Random SWAP Tests: Hunt for Antisymmetry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02103)

**Porter-Thomas convergence in polynomial depth.** Brickwork random circuits of depth O(n^{2m+1} log n) converge to Porter-Thomas statistics within O(1/n^m) total variation. The proof uses approximate design moment bounds and local anticoncentration of inverse moments. [Local random quantum circuits converge to the Porter-Thomas distribution in polynomial depth](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02125)

**State preparation complexity in spin glasses.** Effective profile complexity from metric entropy of Pauli profiles gives state-preparation lower bounds for dense p-spin Hamiltonians, including Ω(n²/log n) one- and two-qubit gates for near-ground-state energy and depth-width tradeoffs. [Beyond Light Cones: State Preparation Complexity in Quantum Spin Glasses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02166)

**Fermionic Gaussian approximation theorems.** The admissibility condition on Majorana covariance matrices imposes a quantitative monogamy constraint on two-point correlations, yielding product-state approximations, a Gaussian de Finetti theorem, and recoverability bounds for spatial Gaussian states. [Approximation theorems for fermionic Gaussian states](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01860)

**Sequential circuits as generalized symmetry.** For "annihilable" symmetries, the sequential circuit fully determines the symmetry action and constrains fusion; for "unannihilable" symmetries like the Cheshire string, an additional 1D sequential circuit is needed. MPO and tensor network operator representations play a central role. [Sequential Circuits as Generalized Symmetry on the Lattice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22394)

**Simulating 1D quantum circuits.** The Sweeping RTM algorithm compresses temporal boundary states jointly to estimate output probabilities of chaotic brick-wall circuits. Numerical evidence suggests the required bond dimension grows subexponentially in the accessible time window. [Strong Simulation of 1D Quantum Circuits via Reduced Transition Matrices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02082)
