## Altermagnetism

Unifying thread across several papers today. An extended Kugel'-Khomskii spin-orbital model shows magnon **and orbiton** excitations in altermagnets emerge from two distinct microscopic mechanisms (inequivalent environments vs. correlation-driven orbital order), with chiral splitting present even for equivalent environments ([Collective magnon/orbiton excitations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10708)). In CrSb, **correlations enhance** nonrelativistic spin splitting, and spin-lattice coupling is identified as a microscopic descriptor ([Correlation-enhanced altermagnetism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11346)). "Hyperspin" ferromagnets extend altermagnetism to noncollinear systems via a conserved higher-dimensional pseudospin ([Hyperspin Ferromagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10930)). A Lieb-based 1D construction reveals that 1D confinement can *cancel* NRSS while SOC splitting survives—hiding altermagnetic functionality in apparently conventional AFM bands ([Néel-Vector-Dependent Splitting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11547)). Experiments: **large piezomagnetism** in cubic MnTe₂ confirmed by both dilatometry and NMR ([Piezomagnetism in MnTe₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11816)).

## Superconductivity
