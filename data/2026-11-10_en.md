# Today's arXiv Condensed Matter Physics — Key Advances

## Machine Learning & Data-Driven Materials Modeling

Several papers push interpretable and transferable ML for materials. **[Interpretable ML of formation energies and band gaps in hybrid perovskites](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09944)** combines XGBoost with SHAP and SISSO descriptors, flagging poor transferability of UPET while screening 180k candidates. **[A data-driven approach for systematic correction of electronic bandgaps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10889)** shows a single ML correction to DFT gaps rivals GW/hybrid accuracy. **[Learning electronic band structures across the s, p, d blocks with an empirical pseudopotential network](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11070)** (EpNet) predicts full band structures directly from coordinates at ~1% of DFT cost. **[Overcoming speed-accuracy trade-offs with medium-sized interatomic potentials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11544)** demonstrates an intermediate-size potential reaching near-best accuracy while running far faster, applied to billion-atom radiation cascades. **[Designing atomic displacements for finite-temperature phonons from few force calculations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12218)** introduces "special optimal thermal cells," cutting force-call counts for anharmonic phonons. Related: **[UniHam](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12509)** (SOC-complete Hamiltonian dataset), **[OPAL-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10380)** (polymorph-aware CSP evaluation), **[MLIP trajectory fine-tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01067)** (better relaxations despite worse forces), and **[WARP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12736)** (finite-T/P anharmonic relaxations).

## Superconductivity

**[Superconductivity in the metallic Wigner crystals of rhombohedral graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10117)** proposes an electronic supersolid, with a soft interband collective mode driving finite-momentum pairing near a Wigner-crystal instability. **[Mimicry of d-wave Andreev signatures by interlayer pairing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10130)** resolves conflicting nickelate evidence: nodeless interlayer pairing can fake d-wave point-contact signatures via mirror-selective barrier effects. **[Ambient-pressure superconductivity in bulk La₅Ni₄O₁₀](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12788)** reports bulk single-crystal square-planar nickelate superconductivity (Tc ≈ 18 K) without pressure or strain. **[Real topology on symmetry-invariant loops enforces pairing zeros](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12454)** establishes a general pairing-obstruction mechanism from 1D real topology. **[Superconductivity beyond band geometry: pair quantum geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06017)** derives an exact effective-mass theorem with a distinct pair-geometric term. Also: **[Visualizing d-wave pairing in Bi-2223 by Josephson tunneling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11339)**, **[Identifiying contributions to the superconducting diode effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10669)**, **[sub-Thouless energy scale in unconventional junctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10675)**, and **[pelletization-pressure engineering of Pr1111](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10083)**.
