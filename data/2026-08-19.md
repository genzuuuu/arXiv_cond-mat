# Condensed Matter Physics Papers — Summary

## Superconductivity & Nickelates

**[Critical couplings of 2D Ising model on various lattices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16949)** — Unified fermionic-field (Kac–Ward) formulation reproduces exact critical couplings for square, honeycomb, triangular, kagomé, and dice lattices, including anisotropic critical surfaces.

**[Unconventional pressure evolution of SDW in La₃Ni₂O₇](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17505)** — Electronic Raman study under hydrostatic pressure shows the SDW gap and T_SDW increase with pressure while the coupling ratio stays ~7.5 (strong coupling). The SDW peak broadens with pressure, indicating progressive loss of long-range order coherence.

**[Bridging ambient- and high-pressure superconductivity in La₂LnNi₂O₇ films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17745)** — Compression via Ln substitution lowers T_c while physical pressure raises it to 67–73 K at 16 GPa. T_c correlates with normal-state transport between T² and T-linear behavior.

**[Flux-tunable superconductivity in topological insulator nano-SQUID](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17404)** — Global superconducting signatures (critical current oscillations, diode sign reversal) suggest a topological transition, but zero-bias peaks appear in both topological and trivial regimes — highlighting the need for combined global+local probes for Majorana identification.

**[Superconductivity at KTaO₃ interfaces: gate-imprinted memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19052)** — Electrostatic gate cycling promotes superconductivity at AlOₓ/KTaO₃ interfaces, with light-induced erasure via polar-nanoregion reorientation and oxygen-vacancy charge trapping.

**[Outstanding T_C enhancement in Y₂NiIrO₆ under compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11590)** — Curie temperature rises from 192 to 243 K under pressure due to concurrent Ni/Ir–O bond compression and angle changes; rock-salt ordering blocks extended superexchange frustration.

## Topological & Quantum Geometry

**[Quantized spin Hall effect in 3D nodal-ring semimetal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17627)** — Spin Hall conductivity scales linearly with nodal-ring radius R, with Rashba vs. Weyl spin-orbit coupling acting as independent switches for conventional vs. unconventional response components.

**[Windmill spin dynamics in Mn₃Sn](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17225)** — A soft eigenmode develops into large-angle chiral windmill precession when the canting angle exceeds a threshold; this produces Berry curvature and a static anomalous Hall effect during precession.

**[Hidden unbounded potential in generalized SSH model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17899)** — A mapping reveals hidden unbounded potentials that drive multifractal critical phases; re-entrant multifractalization and demultifractalization are achieved by balanced competition.

**[Electronic reconstruction at quasicrystal–moiré crossover in TBG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18052)** — Near 29° twist angle, coexistence of dodecagonal quasicrystalline symmetry with emerging moiré periodicity produces strong interlayer hybridization and unusual Landau-level spectra with 4- to 12-fold degeneracy.

**[Emergent spin quantum Hall edge states in 2DEG–superconductor hybrids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05847)** — Class-C topological protection manifests as even-integer quantized spin conductance, robust against disorder; an electrical measurement scheme is proposed.

**[Topology and criticality in non-Hermitian multimodal optical resonators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.05163)** — Multimodal non-Hermitian lattices support topological modes and criticality with loss-engineered control; localization properties can be tuned by external gauge fields.

**[Multiple topological quasiparticles in hexagonal TTe crystals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17557)** — HfTe hosts coexisting type-I/II/III Weyl and nodal-line phases in both electronic and phononic spectra, robust to functional, SOC, and lattice variations.

## Magnetism & Spintronics

**[Intra-atomic magnetic octupoles in Mn₃Sn](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17461)** — Site-localized AMOs couple to cluster magnetic octupoles via bilinear interaction; AMOs act as microscopic operators underlying nonrelativistic spin splitting and connect Mn₃Sn to d-wave altermagnets.

**[Emergent ferromagnetism in NiI₂–NbSe₂ heterostructure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21218)** — Proximity to the superconducting substrate renormalizes exchange interactions and drives monolayer NiI₂ into a ferromagnetic ground state, visualized via Yu-Shiba-Rusinov states.

**[Altermagnetic α-MnTe films growth](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17513)** — High-quality MnTe films grown on various substrates (insulators, topological insulators, metals, ferromagnets); superstructures form on MnTe(0001) after growth and thermal treatment.

**[Defect geometry selects polar and anomalous Hall phases in 2D altermagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17788)** — Point impurities in V₂Se₂O selectively break crystal symmetries, yielding three impurity classes: spin-momentum preservation, hybrid-parity (Edelstein), and metallic ferrimagnetic with anomalous Hall effect.

**[Dressed magnon dynamics in a Bose–Hubbard bath](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17980)** — Magnons form mobile polarons with retarded bosonic clouds, reduced velocities, and magnon–boson entanglement; bath interactions suppress entanglement and bound-state formation.

**[Magnetic droplets in W/CoFeB/MgO spin Hall nano-oscillators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17677)** — Robust high-frequency droplet stabilization demonstrated; droplets can breathe, drift, deform, or escape confinement depending on drive current and field orientation.

**[Spin-bond theory unifying non-relativistic spin splitting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09644)** — Unitary spin phases generate odd-parity p-wave textures; Hermitian amplitudes generate even-parity altermagnetic limits; a mixed non-commuting regime yields synthetic spin-orbit coupling with tunable dynamics.

## Quantum Simulation & Dynamics

**[Hydrodynamization in 1D Bose gases at nonzero temperature](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16980)** — Initial temperature shortens damping and prethermalization times but leaves hydrodynamization coherence time unchanged; a nonzero-temperature dephasing time is defined via rapidity distribution width.

**[Hidden ergodic relaxation in bichromatic Mott lattice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17076)** — Coefficient entropies relax toward GOE predictions while experimentally accessible observables remain nearly unchanged — emergent hidden ergodicity beneath persistent Mott-like order.

**[Long-time dynamics of Majorana tetron qubits under quasiparticle poisoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18042)** — Bloch-Redfield analysis yields steady state, parity leakage, and decoherence rates at arbitrary charging energy; the exponential dephasing suppression is removed by qubit-state energy splitting.

**[Many-anyon braiding in non-Abelian FQHE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17424)** — Hybrid Monte Carlo computes Moore-Read quasihole braiding matrices; global-rotation braiding schemes are robust to finite-size effects, and interactions/electrostatic traps affect fusion-channel energetics.

**[Long-range nonlinear sigma model for singular kicked rotors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17649)** — A nonlocal supersymmetric sigma model reproduces the PRBM Anderson transition with RG analysis to two-loop order, establishing shared infrared physics.

**[Quantum-geometric bound on dynamical instability in bosonic systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12406)** — The Fubini-Study speed equals the Frobenius norm of the symplectic stretching rate; provides λ_max ≤ √2·v_FS, saturated by resonant squeezing.

**[Observation of quantum phase crossovers in semiconductor spin ladder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17789)** — Programmable Heisenberg spin ladder in germanium quantum dot arrays maps rung-singlet, canted antiferromagnetic, and polarized phases with four-point spin correlators.

## Disordered & Glassy Systems

**[Controlling vapor-deposited glasses of organic semiconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17080)** — Review of substrate-temperature control over density and molecular orientation; properties can be tuned to improve OLED efficiency and lifetime.

**[Rejuvenation vs. overaging in PMMA glasses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17086)** — 5000 cyclic loading/unloading cycles leave segmental dynamics unchanged or faster (no overaging); cycled glasses retain memory of original age, recovering on the aging timescale.

**[Inorganic substrate perturbation length scale in glassy organic semiconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17089)** — Interfacial packing is more disordered than bulk; ~8 nm near the substrate is modified, with deposition temperature controlling beyond-interface structure.

**[Frustration without glass in non-Abelian simplicial networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17817)** — SU(2) model with random plaquette couplings shows continuous loss of global compatibility without replica-symmetry breaking — a non-glassy correlated gauge liquid.

**[Electron transport in amorphous materials: review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17814)** — Comprehensive review of Anderson localization, mobility edges, hopping transport, and Kubo-Greenwood simulations in amorphous semiconductors.

## Active Matter & Soft Condensed Matter

**[Motility-induced phase separation in convection rolls](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17692)** — Weak advection suppresses clustering and shifts the critical self-propulsion velocity; reentrant phase behavior appears at very high self-propulsion.

**[Resonant untrapping of active polymers in breathing lattices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17359)** — Environmental fluctuations resonantly accelerate escape from self-generated traps; diffusion increases by up to two orders of magnitude at matching fluctuation rates.

**[Topology of nonequilibrium currents controls active transport](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17954)** — Defect structures (integer indices) in hydrodynamic current fields constrain transport pathways and render them robust to perturbations.

**[Rheology of colloidal depletion gels controlled by brush density](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17764)** — Lower brush density accelerates gelation, increases elasticity and yield stress, and lowers the coordination threshold for elastic nodes.

**[Heat capacity markers for shape and jamming transitions in active systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17903)** — Nonequilibrium heat capacity develops peaks at shape transitions (boundary accumulation) and jamming transitions (cluster reorganization).

**[Equilibrium kink-like torsion in magnetoactive elastomers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23682)** — Stable kink deformations form at transition boundaries between homogeneous states; elastic moment is compensated by magnetoelastic interactions.

**[Machine-learning-guided discovery of Y-Mn-B magnetic compounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17200)** — Graph neural networks + MLIP relaxation + DFT validation identify 5 stable and 61 metastable phases; Mn-chain phases recover magnetic moments (~1.1 μB) with ferromagnetic ordering.

**[Active learning MBE of quantum materials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17742)** — Random-forest SMBO framework navigates growth parameter space for metastable Fe₃Sn, halving prediction error to ~10% within four iterations.

**[eXplainable DFT: self-evolving agent for experiment-simulation mismatches](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26703)** — Resolves 105/112 metal-vs-semiconductor discrepancies; after 60 cases, correct mechanisms rank top-3 for 80% of held-out cases.

**[Quantum geometric tensor preconditioning for RNN quantum states](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18065)** — minSR stabilized with regularization enables robust RNN-NQS training, outperforming Adam on Ising/cluster models and competing on 2D Heisenberg/J₁-J₂.

**[Basis-update and Galerkin time integration in canonical MPS form](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16994)** — BUG time integration expressed as canonical MPS sweeps with first-order error bounds; runtime-vs-accuracy tradeoffs identified versus two-site TDVP.

**[Tape-recorder coarse graining for long-time fermionic transport](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18049)** — Stochastic removal of outgoing reservoir modes with nonperturbative infidelity bounds; benchmarks against HEOM and Landauer-Büttiker results.

**[Iterative tensor network transformations for element-wise functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17135)** — Framework for nonlinear operations in the compressed tensor-train domain; demonstrated on reactive flows and Max-SAT with up to 2⁷⁰ configurations.
