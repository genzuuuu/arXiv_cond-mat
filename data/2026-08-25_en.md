## Topological & Quantum Geometry

**Topological Magnonic Black-White Hole Crystal** — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22783)  
Theoretical work showing that inhomogeneous spin currents in antiferromagnets create magnonic black/white-hole horizons, forming a non-reciprocal magnonic crystal with topological edge states. This establishes a connection between analogue-gravity physics and topological magnonics.

**Second-Chern Bounds in Non-Abelian Quantum Geometry** — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12221)  
Derives quantum geometric bounds for doubly degenerate energy levels in 4D parameter space, showing saturation conditions relate to quaternionic Cauchy-Riemann equations. Four-band Dirac Hamiltonians automatically saturate the determinant bound.

**Flat and Topological Floquet Minibands from Patterned Light in Bilayer Graphene** — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18945)  
Proposes optically imprinting superlattices on bilayer graphene using patterned light fields. The approach yields nearly flat bands in AB-stacked bilayer graphene with tunable period and nontrivial valley Chern numbers.

**Topological Space-Time Waves in Complex Channels** — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22848)  
Experimental demonstration of topological space-time beams created via metasurface resonance. Shows preservation of topology even through random media, enabling crosstalk-free transmission of space-time waves.

**Quantum Geometry Driven Optical Responses in 1T-MX₂ Monolayers** — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23289)  
Develops an 11-band Slater-Koster tight-binding model for 1T-TMD monolayers, establishing connections between quantum geometry, optical spectral weight, and quantum metric via the interband f-sum rule.

**Engineering Exact Mobility Edges in Quasiperiodic Aharonov-Bohm Chains** — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22264)  
Derives exact mobility edges in quasiperiodic chains coupled to an auxiliary lattice threaded by synthetic flux, including anomalous mobility edges separating critical and extended states.

**Flux-Induced Aharonov-Bohm Oscillation in Kitaev Spin Liquids** — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23561)  
Shows that isolated π fluxes imprint interference patterns on the Majorana continuum in Kitaev spin liquids, providing a finite-energy tunneling signature of emergent flux excitations.

## Superconductivity

**Microscopic Mechanism of High-Tc Superconductivity in HgBa₂Ca₂Cu₃O₈** — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08181)  
Ab initio study of triple-layer cuprate Hg1223 under pressure, identifying emergent local attraction from reduced repulsion as the pairing mechanism. Reproduces the experimental pressure-dependent Tc peak.

**Weak Coupling Theory of Nickel-Based 327 Superconductors** — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22759)  
Comprehensive review of RPA, FLEX, and FRG approaches applied to the pressurized bilayer nickelate La₃Ni₂O₇, covering pairing interactions from the two-orbital Hubbard model.

**Three-Dimensional Ising Superconductors via Intercalation** — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23083)  
Systematically designs 16 intercalated NbSe₂/NbTe₂ compounds, identifying four promising 3D Ising superconductors with out-of-plane spin polarization and anisotropic superconductivity.

**Multiple Superconducting Phases in Pressurized UTe₂** — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01843)  
Point-contact spectroscopy reveals Andreev bound states and odd-kz pairing components in UTe₂. Distinguishes multiple superconducting phases via the relative weight of pz vs px(y) pairing.

**Competing Extended-s- and d-Wave Pairing in Stoichiometric FeTe** — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23467)  
First-principles FLEX calculations show competing extended-s and d-wave pairing instabilities in FeTe, with filling-dependent crossover as the dominant spin-fluctuation channel changes.

**No-Go Theorem for Cavity-Enhanced Superconductivity** — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14784)  
Proves that passive cavities cannot enhance superconductivity within minimal coupling, identifying two routes beyond: collective-mode amplification or suppressing competing orders.

**Enhancement of Superconductivity by Magnetic Impurity Polarization** — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17408)  
Extends Kharitonov-Feigelman theory to arbitrary temperatures and field orientations, explaining observed Tc enhancement and penetration depth suppression in dirty superconducting films.
