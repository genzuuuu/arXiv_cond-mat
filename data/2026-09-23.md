### Superconductivity & Topological Phases

**Theoretical & Computational Advances**

A significant theoretical work explores **re-entrant p-wave superconductivity** in a Chern Dartboard Insulator. The study shows that Hatsugai-Kohmoto (HK) interactions can induce superconductivity in a phase previously predicted to be insulating, leading to an insulator-superconductor-insulator transition ([Paper 3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25205)).

In the realm of quantum spin liquids, a proposal outlines a realization of the **Kitaev honeycomb Hamiltonian** in a superconducting metamaterial composed of Cooper-pair boxes and semiconductor-superconductor heterostructures, potentially achieving an equilibrium quantum spin liquid without spin-orbit coupling ([Paper 4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25210)). A related numerical study investigates the **scaling of qubit coherence** in Majorana chains formed by quantum dots, finding that longer chains are better quantum memories but not necessarily better qubits under manipulation due to disorder effects ([Paper 5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25223)). Another numerical study using iPEPS and other methods identifies an extended **symmetric quantum phase**, potentially a Z2 Dirac spin liquid, in a honeycomb Heisenberg model with sublattice-selective interactions ([Paper 8](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25243)).

A unified framework is presented for **model fractional quantum Hall (FQH) states on lattices**, constructing exact parent Hamiltonians. It shows that short-range interactions stabilize Laughlin states at ν=1/3 and 1/4, while two-body onsite repulsion supports a Moore-Read state at ν=1, with the latter being more susceptible to interband mixing ([Paper 30](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25585)).

In a study of **trilayer nickelates**, quantum interference between short-range spin-density-wave fluctuations is shown to generate an orbital density wave, providing a unified microscopic framework for the intertwined density-wave order and high-temperature superconductivity observed in these materials ([Paper 150](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24857)).

**Experimental Observations**

A study on **Ising superconductors** reveals a strongly anisotropic multigap state in (LaSe)1.14(NbSe2)m misfit layer compounds. A fragile gap on the Γ-pocket coexists with a robust gap on the K/K' pockets, suggesting unconventional pairing ([Paper 148](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15446)).

In experimental work on **supercooled water**, ultrafast spectroscopy data is reinterpreted. The observed heat capacity increase is explained by nonequilibrium glassy dynamics rather than a liquid-liquid critical point, providing an alternative view to a prominent hypothesis ([Paper 57](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26442)).

### Magnetism, Spintronics & Topological Materials

**Altermagnets & Unconventional Magnets**

A theoretical proposal establishes a **nonlinear route to Néel order control** in centrosymmetric altermagnets. A nonrelativistic staggered spin polarization, combined with a spin-orbit-coupling-induced uniform component, enables deterministic 180° reversal of the Néel vector, as demonstrated in a model for FeSb2 ([Paper 79](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26709)).

Experimental work on altermagnetic MnTe reveals a **thermally-driven reorientation of the Néel vector**, which points 30° from the a-axis at low temperature before aligning with it around 260 K. This finding helps explain why certain altermagnetic effects only appear well below the ordering temperature ([Paper 120](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.14710)).

Another theoretical study on spin-textured altermagnets shows that smooth spatial variations of the Néel order act as **emergent gauge fields**, leading to a net interband linear dichroism that can be controlled by the magnetic texture ([Paper 129](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14950)).

**Spin Hall & Spintronics**

The **magnetic spin Hall effect (MSHE)** has been electrically identified in a conventional ferromagnet (Co-based multilayer). Reversal of magnetization switches the MSHE signal, confirming its time-reversal-symmetry-odd character and yielding a magnetic spin Hall angle of ~3.8% ([Paper 23](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25406)).

A study on **spin-to-charge conversion** in Bi2Se3/NiFe heterostructures demonstrates a giant enhancement by inserting a Ti spacer layer. The spin Hall angle is enhanced by an order of magnitude, attributed to the Ti layer protecting the topological surface states from interdiffusion ([Paper 139](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01201)).

**Topological Magnons**

A combined experimental and theoretical study establishes the non-coplanar antiferromagnet MnTe2 as a tunable **Weyl magnon material**, hosting symmetry-protected topological nodal lines that can transition into Weyl magnons under an external magnetic field ([Paper 125](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18534)).

### Quantum Transport & Quantum Geometry

**Quantum Geometry & Fermi Liquids**

A new theoretical framework, **Berry-Landau Fermi-liquid theory**, is formulated for spinless fermions. It incorporates quantum geometry (Berry curvature and quantum metric) as a fundamental ingredient, showing that the intrinsic anomalous Hall conductivity is fixed by the dressed quasiparticle Berry curvature, while the Drude weight has both conventional and quantum-geometric contributions ([Paper 70](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26632)).

An exact, closed-form solution for the quantum geometric tensor of the **quarter-flux Harper-Hofstadter model** is derived by exploiting sublattice symmetry, quantifying its lowest band as a nearly ideal Chern band ([Paper 143](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26099)).

**Transport Phenomena**

A theoretical work addresses the puzzle of **giant negative magnetoresistance** observed at low temperatures where electron-electron scattering is weak. It develops a theory of mixed hydrodynamic and non-Markovian magnetotransport, explaining the effect via memory effects and an unconventional viscosity from scattering by disorder ([Paper 144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04097)).

An experimental study observes a **Nernst effect driven by longitudinal spin fluctuations** around the Curie temperature in (Mn,Cr)Sb, where the anomalous Hall effect disappears, breaking the Mott relation. Theoretically, this peak behavior is reproduced by introducing longitudinal spin fluctuations ([Paper 55](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26407)).

### Materials Science & Computational Methods

**Materials Discovery & Characterizations**

A new study reveals that intrinsic switching in **hafnia ferroelectrics** leads to oxygen diffusion and breakdown. Machine-learning molecular dynamics simulations show that heat from localized switching events is not dissipated fast enough, causing an avalanche-like process of oxygen conduction, suggesting ways to mitigate this through pulse design ([Paper 49](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26030)).

A benchmark study evaluates 12 deep generative models for **crystal structure prediction (CSP)**. It finds that most correctly predicted structures by generative models are also found by template-based methods, establishing that current models function largely as implicit prototype libraries rather than de novo predictors ([Paper 59](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26502)).

A study reports bulk superconductivity in **rhombohedral ReO3** under pressure, with a maximum Tc of ~17.5 K. First-principles calculations show strong electron-phonon coupling in the rhombohedral phase, providing a microscopic explanation for the emergence and subsequent loss of superconductivity at higher pressures ([Paper 69](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26628)).

**Computational & Data-Driven Methods**

A new **nullspace-guided adaptive (NGA) bootstrap method** is introduced to improve energy lower bounds for quantum many-body ground states. It dynamically refines the bootstrap basis, achieving nearly exact energy bounds for the transverse-field Ising chain and improving upon state-of-the-art bounds for the Hubbard chain by up to two orders of magnitude ([Paper 35](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25834)).

A **neural network backflow** variational framework with low-rank multi-determinant updates is introduced for simulating strongly correlated fermions. Applied to the 2D Hubbard model, it achieves energies within 0.45% of auxiliary-field quantum Monte Carlo at half-filling and captures stripe patterns at 1/8 doping ([Paper 60](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26544)).

A new **flow-based generative model, UFO-MGen**, is developed for crystal structure prediction. It learns topological features of Wyckoff representations, achieving the highest success and SUN (stable, unique, novel) rates compared to state-of-the-art models, with a fine-tuning module for property-constrained inverse design ([Paper 61](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26547)).

**AI Agents in the Lab**

An **AI agent, Agent-E2MD**, autonomously translates interatomic potential equations into executable LAMMPS pair styles. It successfully handles seven different material-potential pairs, including a new symbolic regression EAM model, validating them against reference energies and forces ([Paper 72](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26657)).

A separate AI agent performs **end-to-end quantum transport measurements** on 2D semiconductors. Through autonomous measurement campaigns lasting up to six days, it determined the spin-orbit coupling energy in monolayer MoS2 and mapped a layer-resolved phase diagram in bilayer MoS2 ([Paper 73](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26661)).
