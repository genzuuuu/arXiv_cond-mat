## 1. Quantum Magnetism & Spin Systems

**[Discrete Scale Invariance of Ising Mesons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35940)**
Shows discrete scale invariance can emerge from internal motion of composite excitations in the marginal 1/r⁴ transverse-field Ising chain, with the scale-anomaly exponent fixed by magnetization and kink mobility—a microscopic realization of quantum limit-cycle physics.

**[Magnetic excitations of the strongly-anisotropic triangular XXZ model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36140)**
Introduces a projected spin-product coherent-state approach for the Ising-anisotropic triangular XXZ model, yielding a pseudo-Goldstone mode and dramatic improvement over linear spin-wave theory versus experiments on K₂Co(SeO₃)₂.

**[Frustrated Gd³⁺ Double Perovskites as High-Performance Magnetocaloric Materials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36538)**
Ba₂GdSbO₆ and Sr₂GdSbO₆ reach the lowest reported ADR temperatures for Gd³⁺ magnets (down to ~67 mK), with frustration suppressing magnetic order while maintaining outstanding entropy density.

**[Magnetic-field-activated transport from band geometry in gapped nodal-line semimetals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36606)** and **[Linear and nonlinear transport responses of topological nodal-line semimetals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36664)**
Develop nonperturbative semiclassical magnetotransport theory, showing orbital magnetic moment activates an intrinsic Hall current via geometric Fermi-surface reconstruction; companion work reveals Klein tunneling protected by nodal topology.

**[Fabrication / Glass physics — Linking Topological Medium Range Order and Density-Wave Coherence to Metallic Glass Ductility](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36041)**
Combines synchrotron G(r) with 4D-STEM to show geometric and topological MRO jointly govern ductility in Zr-based metallic glasses.

## 2. Topological Phases & Band Theory

**[Topological Band Theory for High-Dimensional Parameter Spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35977)**
Extends quantum Hall band theory to high-dimensional flux-parameter manifolds (genus-g surfaces, 4ℓ+2 dimensions), revealing higher curvature invariants in Kubo-like formulae.

**[Irreducible Weyl Semimetals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37927)**

**[Symmetry-enforced topological parity: revisiting 2ℤ classifications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37106)**
Shows momentum-dependent symmetry actions can convert even-integer constraints into odd-integer ones without changing the symmetry algebra, with explicit no-go theorems.

**[Classification of topological phases of matter in stochastic systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38171)**
Shows Markovian constraints alter the matrix space so only two symmetry classes (no symmetry, pseudo-Hermiticity) remain robust—a rigorous framework for active/living matter.

**[Quantum metric induced nonlinear transport in the hidden loop-current phase of RbV₃Sb₅](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36353)**
Third-harmonic transport reveals a hidden time-reversal-breaking phase at ~35 K, with quantum metric quadrupole directly contributing to the longitudinal response.

**[Spatially dispersive photogalvanic effects as a probe of quantum geometric tensor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38102)**
Uses light spatial dispersion to isolate quantum metric and Berry curvature in distinct polarization channels, demonstrated in 1T'-MoTe₂ across its topological transition.

## 3. Superconductivity

**[Uniform-Charge-Density d-Wave Superconductivity in the Pure t-J Model at 1/8 Doping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37191)**
VUMPS on infinite cylinders finds uniform d-wave superconductivity without stripe order, providing methodologically distinct evidence in the long-standing stripe–SC debate.

**[Reversible field-free superconducting diode effect controlled by an antiferromagnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38003)**
Demonstrates field-free SDE in Al proximitized by collinear CuMnAs, establishing that neither spin-split bands nor net magnetization is required—extending nonreciprocity to compensated magnets.

**[Anisotropic interface-confined superconductivity in FeTe-based heterostructures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38148)**
Tunnel-diode measurements of six FeTe heterostructures reveal nodal/anisotropic order parameter independent of overlayer, pointing to the interfacial FeTe as the common origin.

**[Enhanced Critical Currents and Irreversibility Fields in YBa₂Cu₄O₈ Films through Ca-Substitution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36402)**
Ca doping increases hole concentration, raising J_c by up to 10× at 6 T while preserving the pinning mechanism from Y-125 stacking faults.

**[Effect of superconducting fluctuations on nonreciprocal dichroism and gyrotropy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10464)**
Computes fluctuation-induced gyrotropic conductivity in noncentrosymmetric superconductors, tracing it to asymmetric Cooper-pair dispersion underlying the diode effect.
