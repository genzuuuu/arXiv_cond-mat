# Condensed Matter Physics Papers — Key Advances Summary

## Superconductivity & Correlated Electron Systems

- **[Superconductivity in strongly overdoped cuprates: beyond the single-band model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14090)** — High-pressure oxygen overdoping of YBa₂Cu₃O_y shows constant T_c despite hole density exceeding the dome limit; propose a two-band model with a_1-symmetry states as a new theoretical framework.

- **[Coexisting Charge Density Wave and Superconducting Order in Quantizing Magnetic Fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05039)** — In rhombohedral hexalayer graphene, CDW order coexists with superconductivity in quantizing fields, revealing new insight into intertwined orders.

- **[Crystalline Germanium Josephson Junctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08500)** — Epitaxial, fully in-situ-grown Ga-doped Ge Josephson junctions with atomically sharp interfaces; unconventional field-enhanced switching current attributed to quasiparticle-assisted thermalization.

- **[Complete Suppression of Thermomagnetic Instabilities in Nb Superconducting Films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13699)** — Combining Cu overlayers with Ar ion irradiation fully eliminates flux avalanches, restoring stable critical-state behavior.

- **[Electron Doping of La₃Ni₂O₇ Thin Films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30297)** — DFT identifies Zr, Hf, and Th as efficient electron dopants that enhance interlayer hopping and could promote higher T_c.

## Topological & Non-Hermitian Phenomena

- **[Observation of flat-band skin effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19745)** — First experimental observation of non-Hermitian skin effect in a flat band; emerges from dispersive-band spectral topology and disappears at large non-Hermiticity.

- **[Universal aspects of bulk density of states in non-Hermitian lattices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14508)** — Establishes universal bulk DOS structure independent of boundary conditions for finite-range tight-binding models; Brown measure identified as canonical representative.

- **[Small-world structure of quantum computer hardware](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13855)** — Quantum register state networks exhibit small-world properties with percolation transition matching quantum chaos onset, extending to 30 qubits.

- **[Orbital Hall Effect in Weyl Semimetals from quantum geometric band interference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14353)** — Establishes direct link between OAM transport, band geometry, and topology in TaAs-family Weyl semimetals via ab initio theory and minimal models.

- **[Half-integer spectral zeros for leakage suppression in fast transmon pulses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10578)** — Flat-top transmon pulses with cosine ramps yield exact spectral zeros at half-integer ramp-duration conditions, confirmed by four-level simulations.

## Quantum Magnetism & Spin Physics

- **[Probing intrinsic magnetic phases in low-dimensional nearly twin-free NiPS₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14189)** — Intrinsic anisotropy resolved in twin-free crystals; sharp spin-flop transition and broad fluctuation regime characterized.

- **[Microscopic investigation of spin dynamics in Sr₄Mn₂CoO₉](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14222)** — INS reveals persistent low-energy excitations at room temperature; combined crystal-field and exchange analysis explains thermally activated spin relaxation.

- **[Observation of in-plane anomalous Nernst effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14060)** — Demonstrates that the anomalous Nernst effect works with in-plane magnetization, breaking the orthogonality constraint.

- **[Quantum Geometric Kondo Cloud](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14072)** — In flat bands, Kondo screening is governed by quantum geometry; cloud size tensor decomposed into quantum-metric, Berry-connection, and gradient terms.

- **[Harmonic Hierarchy of Altermagnetic Spin Splitting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25128)** — Wavefunction-level framework links orbital geometry to d-wave and g-wave altermagnetic anisotropies in 2D square lattices.

- **[Universal Signature of Hundness and Its Quantification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14480)** — Two correlation factors (z_L, z_H) quantify Hund character; discrepancy captures low-energy effectiveness.

- **[Chiral spin liquid and chiral antiferromagnetism in half-filled moiré Hubbard model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14503)** — DMRG shows displacement field enriches the phase diagram, destabilizing chiral spin liquid and promoting chiral correlations.

- **[Local magnetic properties of RMn₂Ge₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07013)** — Skyrmion-bubble hosting materials show local fluctuations and distinct rare-earth-ordering effects.
