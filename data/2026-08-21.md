## Topological Materials & Phenomena

**[Mirror Chern insulators in 2D altermagnetic Tc₂Cl₂O and Tc₂Br₂O](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19725)** — First-principles identification of monolayer Tc₂X₂O as altermagnetic mirror Chern insulators with nonzero mirror Chern number (C_m=1), helical edge states, and quantized spin Hall conductivity.

**[Tilted p-wave magnet candidate CeNiAsO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19856)** — NMR experiments reveal a commensurate antiferromagnetic order with small out-of-plane moment (m_z≈0.05 μ_B), creating a "tilted p-wave magnet" paradigm that enhances non-relativistic spin splitting.

**[Surface charge density wave in UTe₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12505)** — STM experiments identify primitive CDW wavevectors in UTe₂, showing surface-induced modifications in U 5f electron valence enable a novel purely electron-driven charge ordering.

**[Robust 2D surface superconductivity in γ-PtBi₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04867)** — STM reveals robust surface superconductivity (T_C=2.9 K, H_C2≈1.8 T) linked to Fermi arcs, with quantized vortices and Josephson effect demonstrating 2D macroscopic quantum phase coherence.

**[Cascade of topological transitions in imperfect double helical liquids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10335)** — RG analysis shows how disorder, pairing asymmetries, and interactions in double helical liquids produce cascades of topological phase transitions and revival of Majorana zero modes.

**[Spin-orbit magnetism in altermagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06730)** — Systematic symmetry approach showing that only fourfold-rotation-connected altermagnets exhibit distinct perturbative orders for orbital/spin magnetizations, enabling coaxial Hall effect with magnetization parallel to Néel vector.

**[Ferroelectric altermagnetic Chern insulator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11862)** — Lattice-regularized BHZ model shows electrically controllable Chern topology in 2D altermagnets, with ferroelectric hybridization enabling transitions like C=0→−1 and C=1→2.

**[Engineering altermagnetic symmetry in Cr₁₋ₓMnₓSb](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06173)** — Mn substitution in CrSb enables otherwise symmetry-forbidden anomalous Hall effect, with Landau theory modeling field-driven Néel vector reorientation.

**[Fragile single-cone Dirac quantum walks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05112)** — Shows 2D quantum walks with unpaired Dirac cones require non-symmorphic symmetries broken by spatial inhomogeneities, unlike their 1D counterparts.

**[Topological flat minibands and fractional Chern insulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.22173)** — Programmable platform combining Zeeman field with tunable superlattice potential in Rashba systems yields flat bands with C=1 supporting robust FCI states at fillings 1/3 and 2/3.

**[Josephson transport in He-ion-irradiated YBCO weak links](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20109)** — Unified diffusive-SNS junction description of HIM-fabricated YBCO weak links; reduced interface transparency explains observed dose dependences.

**[Fluctuation-controlled asymmetric kinetics in MITs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20100)** — 1T-TaS₂ shows burst-like avalanches during cooling but continuous transformation during heating, with athermality controlling pathway selection.

**[Strange metal Hall effect in BaFe₂(As₁₋ₓPₓ)₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20325)** — Doping-independent Hall contribution within a fan-shaped region above a putative QCP, consistent with critical fluctuations origin.

**[Pair-density wave from doped altermagnetic Mott insulator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23654)** — DMRG on checkerboard t-J model reveals PDW regime with unconventional wavevector locking (Q_PDW≈2Q_Stripe) coexisting with stripe order.

**[Photogalvanic transport of nonreciprocal Cooper-pair fluctuations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20166)** — Theory of nonlinear optical responses in 2D noncentrosymmetric superconductors above T_c, including photogalvanic effect and photovoltaic Hall effect with channel separation.

**[Theory of frozen flux in superconducting strips](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04961)** — Dynamic-balance model for trapped flux during cooling, yielding quantitative predictions for freezing temperature and vortex density.
