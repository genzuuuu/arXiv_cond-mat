### Superconductivity & Correlated Electron Systems
- **Iron-Based Superconductors (FeSe & Fe-based):** A new quantum-well mechanism is proposed to unify odd/even-parity STS spectra and mirror symmetry breaking in FeSe systems, predicting gap pairs in excellent agreement with experiments ([Even/Odd-parity STS spectra induced by quantum well mirror symmetry breaking in iron-based superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24906)). In Ba(Fe,Co)₂As₂ films, intertwined charge stripes are discovered that modulate vortex bound states, leading to both abelian and non-abelian vortices, with the latter hosting Majorana zero modes ([Intertwined Charge Stripes and Majorana Zero Modes in An Iron-Based Superconductor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15873)).
- **Kagome Superconductors:** Experiments clarify that the field-free superconducting diode effect in CsV₃Sb₅ requires both inversion-symmetry breaking (activated by surface oxidation) and internal time-reversal symmetry breaking linked to CDW order, showing it is stochastic and domain-controlled ([Symmetry Origins of the Field-Free Superconducting Diode Effect in the Kagome Superconductor CsV₃Sb₅](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26022)). In the kagome superconductor SrSn₃, distinct surface and bulk superconducting channels with different gaps and vortex-core states are directly revealed via STM ([Distinct Surface and Bulk Superconductivity in the Kagome Superconductor SrSn₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25271)).
- **PtBi₂ & 2D Superconductors:** Bulk superconductivity is confirmed in centrosymmetric Te-doped PtBi₂, clarifying its s-wave nature ([Probing bulk superconductivity in centrosymmetric Te-doped PtBi₂ single crystals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25093)). A new air-stable 2D superconductor, Nb₂Pd₃Te₅, is realized with an intrinsic quasi-1D pair density modulation ([Realization of Air-Stable Two-Dimensional Superconductor Nb2Pd3Te5 With Quasi-One-Dimensional Pair Density Modulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25331)).
- **Other Superconducting Advances:** A study on disordered 2D films attributes the superconductor-insulator transition to quantum phase fluctuations, even as the finite-temperature transition remains of the BKT type ([Origin of the superconductor-insulator transition in disordered two-dimensional films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07101)). A theoretical proposal for higher-charge superconductivity provides microscopic characterizations via charge-4e/6e expectation values and momentum conservation arguments ([Many-electron characterizations of higher-charge superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23801)).

### Topology, Geometry & Quantum Materials
- **Topological Insulators & Semimetals:** A comprehensive ARPES study shows that the Rashba parameter on topological crystalline insulator Pb₁₋ₓSnₓSe surfaces can be tuned over a wide range (0–3.5 eV·Å) by transition metal adsorption, enabling control over Dirac-Rashba and double Dirac cone states ([Tuning Dirac-Rashba and Double Dirac Cone Surface States of Topological Crystalline Insulator Pb₁₋ₓSnₓSe by Transition Metal Adsorbate](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25156)). The photonic spin-Hall effect is proposed as a non-invasive probe of time-reversal symmetry broken topological phases via its centroid shift ([Photonic spin-Hall effect as a probe for time-reversal-symmetry broken band topological phases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25002)).
- **Non-Hermitian & Higher-Order Topology:** The concept of topological Euler insulators is extended to non-Hermitian systems, revealing unique topological phase transitions and anomalous edge-band overlaps ([Non-Hermitian topological Euler insulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25411)). A study on a non-Abelian Moore-Read fractional Chern insulator shows its topological fingerprint is robust to non-Hermitian skin effects within finite windows ([Fate of the non-Abelian Moore-Read manifold under the non-Hermitian skin effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25816)).
- **Quantum Geometry & Magnetism:** A temperature-dependent non-Abelian gauge potential is derived for magnetization dynamics driven by spin currents ([The temperature-dependent non-Abelian gauge potential](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25437)). A new "viscochiral regime" of electronic transport is predicted, where spatial gradients of Hall viscosity select chiral vortical flow patterns ([Viscochiral Transport: Chiral Selection of Hydrodynamic Vortices by Berry Curvature](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26102)).
- **Quantum Spin Liquids & Magnetism:** Raman spectroscopy is used to map the symmetries of magnon modes in the candidate Kitaev spin liquid Sr₂IrO₄, revealing pressure-enhanced magnetic heterogeneity ([Raman magnon spectroscopy of local interactions and ground state selection in Sr₂IrO₄](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25040)). Pressure-dependent transitions up to 16.3 GPa are identified in the Kitaev candidate Na₂Co₂TeO₆ via Raman spectroscopy ([High-pressure phase transitions in the quantum spin liquid candidate Na2Co2TeO6 probed by Raman spectroscopy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25076)).
- **Moiré & Twisted Heterostructures:** A class of interaction-driven "quantum spin Hall crystals" is predicted at fractional filling of twisted MoTe₂, featuring 9-fold degenerate states with distinct topological phases ([Quantum spin Hall crystals at fractional filling of twisted MoTe₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24989)). Signatures of the ferro-Josephson effect, driven by spin-domain-wall precession, are reported in twisted graphene heterostructures ([Signatures of a ferro-Josephson effect in twisted graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25257)).

### Computational & Theoretical Methods
- **Quantum Many-Body Physics:** A closed-form expression for the full quantum geometric tensor (QGT) is derived for the quarter-flux Harper-Hofstadter model, confirming its near-ideal Chern band properties ([Exact analytical spectrum, eigenstates, and quantum geometry of the quarter-flux Harper-Hofstadter model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26099)). The modular commutator is generalized to gapless systems, showing it measures the parity anomaly at a Dirac critical point ([Parity Anomaly as Modular Commutator with Massless Dirac Fermion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26078)).
- **Materials Discovery & AI:** A high-throughput framework combining diffusion-based structure generation and hierarchical screening identifies hundreds of new magnetic rare-earth transition metal alloys, including 5 thermodynamically stable phases with high magnetization ([High-throughput Discovery of Magnetic Rare Earth Transition Metal Alloys](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25270)). An interpretable physics-informed retrieval-augmented generation language model (PIRAG-LM) is developed for inorganic crystal synthesis planning, achieving 91.4% accuracy and guiding the experimental synthesis of five new compounds ([Interpretable physics-informed retrieval-augmented generation language model for end-to-end inorganic crystal synthesis planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25392)).
- **Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials:** An active-learning workflow is developed to create a robust neural network potential for Nafion, enabling stable 31 ns simulations of large systems with quantitative agreement with experimental diffusion coefficients ([Active Learning of a Neural Network Potential for Large-Scale Atomistic Simulations of Polymer Electrolyte Membranes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20412)).
- **Simulation & Modeling:** A scalable framework coupling OpenLB and LAMMPS is presented for fully resolved simulations of non-spherical particles, revealing distinct settling behaviors of cubes compared to spheres ([A Scalable OpenLB LAMMPS Framework for Fully Resolved Simulations of Hindered Settling of Arbitrary Non-Spherical Particles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26030)).

### Experimental Techniques & Applications
- **Quantum Sensing & Metrology:** A method for traceable scanning magnetometry using individual NV centers is presented, determining NV-sample distance and azimuthal orientation with high precision ([Methods for traceable scanning magnetometry using single nitrogen vacancy centers in diamond: determining orientation, distance and localization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04632)). Wide-field magnetic imaging with diamond quantum sensors is used to capture real-time vortex rearrangement in NbN films under local laser heating ([Wide-field magnetic imaging of shielding-current-driven vortex rearrangement under local heating using diamond quantum sensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14578)).
- **Ultrafast & Terahertz Photonics:** A room-temperature graphene sub-THz detector is monolithically integrated on a silicon dielectric waveguide, achieving 11.5 V/W responsivity and 1.94 GHz bandwidth for D-band operation ([Room-temperature graphene sub-terahertz detector integrated on a silicon dielectric waveguide](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25895)). A compact generator is developed for microsecond-class ultrafast pulsed magnetic fields up to 1 T, enabling magnetization and Faraday rotation measurements on short timescales ([Compact nondestructive generator for microsecond-class ultrafast pulsed magnetic fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23584)).
- **Nanoscale Characterization:** A novel stroboscopic dark-field X-ray microscopy technique images the complete switching cycle of a commercial GaN transistor, resolving electromechanical and thermal strain fields in real-time ([Watching a GaN Transistor Switch: Real-Time Nanoscale Strain and Heat Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25980)). Electrical manipulation of oxygen stoichiometry is demonstrated in multiterminal YBa₂Cu₃O₇₋δ junctions, allowing local control of the superconducting transition ([Electrical manipulation of oxygen stoichiometry in multiterminal YBa₂Cu₃O₇₋δ junctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26045)).

A universal square-root law is identified for the entropy production rate in nonreciprocal networks, arising from directed walk properties ([Directed walks shape a universal square-root law of entropy production rate in nonreciprocal systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25030)).

Lattice-Boltzmann and Langevin simulations of polymers in crowded solutions reveal distinct diffusion regimes governed by system size and hydrodynamic coupling ([Dynamical and conformational behavior of a polymer in a crowded solution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25242)).
