# Condensed Matter Physics Highlights (arXiv, August 2025)

## Topological & Correlated Electron Systems

- [Collinear altermagnetism for 3D chiral higher-order topological insulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27552) — Proposes that altermagnetism (a new collinear magnetic order) can realize the elusive chiral \(C_4\mathcal{T}\)-protected higher-order topological insulator, with concrete material suggestions.

- [Interacting Chern insulator transition on the sphere: revealing the Gross-Neveu-Yukawa criticality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.15338) — Exact diagonalization on a sphere for the \(N=2\) Gross-Neveu-Yukawa criticality closely matches conformal bootstrap predictions, confirming the universality class.

- [Fractional Hall Conductance of Laughlin States from a Topological Index](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27767) — Rigorous Laughlin-pump argument proving fractional quantization of Hall conductance as a topological index, establishing topological stability.

- [Candidate for a Fractional Topological Insulator in Twisted MoTe2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18508) — Pump-probe circular dichroism evidence for a time-reversal-symmetric fractional topological insulator at \(\nu=-4/3\), with an Ising antiferromagnetic signature and an extremely small energy gap to competing states.

- [Topological signatures in the quench dynamics of periodically driven quantum systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27588) — Shows that dc bond currents after quenching graphene with circularly polarized light signal induced topological phases, and that period-averaged conductance quantizes in the weak-coupling bath limit.

- [Euler Topological Metals in 1D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27554) — Rigorous proposal for probing the Euler characteristic of a Fermi sea via a transport response in 1D continuum and lattice models.

- [How Long-Range Tails Reshape Non-Hermitian Spectra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28577) — Shows that infinitesimal long-range hoppings can nonperturbatively reconstruct spectra/skin modes of non-Hermitian systems; formulates a "squeezed" generalized Brillouin zone.

- [Topological Charge-2ne Superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21325) — General framework for topological charge-\(4e\) and higher superconductors via field theory, predicting fractional flux quantization and non-Abelian orders.

- [Gauge invariance, collective modes, and normal-state subtraction in Dirac superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28485) — Bethe-Salpeter analysis shows that gauge invariance uniquely fixes the normal-state subtraction in Dirac superconductors, justifying a widely used procedure.

## Quantum Magnetism & Spin Systems

- [Field-tuned quasiparticles and electronic structure in heavy-fermion YbNi4P2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28119) — Quantum oscillation measurements show strong mass reduction with field and an abrupt Fermi-surface change at 17 T, consistent with single-ion Kondo physics.

- [Interplay between crystal structure and magnetism in CeCrB4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28238) — DFT+U study confirms ferromagnetic ground state, mixed-valence Ce, and molecular-like Cr–Cr bonding states in this candidate quantum spin-dimer material.

- [Strain-driven spin-flop transition in bilayer iridate Sr3Ir2O7](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22473) — First-principles prediction that \(-2.4\%\) biaxial compression flops the easy axis from \(c\) to the \(ab\)-plane, collapsing the giant magnon gap; a charge-neutral handle on spin-orbit order.

- [Chiral enhancement of two-magnon bound states in a triangular-lattice magnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01062) — Scalar chirality selectively binds magnons of one handedness without modifying the one-magnon spectrum; exact diagonalization confirms finite-momentum pairing.

- [Switchable chiral antiferromagnetism through nonlinear magnetic susceptibility](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28546) — First-principles framework for deterministic switching of time-reversal partners of fully compensated chiral antiferromagnets (Mn\(_3X\)N) via nonlinear susceptibility.

- [Field-amplified readouts of weak altermagnetic exchange in MnF2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26540) — Resolves a tiny (+8 μeV) sublattice-odd exchange and shows longitudinal fields linearly amplify it into measurable compensation fields and splitting contrasts.

- [Orbital-Selective Coexistence of Interlayer Spin-Singlet Formation and SDW Order in La3Ni2O7](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27917) — Site-selective \(^{17}\)O-NMR reveals a well-developed interlayer spin-singlet in \(d_{3z^2-r^2}\) orbitals coexisting with stripe SDW order and an anomalous spin reconfiguration.

- [Collinear altermagnetism meets SU(N): Simplified real-space criteria](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27151) — Simple real-space symmetry test for identifying altermagnets by sublattice permutation; demonstrated on Mn\(_2\)SSe prototypes.

- [Strong electron correlations and ligand hybridization for altermagnetism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14248) — DFT+DMFT shows that MnTe is an ideal altermagnet, MnF\(_2\) suppresses splitting by correlations, and RuO\(_2\) is an itinerant altermagnet; both strong correlations and hybridization are needed.

- [Interacting Chern insulator transition on the sphere](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.15338) — See above under topological systems.

## Superconductivity & Quantum Fluids

- [Bad Metal Behavior and Lifshitz Transition of a Nagaoka Ferromagnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01909) — Fermionic FRG for the Hubbard model at \(U=\infty\) reveals paramagnetic Fermi liquid, stripe order, and an extended Nagaoka ferromagnet with a flat incoherent band and a Lifshitz transition.

- [Superconductivity in the repulsive Hubbard model induced by density-assisted hopping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09363) — Analytic and numerical evidence that density-assisted hopping generates attraction in the bonding band of ladders/bilayers, driving BKT transition into a spin-gapped superconducting phase.

- [Fast Dipolar Charge-Polarization Fluctuations: Toward a Unified Pairing Mechanism in Bad Metals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02330) — Effective interaction from mixed kinetic-potential processes yields \(d_{x^2-y^2}\) (cuprates) and \(s^{\pm}\) (iron-based) gap symmetries, suggesting a unified pairing channel.

- [A Three-Fluid Model of Dissipation in Superconducting RF Cavities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28565) — Surface quasiparticle states identified via BdG; modified "three-fluid" model explains the anti-Q slope in superconducting cavities.
