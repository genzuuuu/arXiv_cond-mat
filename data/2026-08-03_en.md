# Condensed Matter Physics arXiv Summary (July 2026)

## Topological & Quantum Geometry

**[Anomalous Boundary Modes in a Floquet Hyperbolic System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28719)** — Constructs an anomalous Floquet topological phase on negatively curved hyperbolic lattices. Introduces a puncture-based spectral-flow diagnostic that avoids ambiguity from extensive outer boundaries of finite hyperbolic patches.

**[Intrinsic Orbital Hall Effect in Degenerate Spin-3/2 Systems driven by the quantum metric](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28744)** — Shows that in rotationally invariant spin-3/2 systems, the orbital Hall effect originates from interband matrix elements of the orbital magnetic moment, governed by the quantum metric rather than Berry curvature.

**[Hidden chiral signatures in ferroaxial K2Zr(PO4)2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29573)** — Reveals that the ferroaxial phase of K2Zr(PO4)2 is antiferro-chiral. Electric fields induce a ferri-chiral state with net electronic chirality, producing distinct Berry curvature dipole responses.

**[Valley- and Orbital-Controlled 2D Chern Insulators Without Spin-orbit Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29486)** — Demonstrates orbital-driven Chern insulating behavior without spin-orbit coupling through staggered potential and orbital coupling competition in a 2D lattice model.

**[A Unified Symmetry Framework For In-plane Anomalous Hall effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29452)** — Establishes a comprehensive symmetry framework connecting spin space group and magnetic space group for in-plane anomalous Hall effects, screening MAGNDATA for candidate materials.

**[Symmetry selection rule for the band-edge shift current in two dimensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28927)** — Formulates an exact angular selection rule unifying band-edge shift current responses in multilayer graphene and kagome lattices, showing gate-switchable bulk photovoltaic response.

## Strongly Correlated & Quantum Magnetism

**[Gapped Parent Hamiltonians for the Strongly Deformed Toric Code](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28740)** — Rigorously constructs local gapped parent Hamiltonians for strongly deformed toric code states with exponentially decaying Wilson loop coefficients, showing these realize trivial gapped phases.

**[Disorder induced time crystal in athermal random field Ising model with non-reciprocal interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28781)** — Shows random field disorder along with non-reciprocal interactions alone can produce a time crystal without external driving in complete graph and 3D, but not in 2D.

**[Generalized Holstein-Primakoff transformation with optimizable bosonic truncation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28983)** — Introduces a transformation interpolating between Holstein-Primakoff and Dyson-Maleev forms, substantially reducing truncation errors in spin-wave calculations.

**[Unwithered Majorana fermions in the bulk of a quantum chain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29615)** — Shows disentangled n-particle Majorana modes penetrate into the bulk without attenuation along disorder lines in the Kitaev chain.

**[Sound attenuation and velocity shift in antiferromagnetic spin-1/2 chains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29392)** — Relates sound velocity shift to free energy derivatives in XXZ chains, finding universal behavior near fully polarized phase and enhanced attenuation.

**[Pairing near the boundary of a box-shaped trap in the BEC-BCS crossover](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29620)** — Shows local pairing exhibits Friedel-like oscillations in weak-coupling BCS regime near box edges, suppressed towards BEC regime.

**[Continuous Tuning of the Charge-Phase Uncertainty in a Josephson Junction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29636)** — Using STM-based tunable junctions at millikelvin temperatures, observes smooth transition between incoherent and coherent Cooper pair flow.

**[Dissipation-enhanced vortex clustering in a compressible quantum fluid](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29475)** — Shows particle loss can enhance transient vortex clustering beyond conservative evolution, strongest at intermediate loss rates.

**[Delayed Formation of Landau Polaritons in Phase-Resolved THz Spectroscopy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29501)** — Observes that Rabi oscillations in Landau polaritons begin after a delay of one cavity round-trip time, demonstrating strong coupling sets up only after cavity mode formation.
