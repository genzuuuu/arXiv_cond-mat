## Superconductivity & Correlated Electron Systems

- [Extended s-wave superconductivity in M-point twisted bilayer SnSe2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27492): FRG study reveals valley-selective spin fluctuations drive unconventional superconductivity in twisted SnSe2, with an extended s-wave state stabilized near half-filling—phenomenologically parallel to iron pnictides.

- [Spin-incoherent Mott-Thouless pumps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27321): Introduces a class of Mott-Thouless pumps operating in highly entropic spin-incoherent states, showing exponentially protected quantized transport when the pumped charge resides in a gapped sector decoupled from hot spin degrees of freedom.

- [Anomalous metal and superconducting phases in rhombohedral graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28425): Reports systematic study of superconductivity in rhombohedral graphene on WSe2, finding both zero-resistance and finite-saturation-resistance regions, with the latter reproducing anomalous metal phenomenology.

- [Single-crystal structural phase diagram of stoichiometric bilayer nickelate La3Ni2O7 under hydrostatic pressure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27607): Establishes a definitive pressure-temperature phase diagram showing a direct transition from charge-ordered orthorhombic to tetragonal structure near 10 GPa, coinciding with bulk superconductivity onset.

- [Third-order nonlinear transport in a percolative two-dimensional superconductor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27641): Observes substantial third-harmonic response in trilayer 1T'-MoTe2 during percolative transition, captured by time-dependent Ginzburg-Landau theory with fluctuating Cooper pairs.

- [FFLO transition and quantum criticality in polarized Fermi gases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28335): Self-consistent t-matrix approach restores Luttinger theorem compliance and places the FFLO transition in the mean-field universality class with unified geometric picture in 2D and 3D.

- [Quantum criticality and non-Fermi liquids: the Wilsonian renormalization group perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10140): Nonperturbative RG reveals generic instability of non-Fermi liquid fixed point at Q=0, suggesting first-order transitions or Q≠0 ordering.

- [Analytical solution of coupled self-consistency and linearised Usadel equations for the dirty superconductors at Tc and with the proximity effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20557): Provides analytical solutions for superconducting films near Tc with proximity effects, reducing the problem to an eigenvalue equation.

- [Symmetry, Sound, and the Quest for Chiral Superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28365): Review of elastic modulus measurements to identify multi-component (chiral) superconductivity, covering heavy fermion, iron pnictide, and Kagome systems.

- [Competing Chern states revealed by quasiparticle charging in moiré rhombohedral graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08710): Bulk-sensitive mesoscopic antidot measurements reveal a thermodynamic phase transition from FCI to an anomalous Hall crystal at low temperatures, excluding an edge-state explanation.

## Optics & Spectroscopy

- [Quasiparticle phono-conversion: filming carriers coalescing into excitons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28417): Time- and momentum-resolved photoemission + excitonic Bloch equations resolve free-carrier to exciton conversion in monolayer WSe2, establishing phononic origin with sequential relaxation cascade.

- [Light-induced trion-exciton competition revealed by ultrafast photoemission](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22523): trARPES reveals purely optical trion generation in Ta2NiS5 without external charge doping via unconventional single-particle pathway.

- [Anomalous Microwave Response in YBCO Resonators beyond the Two-Level-System Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27392): YBCO CPW resonators show TLS-like loss that doesn't saturate nor display power dependence; low-T response better described by paramagnetic defects and logarithmic loss.

- [Directional Photocurrent Generated by Quantum Interference Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05318): Interference between two-photon and three-photon absorption produces a "beam" of electrons in specific direction with narrower angular spread.

- [Molecular Hyperpolarisability as a Screening Descriptor for Second-Order Nonlinear Optics in Ferroelectric Nematic Liquid Crystals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27986): DFT hyperpolarisability calculations with oriented-gas model identify promising NLO candidates; relative trends more useful than absolute values for screening.

- [Robust Fano-like Antiresonances in Large-Area Au-coated Ag-Nanoisland Ensembles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16525): Electrostatic modal analysis identifies Fano-like dips as destructive interference between superradiant/subradiant modes, weakly sensitive to disorder.

## Josephson Junctions & Mesoscopic Systems

- [Hybrid light-matter excitations and spontaneous time-reversal symmetry breaking in two-dimensional Josephson Junctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07256): Quantum LC resonator coupled to graphene-based Josephson junction shows current-phase features indicating spontaneous TRS breaking, with hybridized collective modes.

- [Current-based RF charge sensing in a carbon nanotube](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28313): Current-mode charge sensor at 1.25 MHz achieves 0.15 μe/√Hz sensitivity with single-shot DQD readout at SNR=17.

- [Entropic signatures of the single-impurity Kondo state](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27502): Thermodynamic measurement via Maxwell relation tracks entropy suppression from Kondo singlet formation in GaAs quantum dots; asymmetric dN/dT lineshape confirms screening.

- [Universality of Energy-Space Entanglement in Quantum Impurity Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27394): Energy-space bipartition EE flows to universal constants (integer multiples of ln2) at Fermi-liquid fixed points, classifiable by 1D topological band theory.

- [Optimal Majoranas in Mesoscopic Kitaev Chains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13945): Full microscopic treatment of Kitaev chain hybrids identifies ABS parity-crossings as sweet spots for optimal MZM localization with large gaps.

## Quantum Simulation & Quantum Computing

- [Mitigating quantum decoherence via global optimal control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28479): Tensor-network-based global optimal control compresses gate sequences by an order of magnitude in globally driven superconducting ladders, restoring high fidelities under dissipation.

- [Noise-aware emulation and cross-device validation of neutral atom analog quantum processing units](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28364): Framework propagates dominant noise mechanisms through full computation cycle, predicting observables within uncertainty envelopes across three Pasqal processors.

- [Geometry-Induced Domain-Wall Pinning and ℤ2 Asymmetry in Nominally One-Dimensional Rydberg Arrays](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27358): QuEra experiments reveal pinning and symmetry breaking from geometric effects in 1D Rydberg arrays, including ferromagnetic bonds across vacancies.
