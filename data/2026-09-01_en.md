# Condensed Matter Physics arXiv Summary (August 2025)

## Superconductivity

### Unconventional Superconductivity in Kagome Systems
- **[Direct Evidence of Unconventional Superconductivity in Doped Kagome system RV₃Sb₅](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28848)**: Impurity-bound state spectroscopy in optimally doped RbV₃Sb₅ reveals a fully gapped, multiband superconducting state with pronounced particle-hole asymmetric bound states around nonmagnetic defects. Theoretical comparison rules out conventional s-wave and s± pairing, providing strong evidence for a chiral superconducting order parameter.
- **[Evolving charge order in the CDW state of AV₃Sb₅ metals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29142)**: Muon-spin spectroscopy identifies a second transition within the charge density wave phase for Cs, Rb, and K compounds, indicating additional charge modulation beyond the primary CDW.
- **[Topological pair density waves in kagome superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29156)**: Review of topological PDW states, exploring non-trivial relative phases among PDW components that break time-reversal symmetry, with potential realization in AV₃Sb₅ compounds.

### Nickelate and Iron-Based Superconductors
- **[Doping-driven evolution of pairing symmetry in pressurized La₃Ni₂O₇](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29091)**: Undoped La₃Ni₂O₇ favors s±-wave pairing from repulsive magnetic odd-mode interactions. Hole doping drives a transition to d_xy-wave pairing via γ-pocket expansion, while electron doping preserves s±-wave even through a Lifshitz transition.
- **[Cuprate d-wave superconductivity based on Non perturbative many body theory for spin fluctuation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04168)**: Nonperturbative spin GW method for the Hubbard model reproduces Néel temperature and T_c in quantitative agreement with cuprate experiments, revealing a broad pseudogap region above the d-wave dome.
- **[Tracking unconventional superconductivity in the presence of strongly correlated Fermi arcs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24977)**: Exact incorporation of Fermi arcs in a solvable model modifies BCS equations, producing T_c domes, suppressed superfluid stiffness, and gap-to-T_c ratios exceeding BCS limits, consistent with cuprate observations.
- **[Chemical Perspectives on Cuprate Superconductivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30001)**: Proposes that d-wave Cooper pairs are antiferromagnetically coupled spin dimers protected in antiferromagnetic hole dimers (AHDs), explaining several puzzling cuprate observations.

### Other Superconducting Systems
- **[Superconductivity and Magnetism in Bi-Ni System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28855)**: Review of NiBi₃ intermetallic (4K superconductor) and Bi-Ni bilayer heterostructures with evidence for time-reversal breaking and triplet pairing.
- **[Electronic state of vortices at twin boundaries in a nematic superconductor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28994)**: BdG theory for s±d nematic superconductors shows vortices trapped at twin boundaries bind fractional vortex pairs with weakly-induced s±id components.
- **[Chiral superconductors and competing states across a Lifshitz transition in rhombohedral pentalayer graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29873)**: Quantum oscillations map a Lifshitz transition between circular and annular Fermi surfaces. Chiral superconductivity competes with Wigner crystals and reentrant QH states, with different magnetic field stabilities on each side.
- **[Superconductivity of Tellurium Polyhydride with Tc above 90K](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30139)**: Discovery of superconductivity in TeH₄ with T_c ≈ 91 K at 263 GPa, characterized by face-shared TeH₁₂ cages forming quasi-molecular H₂ units.
- **[Superconducting pairing symmetry and surface spectra in wallpaper fermion systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29691)**: Competition between on-site and off-site pairing in topological crystalline insulators hosting wallpaper fermions reveals twisted surface spectra from hybridization effects.
- **[Superheating Field of Extreme Type-II Superconductors Calculated from the Eliashberg Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29842)**: Eliashberg calculations for Nb and Nb₃Sn show the superheating field ratio H_s/H_c exceeds BCS predictions, with a maximum at intermediate impurity scattering.
- **[Joule heating control in tubular synthetic antiferromagnet skyrmion transport](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29724)**: Optimal control turns the skyrmion Hall effect into a mechanism for reducing Ohmic losses in skyrmion transport within tubular geometries.
- **[Josephson-Phase Reversal of Non-Bloch Andreev Propagation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29787)**: Changing the Josephson phase reverses non-Bloch propagation of Andreev bands in spin-orbit-coupled planar junctions, enabling reconfigurable non-Hermitian transport.
- **[Quadratic pair-breaking absorption edge from Anderson localization in a superconducting wire](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30598)**: Anderson localization replaces the linear Mattis-Bardeen pair-breaking onset with a weaker quadratic Mott edge in disordered superconducting nanowires.
- **[Third-harmonic generation in superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01200)**: Quantum geometry, specifically the quantum metric, can amplify the nonlinear light-Higgs coupling by orders of magnitude, enabling Higgs-mode-dominated THG in the clean regime.
- **[High-Temperature Superconductivity of the Fe-Se-H compound](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27349)**: EPR measurements suggest superconductivity in Fe-Se-H at room temperature, though the interpretation remains controversial.
- **[Superconductivity of Tellurium Polyhydride](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30139)**: Experimental discovery of superconductivity (T_c ≈ 91 K) in TeH₄ at 263 GPa via laser-heating in diamond anvil cells.
- **[CDW Gap Collapse and Weyl State Restoration in Ta₂Se₈I](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07897)**: First-principles study identifies the 2.52 THz CDW amplitude mode as the most efficient coherent phonon pathway for suppressing the CDW gap and restoring Weyl semimetallic behavior.

## Magnetism and Spin Phenomena

- **[Stoner contributions to the magnon equation of motion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30931)**: Linear response theory derives Landau-damped magnon equations with damping and dispersive mass from electron-magnon coupling, demonstrated for itinerant ferromagnets.

### DMI and Chiral Magnetism

## Materials Synthesis and Properties

- **[Extending the Pnictide Chemical Space for Photovoltaics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30402)**: Screening of 104 pnictide compositions identifies NaCaInN₂, NaSrInN₂, and K₄ZnP₂ as promising PV candidates with optimal gaps and defect resistance.
- **[Elastic properties of amorphous LiTaCl₆ solid-state electrolyte](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29404)**: MLFF-MD approach yields Young's modulus 2.84 GPa matching experiment (2.91 GPa), behaving like a soft polymer/gel, while static methods overestimate by >170%.
- **[Cesium Clustering in FLiBe molten salt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29898)**: Total scattering and MD reveal Cs ions cluster extensively with BeF₄²⁻ bridging, with 5 mol% CsF suppressing crystalline Li₂BeF₄ formation.
- **[Two-step transient liquid phase bonding of NiTi to Ti-6Al-4V](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30117)**: NbZrW diffusion barrier enables joining with superelastic behavior to 460 MPa and stable cyclic response.
- **[Orbital occupation selects structural dimensionality in binary transition-metal oxides](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28996)**: CrO exemplifies orbital-occupation-driven selection of 3D vs 2D bonding frameworks with distinct magnetic and ferroelastic properties.
- **[Polaron Conductivity in α-Fe₂O₃ Quenched by Adsorbed NO₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26372)**: DFT+U shows NO₂ adsorption eliminates Fe²⁺ polaron states via 0.72e⁻ transfer, explaining resistance increases in hematite gas sensors.
- **[Halide donors in Ga₂O₃ and Al₂O₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30680)**: Cl on oxygen sites is an unusually shallow donor in (AlₓGa₁₋ₓ)₂O₃ even at high Al fractions, though true n-type conductivity in Al₂O₃ remains difficult.
- **[Electronic phase separation in VO₂(110) ultrathin films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29116)**: Nondimerized insulating phase emerges below ~2.2 nm critical thickness, coexisting with the conventional MIT phase and scaling exponentially with reduced thickness.
- **[Orbital and Spin Edelstein Effects in KTaO₃(110) 2DEGs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31051)**: Combined structural, spectroscopic, and transport analysis shows orbital Edelstein effect largely counterbalances its spin counterpart (~20% contribution needed to explain magnetotransport).
- **[Mobility Enhancement in Si/SiGe Quantum Wells via Buried Si Layer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27377)**: Buried Si layer traps oxygen impurities, reducing background by ~5× and increasing electron mobility without altering the dominant low-density disorder landscape.
