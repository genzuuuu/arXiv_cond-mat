## Altermagnetism & Spin Phenomena

**Symmetry-selective spin splitting control:** [Symmetry-selective nonrelativistic spin splitting in antiferromagnets driven by coherent phonons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01844) — Coherent phonons dynamically induce NRSS in collinear AFMs (MnPS₃), with mode-selective activation of s-wave spin-split states vs. d-wave altermagnetic states based on light polarization.

**Multipole observation:** [Observation of g-wave altermagnetic multipole](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01969) — Direct visualization of g-wave altermagnetic multipole in CrSb via X-ray diffraction with valence electron density analysis, revealing antiferroic ordering of electric hexadecapoles.

**Metallic altermagnets:** [Magnons in Metallic Altermagnetic KV₂Se₂O](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01853) — RPA magnon spectra show directional splittings and anisotropic decay in metallic altermagnets, with ~6 meV gap opening from spin-orbit coupling.

**Thermal effects:** [Symmetry-enforced third-order nonlinear thermal Hall effects in altermagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02008) — Third-order nonlinear THE dominates in d-wave and i-wave altermagnets with out-of-plane Néel order; demonstrated for KV₂Se₂O and MnF₂.

**Nonlinear Hall:** [Systematic display of spin splitting in representative altermagnetic crystals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02767) and [AlterSeeK-Path](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02770) — Novel Brillouin zone paths sampling general k-points reveal altermagnetic spin splitting hidden in conventional band structures; software tool for 54 3D and 12 2D cases.

**Magnon dynamics:** [Nonreciprocal Magnon Hanle Effect in Antiferromagnetic α-Fe₂O₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02631) — Non-reciprocity in Hanle signal when interchanging spin injector/detector roles; field-dependent signal difference reverses sign at compensation field.

**Response measurements:** [Spin Hall Magnetoresistance in Antiferromagnetic Insulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02632) — SMR amplitude scales with sum of absolute sublattice magnetizations; α-Fe₂O₃/Pt shows SMR of 2.5×10⁻³.

**Symmetry display:** [Symmetry-selective topological magnon engineering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02338) — Symmetry analysis and optical/magneto-optical probes of Van Hove singularities in altermagnetic topological systems.

**Theory advances:** [Complete Topological Classification with P and T Symmetries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02025) reveals a topological invariant invisible to K-theory; [Anomalous Superfluid Response in Altermagnetic Superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02263) reports paramagnetic Meissner effect at arbitrary impurity concentrations.

## 2D Materials & Twisted Systems

**Moiré excitons at room temperature:** [Room-temperature local strain control of moiré excitons in MoS₂/WSe₂ heterobilayers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02340) — Tip-induced strain activates and tunes interlayer excitons to telecom wavelengths at 300 K.

**Flat bands in CVD graphene:** [Direct observation of flat bands in near-magic-angle twisted bilayer CVD graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02403) — Nano-ARPES confirms flat bands in CVD-grown TBG, comparable to exfoliation-based samples.

**Defects in hBN:** [Tuning Boron-Vacancy Qubit Coherence through Layer Number in hBN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01812) — T₂ rises from 199 ns (bulk) to 653 ns (monolayer); saturation at 3 layers with sublattice decomposition identifying boron as origin.

**Pressure effects on defects:** [Persistence and emergence of quantum defects through pressure-induced phase changes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02100) — Spin-pair defects retain pressure-independent resonances to 20 GPa while hBN transforms to wBN; irreversible defect landscape reconfiguration enables quantum emitter engineering.

**Magnetism in EuIn₂Sb₂:** [EuIn₂Sb₂: epitaxially stabilized axion insulator candidate](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02114) — MBE growth of new trilayer structure with strong spin-orbit coupling; in-plane AFM at T_N = 10.5 K.

**vDW confinement qubits:** [Molecular spin qubits in a van der Waals bottle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01872) — Cobaltocene confined in SnS₂/CdPS₃ slows spin-lattice relaxation by >2 orders of magnitude, forming ordered superlattices.

**Supramolecular lattices:** [Direct Observation of Zigzag Edge States of a Supramolecular Diatomic Kagome Lattice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02567) — Trip-Phz molecules self-assemble into diatomic Kagome lattice on Pb(111); STM confirms topological edge states from Zak phase quantization.

**Twist-controlled stacking:** [Twist as a Mechanical Switch for Reconfigurable Stacking in h-BN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02723) — Reproducible switching between AA and AB stacking via applied torque; torque maxima scale with contact area.

**Multilayer WSe₂ analysis:** [Supermoiré Reconstruction and Topological Mosaics in Twisted Trilayer WSe₂ and MoTe₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30813) — Relaxation produces topologically inequivalent domains with domain-dependent valley Chern numbers in helical/alternating trilayers.

## Quantum Materials & Correlated Electron Systems

**Spin-chain evolution:** [Evolution from Kitaev to XXZ spin chains via distortion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01805) — Unified microscopic framework for BaCo₂V₂O₈ incorporating CoO₆ octahedral distortions, explaining critical field anisotropy through g-tensor and exchange renormalization.

**Nickelate superconductivity:** [Electronic Structure and Superconductivity in La₁.₅₅Sr₀.₄₅CuO₄/La₂CuO₄ Superlattices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02668) — HAXPES/XAS track chemical potential evolution, enhanced screening, and increased orbital polarization near top of superconducting dome.

**Charge-transfer insulators:** [Charge-Transfer Electronic Structure of NiX₂ (X = S, Se)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02148) — DFT+DMFT reveals smaller charge-transfer energy than previously assumed, substantial ligand-to-Ni charge transfer.

**TBG Hartree-Fock:** [Relaxation effects on Hartree-Fock ground states in twisted bilayer graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02815) — Structural relaxation drives semi-metallic phase at ν=-2 due to particle-hole asymmetry and enhanced Hartree potential.

**Hydride superconductivity:** [Quantum Structural Renormalization and Anharmonic Stabilization of Superconductivity in P4/mmm YScH₈](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02295) — Quantum/anharmonic effects lower stability threshold by ~40% and correct Tc overestimation by ~20 K.

**Vortex physics:** [Anomalous vortex shape in a frustrated superconductor hosting chiral multicomponent order parameters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.06711) — STM shows vortex orientation locked to crystallographic domains, revealing hidden chirality in LiTi₂O₄.

**High harmonics:** [Prominent Dimensional Effects on High-order Harmonic Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02466) — Contrast between 1D SrCuO₂ (robust plateau) and 2D Pr₂CuO₄ (strong thermal suppression); attributed to dimensionality-dependent doublon-holon dephasing.

## Superconductivity & Topological Superconductors

**Josephson effects in twisted graphene:** [Signatures of a ferro-Josephson effect in twisted graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25257) — Sharp resistance peak near B∥=0 from current-driven precession of spin-domain-wall moments; and related metastable domain study in [twisted double bilayer graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25263).

**Majorana physics:** [Topologically protected perfect crossed Andreev reflection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26702) — Flux-tunable four-terminal junction achieves 100% CAR through exact destructive interference; [Vortex-core Majorana coupling to a chiral edge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02260) shows nonmonotonic reorganization with vortex-boundary separation.

**Environment effects:** [Interaction effects on Andreev states from an electromagnetic environment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01935) — General formulas for interaction corrections; corrections can become comparable to unperturbed energies even for weak coupling.

**Bogoliubov Fermi surfaces:** [Altermagnetism Induced Bogoliubov Fermi Surfaces in Topological Superconductivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20049) — Altermagnetic topological insulator + s-wave pairing yields facet-dependent BFSs enabling distinct Majorana zero mode types.

**Critical-current spectroscopy:** [Superconducting gap in covalent bismuth dihydride BiH₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12062) — Pulsed current measurements at 157–176 GPa reveal two-scale s-wave gap structure (≈6.9 and 1.5 meV); Migdal-Eliashberg attributes to anisotropy.

**Chern boundary criticality:** [Chern insulator boundary criticality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18199) — Chiral edge modes delocalize at criticality but retain chiral structure; parity-odd correlation terms encode anomaly coefficients.
