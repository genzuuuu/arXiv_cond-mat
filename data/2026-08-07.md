[Emergent Surface Altermagnetism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05529)
- **Correlated topological-polarization surface states** — FeSb₂ thin films show metallic polar surface states whose nonreciprocal transport is controlled by bulk correlation-driven orbital reconstruction; gating induces a quantum phase transition. [Correlated topological-polarization surface states in FeSb2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05887)
- **1D Dirac modes in pentagonal nanowires** — Twin-plane-stabilized helical crossings in SnTe-class nanowires produce spatially separated core and surface Dirac modes, observable at ~50 nm thickness. [1D Dirac modes in pentagonal topological crystalline insulator nanowires](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05908)
- **Deconfined criticality to nodal d-wave SC** — QMC study finds second-order transition between Néel insulator and d-wave superconductor using parton representation with SU(2) gauge field; gapless Dirac electron dispersion in the SC phase. [Deconfined criticality between AFM insulator and nodal d-wave SC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00762)
- **Anyon-impurity bound states** — Hybrid composites form between mobile impurities and pinned quasiholes in fractional Chern insulators; binding energy reveals fractional charge; offers braiding protocols. [Anyon-Impurity Bound States in Fractional Chern Insulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06233)
- **Quantum Hall interface effective string theory** — Three-paper series develops worldsheet frameworks for dynamical QH interfaces: kinematics (I), Majorana transport on fluctuating Moore-Read worldsheets (II), and open-worldsheet/brane constructions (III). [Part I](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06097), [Part II](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06098), [Part III](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06100)
