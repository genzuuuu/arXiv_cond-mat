## Strongly Correlated Systems & Quantum Magnetism

**PT-Symmetric Multichannel Kondo Systems**: An exact Bethe Ansatz solution reveals four impurity phases (overscreened Kondo, zero mode, YSR, local moment) in PT-symmetric multichannel Kondo models. Notably, impurity entropy becomes non-monotonic in zero-mode and local-moment phases, showing that a real spectrum doesn't guarantee RG irreversibility. [Breakdown of Monotonic Impurity Entropy Flow in PT-Symmetric Multichannel Kondo Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04083)

**Field-Induced Dimensional Reduction in Quantum Antiferromagnets**: In botallackite Cu₂(OH)₃Br, an applied magnetic field polarizes FM chains and suppresses transverse AFM fluctuations, effectively reducing the 2D system to 1D AFM Heisenberg behavior—observable via two-spinon signatures in neutron scattering. [Magnetic-Field-Driven Dimensional Reduction in a Quantum Antiferromagnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04096)

**Quantum Spin Liquid Under Uniaxial Pressure**: Muon spin relaxation in 3D QSL candidate PbCuTe₂O₆ under [Fate of moir\'e flat bands for a weakly repulsive Bose-Einstein condensate in one-dimensional $\mathcal{PT}$-symmetric bichromatic optical lattices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01680) compression reveals enhanced quasi-static correlations without conventional long-range order, suggesting a structurally modified strongly correlated state. [Lifting the degeneracy of quantum spin liquid phase by uniaxial pressure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04782)

**La₃Ni₂O₇ Pairing Symmetry**: Mirror-symmetry analysis of ARPES/STM gap data shows dominant dₓ₂₋ᵧ₂ orbital pairing with nearly isotropic nodeless gap—compatible with Hund's-rule-driven pairing mechanisms. [Orbital-Selective Diagonal-Gap Test of Pairing in La₃Ni₂O₇](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29470)

## Computational Methods & Machine Learning

**GW Perturbation Theory**: Including GW corrections to both band structure and electron-phonon couplings yields 11% mean error for mobilities across Si, GaAs, GaP, diamond, SiC—neglecting them causes >50% errors. [First-principles predictions of carrier mobility using GW perturbation theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04219)

**Symbolic Regression for GW**: Compact analytical functions approximate the GW self-energy from Kohn-Sham descriptors, enabling GW calculations for thousands of atoms at DFT cost. [Electron correlation via symbolic regression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04291)

**Monte Carlo Diagonalization**: Truncated Fock spaces via Monte Carlo construction break the ~20-site limit of exact diagonalization for the Hubbard model. [Monte Carlo Diagonalization for Hubbard Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22975)

**Delocalized Coupled-Cluster Theory**: Translationally invariant CC theory for polarons achieves O(N³) cost, accurately describes Holstein/SSH/Fröhlich models and LiF polaron binding energies. [Delocalized Coupled-Cluster Theory for Polaron Structure and Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04979)

**2PI Vertex Reconstruction**: Monte Carlo reconstruction of the two-particle-irreducible vertex for φ⁴ theory reveals multidimensional soft sector at Ising criticality; parquet equations with local contact vertex reproduce self-energy to <0.1% accuracy. [Two-particle-irreducible vertex of the 2D lattice φ⁴ model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04497)

**Ghost-RISB for Electron-Phonon**: Extended ghost-RISB method handles Hubbard-Holstein model at DMFT accuracy with a fraction of cost, revealing Franck-Condon-like superconducting suppression. [Ghost-RISB for Correlated Electron-Phonon Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04816)

**C09x-PBEc Functional**: New exchange-correlation functional combines C09 exchange with PBE correlation—GGA efficiency with accurate ferroelectric properties; MLIPs trained on it reproduce PbTiO₃ transition temperature. [Exchange-Correlation Functional for Ferroelectric Perovskites](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04806)
