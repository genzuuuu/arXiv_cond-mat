# Today's arXiv Condensed Matter Physics — Key Advances

## Topological Band Theory & Invariants
Several papers recast Chern/$\mathbb{Z}_2$ topology in terms of wavefunction zeros and overlaps. Hwang et al. show crystalline symmetries enforce overlap zeros with fixed vorticities, and that dispersion shifts let you read them off without measuring phases ([Chern Numbers from Symmetry-Enforced Zeros and Dispersion Shifts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25028)). Paiva et al. unify relative Chern and $\mathbb{Z}_2$ invariants via zeros of the overlap-matrix determinant, recovering the Kane-Mele Pfaffian as a special case ([Topological (in)equivalence encoded in zeros of wavefunction overlaps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25037)). Jezequel & Mora extend spectral localizers to a phase-space framework covering higher-order, semimetal, and valley modes ([Spectral localizers in phase space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26160)), while Venderbos derives a gauge-invariant, quantum-metric-based orbital magnetic susceptibility ([Geometric formulation of the orbital magnetic susceptibility](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26356)).

## Fractional Chern Insulators & Anyons
Herzog-Arbeitman et al. construct exact parent Hamiltonians with onsite Hubbard interactions for Laughlin, Read-Rezayi, and Halperin states, proving FCI ground states exist beyond ideal bands ([Exact Fractional Chern Insulators from Hubbard Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25010)). Castro et al. give an operator-based construction of maximally localized anyon coherent states in non-ideal bands ([Anyon Coherent States in Fractional Chern Insulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25034)). Wang et al. find a numerically evidenced Moore-Read-to-Laughlin transition in a six-site Kekulé model ([Interaction-driven phase transition between bosonic Moore-Read and Laughlin states](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25633)). Graham et al. tackle strong Landau-level mixing at $\nu=3/2$ with a momentum-resolved neural-network wavefunction, finding $\sqrt{2}$ quantum dimension and favoring the anti-Pfaffian ([Non-Abelian quantum Hall states at strong Landau level mixing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18967)).

## Altermagnetism & Chiral Magnetism
Dannegger et al. compute hematite's magnon spectrum ab initio, revealing a hybrid g-wave/i-wave splitting structure distinct from hexagonal altermagnets ([Altermagnetic magnon splitting in hematite](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25284)). Jaeschke-Ubiergo et al. show KV$_2$Se$_2$O hosts emergent $d$-wave *surface* altermagnetism, reconciling neutron and photoemission data ([Emergence and Detection of Surface altermagnetism in KV$_2$Se$_2$O](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03551)). Ishii et al. observe scattering circular dichroism from spatially modulated anisotropic magnetic dipoles in BiFeO$_3$'s spin cycloid ([Scattering Circular Dichroism from Spatially Modulated Anisotropic Magnetic Dipoles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25546)). Kuchkin & Kiselev establish stability regions for higher-order hopfions in uniaxial chiral magnets ([Higher-order magnetic hopfions in uniaxial chiral magnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25883)).

## Superconductivity: Mechanisms & Platforms
Leraand & Sudbø find magnon-mediated nodal $p$-wave topological superconductivity in $p$-wave magnets, with Umklapp processes sharply raising $T_c$ near the zone boundary ([Umklapp-enhanced high-$T_c$ topological superconductivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25015)). Mikheeva & König show Majorana hybridization can drive the vortex-unbinding transition first order ([First order vortex-unbinding transition in 2D topological superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25018)). Varada et al. find Majorana flat bands inevitably spawn pair-density-wave or phase-crystal states ([Nonuniform superconducting states from Majorana flat bands](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03859)). Kubo resolves Nb's low-temperature superheating field microscopically (~290 mT, ~67 MV/m for a TESLA cavity) ([Superheating field of clean superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10420)). Naveen & Soori predict gate-tunable 0–$\pi$ transitions and an anomalous Josephson effect in bipolar magnetic semiconductors—without a diode effect ([Josephson effect in bipolar magnetic semiconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24322)).

## Quantum Spin Liquids, Scars & Localization
Sharma & Pandey find exponentially many exact zero modes and PXP-like scars in the spin-1 Kitaev chain ([Quantum many-body scars and zero modes in the spin-1 Kitaev chain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25051)). Cai et al. report disorder-free many-body localization in AgCrSe$_2$, tied to flat phonon bands and off-center Ag displacements ([Disorder-free many-body localization in real quantum materials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17591)). Pascual & Van Houcke map the attractive lattice polaron phase diagram and sharpen the instability criterion for Nagaoka ferromagnetism ([Phase diagram of the attractive lattice Fermi polaron](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26304)).

## Quantum Information & Simulation

Chi et al. introduce neural Gaussian PEPS with $O(N^{3/2})$ contraction, reaching state-of-the-art Hubbard energies at 1024 sites ([Neural Gaussian Tensor Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26604)). Martyn et al. propose "dynamical averaging" to make NQS observables converge quadratically like the energy ([High-Accuracy Neural-Network Quantum States via Randomized Real-Time Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26587)). Warman & Schafer-Nameki bypass the Bravyi-König bound, realizing transversal gates at any Clifford-hierarchy level ([The Clifford Hierarchy from Clifford Stabilizers in 2D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25025)). Aitchison et al. show disordered-noise decoding is governed by noise-distribution entropy and Bhattacharyya coefficients ([When the Noise Gets Noisy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26506)).
