# Condensed Matter Physics Papers Summary (arXiv)

## Superconductivity & Topological Superconductors

- [Topological Superconductors in Doubly-Coupled Nanowires with Altermagnetism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13265): Theoretical prediction of four distinct topological superconducting phases in altermagnet-superconductor nanowires, including class D, class BDI, and two phases with multiple Majorana zero modes per end, controlled by superconducting phase difference and inter-wire coupling.

- [Finite-momentum coupling of Higgs and Bardasis–Schrieffer modes in superconductors with competing pairing channels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12461): Shows via Nambu-Keldysh quasiclassical framework that finite momentum and particle-hole asymmetry generate direct coupling between Higgs and BS modes; clean limit prevents avoided crossing, but disorder can enable it.

- [Inductively-protected Andreev (IPA) spin qubit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13530): Proposes shunting an Andreev spin qubit with an inductor to separate spin-qubit states into distinct potential wells, combining long coherence times of protected qubits with spin degree of freedom advantages.

- [Floquet Quasiparticle Poisoning of Frozonium](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12454): Shows that dynamically frozen fluxonium circuits do not suppress quasiparticle-induced dissipation; identifies drive-assisted Cooper-pair breaking and resonant hybridization effects.

- [Nonrelativistic-Ising superconductivity in p-wave magnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19829): Theory demonstrating that p-wave magnets support only Ising superconductivity with 50:50 singlet-triplet mixing, contrasting with altermagnets.

- [Yttrium Superhydrides Revisited](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11428): Combined experimental and theoretical study of YH₆, YH₉, and YH₁₀ at 140-213 GPa; finds Tc ≈ 218-237 K for YH₆/YH₉ and argues against room-temperature superconductivity in binary yttrium superhydrides.

- [The nature of the "pseudogap" in the insulating phase of highly disordered superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12508): Tunneling spectroscopy on a-InO films plus QMC simulations reveal single-particle gap persists and grows with disorder deep in insulating phase, confirming Cooper-pair insulating state.

- [Thermal Hall tomography of chiral superconductivity in rhombohedral graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12586): Proposes thermal Hall conductance as direct readout of Bogoliubov-de Gennes Chern number in rhombohedral graphene chiral superconductors.

- [Topological invariant responsible for the integer QHE and non-commutative geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08868): Expresses Hall conductivity quantization via topological invariant paired with cyclic cohomology.

## Fractional Quantum Hall & Non-Abelian Anyons

- [Observation of Time-Domain Braiding of Non-Abelian Anyons at ν = 5/2 State](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12897): Reports experimental evidence for non-Abelian anyons via time-domain braiding signatures from partition noise measurements of both downstream Abelian and upstream non-Abelian modes in the particle-hole Pfaffian order.

- [The "Moiré Capacitor Effect" and Stabilization of Fractional Chern Insulators in Rhombohedral Graphene Superlattices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12452): Identifies moiré capacitor effect enhancing moiré potential, stabilizing C=1 parent state and FCIs at ν=2/3 via inter-band fluctuations.

## Graphene & Moiré Systems

- [Emergent trans-moiré orbitals and topology in rhombohedral graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12478): STM imaging reveals trans-moiré orbitals on the distant side of the moiré interface in rhombohedral hexalayer graphene/hBN, explaining FQAHE conditions and vanishing at θ ≳ 1°.

- [Resonant Far-Infrared Spectroscopy of Flat-Band Fermions in Magic Angle Graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12553): First observation of FIR resonances in MATBG using millikelvin platform; topological heavy-fermion model explains light-heavy fermion hybridization and optical selection rules.

## Quantum Magnetism & Spin Systems

- [Dipolar-driven mean-field criticality in the ferrimagnet Eu₂MnSi₂O₇](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12639): Reports first insulating ferrimagnet where long-range dipolar interactions drive mean-field critical behavior, with Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya tilted configuration.

- [Magnetism in antiperovskite (Li₂M)ChO diluted magnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13111): Study of magnetic evolution at fixed 1/3 filling in diluted magnetic sublattice; magnetic anisotropy is key parameter controlling TN (30-90 K).

- [Landau theory and exchange instabilities in Mn₅Si₃: A case against altermagnetism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13483): Landau theory and first-principles argue Mn₅Si₃'s altermagnetic phase is unlikely stabilized without additional physics.

- [Emergence of moiré magnetic chaos in twisted bilayer CrI₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13062): Micromagnetic simulations reveal undriven, autonomous magnetic chaos from interlayer exchange frustration, with extreme initial-state sensitivity.

- [Nonlinear Magnon Magnetic Moment Transport in Triangular-Lattice f-Wave Antialtermagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22614): Spin excitations in 120° ground state carry out-of-plane magnetic moment; predicts Edelstein and spin-splitter effects.
