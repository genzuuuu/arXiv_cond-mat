# Condensed Matter Physics Papers Summary (arXiv)

## Topological & Quantum Spin Liquids

**[Exact quantum spin liquids with topological order on maple-leaf and trellis lattices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04319)** — Constructs spin models with bond-dependent anisotropic interactions yielding exact ℤ₂ quantum spin liquids (Kitaev-like) on maple-leaf and trellis lattices. Using replica exchange QMC, authors map topological phase diagrams, characterize flux sectors and Chern numbers, and identify transitions between ℤ₂ and Ising topological orders.

**[Gapped topological spin-orbital liquid on the honeycomb lattice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06549)** — Large-scale DMRG simulations of the SU(4) Heisenberg model on honeycomb lattice show a gapped spin-orbital liquid with Z₄ topological order, evidenced by topological entanglement entropy near ln(4) and absence of symmetry breaking.

**[Symmetry-protected triplet Weyl complexes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05120)** — Systematic screening of all 1651 magnetic space groups identifies symmetry conditions for triplet Weyl complexes (TWCs). Reports first electronic realization of a {1,1,2}-TWC in chiral carbon allotrope DZQH-C₃₆, with characteristic "S"-shaped surface Fermi arcs.

**[General Construction from Topological Loop States to Topological Invariants in Exactly Flat Bands](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24672)** — Introduces a new topological condition for flat bands that removes band-touching singularities and enables well-defined topological invariants (Chern numbers, ℤ₂ invariants), yielding "top²-flat bands" that flow to correlated topological insulators under interactions.

## Nickelate Superconductivity

**[Layer Architecture Shapes Electronic, Magnetic, and Lattice Interactions in Ruddlesden-Popper Nickelates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04532)** — RIXS comparison of La₃Ni₂O₇ polymorphs shows the alternating monolayer-trilayer structure shares similar excitations with trilayer La₄Ni₃O₁₀, while bilayer exhibits distinct weaker orbital polarization, reduced magnetic exchange, and stronger electron-phonon coupling. An entangled-dimer scenario best describes spin excitations.

**[Ni-O hybridization as a stabilizer for s± superconductivity in La₃Ni₂O₇](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05185)** — Full-spectrum DFT+RPA models including states away from the Fermi level enhance interlayer spin fluctuations, favoring a sign-changing s± superconducting state over the d-wave pairing predicted by low-energy models.

**[Field-angle dependence of magnetoresistance in UTe₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17235)** — Boltzmann transport theory on a GGA+U Wannier model reproduces experimentally observed angular magnetoresistance oscillations in UTe₂, confirming warped quasi-2D Fermi surfaces with a dominant hole band having long relaxation time.

**[Thermodynamic Evidence of Tetracritical Topology in the H-T Phase Diagram of UTe₂ for H ∥ b](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25896)** — Ultrasound velocity measurements provide bulk thermodynamic evidence for an additional high-field phase boundary near 14–15 T, completing local tetracritical topology and supporting field-induced multicomponent superconductivity.

## Quantum Geometry & Flat Bands

**[The influence of quantum geometry on the phase boundary and collective excitations of electron liquids and crystals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04330)** — TDHF analysis of the λ-jellium model shows quantum geometry favors electron crystallization, reduces plasmon dispersion, and suppresses Friedel oscillations via spectral weight transfer to inter-orbital modes.

**[Interlayer Exciton Condensate Stiffness Is Non-Monotonic in Quantum Metric](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04729)** — Exact diagonalization across flat Chern bands reveals superfluid stiffness peaks when band wavefunctions resemble Landau levels. Identical quantum geometry tensors can yield substantially different stiffness values.

**[Robustness of RKKY interactions across a Weyl node-annihilation transition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05219)** — Derives analytical expressions for the full RKKY exchange tensor showing RKKY interactions remain persistent across a Weyl node-annihilation transition, mediated by global Brillouin-zone-integrated quantum metric rather than local Berry curvature monopoles.

**[Breakdown of Anomalous Hall Scaling in Dilute Kondo System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05319)** — Reports intrinsic Berry curvature-driven anomalous Hall transport in non-f-electron Heusler alloy Ru₂Mn₀.₅Ti₀.₅Ge, with non-monotonic AHE violating Fermi-liquid scaling near magnetic transition, restored only with Kondo coherence onset.

## Spin Textures & Magnonics

**[Bias-field control of the Néel skyrmion nonlinearity in a confined nanostructure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04972)** — Shows bias magnetic field tunes both oscillation frequency and nonlinearity coefficient of skyrmion oscillators, with the nonlinearity reversing sign at a critical field — useful for neuromorphic applications.

**[Customized spin spirals in ferromagnetic thin films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05383)** — Demonstrates oblique-incidence deposition to create tunable vertical spin spirals at room temperature in single films via crossed uniaxial surface anisotropies, confirmed by polarized neutron reflectometry.

**[In silico design of magnonic lasing in constricted waveguides](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02338)** — Micromagnetic simulations show double-hole cavity formation with analogue event horizons in constricted ferromagnetic waveguides, enabling resonant spin-wave amplification. DMI can substantially reduce critical current density.

**[Emergent odd viscoelasticity in chiral soft glassy materials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04693)** — Introduces a chiral soft glassy rheology model predicting odd viscosity growing as active rotation frequency decreases, with odd viscoelastic spectra showing resonance around ω = 2Ω combined with glassy power laws.

## Spintronics & Magnetotransport

**[Room-temperature field-free SOT logic device based on Fe₃GaTe₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04600)** — First room-temperature, field-free van der Waals magnet spin logic device showing critical switching current of 1.8×10⁷ A/cm² and potential ultralow write power of 1.5 fJ/bit at 1 ns.

**[Evidence of Crystal-Field-Mediated Anisotropic Orbital Rashba Effect in Epitaxial Ru/FM Heterostructure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04642)** — Decomposes orbital torque into isotropic bulk and anisotropic interfacial contributions, showing interfacial crystal-field coherency is key for engineering orbital Rashba textures.

**[Challenges in orbital current-driven domain wall motion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04811)** — Despite finite damping-like torques in Mn/GFC and Ti/GFC, no current-driven DW motion observed; weak torque conversion and insufficient chiral DW stabilization identified as key challenges for orbital-driven devices.

**[Cross-Layer Anomalous Hall Transport driven by Néel-Vector rotating in the Altermagnet candidate V₂Te₂O](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05085)** — First-principles calculations reveal giant cross-layer anomalous Hall conductivity (~255 S/cm) activated by rotating Néel vector in van der Waals altermagnet, exceeding in-plane response by two orders of magnitude.

**[Generalized Helicity-Dependent Magnetization Switching via Substrate Phonons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05064)** — Demonstrates substrate-mediated phononic magnetization switching across six point groups, showing efficiency governed by full heterostructure optical response rather than substrate absorption alone.
