# Condensed Matter Physics arXiv Summary (August 2025)

## Superconductivity & Strongly Correlated Systems

**[Exotic superconductivity in the doped Kitaev quantum spin liquid](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03024)** — Using many-variable variational Monte Carlo, this work reveals robust triplet p-wave superconductivity coexisting with ferromagnetism in doped Kitaev spin liquids for ferromagnetic Kitaev interactions. For antiferromagnetic interactions, pairing changes from triplet p-wave at low doping to singlet d+id at intermediate doping.

**[Large scale neural quantum states reveal the interplay between superconductivity and quantum criticality in the Hofstadter-Hubbard model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02753)** — Neural quantum states on up to 432 sites provide strong evidence for a continuous topological phase transition in the triangular-lattice Hofstadter-Hubbard model. Upon doping, a topological superconductor with d+id pairing emerges, with superfluid stiffness strongly enhanced near the critical point. The energy scale is set by proximity to the transition rather than the parent state.

**[Pressure-induced Superconductivity in Thermoelectric Semiconductor Mg3Sb2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03058)** — Reports discovery of pressure-induced superconductivity in Mg3Sb2, with metallization at 8.7 GPa and a dome-shaped Tc peaking at 3.3 K at 12.6 GPa, accompanied by a structural phase transition and carrier-type crossover.

**[Pressure induced magnetic-field-free superconducting diode effect in NbSe2 flake](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03072)** — Demonstrates that pressure alone can break inversion symmetry and induce a magnetic-field-free superconducting diode effect in NbSe2 without heterostructures, with even-in-B behavior implying absence of explicit time-reversal symmetry breaking.

**[Two-phonon pairing and superconductivity in SrTiO3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03823)** — Explores whether Ngai's two-phonon pairing mechanism explains superconductivity in doped STO. Despite significant electron-phonon matrix elements, phase space considerations reduce the effective coupling. The theory predicts monotonically increasing Tc with doping, inconsistent with experiments.

**[Surface-Selective Probe of Spin-Triplet Superconductivity in Rhombohedral Graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03870)** — Uses one-sided WS2 proximity as a surface-selective spin-orbit probe in pentalayer rhombohedral graphene. The asymmetric suppression of superconducting pockets provides additional evidence for spin-triplet pairing.

**[Thermodynamic phase transition, pairing symmetry and Fermi surface topology in Ruddlesden-Popper nickelate films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03908)** — ARPES study of La2PrNi2O7/NdAlO3 heterostructures reveals a superconducting state without pseudogap, direct observation of nodeless order parameter, and electronic specific heat jump at Tc.

**[Correlation-renormalized spin-fluctuation pairing and the stabilization of s± superconductivity in pressurized La3Ni2O7](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11786)** — Combines DMFT with self-energy-renormalized RPA to show that strong correlations reverse the pairing hierarchy, stabilizing s± over dxy pairing in pressurized La3Ni2O7.

**[Evidence for superconductivity at 190 K in a pressure-overdoped cuprate](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00942)** — Reports Tc climbing continuously to 190 K at 60 GPa in Bi-2223 under quasihydrostatic conditions, potentially revealing a second superconducting regime at high hole doping.

**[Negative Differential Capacitance from Composition-Dependent Stern Capacitance in a Binary Mixture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03189)** — Develops thermodynamic theory of electric double layers where composition-dependent Stern capacitance leads to negative differential capacitance and voltage-induced first-order transitions.

## Quantum Spin Systems & Magnetism

**[Spin-charge separation in the triangular-lattice Hofstadter-Hubbard model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02727)** — Using NQS and PEPS, authors observe spin-charge separation directly in real space through numerical spin-pumping simulation and real-time dynamics in the putative chiral spin liquid regime.

**[Fractal deconfinement and confinement in Sierpinski ice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02741)** — Studies the six-vertex model on the Sierpinski gasket, finding entropic charge confinement in the ice model but fractal deconfinement in a four-vertex limit, with the string joining charges having fractal dimension ~1.3.

**[Tunable Skyrmions in a Topological Wigner Crystal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02832)** — Shows that competition between ferromagnetic exchange and chiral interaction on a triangular lattice stabilizes a zero-field skyrmion crystal with continuously tunable density. At large chiral coupling, a 24-site tetra-skyrmion crystal emerges.

**[Magnetic and crystal electric field excitations in a spin-orbit coupled frustrated hyperkagome magnet Nd3Li3W2O12](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03365)** — Comprehensive study of hyperkagome antiferromagnet revealing Kramers' doublet ground state with Jeff = 1/2 at low temperatures via magnetization, heat capacity, and INS.

**[Spin-Chirality-Driven Bulk Photovoltaic Effect in van der Waals Magnet CrSBr](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03492)** — Establishes vector spin chirality as a tunable knob for unconventional time-reversal-even magnetic bulk photovoltaic effect, including chiral shift and injection currents switchable by magnetic-field-induced spin canting.

**[Vector-field control and emergent basal-plane anisotropy of magnetic textures in (Fe0.63Ni0.3Pd0.07)3P](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03513)** — Combined SANS and ptychography reveals deterministic vector-field control of magnetic stripe domains and temperature-driven evolution of effective DMI landscape in this noncentrosymmetric room-temperature magnet.

**[Strain Tuning of Orbital-Driven Giant Magnetoresistance in van der Waals ferrimagnet Mn3Si2Te6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03568)** — Demonstrates that strain can control chiral orbital-current domains and electronic structure in Mn3Si2Te6, establishing a unified microscopic mechanism for its unconventional colossal magnetoresistance.

**[Local magnetic order in vacancy-disrupted spin ice Ho2TiO5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03850)** — Neutron scattering reveals that Ho moments retain local <111> Ising anisotropy forming spin-in/spin-out configurations even in vacancy-disrupted Ho2TiO5, with local ice-rule tendency persisting on a topologically incomplete magnetic network.

**[Emergence of Chiral Helimagnetic Order in Chromium-intercalated Tantalum Disulfide CrTa3S6 Powders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03953)** — Highly sensitive change in magnetic properties with Cr intercalation quantity x: chiral helimagnetism below x=0.996, ferromagnetism above 1.000.

**[Magnetoresistance in Magnetic Weyl semimetal Mn3ZnC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03414)** — Investigates MR in this antiperovskite magnetic nodal line semimetal, finding sign changes in MR curves attributed to drastic carrier mobility changes and unusual positive MR in FM state at low field.

## Topological Materials & Quantum Geometry

**[Quaternion-Kahler geometry of time reversal symmetric crystals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03178)** — Shows that Kramers pair band geometry is quaternionic, unifying quantum metric with SU(2) Berry curvature components. Non-negativity imposes local metric-curvature inequalities whose saturation defines non-Abelian ideal bands.

**[Broken site symmetry of Fe adatoms on Bi2Te3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02770)** — Combined STM and DFT reveal static Jahn-Teller distortion reducing adsorption symmetry from C3v to C1v for Fe adatoms on Bi2Te3(111), explaining reduced symmetry in dI/dV maps.

**[Chaos and Diffusion in Twisted Bilayer Graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02916)** — Numerical analysis shows finite systems at commensurate and incommensurate angles exhibit chaos. Mean free path scales with system size, indicating diffusive processes from border-induced scattering.

**[Symmetry-Guided Computational Screening of Two-Dimensional Altermagnets with ab initio Hubbard Corrections](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02925)** — High-throughput screening of 2710 2D materials identifies 24 robust altermagnetic candidates including metallic Fe2Si2SbO9 and insulating CoBrO with large spin splittings.

**[Emergence and Detection of Surface altermagnetism in KV2Se2O](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03551)** — Explains contradictory experimental evidence by showing the (001) surface exhibits d-wave altermagnetism while the bulk is antiferromagnetic. Predicts a large nonlinear Edelstein response localized at the surface.

**[Topological properties and phase diagram of the triangular Hofstadter model with staggered flux](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03278)** — Studies topological phases in a model relevant for twisted bilayer TMDs, finding rich phase diagrams with dependence on dimerization and magnetic flux.

**[Magnetic Breakdown and Anomalous Quantum Oscillation in Rhombohedral Tetralayer Graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27207)** — Identifies anomalous Shubnikov-de Haas effects from magnetic breakdown near Van Hove singularities, potentially explaining recent quantum oscillation experiments in rhombohedral tetralayer graphene.

**[Hidden Quantum Geometry in Bilayer Exciton Condensates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03049)** — Reveals that excitonic order generates nontrivial hidden quantum geometric effects in correlated electron-hole bands, manifesting in characteristic out-of-plane polarization response to in-plane AC fields.

## Quantum Criticality & Non-Equilibrium Physics

**[Universal crossovers in weakly-monitored quantum critical states](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02716)** — Studies post-measurement ensembles of critical and tricritical 1D Ising chains. Critical Ising flows to projective-measurement fixed point with area-law entanglement, while tricritical Ising shows a weak-measurement fixed point with logarithmic entanglement.

**[Two-Parameter Ansatz for the Violation of Eigenstate Thermalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02744)** — Introduces a two-parameter ansatz distinguishing "fading ergodicity" from "trapped ergodicity" for ETH violation. Finds ETH violation in J1-J2 chains is consistent with trapped ergodicity.

**[Nonlocality-induced critical-length hierarchy from non-Hermitian competition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02746)** — Shows long-range hoppings fundamentally reorganize critical behavior in non-Hermitian lattices, producing hierarchy of scaling laws beyond the conventional logarithmic law. Identifies two novel nonlocal mechanisms.

**[Scale-covariant liquid in nonlocal high Tc strange metals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03013)** — Proposes that poorly screened effective repulsions with distance-dependent exponent alpha naturally interpolate between Mott insulating and Fermi liquid limits, explaining scale-covariant self-energy without invoking quantum criticality.

**[Many-body cages: disorder-free glassiness from flat bands in Fock space, and many-body Rabi oscillations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13086)** — Introduces many-body caging as a novel mechanism for nonthermal behavior, with eigenstates localized on subgraphs of the many-body state graph, creating flat bands and glassy eigenspectrum order without disorder.

**[Strong Quantum Mpemba Effect from Exact Slow-Mode Selection in Constrained Rydberg Chains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17975)** — Identifies exact slow-mode selection mechanism in dephased constrained Rydberg chains where the Hamiltonian itself becomes an exact Liouvillian eigenmode, enabling strong quantum Mpemba acceleration for translationally invariant states.

## Quantum Information & Computation

**[Typical Output States of Monitored Random Clifford Circuits: A Graph-Theoretic Approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03102)** — Develops graph-state framework showing random stabilizer states converge to Erdős-Rényi graph ensemble. Resolves GHZ entanglement problem (mean g3 = 1.204 for even N) and locates MIPT critical point at pc = 0.1608.

**[Dynamic Induction of Lattice Gauge Theories on a Quantum Computer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02756)** — Introduces paradigm where Gauss's law dynamically generates gauge theory from simpler Hamiltonian. Implements on 101 qubits with 5x reduction in entangling-gate depth per Trotter step.

**[Computational complexity of Berry phase estimation in topological phases of matter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13423)** — Proves BQP-completeness of Berry phase estimation with guiding state, establishes UQMA ∩ co-UQMA-completeness without guiding state, and provides new quantum algorithm with theoretical guarantees.

**[Entangling Topological Invariants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03634)** — Characterizes obstructions to local tensor-product descriptions in occupied multiplets, showing momentum-dependent mixing adds Gauss-Codazzi curvature and global factorization requires C2 divisible by gcd(p,q).

## Strongly Correlated & Frustrated Systems

**[Magneto-oscillations, nonlinearity, and nonreciprocity of Coulomb drag in quantum circuits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02812)** — Derives closed expression for linear drag conductance in coupled quantum point contacts, investigating oscillatory response as function of gate voltage and magnetic field.

**[Lifshitz transitions and isospin polarization in twist-decoupled monolayer-bilayer graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02899)** — Uses MLG to bias proximal BLG without displacement field, revealing three-fold degenerate quantum Hall states and spontaneous broken symmetry near valence band edge.

**[Disorder-Induced Entanglement Phase Transitions in Non-Hermitian Systems with Skin Effects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.12342)** — Shows area-law entanglement in pristine Hatano-Nelson model develops into logarithmic scaling for small disorder, then reenters area-law through an entanglement phase transition.

## Computational Methods & Machine Learning

**[AccelNet: Exact backward-compatible acceleration of polynomial angular descriptors through Cartesian moment factorization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03280)** — Presents exact method for accelerating existing neural-network potentials without retraining by exploiting hidden finite-rank structure of angular terms. Reproduces descriptors to floating-point roundoff.

**[Physics-Informed and Knowledge-Driven Generative AI for Autonomous Discovery of Porous Oxide Energy Materials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02858)** — Roadmap for advancing generative AI beyond crystal generation toward physics-informed inverse design, identifying the "Missing Data Problem" as fundamental bottleneck.

**[Machine Learning Bandgap Prediction of Nanoporous Graphenes with Water](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03680)** — Combines DFT, AIMD, and interpretable ML to reveal how water dipole orientation, distance, and ribbon-resolved dipole moments control bandgap modulation in nanoporous graphenes.

**[Machine Learning-Guided Screening of Advantageous Solvents for Solid Polymer Electrolytes in Lithium Metal Batteries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03688)** — Universal criterion linking electronic and macroscopic properties via ML on ~10,000-solvent dataset identifies two optimal solvents. Experimental validation achieves 4.5V window and 86.7% capacity retention over 500 cycles.

**[Physics-Informed Machine Learning for Refractory Alloy Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03805)** — Combines quantile gradient boosting with Born stability constraints to predict eight mechanical properties of refractory CCAs with R² = 0.89-0.97 and 100% Born stability compliance. Establishes design rules: VEC = 6.0-6.4, δ < 10%, Re-Mo-Cr systems.

**[Guided Synthesis of EMT Zeolites by Machine Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03760)** — ML models trained on 174 synthesis attempts predict EMT formation conditions. Experimental validation confirms EMT crystallization in 5 of 6 predicted conditions.

## 2D Materials & Excitons

**[Alloy engineering of excitonic properties in TMD monolayers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03347)** — Systematic study of MoS2xSe2(1-x) alloys showing continuous optical gap tuning over ~0.35 eV, with circular polarization increasing from ~0% in MoSe2 to ~15% in MoS2.

**[Manipulation of localized excitons in CrPS4 by temperature and magnetic field](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03543)** — Many-body perturbation theory plus experiments reveal multiple spin-allowed excitons and spin-flip excitations localized on Cr³⁺ ions, with temperature-dependent redistribution providing optical signatures of magnetic phase transitions.

**[Toward two dimensional MoS2 electrets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03602)** — Sulfur vacancy engineering converts monolayer MoS2 into an electret storing quasi-permanent electrostatic charge (up to 1 μC/cm²) with retention of hundreds of days, creating co-localized optical and electrostatic contrast.

**[Excitonic structure in CsPbBr3 nanocubes, nanorods and nanoplatelets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16142)** — Variational effective mass model captures quantum confinement, dielectric confinement, correlations, and polaronic effects, showing radiative rates speed up from cubes to platelets to rods.

**[Magnetic-field control of Fermi polaron fine structure and polarization in strained monolayer semiconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03853)** — Theory of attractive Fermi polaron g-factor renormalization showing continuous tuning of energy splitting and polarization via strain, magnetic field, and density control.

## Phonons & Thermal Transport

**[Anisotropic Phonon Heat Flow and Thermoelectric Response in Tetragonal GeS2 and GeSe2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03478)** — DFT-based study reveals pronounced lattice-transport anisotropy with in-plane thermal conductivity 22x larger than cross-plane. Peak zT = 0.257 for n-type cross-plane GeS2 at 800K.

**[Exact Resonances Are Not Sufficient for Phonon Energy Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03180)** — Shows that exact resonance matching, nonzero interaction coefficients, and network connectivity don't guarantee persistent energy diffusion. Symmetry-enforced balance relations drive resonant collision currents to nonthermal zero-flux states.

**[Advances in Phonons: From Band Topology to Phonon Chirality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06179)** — Review of topological and circularly polarized phonons, presenting unified framework for classifying phonon modes and discussing Weyl phonons with simultaneous topological and rotational chirality.

**[Contrasting anisotropic electron-phonon-spin coupling in Fe3GeTe2 and Fe5GeTe2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03193)** — Helicity-resolved Raman spectroscopy reveals chiral mode splitting doesn't track magnetization, indicating spin-orbit-coupled electronic interactions beyond simple time-reversal breaking.

**[Polytype-Dependent Upconversion Photoluminescence in 3R-MoS2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03780)** — First observation of upconversion PL in rhombohedral MoS2 via exciton-exciton annihilation. The intensity is strongly polytype-dependent, providing sensitive probe of stacking order.

**[Enhancement of far-field thermal emission via polaritonic cavity modes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03621)** — Demonstrates tunable far-field thermal channel from thermally excited guided modes, with SiO2-covered silicon cavities enhancing emissivity by up to 200% near polaritonic resonance.
