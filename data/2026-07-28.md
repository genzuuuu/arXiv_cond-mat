### Superconductivity and Topological Matter

*   **Pair-Density Wave from Altermagnetism:** A study using large-scale DMRG shows that doping an altermagnetic Mott insulator in the checkerboard $t$-$J$ model leads to a pair-density wave (PDW) superconductivity with unconventional wave-vector locking. [Pair-Density Wave from Doping an Altermagnetic Mott Insulator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23654)
*   **Engineering Non-Abelian States:** A differentiable framework using gradient-based exact diagonalization is introduced to optimize Haldane pseudopotentials. This successfully designs a two-body interaction that stabilizes the non-Abelian Moore-Read state with over 99% overlap. [Engineering two-body interaction for the Moore-Read State](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23695)
*   **Non-Reciprocal Critical Dynamics:** A field-theoretic RG analysis shows that non-reciprocal couplings between two order parameters are RG-irrelevant near criticality, meaning the critical behavior is governed by the equilibrium Model A universality class. [Non-Reciprocal yet Equilibrium Critical Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24252)
*   **Magnetic-Field-Induced Superconductivity:** Magnetotransport measurements on UTe₂ reveal that a field-induced superconducting phase emerges from a strange metallic state with Planckian linear resistivity, suggesting a shared mechanism. [Strange metallicity encompasses high magnetic field-induced superconductivity in UTe2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12131)
*   **Chiral Superconductivity in Kagome Metals:** A theoretical study shows that loop-current order in kagome metals induces nematic chiral $d$-wave superconductivity, where the orbital magnetization stabilizes a chiral channel and explains how impurities suppress this state. [Nematic and chiral superconductivity emerging within the loop-current phase in kagome metals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04433)
*   **Flat Bands from Graph Topology:** A prescription for constructing exact flat bands on the faces of arbitrary graphs is introduced, protected by local graph topology and leading to compact localized states. [New class of exactly flat topological bands - compact localised states protected by local graph topology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22831)
*   **Spectral Topology of Domain Walls:** A study on 1D non-Hermitian models in a ring geometry with two domains shows that eigenstate localization originates from differences in spectral winding numbers, leading to new types of traveling-wave skin modes. [Spectral Topology and Non-Bloch Band Theory for Domain-Wall Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22976)
*   **Non-Abelian Anyon Statistics via DC Conductance:** An X-shaped chiral Majorana interferometer is proposed to directly detect non-Abelian statistics through the DC conductance tensor, which is nonzero only when the fermionic fusion channel is activated. [DC Conductance of X-shaped Majorana Interferometer reveals Non-Abelian Anyon Statistics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24171)
*   **Hidden Topology and Quantum Metric Bounds:** A dimension-reduction framework establishes a nonzero lower bound on the quantum metric integral (QMI) from 1D topological obstructions, even when 2D topology is trivial. [Hidden topology and strong quantum metric bounds in trivial systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24208)
*   **Berry Curvature Effects in Chiral Graphene:** The Berry curvature and anomalous thermal Hall/Nernst transport of quasiparticles in chiral superconducting rhombohedral graphene are studied, showing that the Bogoliubov Fermi surface modulates these effects. [Berry curvature effects of chiral superconducting rhombohedral graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23764)
*   **Probing Topological Magnon Edge States:** An all-optical THz spectroscopy protocol is proposed to detect topological magnon edge modes in 2D ferromagnets via parametric amplification and magnetoelectric coupling. [Signatures of Topological Magnon Edge States in THz Spectroscopy and Cavity Response](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23170)
*   **Current Cross-Correlations for Majorana States:** A study proposes that current cross-correlations are a robust diagnostic for "poor man's Majorana" modes, capturing non-locality and showing stability around sweet spots. [Current cross-correlations as probes for poor man's Majorana states](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23498)
*   **Overdoped Cuprates and BCS Physics:** An analysis argues that overdoped cuprates can be described by a Fermi-liquid-based BCS theory at low energies, once material disorder is accounted for, predicting behavior for an ideal disorder-free sample. [Emergence of Fermi-liquid and BCS physics in overdoped cuprates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25767)
*   **Superconducting Proximity Effect:** A DMFT study of a strongly correlated Hubbard junction coupled to BCS leads reveals two gapped solutions (Mott-like and proximitized), separated by a first-order transition, with a correlated metal appearing at a $\pi$ phase bias. [Strongly correlated Josephson junction: proximity effect in the single-layer Hubbard model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14796)
*   **Anomalous Localization in Doped Topological Insulators:** An analytical framework for transport in magnetically doped 2D topological insulators predicts an anomalous localization regime with conductance scaling as $\ln {\cal G} \propto -\sqrt{L}$, in agreement with experiments on HgTe quantum wells. [Anomalous Localization in Magnetically Doped Two-Dimensional Topological Insulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24373)
*   **Topological Order from Anyon Condensation:** A study of anyon condensation in symmetry-enriched topological (SET) phases, with a focus on multifusion categories, shows that condensates can preserve global symmetry and provides explicit constructions. [Anyon Condensation In Symmetry-Enriched Topological Phases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24740)
*   **Topological Magnon-Phonon Hybridization:** A theoretical study of magnon-phonon coupling in a $d$-wave altermagnet predicts the emergence of hybrid quasiparticles with finite phonon angular momentum, leading to a phonon angular momentum splitter effect. [D-Wave Phonon Angular Momentum Texture in Altermagnets by Magnon-Phonon Hybridization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08357)
*   **Fermionic Entanglement and Rényi Scaling:** A model shows a Rényi-index-dependent change in the leading entanglement scaling, demonstrating that von Neumann and second Rényi entropies can scale differently. [Detection of a Rényi Index Dependent Transition in Entanglement Entropy Scaling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24533)

### Quantum Matter and Magnetism

*   **Vortex Nucleation in Supersolids:** Experiments and theory show that rotating a dipolar supersolid leads to synchronization of its crystal motion with an external drive, triggered by quantum vortex nucleation. [Synchronization in rotating supersolids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.11976)
*   **Correlated Transport in 2D CrS₂:** Catalyst-free CVD growth of layered 1T-CrS₂ reveals a robust ferromagnet with a topological Hall effect below 30 K, driven by electronic correlations and spin-orbit coupling. [Observation of correlation-driven topological transport and robust ferromagnetism in 2D CrS₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23625)
*   **Exciton Splitting in Soft Perovskites:** Polarization-resolved ultrafast spectroscopy on MAPbI₃ nanowalls reveals a ferroelastic excitonic splitting in the orthorhombic phase, with 45 meV energy separation and orthogonal selection rules. [Ferroelastic exciton splitting in hybrid perovskite nanowalls](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23051)
*   **Field-Tuned Fan Phase:** Single-crystal neutron diffraction of an Ising-like triangular antiferromagnet reveals a field-tuned incommensurate fan phase just below saturation, challenging simple XXZ models. [Field-tuned incommensurate fan phase in an Ising-like triangular antiferromagnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23173)
*   **E₈ Spectrum in a Quantum Magnet:** Inelastic neutron scattering in a quasi-2D quantum magnet Cu₂(OH)₃Br provides evidence for an emergent E₈ symmetry and massive bound state excitations near a field-induced dimensional reduction. [Observation of an emergent energy scale close to dimensional reduction in a quasi-two-dimensional quantum magnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23547)
*   **Interaction-Driven Electronic Ferroelectricity:** Scanning tunneling microscopy and ab initio calculations on bilayer 1T-TaSe₂ show that interlayer Coulomb interactions, not lattice distortions, can drive ferroelectricity. [Interaction-driven electronic ferroelectricity in van der Waals heterostructures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24087)
*   **Hybrid-Parity Sliding Multiferroics:** A new class of sliding multiferroics is proposed, coupling ferroelectric polarization to nonrelativistic spin splitting (NSS) components. In bilayer VBr₂, sliding switches the polarization and locks the signs of NSS components. [Hybrid-parity sliding multiferroics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24337)
*   **Magnetic Anisotropy in Fe₃GeTe₂:** Electron spin resonance on near-stoichiometric Fe₃GeTe₂ quantifies its strong uniaxial anisotropy, large magnon gap, and shows high sensitivity of spin dynamics to Fe content. [Magnetic anisotropy in the near-stoichiometric van der Waals ferromagnet Fe₃GeTe₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24543)
*   **Quantum Oscillation Signatures of Altermagnetism:** First-principles calculations on hole-doped RuO₂ predict unique angle-dependent quantum oscillation frequencies that can serve as a fingerprint for its altermagnetic state. [Quantum oscillation fingerprints of altermagnetism in hole-doped RuO₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24599)
*   **Altermagnetic Mott Insulator and Pairing:** DMRG studies of the doped checkerboard $t$-$J$ model reveal a transition from a uniform $d$-wave superconductor to a pair-density wave regime coexisting with stripe order, driven by altermagnetic exchange. [Pair-Density Wave from Doping an Altermagnetic Mott Insulator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23654)
*   **Emergent Integrability in a Rydberg Chain:** A Rydberg-atom chain shows that the state of the system, not just the Hamiltonian, determines the degree of integrability breaking, with ballistic or diffusive transport depending on particle density. [Emergent integrable dynamics in a non-integrable Rydberg-atom chain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22933)

### Spin, Chirality, and Transport

*   **Radiative Spin Caloritronics:** A new "spin thermal Hall effect" is predicted, where a radiative heat current generates a transverse accumulation of spin angular momentum in nonreciprocal magneto-optical systems. [Radiative Spin Caloritronics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22845)
*   **Theory of Andreev Reflection with Anisotropic Scattering:** A generalized model for Andreev reflection spectroscopy incorporates spin-dependent anisotropic interface scattering, resolving discrepancies in extracted material properties. [Theory of Andreev reflection spectroscopy with anisotropic spin-dependent scattering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22747)
*   **Spin-Orbit Torque Dynamics in Helimagnets:** A continuum theory for a quasi-1D helimagnet shows that domain walls connecting opposite chiralities can be driven by current and that chirality switching is possible via the adiabatic spin-transfer torque in a magnetic field. [Current- and field-driven domain wall dynamics and chirality switching in a planar helimagnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23252)
*   **Spin Relaxation Separates Current Injection from Dissipation:** A variational study of a spin Hall bar reveals that Joule dissipation vanishes when the load is placed at a distance greater than the spin relaxation length, a key feature of pure spin current injection. [Spin-Hall devices: spin relaxation spatially separates current injection from Joule dissipation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23589)
*   **Chiral Dynamics of an Intruder:** An analytically tractable model for a chiral intruder in a nonequilibrium bath connects geometry to ratchet effects in the dilute regime and to hydrodynamic edge currents in the dense regime. [Chiral Dynamics of an Intruder across Dilute and Hydrodynamic Regimes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23221)
*   **Anomalous Spin Transport in Dilute Networks:** A continuous-time random walk model for ¹³C polarization transport in diamond reveals anomalous transport characterized by heavy-tailed waiting times and subdiffusive mean-squared displacement, due to geometric trapping in clusters. [Continuous-Time Random Walk Description of Anomalous Spin Transport in Dilute Dipolar Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22626)
*   **Graphene Photodetectors:** Large-area CVD graphene on SiO₂/Si is shown to be a scalable platform for photodetectors, based on a photogating mechanism, with sensitivity decreasing with the number of layers. [Large-area CVD Graphene for Photogating-Based Photodetection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24344)
*   **Graphene THz Detector:** An antenna-coupled graphene photothermoelectric detector achieves a bandwidth exceeding 43 GHz, suitable for high-speed wireless communication. [Graphene Zero-Bias Sub-Terahertz Turnkey Detector with Above 43 GHz Bandwidth](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03554)

[Microphase Separation in Quorum-Sensing Active Particles with Competing Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23259)

*   **Shear-Mode Piezoelectric Response of Ferroelectric Nematics:** Direct piezoelectric shear-mode coupling constants are quantitatively measured for the first time in ferroelectric nematic liquid crystals. [Shear-mode Direct Piezoelectric Response of Ferroelectric Nematic Liquid Crystals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23297)
*   **Rare Events and Redundancy in Target Search:** A study on random walkers with power-law distributed jumps shows that redundancy (using many walkers) is extremely effective for finding a target, with a speed-up scaling as $1/N$. [Rare Events and Redundancy in Random Walkers Target Search in a Finite Domain](https://arxiv.org/ abs/2507.09452)
*   **Pushy Random Walk:** A new minimal model for transport in deformable media is introduced where a walker can push obstacles, leading to subdiffusive cavity growth in 1D and 2D. [Pushy Random Walk: A Minimal Model for Transport in Deformable Media](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07387)
*   **Entanglement in Quadratic Dissipative Systems:** A hydrodynamic description for the entanglement negativity in open fermionic and bosonic systems is developed, benchmarked on Kitaev and harmonic chains. [Fate of entanglement in quadratic Markovian dissipative systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15328)
*   **Quantum Fluctuations in Helium-4:** Path integral simulations of distinguishable helium-4 reveal a quantum fluctuation-driven transport crossover to a "gas-like" liquid state at low temperatures, characterized by superdiffusion and ultralow viscosity. [Quantum fluctuation-driven transport crossover between two liquid states in distinguishable helium-4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23127)
*   **Percolation and Yielding in Colloidal Gels:** Simulations expose a structural mirror between rigidity percolation and yielding in bimodal colloidal gels, identifying the same mesoscale bottlenecks as critical for both stiffness and failure. [Bimodal colloids highlight the structural mirror of rigidity percolation and yielding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22901)

[MatDiffract: A Material-Informed Automated Analysis Platform for X-ray Powder Diffraction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20880)
