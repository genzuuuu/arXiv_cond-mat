- **[Bounds for Apparent Second-Law Violations in Quantum Trajectories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10118)** — Proves a sharp fluctuation-theorem floor for negative entropy production; yields 'frequent but mild' law.
- **[Spin-current model of electric polarization with tensor gyromagnetic ratio](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22968)** — Predicts polarization from anisotropic g-factor in cycloidal and helicoidal orders.
