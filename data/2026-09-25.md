## Topological Phases, Quantum Geometry & Orbital Effects

Several works refine how topology and quantum geometry manifest in real materials. [Nodal-Line Semimetals with Non-Quantized Berry Phase](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29719) extends the nodal-line paradigm to rings carrying arbitrary fractional or irrational Berry phases, producing Landau-level splitting detectable via Shubnikov–de Haas oscillations and two surface states localized on the *same* boundary. [Power-law growth of shift current with superlattice period in flat Chern bands](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28687) shows the shift current is proportional to the cyclotron shift, yielding universal scaling laws verified in chiral Landau levels and moiré skyrmion crystals. [Interaction induced flattening of optical transition quantum geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28642) argues excitons possess uniform, flat quantum geometry, zeroing third-order circular photoconductivity—a striking interaction effect.

On the orbital front, [Theory of extrinsic contributions to the full orbital current](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28628) develops a quantum kinetic theory for wavevector-off-diagonal density matrices, finding disorder corrections comparable to the intrinsic orbital Hall current. [Valley Berry curvature dipole induced nonlinear valley Hall effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29226) proposes a new nonlinear valley response, demonstrated in strained Nb₃SBr₇. [Projected amorphous topological insulators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30260) and [Upper critical dimension for dirty Weyl semimetal-to-metal quantum phase transitions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30265) characterize topological order and criticality under strong disorder.

## Superconductivity: Pairing, Topology & Fluctuations

PDW order takes center stage. [Weak-Coupling Pair-Density-Wave from Momentum-Space Nonsymmorphic Symmetry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28630) identifies momentum-space nonsymmorphic symmetry as a generic route promoting PDW to a leading instability, including a topological spin-triplet state with BdG Chern number 8. [Majorana zero modes in half-quantum vortices of pair density wave superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28636) shows vortex-core energetics favor fractionalization near a PDW–Dirac-semimetal transition, enabling Majorana lattices.

Nickelates feature in [Nodal Orbital-Anti-Phase Superconducting State in Bilayer Nickelates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29263), where orbital-anti-phase gaps create extra nodes and modify low-temperature superfluid stiffness. [Hidden Zeeman Field in Odd-Parity Magnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15147) reveals OPMs host a hidden Zeeman field enabling robust topological superconductivity with large Majorana regions. On fluctuation physics, [Imaging how fluctuations destroy superconductivity in two dimensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28837) uses scanning magnetic susceptibility to show quantum fluctuations and spatial inhomogeneity jointly suppress 2D superconductivity. [Stimulation of superconductivity in $d$-wave superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28624) finds the Eliashberg enhancement window is bounded and not pinned to the gap edge.

Under pressure, [Hidden magnetic order within the pressure induced superconducting dome of UTe2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29168) uncovers a magnetic order inside the SC dome whose extrapolated quantum critical point coincides with maximum $T_c$. [Bounds on Bose-Einstein Condensation of Higgs modes in a Superconductor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29896) derives a density window for optically pumped Higgs condensation, while [Superconducting spin valve as a sensitive probe of spin-orbit coupling anisotropy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29244) extracts a 5% Dresselhaus contribution. Material discovery continues with [Superconductivity in structurally complex $\sigma$-Phase Re-X](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29354) and [Superconductivity and Band Topology in Functionalized 2D Hexagonal MBenes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29110), predicting $T_c$ up to ~32 K in Ti₂B₂O₂.

## Altermagnets, Frustrated Magnets & Spin Systems

[Altermagnetic Kondo Logic via Floquet Symmetry Conversion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28680) overcomes the symmetry-enforced blindness of the Kondo probe to altermagnetic splitting via an in-plane AC field, enabling field-controlled Kondo logic. [Antisymmetric breathing in altermagnetic skyrmions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29190) shows perpendicular fields create a radius difference between sublattice skyrmions, driving a 49.5 GHz antisymmetric breathing mode. [Spin-orbit coupling effects in altermagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01780) connects weak ferromagnetism and relativistic splitting via spin space groups.

[Defect Poisoning of Quantum Spin Ice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28643) warns that dilution as low as 2% generates vacancy-induced fluctuations that dominate and obscure quantum spin ice signatures. [From dimensional reduction to tetramerization in mixed ferro-antiferro breathing pyrochlores](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30162) combines Monte Carlo, PFFRG, and DMRG to reveal a tetramerized nonmagnetic window, with predictions for chromium thiospinels. [Exact phase diagram of the XXZ Heisenberg chain in a staggered magnetic field](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29728) provides an exact degenerate ground-state manifold on the ferromagnet–Luttinger-liquid boundary. [Exact selection of a toric-code vison crystal in a flux-conditioned Kitaev model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29004) realizes exact gauge-matter feedback selecting a topologically ordered vison crystal.

[Competing magnetic and spin vestigial orders from continuum field theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28620) maps the phase structure of competing AFM and spin-nematic orders, finding a first-order multicritical point and applications to Na₂Co₂TeO₆. Polymer/soft-matter spin analogs appear in [Orientational Ordering of Janus Particle Arrays as Classical Compass Spin Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30025), which resolves sixfold order via order-by-disorder with two BKT transitions.

## Computational & Methodological Advances

Green's function methods see major efficiency gains. [Compact representation of strongly correlated Green's functions: the MOR+EC way](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28647) combines eigenvector continuation and model order reduction, achieving 16–91× speedups in DMFT with ~10⁻⁴ relative error. [Full-frequency GW from Cayley-transformed self-energy moments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29271) reformulates GW via bounded Cayley moments, giving full-frequency spectra without plasmon-pole models. [Exact benchmarks for the plasmon-pole approximation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29635) quantifies PPA failure in multi-scale screening and proposes a computational diagnostic for its breakdown.

[Real-axis least-squares bath discretization for real-time transport in quantum-dot Josephson junctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30000) extends the driven Liouville–von Neumann approach to BCS leads, compressing cost by over an order of magnitude while recovering Shapiro locking and Andreev structure. [Plane-Wave Photon-Fock Cavity QED-DFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28786) casts first-principles cavity QED of solids into standard plane-wave machinery, revealing a quantized Chern number sequence in chiral-cavity graphene.

Machine learning permeates several frameworks. [Neural-Network Solutions to Real-Space Charge Density and Generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14906) introduces AIDEN for equivariant real-space charge density with zero-shot transfer. [Complete Neural Electronic Initialization Accelerates Materials DFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21759) presents AugNet for PAW augmentation occupancies, recovering up to 62% of potential initialization savings. [Atom-Resolved Machine Learning of Dielectric and Piezoelectric Response](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29978) predicts responses for 314 AlN/ScN stackings, identifying a candidate exceeding reference $d_{33,f}$ by 63%. [Hamiltonian learning reveals optoelectronic mechanisms across thermodynamic state space in soft semiconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29361) builds FLOW-OTTER to connect thermodynamic trajectories to band-gap mechanisms in halide perovskites.

[Full-frequency GW...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29271) and related methods [Beyond Bond Gauge: Exact Tensor-Network Tangent Spaces at Weighted Graph States](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30066) and [Leg-Tied Tensor Network States](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30101) advance variational and tensor-network representations, with LETTA reaching larger-MPS accuracy using an order of magnitude fewer parameters.

[Observation of Universal Quantum Chaos at Shallow Depths](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28623) demonstrates that universality emerges at shallow circuit depths on a superconducting processor, with distinct classes for lattices, chains, and rings. [Eigenoperator Entanglement Statistics in Local Lindbladians](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29215) identifies a universal log-normal eigenoperator-entanglement distribution for local Lindbladians, distinct from random-matrix predictions. [Associative Memory for Quantum Entangled States](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28726) generalizes Hopfield storage to entangled states, with capacity scaling suggesting quantum advantage. [Deep thermalization and Hilbert space ergodicity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30248) reviews how maximally random state distributions emerge from unitary dynamics, while [Classical Cellular Automaton for Measurement-Only Entanglement Transitions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28743) provides an exact classical matching representation of monitored entanglement dynamics.

[Amplified Memory and Finite-Time Regularity in Driven Non-Hermitian Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29137) isolates genuine drive history via imaginary-energy action, finding the finite-time propagator remains regular near exceptional endpoints. [Quantization and Mirror Reduction Do Not Commute in Hamiltonian Embeddings of Nonreciprocal Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28494) shows canonical quantization of a dissipative embedding is a singular semiclassical construction.
