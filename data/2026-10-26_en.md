# Today's arXiv Condensed Matter Physics — Key Advances

## Quantum Criticality, Entanglement & Mixed-State Order
A strong theory cluster probes how entanglement encodes beyond-CFT data. [Lattice Symmetry Remnants in Spatial and Temporal Entanglement of Quantum Critical Spin Chains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31676) shows LSM anomalies and lattice symmetries remain visible in universal entanglement via constrained BCFT boundary realizations. [Entanglement Asymmetry and Asymmetry Contour under Boundary Symmetry Breaking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32733) introduces an "asymmetry contour" giving spatially resolved symmetry-breaking information. [Succession of Ising criticality and its threshold in critical quantum Ising model subject to X+ZZ decoherence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17871) finds remnant Ising criticality (c=1/2) survives moderate decoherence before strong-to-weak SSB sets in. [Constructing Large and Structured Decoherence-Free Subspaces in Hybrid Quantum Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12348) builds exponentially large DFS with volume-law entanglement via Lie-algebra null states.

## Altermagnetism & Frustrated/Kagome Magnetism
[Altermagnetism in Folded Iron Chain Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31732) demonstrates chain-stacking altermagnetism (K₃Fe₂Se₄ family, d-wave, isotropic splitting) without C₄ symmetry. [Intrinsic Raman Modes and Crystal Symmetry of Altermagnetic α-MnTe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32261) reassigns spurious Raman features to Te precipitates and confirms centrosymmetric D₆h symmetry. [Compass Correlations in a Square-Lattice Magnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32157) reports first neutron evidence of compass correlations (ErRu₂Si₂) with dipolar contributions. [Triplon dynamics from partial Kondo screening in frustrated heavy-fermion systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32283) finds magnon–triplon hybridization in the partially disordered phase.

## Superconductivity: Unconventional Pairing & New Platforms
[Heterogeneous superconductivity emerging from a charge-stripe state in kagome lattices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32097) ties a short-range stripe CDW to spatially inhomogeneous, sign-changing pairing in CsV₃Sb₅₋ₓSnₓ. [Strain-tunable van Hove singularity and superconducting dome in monolayer kagome metal CsTi₃Bi₅](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32359) predicts a strain-tuned T_C dome (max 3.6 K at +2%). [Unifying description of competing chiral and nematic superconducting states in twisted bilayer graphene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06550) and [Topological multicomponent superconductivity with sizable s-wave admixture in twisted bilayer cuprates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08235) clarify chiral/nematic competition and s+d chiral topology. [Ideal interaction dynamics of defects and background fields in condensed matter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32973) is actually a modeling paper (Euler–Poincaré defect-field coupling); for superconductors see [Majorana Edge-to-bound State Transition in Interferometric Superconductor Rings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32767) and [Finite-Temperature Transconductance of Multiterminal Josephson Junctions with Topological Andreev Spectra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32839). Also: [Interplay of Chemical Disorder, Vortex Pinning, and Electron Correlations in the Mechanically Robust Medium-Entropy Alloy Superconductor Nb₀.₄₅Ta₀.₂₅Ti₀.₁₅Hf₀.₁₅](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32449).

## Topological Matter & Correlated Fermions
[Vacancy-driven topological transition of a Chern insulator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30241) explains strong-disorder Chern-number change via hybridizing in-gap vacancy ring states. [First- and Second-Order Electron Position Correlators Reveal Topological Phase Transitions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32743) recasts topology in correlator language. [Emergent relativistic symmetry from interacting fermions on the honeycomb bilayer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22259) finds Gross-Neveu-Ising criticality (z=2→1 crossover). [Spontaneous Decay of High Energy Composite Fermions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32763) shows energetic composite fermions fragment into quasiparticles and whole electrons. [Framework to identify defect-insensitive electron transport in topological semimetals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29848) standardizes NEGF defect screening (CoSi, PtAl, NbAs).

## Quantum Spin Liquids, Criticality & Spin Glasses
[SO(5) multicriticality in two-dimensional quantum magnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06607) argues deconfined criticality is preempted by a proximate SO(5) multicritical point. [Existence and Absence of Spin-Glass Phases on Migdal–Kadanoff Hierarchical Lattices: A Complete Classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32291) gives a rigorous necessary-and-sufficient condition. [Tuning the magnetic properties of Kitaev materials via the antiferromagnetic proximity effect: Novel phases and application to an α-RuCl₃/MnPS₃ bilayer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22310) predicts proximity-driven antichiral Kitaev spin liquid and skyrmion crystals. [Kagome goldene with flat bands and Dirac nodal line fermions via line-graph epitaxy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07945) reports elemental kagome goldene with flat band.

## Phonons, Thermal Transport & Spin-Phonon Coupling
[Phonon evidence for a partial, re-entrant spin reorientation near room temperature in LaFe₁₋ₓNiₓO₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31725) uses collective Raman anomalies plus a Landau spin-phonon model to infer partial Néel-vector reorientation. [Intrinsic Paramagnetism of Optical Phonons and Thermal Hall Effect in Quantum Paraelectrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32224) derives κ_xy ∝ A₊²−A₋² with power-law scaling near the quantum critical point. [Nanoscale origin of thermal anisotropy in monoclinic β-Ga₂O₃](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31976) pins anisotropy to short-MFP phonons above 4 THz. Also [Nonlocal Thermoelectric Transport in Degenerate Electron Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32272) (Guyer-Krumhansl form, validated in Cu/Ni) and [Extended Hydrodynamics of Planar Heisenberg Ferromagnetic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32929).

## Magnonics, Skyrmions & Time Crystals

[Floquet Engineering of Momentum Gaps in Magnonic Time Crystals: Slow-Drive Amplification and In-Gap Propagation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32766) shows waveform-controlled high-order momentum gaps. [Dipole chains of Bloch point singularities revealed by X-ray magnetic tomography](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31952) images stable Bloch/anti-Bloch chains down to 60 nm. [Robustness of Skyrmion-Mediated Magnetization Switching in Perpendicular Magnetic Tunnel Junctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32944) separates recoverable timing shifts from irreversible easy-axis disorder. [Laser-induced metastable (anti-)skyrmion states with higher-order topological charge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13112) nucleates |Q| up to 3. Also [Multifractal Magnons Around Localized Defects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32457) and [Coupling phase interference effects in a multimode cavity magnonics system](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20234).
