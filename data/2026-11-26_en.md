## Unconventional Superconductivity & Pairing

A DMRG study shows robust pairing in a four-flavor t–J_A model even at infinite Hubbard U, driven by phonon-mediated anti-Hund's coupling — with Cooper pairs living on bonds rather than from direct attraction, pointing to a strongly coupled superconductor beyond BCS and possible relevance to magic-angle graphene — [Pairing from anti-Hund's coupling under infinite Hubbard repulsion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33286).

ARPES reveals periodic modulations reminiscent of cuprate "hot spots" in doped bismuthates, consistent with electrons coupling to fluctuating density waves from residual bipolarons — [Spectroscopic Signatures of Polaronic Density Wave Fluctuations in Superconducting Bismuthates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33422).
