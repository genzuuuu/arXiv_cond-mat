## 1. Superconductivity — Mechanisms & Correlations

**New nickelate synthesis & pressure studies.** 
- Heavily Sr-doped La₂SrNi₂O₇₋δ achieves a tetragonal structure with straight Ni-O-Ni bonds, yet lacks superconductivity due to severe deviation from half-filling of the Ni-3d_{z²} band. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00542)
- First successful synthesis of Sm₄Ni₃O₁₀₋δ via HPHT shows a density-wave transition at ~180 K; superconductivity not reached up to 80 GPa due to bent Ni-O-Ni bonds. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00574)
- A unified microscopic picture explains the superconducting dome in La₀.₈Sr₀.₂NiO₂ under pressure: initial enhancement from kinetic scale increase, suppression from self-doping; d-wave pairing remains stable. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00586)

**Cuprate physics.**
- Experimental study of Tl₂Ba₂CuO₆ shows sharp superconducting anomalies persist far into the overdoped regime, consistent with smoothly decreasing pairing strength rather than disorder-driven suppression. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01213)
- A comment argues two-gap behavior in quadruple-layer cuprate (Cu,C)Ba₂Ca₃Cu₄O₁₁ reflects weakly coupled inner/outer planes whose local T_c is determined by nearest-neighbor structure. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00467)
- First-principles DFT+CDMFT shows apical-oxygen displacement modulates superconductivity primarily through hole-doping changes, not charge-transfer gap modifications. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16017)

**Flat-band & exotic superconductors.**
- YSR states in flat-band superconductors show a clean separation between universal localization length (set by compact localized states) and impurity-tunable quadratic spread. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00338)
- The in-plane critical field of Ising superconductors generates s+if pairing, producing a superconducting diode effect tunable by triplet-channel strength. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01476)
- MuSR confirms Pd₂ZrIn as a dirty-limit, fully gapped s-wave superconductor with preserved time-reversal symmetry. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19283)

**2D superconductor thermodynamics.**
- For 2D superconductors, the lower critical field is fundamentally size-dependent, scaling as ~A⁻¹ln(A/A₀) in weak screening and A⁻¹/² in strong screening, making the thermodynamic limit singular. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01602)

**Other.**
- Superconductivity in Te polyhydride with T_c ≈ 91 K at 263 GPa. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30139)

## 2. Topological Materials & Phases

**Majorana physics.**

- A nonabelian anyon violates Haag duality, implying gapped phases can fail the uniqueness-of-purifications principle at every point. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01267)
- Cavity embedding shifts the topological phase boundary of Majorana wires to lower Zeeman energies, especially in the semiclassical photon regime. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00926)
- Number-conserving DMRG study finds Majorana edge modes in isolated wires, revealed by correlation diagnostics and parity-dependent edge effects. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00158)

**Fractional & quantum Hall physics.**
