### Topological and Quantum Matter

**New Theoretical Frameworks and Models:** This group presents new models for topological phases, including pair-density-wave (PDW) superconductivity and higher-order topology. A key theme is the use of symmetry to stabilize and control topological behavior.
- A new class of microscopic models is developed where PDW order emerges from non-symmorphic momentum symmetry, suggesting moiré systems and altermagnets as potential hosts. [Pair density wave order from non-symmorphic momentum symmetry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05603)
- A general method is introduced for deriving simple real-space topological invariants, extending the spectral localizer formalism to classify weak, crystalline, and other phases with no previously known invariants. [Simple invariants for band topology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07775)
- A theory for staggered damping-like spin-orbit torque in synthetic antiferromagnets is developed, predicting that the domain-wall velocity depends on its internal structure (Néel vs. Bloch). [Theory of staggered damping-like spin-orbit torque on synthetic antiferromagnetic domain walls](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07055)
- This work develops a generalized Ginzburg-Landau theory for transitions breaking non-invertible symmetries, using the Symmetry Topological Field Theory to identify local order parameters. [Ginzburg-Landau Theory for Non-Invertible Symmetry-Breaking Transitions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06751)

**Topological & Correlated Electronic Structure:** This cluster uses first-principles and advanced computational methods to investigate topological and correlated phenomena in novel materials.
- The first-principles study accurately predicts the electric-field-driven topological phase transitions in silicene, germanene, and stanene by using self-consistent screened electronic structures, providing a marked improvement over previous approaches. [First principles calculations of electric-field-driven topological phase transitions in silicene, germanene and stanene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05633)
- The research develops a unified framework for multiorbital Hofstadter models on any lattice, providing gauge-covariant magnetic Bloch sums that are directly compatible with first-principles calculations, as shown for monolayer MoS2. [Gauge-covariant magnetic Bloch sums for general multiorbital Hofstadter models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05646)
- This work uncovers that surface defects cause the elusive surface magnetic gap in MnBi₂Te₄ to close, and shows that tuning the surface electrostatic potential via electric fields can restore the gap. [Restoring the Surface Magnetic Gap in MnBi₂Te₄](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06915)

**Majorana and Parafermion Physics:** Research here centers on engineering more exotic non-Abelian excitations beyond Majorana fermions.
- The paper proposes that bound states of 1/3 magnetic flux quanta in multicomponent superconductors will exhibit non-Abelian statistics of Z3 parafermions and describes a scheme for realizing braiding operations. [1/3-Flux Bound States in Multicomponent Superconductor Exhibiting Non-Abelian Statistics of Z3 Parafermions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08326)
- A model for a 3D second-order topological superconductor is built from nanowires, which hosts gapless helical Z2q parafermionic hinge modes, encompassing Majorana modes in the non-interacting limit. [Z2q parafermionic hinge states in a three-dimensional array of coupled nanowires](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07313)

**Quantum Dynamics & Information Spreading:** This set investigates quantum dynamics, non-equilibrium behavior, and how information spreads in many-body systems.

[Kibble-Zurek Dynamics in Two-dimensional Frustrated Systems with a Neural Foundation-state Subspace Method](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05556)

### Superconductivity

**Conventional and Unconventional Superconductors:** This group reports new experimental results on various superconducting systems, exploring their phase diagrams and the nature of their pairing.
- New experiments map the vortex melting phase diagram of (La,Pr)₃Ni₂O₇, finding nearly isotropic superfluid density that implies a strong 3D contribution from the γ band. [Nearly Isotropic Vortex Solid in (La,Pr)₃Ni₂O₇ Thin Films](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07510)
- A magneto-mechanical gyroscope with spintronic readout is demonstrated, using magnetic tunnel junctions to sense motion and extract angular rate. [A magneto-mechanical gyroscope with spintronic readout](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05668)
- This paper reports the first observation of bulk superconductivity in an intrinsically grown bulk moiré superlattice material, (Sr6TaS8)1+x(TaS2)8, with a Tc of 2.5 K. [Discovery of Superconductivity in a Bulk Moire Superlattice Material](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06622)

**Superconducting Devices and Effects:** This cluster focuses on applications and unique effects in superconducting systems.
- A junction-free, polarity-tunable superconducting diode effect is realized in an all-van der Waals NbSe2/Fe3GeTe2 heterostructure, demonstrating its use in an XNOR logic gate. [Junction-free polarity-tunable superconducting diode effect enables XNOR logic operation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09097)
- A theory for thermally activated phase slips in noncentrosymmetric superconducting wires predicts a magnetochiral anisotropy in the resistance that is linked to the superconducting diode effect. [Magnetochiral anisotropy of thermally activated phase slips in noncentrosymmetric superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08237)
- π₀-junctions and a Josephson diode effect are predicted for junctions with a PT-symmetric antiferromagnet, with both effects controlled by the Néel vector orientation. [Néel-Vector Control of the Josephson Diode Effect in PT-symmetric Antiferromagnets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08278)

**Iron-Based and Cuprate Superconductors:** This set of papers provides new insights into the pairing mechanisms and order in high-temperature superconductors.
- Scanning SQUID and STM studies on stoichiometric FeTe reveal a nodal or deep-minima superconducting state, which is a departure from the behavior seen at intermediate Se/Te compositions. [Signatures of nodal superconductivity in stoichiometric FeTe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08116)
- High magnetic field measurements uncover an extended strange metal regime above the superconducting dome in FeSe1-xTex, linking it to pure electronic nematic criticality and disorder. [Extended strange metal regime in a pure nematic quantum critical superconductor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05875)
- This analysis suggests a chemical perspective on cuprate superconductivity, where d-wave Cooper pairs are protected spin dimers within antiferromagnetic hole dimers. [Chemical Perspectives on Cuprate Superconductivity: Hole-Protected Spin Dimers as d Wave Cooper Pairs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30001)

**Theoretical & Computational Superconductivity:** This cluster presents new computational and theoretical methods for studying superconducting systems.
- A comprehensive microscopic calculation method for coherence lengths and penetration depth in multiband superconductors is introduced, agreeing well with Bogoliubov-de Gennes calculations but with reduced computational cost. [Microscopic calculation of coherence lengths and magnetic penetration depth in multiband superconductors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06014)
- The study theoretically explores field-induced triplet superconductivity in monolayer NbSe2, finding that a chiral-d singlet state induces a corresponding chiral-p triplet component. [Symmetry-selective field-induced triplet superconductivity in Ising-superconductor monolayer NbSe2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06752)
- A model of superconducting 3D constrictions shows that geometry itself can redistribute current and suppress "current crowding" in a tunable way with magnetic field. [Geometry-induced current decrowding in superconducting 3D constrictions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08349)
